## Limits

- Failure-selection descriptions should not be extrapolated to arbitrary nonfinite rewards. [MEM-OBJ-trajectories](memory.md#mem-obj-trajectories), [RT-RTE-failure-review](runtime.md#rt-rte-failure-review) and [EPI-RTE-failure-shaping](epistemic.md#epi-rte-failure-shaping) describe below-threshold selection, while [EPI-RTE-score-check](epistemic.md#epi-rte-score-check) already records the lack of nonfinite reward validation. SRC-1, `src/ecdysis/evolution.py` converts rewards to floats and excludes a failure when its reward is missing or greater than/equal to the threshold. A NaN comparison does not satisfy that exclusion, so NaN can enter failure review despite not being below threshold. Withhold a universal strict-less-than guarantee or valid-score interpretation for nonfinite inputs. This local exceptional-input omission does not change the finite-score workflow, metadata visibility, strict candidate-reference comparison, memory consumers or epistemic licensing conclusions.
