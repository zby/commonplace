### Containers, parts and distinct referents

[EPI-OBJ-outcome](epistemic.md#epi-obj-outcome) is not identical to [MEM-OBJ-intermediate-representation](memory.md#mem-obj-intermediate-representation). The former isolates outcome classification/evaluation intelligence; the latter also contains categorized thoughts, patterns, confidence and trajectory descriptors. SRC-1 §3.1.1 supports this narrower-part relation. The intermediate remains a valid container, so neither supersession nor replacement by the outcome alone is warranted. [EPI-OBJ-decision-analysis](epistemic.md#epi-obj-decision-analysis) instead identifies backward attribution and proposed improvements downstream of that representation, under SRC-1 §3.1.2; it is not another name for the intermediate input.

[EPI-OBJ-description-index](epistemic.md#epi-obj-description-index) combines description text and retrieval embeddings. [MEM-OBJ-descriptions](memory.md#mem-obj-descriptions) separates the text, while [MEM-OBJ-embeddings](memory.md#mem-obj-embeddings) also includes tip-content/purpose and query vectors. SRC-1 §§3.2.1–3.3.1 therefore establishes overlap, not identity between the index and either memory record or their entire union. The epistemic record distinguishes textual and numerical parts in its findings; separating them does not make those findings wrong. It remains a valid container alongside the memory records, without a split supersession. Both reports preserve the unresolved relationship between content/purpose vectors and description indices; neither supplies a complete executable schema.

The memory objects are not duplicates of runtime components, routes or authority paths. In particular, [RT-CMP-text-embedding](runtime.md#rt-cmp-text-embedding) is the producing model, not its vector outputs. [RT-CMP-gpt4](runtime.md#rt-cmp-gpt4) is likewise not the retained guidance it produces or consumes. Both reports preserve opaque provider state and unpinned-version limits without attributing parameter learning.

The epistemic routes are material parts, not aliases of the runtime routes. [EPI-RTE-report-interpretation](epistemic.md#epi-rte-report-interpretation), [EPI-RTE-self-outcome](epistemic.md#epi-rte-self-outcome) and [EPI-RTE-attribution-generation](epistemic.md#epi-rte-attribution-generation) separate report interpretation, unlabeled checking and causal/transfer conjecture inside [RT-RTE-trajectory-extraction](runtime.md#rt-rte-trajectory-extraction). [EPI-RTE-conflict-check](epistemic.md#epi-rte-conflict-check) and [EPI-RTE-conflict-disposition](epistemic.md#epi-rte-conflict-disposition) separate comparison from successor inclusion inside [RT-RTE-tip-consolidation](runtime.md#rt-rte-tip-consolidation). [EPI-RTE-benchmark-check](epistemic.md#epi-rte-benchmark-check) isolates external scoring within [RT-RTE-agent-execution](runtime.md#rt-rte-agent-execution). Their declared containment preserves valid end-to-end routes; no supersession is warranted.

### Convergence and integration dispositions

The [workflow account](workflow.md#workflow-account) selects subtask extraction and LLM-guided retrieval from runtime's alternatives. Its model-call handoffs agree with memory's extraction annotation: scoped extraction does not establish separate security principals or a deployed multi-agent runtime. It declares no competing records. Its five-tip setting concerns [RT-RTE-llm-selection](runtime.md#rt-rte-llm-selection), not every retrieval configuration. Memory's budget issue is therefore resolved as a shared configuration limit: [RT-RTE-cosine-retrieval](runtime.md#rt-rte-cosine-retrieval) retains top-3 and uncapped alternatives, and none of the reports establishes a token cap.

Memory's rationale-preservation issue converges with [EPI-CLM-provenance-validation](epistemic.md#epi-clm-provenance-validation): explanatory purpose and source IDs are specified, but full attribution retention, dereferenceable evidence and executed auditing are not. This is uncertainty about the audit path, not a disagreement about the positive metadata design.

Admission and acceptance also remain compatible. Runtime, memory and workflow describe model-mediated semantic admission during consolidation. The epistemic conflict-check and disposition records recognize outcome-consuming curation while withholding demonstrated acceptance of a tip's causal explanation or general applicability. Thus operational retention is positive even though the epistemic ledger finds no post-acceptance lifecycle integration within the document boundary. That finding does not negate consolidation or claim absent deployed functionality.

Delivery, completion and benchmark success are separate in all four accounts. [RT-BAP-guidelines-channel](runtime.md#rt-bap-guidelines-channel) describes advisory prompt delivery; workflow distinguishes that event from consumer termination and scoring; the epistemic benchmark part limits its oracle to task/scenario results. Memory's unresolved physical persistence, rejection, withdrawal, reload, rollback and online-update scheduling remain limits, not evidenced absences. No report establishes an automatic repair attempt or memory write after each held-out failure.

[EPI-CLM-causal-attribution](epistemic.md#epi-clm-causal-attribution), [EPI-CLM-provenance-validation](epistemic.md#epi-clm-provenance-validation) and [EPI-CLM-feedback-cycle](epistemic.md#epi-clm-feedback-cycle) are distinct claims, not duplicates of [RT-CLM-reported-results](runtime.md#rt-clm-reported-results). They concern explanatory accuracy, auditability and a repeated improvement cycle respectively; runtime's claim concerns configuration-level outcomes. The reports converge on gains and counterexamples as attributed results, not component-isolated causal evidence. The source's broad improvement language and five-tip language are tensions already retained within runtime and epistemic, not disagreements between those reports.

Runtime's theory accounts and epistemic's part-level assessments retain the same separately bounded conditions: localized guidance and intended semantic consumption are claimed; retained content-directed criticism and iteration are uninspected. Memory and workflow do not close those gaps. All preserve the missing specific-guidance-to-later-behavior causal link rather than inferring exercised self-improvement from delivery or aggregate gains.
