### Omission questions and stopping decision

The frozen tree, host registration and producer/consumer dependencies guided three consequential challenges beyond the reports' listed passages. First, could workspace inputs bypass task links and relevance selection? Yes: live saved-panel projects have an automatically wired projection, retained below as an inventory limit. Second, could other shipped publishers invalidate the admission account? Inspection of SRC-1, `applications/session-management/publish_update.py` and `semantic-store/authoring/publication.py` found additional explicit maintenance writers. The update publisher has its own receipt predicate and preserves existing task/instruction state; the authoring publisher writes directory and instruction memories. These fit the reports' already plural, nonexclusive admission account. They do not invalidate the initial-publication gate or establish per-content human control. Their presence does not justify an exhaustive publisher comparison.

Third, could a separate evaluator force generic production publication, authenticate model summaries or establish measured learning? The traced candidate-test, publication, completion and benchmark consumers do not provide those stronger links. The reports already withhold them rather than emit system-wide absences. Uninspected acceptance suites and native backend/storage leaves therefore remain possible additional checks, not evidence against the supported local wiring findings. Further omission expansion stopped because the selected challenges have dispositions and no identified material contradiction remains. A full native security, durability, evaluator or publisher audit is not necessary for the bounded conclusions these reports actually retain.
