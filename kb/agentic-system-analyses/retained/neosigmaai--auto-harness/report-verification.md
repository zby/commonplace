- [RT-RTE-prepare-baseline](runtime.md#rt-rte-prepare-baseline) and [RT-RTE-gate-admission](runtime.md#rt-rte-gate-admission) do not establish same-population baseline/gate comparability for a non-default Harbor dataset. SRC-1 `prepare.py` omits the configured dataset in both Terminal baseline constructors, while `gating.py` and the benchmark CLI forward it. The preparation constructor includes:

  > all_runner = TerminalBenchRunner(
  >                 agent_model=cfg.get("agent_model"),
  >                 split=None,  # run all tasks
  >                 env_provider=cfg.get("env_provider", "e2b"),
  >                 n_concurrent=cfg.get("max_concurrency", 50),
  >                 reasoning_effort=cfg.get("reasoning_effort"),
  >             )
  > --- `prepare.py`

  The gate supplies:

  > gate_runner = TerminalBenchRunner(
  >             agent_model=cfg.get("agent_model"),
  >             split=cfg.get("gate_split", "test"),
  >             env_provider=cfg.get("env_provider", "e2b"),
  >             n_concurrent=cfg.get("max_concurrency", 50),
  >             dataset=cfg.get("dataset", "terminal-bench@2.0"),
  > --- `gating.py`

  SRC-1 `benchmark.py` defaults to `terminal-bench@2.0`. A custom experiment's initial split and score floor therefore cannot be assumed to describe the later configured population. Withhold the README's broader custom-Harbor “all work as-is” implication. The [epistemic boundary](epistemic.md#source-and-claim-boundary) and [reconciliation](reconciliation.md#reconciliation) retain this qualification.
