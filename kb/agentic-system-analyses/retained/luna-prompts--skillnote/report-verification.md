### Omission questions and stopping decision

The search basis was the frozen tracked tree, hook manifest, CLI dispatch and producer/consumer references, not only the submitted anchors. I investigated three consequential questions: whether conversational traces create another retained candidate; whether selection/cleanup bypasses normal sync cache guards; and whether feedback reaches a later consumer other than context-bundle. The additional mechanisms below make the inventories incomplete, but the reports already disclaim universal history, revocation, semantic evaluation and complete route coverage. No emitted comparison value or complete negative needs repair.

SRC-1, `plugin/hooks-handlers/prompt-watch.sh`, is synchronously wired by `plugin/hooks/hooks.json`. Its explicit-save branch emits additionalContext pointing toward skill-push; its linguistic-signal branch extracts a sentence and writes a local pending draft with the utterance, signal, session and a manual refinement instruction. This is automatic candidate extraction, not automatic registry adoption. A scoped search for draft consumers found this writer, not a wired automatic reload/adoption chain. The file itself affords later human review:

> If this looks worth turning into a full skill, run \`/skillnote:skill-push\` to refine and publish it. Delete this file to dismiss the draft.
> --- `plugin/hooks-handlers/prompt-watch.sh`

SRC-1, `backend/app/api/setup.py`, installs a shell wrapper invoking the picker before interactive Claude launch. In `plugin/bin/skillnote-pick`, initial cleanup and collection change/skip branches can remove managed copies independently of sync. In particular:

> if changed and current is not None:
>         _clean_skills_dir()
>     _run_sync()
> --- `plugin/bin/skillnote-pick`

This prevents exporting a handler-local cache guarantee to every shipped installation-management path. It does not contradict the reports' path-scoped cache findings.

SRC-1, `plugin-openclaw/skillnote/SKILL.md`, supplies a distinct doctrine-level consumer of analytics:

> | Past 20 skills, picking accuracy degrades | Bias toward skills with high `call_count`/`avg_rating` (visible in `<host>/v1/analytics/top-skills`); a future version offloads picking to a server-side resolver |
> --- `plugin-openclaw/skillnote/SKILL.md`

I traced its producer in SRC-1, `backend/app/api/analytics.py`: top-skills combines call counts, SkillRating means and usage outcomes, unlike context-bundle's Comment means. The shipped prompt requests use of those aggregates, but no host execution is evidenced. Searches for context-bundle and session-eval callers also located SRC-1, `plugin-openclaw/skillnote-doctor/SKILL.md`: its bundle call is a health probe, not demonstrated semantic resolver wiring. The current hook manifest has no Stop evaluation producer. These searches support the reports' caution; they do not establish system-wide absence.

Further optional omission expansion stopped because each selected question has a supported disposition, and no identified challenge remains capable of invalidating the retained central conclusions without first establishing additional evidence. Remaining available files alone do not justify an exhaustive audit. Complete authoring, cleanup and feedback-consumer comparisons must remain withheld as specified below; the architectural distinctions, supported positive routes and unobserved-operation assessments remain publishable.
