**Workflow and admission.** The selected [maintenance workflow](workflow.md#workflow-account) connects shipped steps rather than unrelated capabilities. Its partial-result, retry and rejection account does not promise whole-run rollback or automatic criticism-driven repair. Parent compatibility checks and body/citation restoration are correctly scoped to [RT-RTE-revision-admission](runtime.md#rt-rte-revision-admission), not semantic truth or restoration of the entire prior state. The separate chat path remains visible: [RT-RTE-chat-turn](runtime.md#rt-rte-chat-turn) supports apply-mode creation without the selected approvals required for update, move and delete. The text therefore does not imply universal human review or a shared grounding gate.
