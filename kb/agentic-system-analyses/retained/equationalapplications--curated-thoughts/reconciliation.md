# Curated Thoughts reconciliation

## Reconciliation

The reports share the [frozen whole-system boundary](boundary.md#boundary-and-evidence) and its [Source register](boundary.md#source-register). Their connections below concern inspected implementation and declared intent, not supplied executions. No source reread was needed. No correction answers or refusal were supplied. No full-referent duplicate requiring supersession, or disagreement requiring an unresolved-conflict paragraph, was identified. The overlapping records describe containers, narrower operations or different consumers; none warrants replacing another report's finding.

### Objects and authority paths

[EPI-OBJ-source-chunks](epistemic.md#epi-obj-source-chunks) concerns the numbered indexed source material assembled for synthesis. [MEM-OBJ-source-material](memory.md#mem-obj-source-material) covers vault files and indexed chunks, including their acquisition and storage. Both connect external source assertions to [RT-RTE-librarian-synthesis](runtime.md#rt-rte-librarian-synthesis). Whole-record identity is ruled out; neither record's findings become wrong when the part is distinguished.

[EPI-OBJ-wiki-facts](epistemic.md#epi-obj-wiki-facts) identifies retained title/body assertions and their metadata. [MEM-OBJ-wiki-content](memory.md#mem-obj-wiki-content) also includes entity summaries and symbolic entity/edge/task structure. The fact object is narrower than that retained-content container. [EPI-OBJ-fact-evidence](epistemic.md#epi-obj-fact-evidence) separately identifies quotation and lineage rows, while [MEM-OBJ-verification-ledgers](memory.md#mem-obj-verification-ledgers) identifies confirmation, attestation and denial carriers. Neither evidence object is identical to fact prose or to the other metadata object: live-source anchoring, content-hash endorsement and denial suppression have different consumers and meanings. No object supersession follows.

[MEM-OBJ-embedding-blobs](memory.md#mem-obj-embedding-blobs) is the retained numerical output of an embedding process; [RT-CMP-embed-profile](runtime.md#rt-cmp-embed-profile) is the configured inference component. Their producer/product connection does not establish identity or weight adaptation. [EPI-OBJ-proposal-items](epistemic.md#epi-obj-proposal-items) likewise holds produced proposals rather than the static instructions in [RT-OBJ-synthesis-prompt](runtime.md#rt-obj-synthesis-prompt).

[MEM-BAP-retained-fact](memory.md#mem-bap-retained-fact) and [RT-BAP-librarian-guidance](runtime.md#rt-bap-librarian-guidance) share the librarian as one consumer, but the former follows retained content into librarian and external-agent invocations, while the latter follows shipped guidance and assembled messages into synthesis. Their input scopes and horizons differ. They overlap without being interchangeable authority paths. Neither is the symbolic capability restriction in [RT-BAP-bridge-tool-gate](runtime.md#rt-bap-bridge-tool-gate).

### Generation, checking, admission and retention

[EPI-RTE-evidence-filter](epistemic.md#epi-rte-evidence-filter) is explicitly part of [RT-RTE-librarian-synthesis](runtime.md#rt-rte-librarian-synthesis). [EPI-RTE-anchor-check](epistemic.md#epi-rte-anchor-check) and [EPI-RTE-fact-retention](epistemic.md#epi-rte-fact-retention) are explicitly parts of [RT-RTE-proposal-admission](runtime.md#rt-rte-proposal-admission). These parts separate checks and persistence without invalidating the broader routes, so no combined record is superseded.

The evidence-filter finding permits a nonempty added fact to reach proposal storage after unknown references are omitted. The anchor-check finding prevents an unanchored fact_add from reaching live entry storage through the shared commit path. These are successive gates, not conflicting findings about one gate. The [workflow account](workflow.md#workflow-account) likewise distinguishes proposal storage from final admission. Its statement that facts can survive omitted references concerns proposal validation, not an assertion that the later anchor gate accepts them. The epistemic refinement therefore blocks an inference from permissive proposal filtering to unanchored committed facts without correcting the runtime or workflow findings.

The memory annotation on [RT-RTE-librarian-synthesis](runtime.md#rt-rte-librarian-synthesis), the epistemic evidence-filter account and the workflow model-delegation account converge on a separate limit: the model sees truncated context, but identifier validation uses the full pre-truncation collections. Valid identifiers do not prove source visibility or semantic support. [MEM-OBJ-wiki-content](memory.md#mem-obj-wiki-content) and the memory annotation on [RT-RTE-proposal-admission](runtime.md#rt-rte-proposal-admission) further distinguish persisted proposal reasoning from the bodies selected for later consumers. Retention of reasons is not universal delivery of those reasons.

[RT-OBJ-folder-rules](runtime.md#rt-obj-folder-rules), [RT-RTE-proposal-admission](runtime.md#rt-rte-proposal-admission) and the [workflow account](workflow.md#workflow-account) agree that automatic accept-all and manual decisions are alternative admission controls. [EPI-RTE-fact-confirmation](epistemic.md#epi-rte-fact-confirmation) adds a personal-endorsement architecture, not proof that every generated proposition receives human approval or an evidence-consuming acceptance event. The reports neither equate transaction consistency with truth nor infer exercised acceptance from a human-facing interface.

### Withdrawal, correction and recovery

These are overlapping native subroutes, not duplicate complete maintenance systems. The different GUI backend remains a limit on policy generalization. The runtime container stays valid; its external-engine admission uncertainty is not resolved by inspecting native healing. [EPI-RTE-source-forgetting](epistemic.md#epi-rte-source-forgetting) is a separate exact-source-reference hard-erasure operation, not healing or delayed prune.

[EPI-RTE-supersession-pending](epistemic.md#epi-rte-supersession-pending) and the [memory write-side account](memory.md#write-side) independently identify the supersession application's pending state. The status operation is not identical to ordinary [RT-RTE-deposit-kick](runtime.md#rt-rte-deposit-kick), nor does its limitation negate an existing-chain reader.

The [workflow account](workflow.md#workflow-account) connects source-file acknowledgement, worker progress, proposal production, admission and later return as separate milestones. It agrees with [RT-RTE-librarian-synthesis](runtime.md#rt-rte-librarian-synthesis) that logged generation failure can return successful unit status without proposals. These findings prevent reading a kick's ingested label as completed knowledge delivery. Watcher handoff, pending review, transaction rollback and embedding repair remain bounded recovery routes, not an exactly-once completion guarantee.
