## Bounded conclusion

**Formal proof authority and source authority are different routes.** The proof/disproof contract checks an exact Lean target; a sketch establishes a parent only under imported child propositions, with later Proved-child closure required. No actual accepted candidate, successful checker execution or backend implementation was supplied. This does not negate the workflow's explicit formal criteria; it prevents attributing exercised acceptance or unconditional warrant to a concrete candidate. See [RT-RTE-solver-proof](runtime.md#rt-rte-solver-proof), [RT-RTE-reduction-proof](runtime.md#rt-rte-reduction-proof), SRC-1, `references/prove.md`.

**The source-facing criterion is faithfulness, not ease or formal truth.** Captain checks, blind testimony, human comparison and moderator feedback are separately prescribed. The auditor is explicitly forbidden to decide faithfulness. Human confirmations are intended to accept source-faithful, well-posed mission items for that mission; public moderation admits the mission to the catalog after declarations are already published. Direct publication and upload-first are counterexamples to a universal human or moderator gate. See [RT-RTE-proposal-audit](runtime.md#rt-rte-proposal-audit), [RT-CLM-human-gate](runtime.md#rt-clm-human-gate), SRC-1, `references/mission_captain.md`, `references/mission_auditor.md`.

**Retention and operational reliance can precede proof.** Open child targets are published and imported as tracked assumptions; milestone links authorize solvers to use the attested formalization as-is. Failed attempts, human flags and captain reasons are prescribed inputs to later selection/revision. Descriptions, explanations and source fields may change independently of immutable formal declarations; they are not newly verified merely because their associated proof has a status. Deprecation hides discovery while preserving imports and proof status; unlinking preserves mission membership; deletion can destroy milestone history. These distinctions bound withdrawal and lineage claims. See [RT-RTE-content-publish](runtime.md#rt-rte-content-publish), [RT-BAP-milestone-guidance](runtime.md#rt-bap-milestone-guidance), [EPI-OBJ-description](#epi-obj-description), [EPI-OBJ-review](#epi-obj-review), SRC-1, `references/contribute.md`, `references/mission_captain.md`.

**Campaign attestation accepts comparability, not an unproved bound's truth.** The moderator checks the shared-definition template and records a number; an Open goal is a legitimate proposed-value entry. The latest-value field excludes Open goals and uses recording recency, while preferred value sorting is a separate rule. Therefore neither entry inclusion nor newest timestamp independently establishes mathematical achievement or optimality. See [EPI-RTE-campaign-review](#epi-rte-campaign-review), [EPI-RTE-campaign-admission](#epi-rte-campaign-admission), [EPI-RTE-campaign-selection](#epi-rte-campaign-selection), SRC-1, `references/campaigns.md`.

**Extraction has inspectable, narrower authority than conversion.** The helpers derive facts from elaboration and syntax, with a concrete deduplication/filter trace in [EPI-RTE-extraction-facts](#epi-rte-extraction-facts). Original-position restrictions and recursive-local exclusions bound the result reaching the consumer. Prescribed exact-payload builds and original/staged elaborated-type diffs are needed after generation; no shipped generator or executed comparison establishes semantic preservation here. Source mathematical warrant remains unknown, whereas helper-derived structural facts have implementation support in their stated domain. See [EPI-CLM-graph-truth](#epi-clm-graph-truth), SRC-1, `scripts/extract_sketch_info.lean`, `scripts/extract_decl_graph.lean`, `references/upload_full_project.md`.

No concrete newly accepted ampliative mathematical claim is individuated. Source import is acquisition; intended translations remain indeterminate without input/output pairs; checked formal derivation is a distinct, conditional doctrine route. Quality signals and collection membership have ranking/editorial authority rather than truth authority. Version mismatch can request replacement of behavior-guiding instructions without a truth-apt product or demonstrated improvement. See [EPI-RTE-quality-signals](#epi-rte-quality-signals), [EPI-RTE-collection-admission](#epi-rte-collection-admission), [RT-RTE-workspace-refresh](runtime.md#rt-rte-workspace-refresh), SRC-1, `references/curate.md`, `references/collections.md`, `SKILL.md`.

Model parameters, version resolution and internal truth-apt representations remain uninspected: see [RT-CMP-agent-model](runtime.md#rt-cmp-agent-model). That uncertainty does not erase inspectable workflow affordances. Product-review iteration, later-use instructions and instruction refresh do not independently establish improved future capacity, reflection about the workflow, autonomous performance of every internal operation, or exercised self-improvement. The human-dependent proposal route and the separately host-dependent solver route keep their independent property limits in the supplied records. No system-wide epistemic score or complete-negative verdict follows.
