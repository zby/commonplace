## Bounded conclusion

The inspected wiki routes acquire external/session content and deliver retained claims to later answer/write decisions; see [RT-RTE-wiki-triage](runtime.md#rt-rte-wiki-triage), [EPI-RTE-wiki-compose](#epi-rte-wiki-compose), [EPI-RTE-wiki-retain](#epi-rte-wiki-retain), [RT-BAP-wiki-guidance](runtime.md#rt-bap-wiki-guidance). Source attribution and body hashes preserve pointers/self-consistency, not source truth; [EPI-RTE-raw-capture](#epi-rte-raw-capture). Requested deterministic repair and active archive withdrawal differ from accepted-claim integration; [EPI-RTE-wiki-remediate](#epi-rte-wiki-remediate), [EPI-RTE-wiki-archive](#epi-rte-wiki-archive). Archive changes active navigation but does not establish complete exclusion from the query's recursive Markdown search. Semantic warrant remains unknown. First synthesis is explicitly retained while user validation is pending; confidence is not a recorded evidence-consuming truth acceptance. Cross-page reasoning supplies candidate production, but no inspected input/output establishes entailment or non-entailment. Thus neither accepted ampliative knowledge nor its lifecycle integration is established by this witness.

Checks have different domains and forces. Wiki lint establishes only mechanical predicates and its exit permits warnings; [EPI-RTE-lint-check](#epi-rte-lint-check), [EPI-RTE-lint-dispose](#epi-rte-lint-dispose). Semantic conflict checking requests that both positions survive with human-facing dispute signals, not that retrieval is blocked; [EPI-RTE-wiki-critic](#epi-rte-wiki-critic), [EPI-RTE-wiki-dispute](#epi-rte-wiki-dispute). Intent-preserving task repair has proposed computational veto and rollback see [RT-RTE-readiness-repair](runtime.md#rt-rte-readiness-repair); it is not a universal pre-write gate.

A stronger named acceptance criterion exists for task completion: code/tests plus every body/Acceptance item and design signal precede audited stamping. Later close-out consumes that status, rechecking on concrete drift evidence; [EPI-RTE-task-audit](#epi-rte-task-audit), [EPI-RTE-task-stamp](#epi-rte-task-stamp). This licenses task-specific completion reliance under the protocol, not general factual truth, test completeness or observed success. Initiating the audit is human control of the request; the model determines the verdict/stamp.

The wiki evaluator implements scoped artifact checking, final run-completion gates and regression reporting, not product-change acceptance or causal explanation. Normalization omits some response context; report predicates are self-report checks. Assertion averages and final passed are distinct and remain distinct in aggregation; [EPI-RTE-report-normalize](#epi-rte-report-normalize), [EPI-RTE-scenario-check](#epi-rte-scenario-check), [EPI-RTE-scenario-dispose](#epi-rte-scenario-dispose), [EPI-RTE-benchmark-rollup](#epi-rte-benchmark-rollup). These deterministic deductions are warranted conditional on input artifacts and inspected fixed interpretation, not on claims that a worker actually complied.

SCHEMA lint acknowledgements directly adapt future report behavior without a truth-apt content route: info matches disappear from live counts, with normal-mode acknowledgement and quiet-mode omission; [EPI-RTE-lint-acknowledge](#epi-rte-lint-acknowledge). This is policy-input consumption; the inspected shaper can author size exceptions, so a mechanically accepted finding need not evidence human review. It neither proves factual endorsement nor suppresses blocking issues.

Standalone compression/editing provides fidelity contracts and requested self-checks, not demonstrated semantic preservation or independent acceptance; [EPI-RTE-text-reshape](#epi-rte-text-reshape), [EPI-RTE-text-check](#epi-rte-text-check). Parameters remain opaque see [RT-CMP-host-model](runtime.md#rt-cmp-host-model). These positives leave learning benefit, exercised reflection, theory-specific content criticism/iteration and self-improvement attribution independently unestablished: no selected retained theory instance or operation-responsive method revision and later exercised consumer trace was assessed. Computational ownership is supported for bookkeeping and requested task/wiki substeps; it does not imply autonomous reliable knowledge production. Remaining skill/evaluator families prevent a whole-system negative or a single epistemic verdict.
