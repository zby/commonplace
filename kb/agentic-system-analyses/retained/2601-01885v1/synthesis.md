# AgeMem synthesis

## Bounded synthesis

### Evidence and architectural purpose

AgeMem makes long-term memory editing and short-term context management actions of the same task policy. Its claimed work is to learn when to retain, retrieve, revise, remove or compress information for long-horizon tasks, rather than leave every memory operation to a fixed external schedule. The evidence here is SRC-1: the complete HTML capture of arXiv 2601.01885v1, labelled `arxiv-2601.01885v1-html-2026-10-12`, with applicability cutoff 2026-10-12. This is a **doc-grounded, complete-artifact/partial-loop** analysis. The paper includes prompts, schemas, algorithms, evaluator descriptions and attributed experiments, but no frozen executable callers, raw runs or independently inspectable experimental artifacts. Architectural findings therefore describe a declared design, not verified implementation or observed activation ([boundary](boundary.md#boundary-and-evidence), [source register](boundary.md#source-register)).

The declared runtime divides responsibility between a driver, an agent policy, memory/context tools and training/evaluation consumers. The driver supplies messages, stages and bounded iteration. The policy chooses language, final answers and explicit calls. Tools change an external store or the active conversation context. A summarizer generates replacement prose; an encoder supplies cosine matching. Training scorers feed an optimizer, while benchmark evaluators feed reported comparisons. Agentscope and Trinity are named external frameworks, not inspected implementations. The Qwen policy backbones, encoder, summarizer and Qwen-Max evaluator have separate consumption roles; their exact deployed versions and provider behavior are not frozen ([RT-RTE-task-rollout](runtime.md#rt-rte-task-rollout), [RT-RTE-policy-update](runtime.md#rt-rte-policy-update), [RT-CMP-agent-policy](runtime.md#rt-cmp-agent-policy), [RT-CMP-text-encoder](runtime.md#rt-cmp-text-encoder), [RT-CMP-summarize-model](runtime.md#rt-cmp-summarize-model), [RT-CMP-qwen-max](runtime.md#rt-cmp-qwen-max)).

### Construct memory, interrupt context, then answer

The representative workflow is one connected three-stage training episode, not a reconstruction joining unrelated capabilities. Each independent rollout starts with an empty long-term store. Stage 1 exposes supporting information and permits memory construction. Stage 2 clears the short-term context while preserving the store, then introduces distractors. Stage 3 appends the formal query to the context remaining from Stage 2 and uses the store to solve the task. The driver owns stage transitions and turn limits; the same policy handles successive decisions. Independent rollout samples are not delegated workers sharing mutable state ([RT-RTE-task-rollout](runtime.md#rt-rte-task-rollout), [workflow account](workflow.md#workflow-account)).

A consequential exception to fully agent-selected memory timing occurs in Stage 1: the driver retrieves against each sampled message **before** the next policy action. This automatically supplies selected stored content that can influence what the policy writes or revises. Explicit Retrieve_memory instead fulfills a policy-selected query, entry count and optional metadata restriction. Both routes use cosine top-k selection and insert results into active context; neither proves full-store awareness. The typical count of 3–5 entries is not a token budget. Filter order, result rendering and the exact model-visible state remain unresolved ([RT-RTE-stage-retrieval](runtime.md#rt-rte-stage-retrieval), [MEM-RTE-requested-retrieval](memory.md#mem-rte-requested-retrieval)).

The store separates readable content, dense encodings and access metadata. Add creates an entry; Update replaces its text and regenerates its embedding and modification metadata; Delete removes its tuple from future store candidates. Retrieved identifiers target Update/Delete. These are model-generated requests installed by described tool semantics, not human-approved edits: Delete's confirmation boolean is supplied by the model. The physical backend and cross-process reload are unspecified. Metadata may include source information, but no authenticated provenance or trust elevation is established. At the later policy consumer, retrieved text supplies interpreted knowledge/context, not an enforced instruction or accepted truth ([MEM-OBJ-content](memory.md#mem-obj-content), [MEM-OBJ-embedding](memory.md#mem-obj-embedding), [MEM-OBJ-access-metadata](memory.md#mem-obj-access-metadata), [MEM-RTE-store-maintenance](memory.md#mem-rte-store-maintenance), [MEM-BAP-retained-context](memory.md#mem-bap-retained-context)).

Summary and Filter alter what subsequent decisions can see. Summary sends a selected span to an LLM and replaces the originals with derived prose. Its prompt requests preservation of important information, intentions, decisions and unresolved questions, but no independent fidelity gate is established. Filter removes whole messages by similarity to a criterion; conflicting keep/remove descriptions and unknown automatic criterion derivation prevent a reliable relevance-preservation claim. Mixed relevant and irrelevant content within one message further limits fact-level removal. These operations can reduce context, but their declared preservation goal is not an invariant. Context reset, filtering and summary replacement also do not establish withdrawal of information from other retained copies or learned weights ([RT-RTE-context-summary](runtime.md#rt-rte-context-summary), [MEM-OBJ-summary](memory.md#mem-obj-summary), [RT-RTE-context-filter](runtime.md#rt-rte-context-filter), [MEM-RTE-requested-retrieval](memory.md#mem-rte-requested-retrieval)).

Answer emission terminates the Stage 3 loop and returns the answer with its trajectory. Exhaustion without an answer leaves NULL. This is algorithmic delivery, not demonstrated external receipt or correctness-gated release. The prompt requests structured tool-call/answer blocks, but no executable parser establishes rejection of malformed requests or atomic multi-call execution. Terminal penalties and subsequent optimization are not retry, context restoration or repair of that completed episode ([RT-RTE-task-rollout](runtime.md#rt-rte-task-rollout), [RT-CLM-tool-protocol](runtime.md#rt-clm-tool-protocol), [MEM-CLM-case-protocol](memory.md#mem-clm-case-protocol), [workflow account](workflow.md#workflow-account)).

### Evaluate experience and change later decisions

The strongest supported improvement contribution is a specified trajectory-to-parameter update path accompanied by an author-reported bundled reward comparison. Collected states, actions and outcomes receive terminal scores; group-normalized advantages are broadcast uniformly to earlier timesteps; KL-regularized GRPO updates policy weights for subsequent decisions. The comparison of full composite reward against answer-only optimization reports HotpotQA answer judgment increasing from 0.509 to 0.544 and memory quality from 0.479 to 0.533. Tokens also rise from 2078 to 2117 and calls from 3.93 to 4.92, so this contrast does not show uniformly lower resource use. Its treatment is the reward bundle, not an individual subreward or memory action. Independent reproduction and finer causal attribution remain unresolved ([MEM-OBJ-experience](memory.md#mem-obj-experience), [RT-RTE-policy-update](runtime.md#rt-rte-policy-update), [RT-BAP-trained-policy](runtime.md#rt-bap-trained-policy), [EPI-CLM-reward-benefit](epistemic.md#epi-clm-reward-benefit)).

The checks have different licenses. Training answer reward compares an answer with a reference. Storage reward labels entries relative to the query and expected answer. Retrieval reward judges relevance. Preservation reward checks query-derived tokens or phrases, not all factual relations or supporting reasoning. Maintenance reward records whether Update/Delete happened, not whether the edit was justified. Resource scores measure economy, timing and violations. Their consequential consumer is the optimizer, not a specified per-entry truth-acceptance or live-answer veto gate ([EPI-RTE-answer-reward](epistemic.md#epi-rte-answer-reward), [EPI-RTE-storage-reward](epistemic.md#epi-rte-storage-reward), [EPI-RTE-relevance-reward](epistemic.md#epi-rte-relevance-reward), [EPI-RTE-context-reward](epistemic.md#epi-rte-context-reward), [EPI-RTE-telemetry-reward](epistemic.md#epi-rte-telemetry-reward)).

Benchmark evaluation is a separate reporting route. Qwen-Max judges HotpotQA supporting-fact coverage/relevance and answer equivalence; simulated environments supply task success/progress. These checks support their reference-relative domains, not general reusable truth, reasoning-process validity or replay safety. Generated memory strings, answers and summaries can contain factual candidates, but paired semantic evidence does not establish whether they preserve inputs, derive entailed conclusions or introduce conjectures. Retention and retrieval alone establish neither acceptance nor later integration of an accepted claim ([EPI-RTE-mq-evaluation](epistemic.md#epi-rte-mq-evaluation), [EPI-RTE-answer-evaluation](epistemic.md#epi-rte-answer-evaluation), [EPI-RTE-environment-evaluation](epistemic.md#epi-rte-environment-evaluation), [EPI-CLM-memory-quality](epistemic.md#epi-clm-memory-quality), [EPI-RTE-language-content](epistemic.md#epi-rte-language-content), [epistemic conclusion](epistemic.md#bounded-conclusion)).

### Independent property assessments and scenarios

Learning has an attributed positive at the bundled training-treatment level: the reported comparison suggests improved future task capacity, while the specified weight path explains how experience could affect later action. Automatic conversation-to-store extraction, continuation summaries and trajectory-fed weights also have retained results and later consumers. These write routes do not by themselves demonstrate capacity gain ([EPI-CLM-reward-benefit](epistemic.md#epi-clm-reward-benefit), [MEM-RTE-store-maintenance](memory.md#mem-rte-store-maintenance), [MEM-OBJ-summary](memory.md#mem-obj-summary), [RT-RTE-policy-update](runtime.md#rt-rte-policy-update)).

Other properties remain independent. Within the documented curriculum, computation selects actions and applies updates without specified per-action human veto; this is local autonomy under researcher-defined guidance, not an autonomous theory-builder finding. Reflection is unestablished: selected retrieval of the system's store can inform maintenance, but the evidence does not close both causal directions between a self-representation and behavior. Exercised self-improvement is likewise unestablished. The design specifies an independent improvement objective, reward-responsive changes to its own policy and a later parameter-consumption path; inspectable subsequent operation causally dependent on that change is missing. This does not erase the positive update design or attributed learning result ([RT-RTE-policy-update](runtime.md#rt-rte-policy-update), [RT-BAP-trained-policy](runtime.md#rt-bap-trained-policy), [epistemic conclusion](epistemic.md#bounded-conclusion)).

Theory-builder conditions 1–4 are each unestablished at this documentary boundary: (1) no formulated method theory is established as the updated object; (2) dependence of decisions on what such a theory says is not established by parameter influence or text delivery; (3) terminal ranking does not establish stated content-directed criticism identifying blame and resulting revision; (4) repeated optimization does not establish retention and next-round use of such criticism. Whether criticism of a consumed theory improved future action capacity is therefore also unestablished. Opaque model processing prevents turning these limits into absence claims ([RT-RTE-policy-update](runtime.md#rt-rte-policy-update), [EPI-RTE-language-content](epistemic.md#epi-rte-language-content), [epistemic conclusion](epistemic.md#bounded-conclusion)).

For the intended delayed-query/distractor scenario, the discriminating design is store retention across a context reset, followed by selected read-back and active-context reduction. This structurally connects earlier exposure to later answering, but actual early query/reference isolation and preservation are unresolved. For updating conversational preferences, the design permits later retrieval of revised content, but illustrative cleanup can discard historical reasons and cannot prove validated execution. For restart-persistent or cross-session reuse, this capture supports no storage durability guarantee. A frozen driver showing serialized model inputs, dispatcher rejection and persistence paths would change the implementation assessment; raw intervention-linked runs and checkpoint-to-later-operation traces would change activation, attribution and exercised-self-improvement assessments. Preservation tests and authenticated provenance/retraction paths would change the stronger information-lifecycle conclusions ([RT-CLM-context-reset](runtime.md#rt-clm-context-reset), [RT-RTE-task-rollout](runtime.md#rt-rte-task-rollout), [MEM-CLM-case-protocol](memory.md#mem-clm-case-protocol), [report verification limits](report-verification.md#limits)).
