### Consequential omission questions and stopping decision

Omission questions were selected from the frozen tracked tree and shipped state producers/consumers, not only the submitted evidence passages. I rechecked the previous verification's local findings rather than accepting them by inheritance.

- **Do host alternates change the capture or compaction conclusion?** SRC-1 `.dsh-plugin/lib/autosave.js` links transcript flush, scheduled hooks and host lifecycle events. Hook completion is not awaited at the turn-stopping serial point, and non-silent checkpoint prompts are not relayed. Paired with Python hook routing and Hermes's no-op pre-compression, this preserves the best-effort capture/host-owned compaction conclusion, while preventing a uniform cross-host prompted-checkpoint claim. The first limit retains that distinction.
- **Does graph maintenance or a shipped checker change the epistemic conclusion?** Read SRC-1 `mempalace/cli/cmd_kg.py`, `mempalace/kg_normalize.py`, the temporal writer and `mempalace/fact_checker.py`. Normalization persists model-proposed predicate/object rewrites for editing and later explicit application; vocabulary validation is not verification that meaning was preserved. The checker compares parsed claims with retained graph/registry content and can return a failing process result. These are material additions to a partial inventory, but not independent world-evidence acceptance. The reports' bounded positive account and explicit refusal of a system-complete negative remain valid. The second limit withholds complete graph-curation/evaluator comparisons.
- **Do evaluator details support stronger recall or cross-system metric claims?** Raw benchmark construction uses user turns rather than the full live capture loop; missing corpus indices can be appended after retrieved ranks. The NDCG formula normalizes only the relevance labels already present in top-k. This preserves label-relative recall interpretation but prevents an unqualified standard-NDCG comparison. The third limit retains the denominator issue.

Further optional expansion stopped because each selected material challenge has a supported disposition, and no identified contradiction or unsupported central claim is left without one. Additional native/backend, provider, parser, repair/backup/replication and evaluator files do not themselves require exhaustive tracing. The reports already name these alternatives and withhold their reliability, global deletion, source-fidelity and system-complete epistemic conclusions.
