# LLM Wiki Coordination synthesis

## Bounded synthesis

### A coordination package, not the agent host

LLM Wiki Coordination supplies filesystem procedures for agents maintaining a Markdown wiki, plus a deterministic Python audit. Its claimed work is to preserve discussion, direct subsequent contributions, distinguish proposals from approved structural knowledge, and maintain integrity. External agents interpret instructions and edit files; a user recognizes closure and approves canonical status. The package does not call models, schedule participants, inject context, intercept writes or generate canon automatically. This is a whole-system analysis of the shipped coordination package, not of an enclosing autonomous application. See [RT-OBJ-coordination-protocols](runtime.md#rt-obj-coordination-protocols), [RT-ABS-runtime-orchestration](runtime.md#rt-abs-runtime-orchestration) and [RT-BAP-protocol-guidance](runtime.md#rt-bap-protocol-guidance).

The evidence is SRC-1, `https://github.com/AEVYRA/llm-wiki-coordination`, frozen at `126749634d1c8c2fd6141f59711c882c2d629699`, with analysis cutoff 2026-10-14. The accepted reports inspected every tracked file, including all protocols and the sole executable. Audit findings are code-grounded; most agent behavior is doctrine/design. Private-use and repair statements are reported operation, not supplied execution traces. No adopting wiki, target execution or causal experiment establishes successful delivery or benefit. The [boundary and source register](boundary.md#source-register) specify these layers and exclusions. This synthesis uses the supplied members only; no source reads or new evidence were added.

### Preserve a question and make the next contribution depend on prior work

The representative workflow starts with a structural wiki question. An initiating agent creates stable context and open questions in `thread.md`, participants and coordination settings in `meta.yaml`, and an initial numbered contribution. Subsequent open questions are appended; each contribution gets its own file rather than replacing prior prose. These are prescribed write rules, not filesystem protections. The [workflow account](workflow.md#workflow-account) follows this explicitly connected discussion-to-canon procedure; no concrete completed instance was supplied. See [MEM-OBJ-thread-context](memory.md#mem-obj-thread-context), [MEM-OBJ-thread-meta](memory.md#mem-obj-thread-meta) and [MEM-OBJ-thread-entries](memory.md#mem-obj-thread-entries).

The next participant reads the preceding contribution, scores its Novelty, Coherence and Robustness from 0 to 1, and records `last_eval` in the new entry. Accumulated scores are compared with a target; the greatest deficit guides the next contribution's emphasis. A Robustness deficit asks the participant to act as critic, not a scheduler to launch a separate critic agent. The retained prose supplies arguments and reasons; symbolic vectors organize attributed judgments. Neither their sum nor target attainment establishes truth. Participants and the external host own selection, file reading and execution. See [RT-RTE-thread-participation](runtime.md#rt-rte-thread-participation) and [EPI-RTE-peer-judgment](epistemic.md#epi-rte-peer-judgment).

Separate files avoid rewriting the whole history, but do not establish bounded context cost. Participants must also derive cumulative assessments from retained entries; no agent-facing aggregation helper, token budget, truncation rule, isolated context frame or automatic reload path is shipped. The audit's internal sum is diagnostic, not a model-context service. Actual history delivery remains external. See [MEM-CLM-context-economy](memory.md#mem-clm-context-economy) and [memory read-back](memory.md#read-back). Historical discussion can also be imported without reconstructing peer-evaluation lineage; preservation does not restore unknown source warrant. See [EPI-RTE-archival-import](epistemic.md#epi-rte-archival-import).

The design separately affords cross-thread orientation estimation: sum and normalize an agent's past contribution vectors into `zhat`, then use that working estimate as preferred-direction guidance. It describes participation orientation, not contribution truth. Its update writer, history window, admission and later delivery are unresolved. The reports disagree whether its doctrine status is `afforded` or `claimed`; neither establishes wiring or operation. See [MEM-RTE-profile-refinement](memory.md#mem-rte-profile-refinement), [EPI-RTE-zhat-update](epistemic.md#epi-rte-zhat-update) and [report verification limits](report-verification.md#limits).

### Recognize closure, then review the resulting page separately

A closer evaluates the preceding contribution, records a cumulative checkpoint, summarizes discussion and proposes canon-page changes. The user may refuse Recognition, including for an under-target axis. Recognizing this closure is not equivalent to approving the resulting structural page. Page authors initialize revisions and endorsements; each reviewer must read the actual page in the same session before endorsing it. Full canon requires all active agents accepted/contributed, no stale endorsements and explicit user approval. Soft canon instead requires at least two-thirds active agreement, remaining pending/stale statuses aged at least 14 days, no disputes and explicit user approval. These are external-agent/user obligations, not an implemented admission gate. See [RT-RTE-canon-admission](runtime.md#rt-rte-canon-admission) and [MEM-OBJ-consensus-block](memory.md#mem-obj-consensus-block).

The procedural delivered result is an approved structural page with revision-specific endorsements, not merely a closing entry. Significant changes—more than 30% or structural reorganization—require revision increment and staling others' endorsements; revision mismatch also requires renewed review. Disputed soft canon returns to working. Premature closure can be challenged through a reopen entry that evaluates the closure and records why it was premature; one reopen is allowed per closure. These recovery paths preserve criticism rather than rolling back old prose. Their execution remains unobserved. See [workflow recovery and delivery](workflow.md#workflow-account) and [RT-RTE-canon-admission](runtime.md#rt-rte-canon-admission).

The resulting authority is bounded. Endorsements record agreement at a revision, not authenticated identity or factual warrant. No concrete candidate/evidence/criterion pair establishes acceptance of a particular ampliative claim, and no completed canon transformation establishes faithful preservation of reasons or novel inference. Candidate generation and canon derivation therefore remain semantically indeterminate at this boundary. See [EPI-RTE-entry-authorship](epistemic.md#epi-rte-entry-authorship), [EPI-RTE-canon-review](epistemic.md#epi-rte-canon-review), [EPI-RTE-canon-integration](epistemic.md#epi-rte-canon-integration) and [MEM-OBJ-canon-pages](memory.md#mem-obj-canon-pages).

### Diagnose stored structure without treating a pass as approval

The separate maintenance path invokes the audit with a wiki root and flags. Python reads Markdown under `wiki/` and dialogue specifically under `wiki/agents/dialogue`; it runs L0 hygiene, L1 graph and L2 protocol checks unconditionally, adding L3 cross-protocol checks only when the layer setting is at least 3. It prints text or JSON findings and, on normal completion, exits 1 if any HARD finding was collected and 0 otherwise. Direct method calls lack that terminal gate; parser-shape or filesystem failures may interrupt completion. The CLI neither repairs files nor persists reports. External agents perform semantic review, save reports/log summaries and escalate contested Hard findings to the user. Audit is not mandated at every dialogue handoff or canon promotion. See [RT-RTE-audit-invocation](runtime.md#rt-rte-audit-invocation), [RT-BAP-audit-exit](runtime.md#rt-bap-audit-exit) and [MEM-RTE-audit-retention](memory.md#mem-rte-audit-retention).

Its findings establish local predicates, not a general integrity warranty. Vector consistency does not validate semantic scores; accepted/pending checks do not enforce canonical quorum, revision validity or user approval. Key-custody text heuristics and signature-field presence do not authenticate anything. There is no raw-source baseline/approval comparison or semantic contradiction evaluator. Work blocking requires an external caller honoring the exit result. See [EPI-RTE-vector-check](epistemic.md#epi-rte-vector-check), [EPI-RTE-consensus-check](epistemic.md#epi-rte-consensus-check), [EPI-RTE-crypto-check](epistemic.md#epi-rte-crypto-check), [EPI-RTE-cross-check](epistemic.md#epi-rte-cross-check) and [EPI-ABS-source-check](epistemic.md#epi-abs-source-check).

Two source-level countercases materially narrow the package's stronger guarantees. Alternate evaluation references receive WARN without resolving older authors, so placing evaluation in the next file does not make self-evaluation impossible. Archival metadata bypasses metadata and entry-chain checks even after reopening, although the prose requires normal schema for new entries. Other audit passes still run. Adopter-chosen roots outside the fixed dialogue traversal also lack these diagnostics unless the checker is adapted. These are inspected branches, not observed exploits. See [RT-CLM-peer-evaluation](runtime.md#rt-clm-peer-evaluation), [RT-RTE-archival-audit](runtime.md#rt-rte-archival-audit) and [RT-RTE-audit-invocation](runtime.md#rt-rte-audit-invocation).

### Retained adaptation is specified; improved capacity is not demonstrated

The strongest supported adaptive contribution is the prescribed conversion of prior agent contributions into durable peer assessments and closure summaries/checkpoints that later participants use for direction or continuation. Under the comparison's trace-fed-write definition, this supports a local **claimed** trace-learning path from recorded conversational contributions, classified as session logs. It does not establish a shipped automatic writer, exercised learning or improved future-action capacity. Agent admission without approval of every score is distinct from manual closure/canon approval. [Profile verification](profile-verification.md#verification) confirms these local distinctions; [MEM-OBJ-thread-entries](memory.md#mem-obj-thread-entries), [EPI-RTE-peer-judgment](epistemic.md#epi-rte-peer-judgment) and [RT-RTE-thread-participation](runtime.md#rt-rte-thread-participation) supply their warrant.

There is also a concrete reported method repair: the changelog identifies an inverted deficit-weighting formula and retains its correction and rationale. This is criticism of an addressable coordination rule, not merely a changed page. However, no supplied later operation shows that the corrected rule was consumed or that future capacity improved. The comparison is a reported formula correction, not a causal evaluation of the whole package. See [RT-RTE-package-revision](runtime.md#rt-rte-package-revision).

The independent theory-builder conditions remain distinct. For participation, condition 1, localized method content, is `afforded`; condition 2, decisions depending on it, is `claimed`; condition 3, content-directed criticism, is `claimed`; condition 4, retained criticism shaping another round, is `claimed`, not observed. For the reported package repair, conditions 1–3 retain those respective statuses, while condition 4 is `uninspected`: retained correction does not establish a next criticism round. Candidate theories in an adopting wiki are uninspected. See [RT-RTE-thread-participation](runtime.md#rt-rte-thread-participation), [RT-RTE-package-revision](runtime.md#rt-rte-package-revision) and [RT-RTE-canon-admission](runtime.md#rt-rte-canon-admission).

Learning as improved future-action capacity remains `uninspected`. Reflection is `claimed`: represented participation and method criticism are intended to affect guidance, but own-operation traces do not establish both causal directions. Reflective and autonomous theory-building remain `uninspected`; there is no role-by-role computation trace, and user approval is explicitly prescribed. Exercised self-improvement at the shipped-package boundary is not established: reported repair changes retained organization, but subsequent live behavioral dependence and an independently evaluated improvement objective are missing. None of these properties follows from the trace-learning classification or from another property. See [RT-RTE-package-revision](runtime.md#rt-rte-package-revision) and [RT-RTE-thread-participation](runtime.md#rt-rte-thread-participation).

For an externally scheduled, cooperative discussion-to-document scenario, the package specifies durable handoffs, separate page review and recoverable objections. For a scenario requiring automatic or adversarially robust admission, authenticated review, bounded context delivery or demonstrated knowledge improvement, those procedures and retrospective checks do not establish the required guarantees. A downstream trace linking contributions, criticism, user decisions, page revisions and subsequent consumption would change the operational assessment. A host-enforced admission path covering alternate roots, archival reopening, quorum and revision rules would change the enforcement assessment. A comparison of subsequent behavior against an independently specified objective would be needed to establish improvement, not merely more retained content.
