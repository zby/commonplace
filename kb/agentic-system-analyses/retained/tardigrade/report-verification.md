> return [packageReturned({ ...stamp, ...spillPointer(key, json.length, json.slice(0, spillPolicy.previewChars), spillPolicy.note), at })]
> --- `packages/code/src/package/calls.ts`

This confirms a further producer of the same raw spill mechanism, not a different semantic memory or a contradiction of the explicitly partial producer inventory. The missing-bytes branch returns the recorded `result` field, not an automatic reconstruction from `preview`; the reports do not establish reliable retrieval after backend loss.

For alternate callers, SRC-1, `examples/rlm/actor.ts`, `examples/react-rlm-chat/server/actor.ts` and `packages/deprecated/agent/src/packages/agents.ts`, show legacy delegation beyond the current library record. Its child output can be decoded against a supplied contract:

Further optional omission expansion stopped because these selected questions have positive or limited dispositions, no identified challenge invalidates the central current/legacy account, and the reports already withhold universal coverage and operational benefit. Full legacy child internals, Cloudflare scheduling, arbitrary custom evaluators and formal-model soundness remain unassessed; they cannot support system-complete negative comparisons.
