# Tracecraft report verification

## Verification

The reports support a bounded implementation account of bucket-backed coordination and transcript retention, not an enclosing model runtime or demonstrated agent learning. I read all supplied members, types, reconciliation, empty correction-answer files and the structural check. The structural check reports no findings. I independently inspected the frozen source rather than accepting analyst checks as verification. HEAD matches the reviewed revision and the checkout is clean. Source evidence below is SRC-1 implementation or explicitly identified doctrine/design; no target code, tests, examples or services were executed.

### Record and behavior checks

I checked all 37 analyst declarations: the runtime's components, objects, eight routes, two claims and two authority paths; memory's ten operative objects, evaluator route and history claim; epistemic's two objects, four routes and two claims; and workflow's handoff object. I compared their source anchors, operative referents, evidence layers, conclusion statuses and annotations with their producers and consumers. The retained passages support the load-bearing findings. Wired implementation, doctrine-only checking requests and uninspected operation remain distinct. There are no declared evidenced absences to validate as system-wide negatives.

- **Configuration and backend authority:** direct reads of SRC-1 `sdk/tracecraft/config.py`, `sdk/tracecraft/store.py`, `sdk/tracecraft/cli/init_cmd.py`, `sdk/tracecraft/s3.py` and `sdk/tracecraft/hf.py` support [RT-CMP-get-store](runtime.md#rt-cmp-get-store), [RT-OBJ-config](runtime.md#rt-obj-config), [RT-RTE-init-config](runtime.md#rt-rte-init-config) and [MEM-OBJ-agent-registration](memory.md#mem-obj-agent-registration). Configuration precedes backend admission, agent attribution can be overridden, project prefixes are not authorization, and HF uses check-then-write rather than service-atomic claim admission. The reports correctly withhold universal atomicity, deployed privacy and authenticated-agent conclusions.
- **Task progression and delivery:** SRC-1 `sdk/tracecraft/cli/steps.py`, `sdk/tracecraft/protocol.py`, `sdk/tracecraft/cli/status.py` and `sdk/tracecraft/cli/agents.py` establish [RT-RTE-step-control](runtime.md#rt-rte-step-control), [RT-OBJ-step-state](runtime.md#rt-obj-step-state), [RT-BAP-step-polling](runtime.md#rt-bap-step-polling) and the separate [MEM-OBJ-handoff](memory.md#mem-obj-handoff). Status wins over claim fallback; completion writes status before handoff; ownership is bypassable; review state has no independent-reviewer gate. [EPI-RTE-git-files](epistemic.md#epi-rte-git-files) imports tracked diff paths, with an ambiguous empty fallback, rather than certifying task correctness. The [workflow delivery account](workflow.md#verification-handoff-and-delivery) does not confuse a receipt or waiter return with recipient uptake.
- **Shared content and reader position:** SRC-1 `sdk/tracecraft/cli/memory.py`, `sdk/tracecraft/cli/messages.py` and `sdk/tracecraft/protocol.py` support [RT-RTE-memory-write](runtime.md#rt-rte-memory-write), [RT-RTE-mail-exchange](runtime.md#rt-rte-mail-exchange) and their separately declared memory parts. History precedes live replacement; default get drops provenance; history limits select versions, not tokens. Cursor advancement follows display but uses listed keys, a trailing grace window and bounded seen set. The reports correctly reject unconditional delivery and immutable-history guarantees. Direct backend mutation supplies the counterpath for [MEM-CLM-history-immutability](memory.md#mem-clm-history-immutability). SRC-1 `sdk/tracecraft/cli/artifacts.py` supports [RT-RTE-artifact-share](runtime.md#rt-rte-artifact-share) and [MEM-OBJ-artifact-file](memory.md#mem-obj-artifact-file) as opaque byte transfer without semantic admission or automatic model delivery.
- **Transcript transformations and recovery:** I read SRC-1 `sdk/tracecraft/cli/session.py`, `sdk/tracecraft/redact.py` and every module under `sdk/tracecraft/harness/`. They support [RT-CMP-harness-registry](runtime.md#rt-cmp-harness-registry), [RT-RTE-session-mirror](runtime.md#rt-rte-session-mirror), [RT-RTE-session-compact](runtime.md#rt-rte-session-compact) and memory's payload, metadata and local-cursor separation. [RT-CLM-mirror-idempotence](runtime.md#rt-clm-mirror-idempotence), [RT-CLM-secret-redaction](runtime.md#rt-clm-secret-redaction) and [EPI-RTE-regex-scrub](epistemic.md#epi-rte-regex-scrub) preserve the correct distinctions between normal-path suppression, pattern replacement, semantic uncertainty and privacy guarantees.
- **Checking and change admission:** direct reads of SRC-1 `examples/claude-code/CLAUDE.md` and `examples/hermes-agent/SKILL.md` support [RT-BAP-agent-prompt](runtime.md#rt-bap-agent-prompt) and [EPI-RTE-read-verification](epistemic.md#epi-rte-read-verification) as requested behavior, not observed compliance. SRC-1 `benchmarks/contention_bench.py` trial/sweep code, `benchmarks/messaging_bench.py` preflight/trial/cleanup code, and the cited memory and compact test bodies support [MEM-RTE-evaluator-readback](memory.md#mem-rte-evaluator-readback) and [EPI-RTE-bucket-evaluation](epistemic.md#epi-rte-bucket-evaluation).
