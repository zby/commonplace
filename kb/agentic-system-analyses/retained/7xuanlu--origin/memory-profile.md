## Comparison rationale

All ten axes remain partial over the named inventory. A resolved database or summary route does not resolve the graph/community/vocabulary, import-distillation, client-projection and repair alternatives that the accepted reports leave included. Local known units describe only their named operative parts. No available-but-uninspected branch is classified as absent. The provider components describe inference dependencies, not weights accumulated through use; their unknown internal updates cannot be substituted for a supported Origin memory route.

The profile uses the canonical declarations without supersession: the supplied reconciliation establishes none. The scheduler remains a valid container for enrichment, recaps and rollup. Page refresh remains a valid container for revision cards and lexical citation processing. Later claim/support state and truth exposure are distinct from that container, so their enforcement and invalidation findings do not upgrade synthesis into a semantic guarantee. See [RT-RTE-ambient-work](runtime.md#rt-rte-ambient-work), [RT-RTE-page-refresh](runtime.md#rt-rte-page-refresh), [EPI-RTE-support-publish](epistemic.md#epi-rte-support-publish) and [EPI-RTE-truth-exposure](epistemic.md#epi-rte-truth-exposure).

The libSQL carrier is both relational storage and a SQLite-family substrate. Its retained dense-vector access structures also support vector storage. These labels do not assert three separate services. The lint-cache substrate remains not-determinable: the accepted records establish caching and reuse, not whether its carrier is volatile or persisted. No in-memory storage finding is retained from that inference. See [EPI-OBJ-lint-report](epistemic.md#epi-obj-lint-report) and [EPI-RTE-lint-assessment](epistemic.md#epi-rte-lint-assessment). Dense vectors are parametric representations; database identities and exposure flags are symbolic; delivered prose is natural language. A readable revision card or lint display does not classify every field of its opaque machine payload. See [MEM-OBJ-memories](memory.md#mem-obj-memories), [MEM-OBJ-revision-cards](memory.md#mem-obj-revision-cards) and [EPI-OBJ-repair-manifest](epistemic.md#epi-obj-repair-manifest).

Read-back separates the requesting caller from automatic selection for later task consumers. Recall and Brief reload are pull even when code ranks or supplements the response. Automatic enrichment, burst summarization and source-change compilation push selected retained input into inference tasks; they do not establish automatic foreground-session knowledge injection. Page-linked source identity actually selects snippet content, whereas a requested page ID or manifest ID alone does not prove identifier-based push. Unspecified candidate ranking remains unresolved. See [RT-RTE-recall-search](runtime.md#rt-rte-recall-search), [MEM-RTE-brief-handoff](memory.md#mem-rte-brief-handoff), [MEM-RTE-burst-recaps](memory.md#mem-rte-burst-recaps), [RT-RTE-page-refresh](runtime.md#rt-rte-page-refresh) and [EPI-RTE-claim-judgment](epistemic.md#epi-rte-claim-judgment).

Trace learning here is the profile's automatic trace-to-durable-artifact relation, not demonstrated improved capacity. The instructed handoff route is afforded; wired daemon admission does not upgrade external session interpretation to observed operation. Conversational enrichment provides a wired qualifying branch, while corpus-wide recap/rollup origins remain mixed. At update consumers, retained session evidence has learning authority; downstream Brief/capture prose provides knowledge or continuation guidance. These are separate forces. Imported conversations alone and raw access logs alone do not establish qualifying automatic derivation. See [MEM-RTE-brief-handoff](memory.md#mem-rte-brief-handoff), [MEM-RTE-enrichment-slices](memory.md#mem-rte-enrichment-slices), [MEM-RTE-import-inputs](memory.md#mem-rte-import-inputs) and [MEM-OBJ-access-state](memory.md#mem-obj-access-state).

Duplicate acquisition checks do not establish deduplication over retained entries. Pending-to-active confirmation supports the narrow promotion unit; uninspected vocabulary promotion is not inferred. Dismissal can keep an independent capture, delete a page proposal, or only resolve a queue item. See [MEM-RTE-summary-rollup](memory.md#mem-rte-summary-rollup), [MEM-RTE-review-decisions](memory.md#mem-rte-review-decisions) and [EPI-RTE-refinement-decision](epistemic.md#epi-rte-refinement-decision).
