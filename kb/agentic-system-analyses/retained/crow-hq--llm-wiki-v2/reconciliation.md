### Record identity and containment

No whole-record supersession is established. The following overlaps do not make the records interchangeable:

- [MEM-RTE-source-reduction](memory.md#mem-rte-source-reduction) is an explicitly declared part of [RT-RTE-source-filing](runtime.md#rt-rte-source-filing). The latter retains preparation, admission, persistence and enrichment beyond reduction. [EPI-RTE-merge-guard](epistemic.md#epi-rte-merge-guard), [EPI-RTE-guard-choice](epistemic.md#epi-rte-guard-choice), [EPI-RTE-revision-check](epistemic.md#epi-rte-revision-check) and [EPI-RTE-revision-admit](epistemic.md#epi-rte-revision-admit) declare other parts, separating evidence computation from its consequential consumer. None replaces the whole filing route.
- [EPI-OBJ-note](epistemic.md#epi-obj-note) spans candidate/draft bodies and stored main bodies. [MEM-OBJ-note-body](memory.md#mem-obj-note-body) describes the retained main-body portion and its later consumers. The candidate-to-stored container remains useful and is not wrong merely because the retained portion has its own record. Whole-record identity is ruled out; neither record supersedes the other.
- [EPI-OBJ-source](epistemic.md#epi-obj-source) concerns assertions in supplied Source.text and raw bodies. [MEM-OBJ-raw](memory.md#mem-obj-raw) describes persisted extracted text together with origin, sequence, hash and removal metadata. Imported assertion content overlaps, but the input/assertion scope and the persisted mixed-content object are not identical.
- [EPI-OBJ-answer](epistemic.md#epi-obj-answer) is a transient output object, not [RT-RTE-question-answer](runtime.md#rt-rte-question-answer), its producing route, or [MEM-OBJ-note-body](memory.md#mem-obj-note-body), a retained input. [MEM-RTE-note-read](memory.md#mem-rte-note-read) delivers a requested note without the question route's relevance selection or answer generation; these are alternate consumers, not duplicate routes.
- [MEM-OBJ-answer-cache](memory.md#mem-obj-answer-cache) and [MEM-OBJ-replay](memory.md#mem-obj-replay) retain outputs rather than the parameters described by [RT-CMP-classifier](runtime.md#rt-cmp-classifier) and [RT-CMP-llm](runtime.md#rt-cmp-llm). Their keys, covered model kinds and persistence differ. [MEM-RTE-answer-reuse](memory.md#mem-rte-answer-reuse) connects the two reuse implementations without identifying their stores.
- [MEM-BAP-retained-knowledge](memory.md#mem-bap-retained-knowledge) concerns delivered retained evidence. [RT-BAP-model-instructions](runtime.md#rt-bap-model-instructions) concerns rendered role guidance; [RT-BAP-client-instructions](runtime.md#rt-bap-client-instructions) asks an external client to use memory and follow relay restrictions. A common model consumer does not make these channels or authority sources identical.
- [EPI-RTE-route-audit](epistemic.md#epi-rte-route-audit) and [EPI-RTE-audit-apply](epistemic.md#epi-rte-audit-apply) distinguish classifier proposals from consequential moves. [MEM-OBJ-proposals](memory.md#mem-obj-proposals) is instead the durable caller-supplied area queue. [EPI-RTE-health-check](epistemic.md#epi-rte-health-check) consumes log counts, whereas the log's split-attempt consumer in [MEM-OBJ-log](memory.md#mem-obj-log) suppresses later attempts. Shared storage or move primitives do not establish route identity.

### Convergence and integration-issue dispositions

**Delivery is not semantic acceptance.** The workflow's annotations use the runtime's existing filing and question records rather than declaring duplicates. Its structural rejection, post-write relating failure and caller-owned retry account agrees with [RT-RTE-source-filing](runtime.md#rt-rte-source-filing). The epistemic check/disposition parts add consequential heuristic checks without turning the runtime's factual-preservation claim into an enforced guarantee: see [RT-CLM-factual-preservation](runtime.md#rt-clm-factual-preservation), [EPI-RTE-step-check](epistemic.md#epi-rte-step-check) and [EPI-RTE-guard-choice](epistemic.md#epi-rte-guard-choice). These are complementary scopes, not conflicting statuses.

**Full text means the delivered display, not the serialized file.** Memory integration issue 1 qualifies runtime and workflow shorthand. [MEM-OBJ-note-body](memory.md#mem-obj-note-body) retains the `full_text` construction; [MEM-OBJ-note-metadata](memory.md#mem-obj-note-metadata) identifies omitted control fields and See-also links. [MEM-RTE-note-read](memory.md#mem-rte-note-read) and [RT-RTE-question-answer](runtime.md#rt-rte-question-answer) deliver that display through different routes. Neither the runtime nor workflow requires that all frontmatter or linked targets reach the answering writer. No cross-report disagreement follows.

**Removal, purge and rebuild are different operations.** Memory integration issue 3 extends the runtime's statement that removed-source notes remain. [MEM-OBJ-raw](memory.md#mem-obj-raw) and the memory maintenance annotation describe marking, reconstruction and mixed-source refiling; [EPI-RTE-origin-purge](epistemic.md#epi-rte-origin-purge) is a declared withdrawal part of [RT-RTE-manual-maintenance](runtime.md#rt-rte-manual-maintenance). Their conclusions agree that lineage-based deletion does not establish universal semantic erasure or rollback. The memory annotation's placement under maintenance does not relocate the epistemic revision parts out of filing.

**Evaluation is an offline consumer, not an online oracle.** Memory integration issues 2 and 6 connect [MEM-OBJ-evaluation-results](memory.md#mem-obj-evaluation-results), [MEM-OBJ-replay](memory.md#mem-obj-replay) and [EPI-RTE-fact-score](epistemic.md#epi-rte-fact-score). Stored results, response substitution and lexical scoring are different referents. The runtime's retrieval-evaluator account tests selected-note identity without generating an answer. [EPI-CLM-benchmark-results](epistemic.md#epi-clm-benchmark-results) retains attributed aggregate results and the unavailable contradiction/revision harness. These limits reinforce, rather than contradict, memory's narrower evaluator coverage. None supplies observed factual correctness or component-level causal attribution.

**Caller and provider uncertainty remains local.** Memory integration issues 4 and 5 leave no whole-route duplicate and preserve unresolved authorship and approval provenance. Runtime relay and maintenance findings do not depend on a verified human principal: see [RT-RTE-client-relay](runtime.md#rt-rte-client-relay) and [RT-RTE-manual-maintenance](runtime.md#rt-rte-manual-maintenance). Provider identity limits remain in the component records. The epistemic bounded conclusion and workflow's lack of an autonomous critic-author cycle do not negate implemented storage, checks or read-back, and do not establish absent internal reasoning.
