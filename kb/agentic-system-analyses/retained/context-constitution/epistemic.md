## Bounded conclusion

The policy proposes acquired experience and retrieved context, durable memory edits, summaries, indexes and model-visible guidance. Acquisition makes information available but supplies no disclosed source-truth check; source warrant for actual retrieved/retained propositions is unknown. Summarization and generalization are declared, but actual transformation classes remain indeterminate without inputs, outputs or code. Neither an entailed derivation from warranted premises nor an accepted ampliative proposition can be identified from this source set.

Two distinct evaluative policies matter: memory-quality reflection asks whether prompts correct recurring mistakes and improve future user help; pre-pruning examination asks whether historical relevance and retrieval readiness justify removing always-visible content. Their intended uses and scopes are operational usefulness and placement, respectively. Neither supplies a recorded candidate-linked acceptance decision. Successful Git push is a separate propagation condition, not either evaluator. Thus retention and future operational use can precede or occur without established epistemic acceptance. No post-acceptance lifecycle integration can be traced.

Changed system memory has a declared later consumer, channel and horizon: the underlying model receives it as system-prompt context across calls; external context instead depends on relevance selection and retrieval. Both exercise natural-language influence rather than guaranteed compliance. Persona and guideline edits can directly adapt policy without producing a truth-apt claim. Compaction changes the model's evidence view without a supplied fidelity check; retrieving raw history is a recovery opportunity, not proof of repair. Reflection and Defragmentation can alter shared memory despite separate message contexts, but their result approval and withdrawal semantics remain unspecified.

The positive conclusion is an explicit improvement-directed context policy with retrospective assessment and later behavioral-authority paths by doctrine. Implementation, exercised reliance, acceptance, benefit and transfer are not shown. Distributed-parametric contents and change mechanisms remain uninspected. For the independent learning, reflection, theory-builder and self-improvement assessments, see [RT-RTE-memory-admission](runtime.md#rt-rte-memory-admission): localized published theory does not establish consumption, content-directed criticism or iteration; guidance about self-representation does not demonstrate either causal direction in operation. No outcome or comparison closes the evidence-to-update-to-live-authority-to-changed-operation chain. These limits apply separately and do not negate the declared routes or imply a single system-wide epistemic verdict.
