## Limits

- The profile's `behavioral_authority` units “Retained content/features consumed by context and intake selectors” and “Recovery metadata and Hiera retained execution controls at their code/agent consumers” aggregate several distinct retained-part/consumer/effect paths. See the [profile rationale](memory-profile.md#comparison-rationale), [MEM-RTE-context-compilation](memory.md#mem-rte-context-compilation), [MEM-OBJ-checkpoints](memory.md#mem-obj-checkpoints), [MEM-RTE-ledger-replay](memory.md#mem-rte-ledger-replay) and [EPI-RTE-hiera-select](epistemic.md#epi-rte-hiera-select). Their notes and citations support the emitted forces, but these coarse units are not a one-to-one inventory suitable for counting mechanisms or comparing authority coverage at individual consumers. Withhold those finer comparisons. The axis already expresses partial inventory coverage; this granularity issue does not invalidate the supported force findings or require withdrawing a system-level positive.
