Every axis remains `partial`; none asserts a complete system inventory, bounded absence or inapplicability. Included offline alternatives remain explicitly uninspected. All positive bases are `wired`, consistent with implementation evidence and the absence of supplied runs. The partial research-state source unit and indeterminate semantic-curation unit preserve their separate uncertainties.

## Limits

- The partial inventory is coarser than a complete mechanism-level comparison. The profile's storage units do not separately enumerate the installed program slot identified by [RT-OBJ-artifact-program](runtime.md#rt-obj-artifact-program), and its signal units acknowledge but do not separately emit coarse verified-status/recency selection for the program view described by [MEM-OBJ-program-skills](memory.md#mem-obj-program-skills). Several authority and direction units also combine named consumers or operations that share the same emitted value. The [profile](memory-profile.md#comparison-rationale) supports the existence of its emitted values, but consumers should withhold complete object counts, complete per-selector value sets and one-to-one route comparisons from these grouped units. This is not a blocker: the axes already express partial coverage, the omissions add no new system-union value, and the stated local mechanisms remain supported.
