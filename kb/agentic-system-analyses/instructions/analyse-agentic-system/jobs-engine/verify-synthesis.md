# Verify synthesis

Give public readers an independent judgment of the synthesis's supported conclusions and the limitations that constrain them.

Judge the exact supplied `synthesis` against
`boundary`, `runtime`, `memory`, `epistemic`, `reconciliation`,
`report-verification` and `profile-verification`. Assess `synthesis-answers`
and `synthesis-refusal`, when supplied, afresh, not as established repairs or
defects.

## Judge the public account

Check every substantive statement against its cited records and reconciliation
supersessions. Read the text as public readers will, without supporting-member
context. Every limit declared in record and profile verifications and every
`Unresolved conflict:` must appear in Limitations with affected IDs and the
conclusion readers should withhold. Reassess previous-verification limits on
retry; retain still-warranted limits and verify that the public text carries
their consequences. Preserve supported contributions beside unresolved parts
without an aggregate completeness claim. Assess independent properties
independently; do not bundle negatives or infer improved capacity from
trace-fed retention. Apply `synthesis-type` without added conditions.

Use the verification type's materiality threshold. Unsupported statements,
hidden coverage gaps, unwarranted absence or completeness, or omitted declared
limits can be blockers. Explain the incorrect reader inference and why a stated
limit cannot contain it. Local omissions or imprecise wording that leave the
bounded conclusions valid can be limits; do not demand exhaustive restatement
or editorial polish. Faithful uncertainty is neither blocker nor limit.
Structural acceptance does not establish support. Neither budget exhaustion
nor a predecessor objection settles materiality.

Write no correction and add no evidence. A record fault discovered here is a
limit for the public text, not a reason to reopen reconciliation. If it cannot
be stated without making the synthesis misleading, write `problem` and stop.
