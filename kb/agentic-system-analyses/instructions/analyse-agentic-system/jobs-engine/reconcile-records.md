# Reconcile the pinned analyst records

State how the three reports connect and where they disagree so the report verifier can judge them as one bounded account.

Reconcile the reports afresh; do not copy an old connection after its
evidence changes. Where the analysts' answers are supplied, assess what their
corrections and declines mean for cross-report disagreements.

## Connect without judging

State every identity between records as a supersession under the records
contract, or rule it out. Faithful uncertainty alone is not a disagreement. A
report relying on another report's record disagrees when that record no
longer says what the dependent finding requires. Describe a declined
objection concerning two reports with both positions, not as an
automatically established defect. Do not select the strongest-sounding
status or strengthen or narrow a report's finding.
