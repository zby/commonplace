# Archived generated reviews

Generated reviews published by `analyse-agentic-system` under the
single-file result contract, moved here on 2026-09-28 when the retained
results they pin were archived under
`kb/agentic-systems/reports/retained-archive/`. Every review keeps
its `analysis-result` or `analysis-artifact` pin, rewritten to the archive path, and every pinned
hash still verifies.

These are superseded projections. They are not read by the comparison
loaders and are not regenerated in place: each system returns to
`kb/agentic-systems/reviews/` only as a fresh run under the current
method, scheduled by the corpus-refresh workshop
(`kb/work/agentic-memory-refresh`). Hand-authored reviews were never
generated and stay in `kb/agentic-systems/reviews/`.

The 2026-10-01 relocation changes pin paths while retaining archived payload
and manifest bytes. Historical quotation attributions and producing commits
remain as recorded.
