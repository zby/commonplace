# Focus a prose-simplification pass

Use these prompts after the argument and evidence are settled. They are heuristics, not rules. Indirect wording, repetition, and helper verbs may be worth keeping when they add nuance, contrast, emphasis, or rhythm.

## Revise

1. Look for places where the reader must decode an abstraction before seeing the mechanism. Consider stating who does what and what follows first.
2. Look for helper verbs or repeated phrases that can be removed without losing meaning.
3. Compare the revision with the original. Check its causal direction, uncertainty, conditions, scope, evidence strength, and unit of analysis. Keep or restore wording when the revision loses nuance or worsens the flow.

## Verify

- Check that the passage is easier to understand, whether or not it is shorter.
- Check that no material claim or qualification changed.
- Re-read the whole artifact for lost contrasts and transitions, then run its validator.
