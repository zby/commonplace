# Narrow overclaims

Make every load-bearing sentence assert only what its evidence can show, and
mark what it cannot as a gap rather than filling it.

Effort: judgment. Deciding what the evidence bounds needs the session model, and the parent context, where the conversation that raised the overclaim is the evidence.

Apply these moves, each to the sentence that needs it:

- **From held to judged.** A claim about what a system holds, believes, or
  lacks becomes a claim about what its gate, code, or outputs judge. "None
  holds a theory" becomes "none is set up to test that; what they retain is
  judged by score, not by explanation."
- **No attributed expectations.** Do not say what builders or authors expect
  unless a publication says it. State the rival hypothesis on its own as the
  simplest alternative, and let the artifact deny it.
- **Name the instrument actually used.** Where the artifact claims its method
  can decide something precisely, replace the claim with the instrument that
  produced the result in the artifact and a sentence saying what is not yet
  stated.
- **Narrow, do not delete.** A weakened sentence keeps its place and its
  link. The reader should still see the point and its limit.

Report each changed sentence as before and after, one line each, with the
evidence that bounds it.

Preserve: claims the packet marks as defended. Narrow a defended claim's
wording only if the operator confirms.
