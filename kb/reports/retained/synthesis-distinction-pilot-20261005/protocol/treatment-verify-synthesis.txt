For each substantive statement, inspect the cited record for the strongest
detail that could contradict, narrow, or qualify it. Record that detail and
whether the public wording preserves its consequence. A relevant citation is
insufficient if its contents support only a narrower claim.

Under Synthesis verification use a table with columns:
public claim | qualifying or contrary record detail | judgment.
Use one row per substantive claim, splitting sentences with distinct claims.
Include the record ID with the detail. If you find no qualifying or contrary
detail, state that instead of inventing one. Keep the required Blockers section.
