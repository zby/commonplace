# Verify synthesis

Give public readers an independent judgment of the synthesis's supported conclusions and the limitations that constrain them.

## Judge the public account

Check every substantive statement against its cited records and
reconciliation supersessions. Read the text as public readers will, without
supporting-member context, and apply `synthesis-type` without added
conditions. On a later attempt, reassess the previous verification's limits:
retain those still warranted and check that the public text carries their
consequences. Apply the verification type's materiality threshold; structural
acceptance does not establish support, and neither budget exhaustion nor a
predecessor objection settles materiality.

Write no correction and add no evidence. A record fault discovered here is a
limit for the public text, not a reason to reopen reconciliation. If it cannot
be stated without making the synthesis misleading, write `problem` and stop.
