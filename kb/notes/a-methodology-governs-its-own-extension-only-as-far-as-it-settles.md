# A methodology governs its own extension only as far as it settles the meta-decisions it raises

A [methodology](./definitions/actionable-methodology.md) maps represented
conditions to interventions and is actionable for an operator able to carry
them out. Extending a system according to that methodology can raise further
decisions: what form a new artifact takes, how its use is checked, and what
authority it acquires. The method governs those decisions only as far as its
retained content supplies or imports a basis for them. Call this **closure
under its own recommendations**.

A capable actor may make the remaining decisions successfully. That shows the
capacity of the actor-plus-method system, not that the method settled choices
it left open. This distinction is useful when attributing reliable execution or
deciding what knowledge to retain for later use.

## Closure and reflection are separate properties

A [reflective system](./definitions/reflective-system.md) has a causally connected
self-representation available to its processes. It can revise that representation
through judgment without a method settling every revision. A fixed pipeline
can follow a complete method for a bounded task without representing or changing
itself.

Neither property therefore entails the other. This note concerns the content
of the retained method. [Computational
closure](./methodological-and-computational-closure-track-different-changes.md)
instead concerns whether execution requires human decisions.

## Routing and decision content settle separately

A methodology can govern a decision in three ways:

1. **Name a decider.** A maintainer approves a class of change. This settles
   routing, not the criterion used to approve it.
2. **Supply or import a criterion.** A retained rule or referenced decision
   procedure constrains which result is acceptable.
3. **Determine the result.** The method or an invoked procedure fixes the
   relevant outcome under the stated conditions.

Only the second and third settle decision content, and only to the extent
that they actually constrain the consequential choice. An unarticulated human
criterion or an implicit model judgment can be useful without becoming part of
the method's stated content. [Explicit
retention](./only-explicit-retention-is-durable-writable-and-addressable.md)
makes a criterion easier to inspect and revise; the distinction does not rest
on a claim that all other forms of governance are impossible.

The method need not duplicate what it imports, but it must identify the
criterion or result and the pathway must use it. If a choice's variation is
tolerable, the method can leave it outside the consequential decision set.
That narrows the claim rather than supplying the missing criterion.

[Underspecified instructions](./agentic-systems-interpret-underspecified-instructions.md)
can allow different interpretations. Whether that variation matters must be
assessed against outcomes, rather than treating every unstated detail as a
reason the method must expand.

## Three decisions an extension can raise

**Representational form.** Should a new operation remain model-interpreted or
be assigned to code, a schema, or another symbolic consumer? A method can supply
[decision heuristics](./codify-versus-llm-decision-heuristics.md) and conditions
for [codification](./definitions/codification.md). Having a written instruction
does not settle that choice, and new theory does not automatically require new
code when existing machinery can apply it adequately.

**Checking.** What evidence warrants using the artifact for the intended task?
A method can specify tests, comparison cases, criticism, or later exposure.
The [verification boundary](./the-boundary-of-automation-is-the-boundary-of-verification.md)
is a limit on warranted acceptance, not a theorem that an unchecked artifact
cannot be correct. Identifying an oracle does not by itself establish its
adequacy or independence from the proposed change.

**Authority and retention.** How does the accepted artifact acquire a
consumer, channel, and force? The [behavioral-authority
account](./definitions/behavioral-authority.md) distinguishes producing a note
or gate from making it govern later operation. A method that names neither
its consumption path nor who can authorize it leaves that part of the change
unsettled.

These are useful axes for examining a recommendation, not an exhaustive list
of every decision an open-ended system can face. Their separation permits a
method to settle one while leaving another to judgment.

## Search is another question

In a [proposal-selection loop](./a-proposal-selection-loop-requires-search-evaluation-and-retention.md),
something must find candidates before they can be evaluated and retained.
A method may govern the form, checking, and authority of a recommendation it
already has without specifying how that recommendation was found. A claim that
the method also governs search needs its own account.

Other learning architectures can make evidence-responsive changes without a
separate proposal and admission event. The same question about retained
guidance still applies, but the loop's stages should not be imposed as a
universal architecture.

## What the assessment can establish

The [Commonplace reference
case](./evidence/commonplace-as-a-reflective-system.md) records decisions
settled for a completeness mark alongside decisions that still depended on
judgment. It is an application of the distinction, not proof that Commonplace
settles all self-extension decisions.

For another pathway, name the consequential decisions, the retained content
said to settle them, and the actual consumption path. A decision outside that
content bounds the method's coverage. Reliable execution beyond that boundary
can show that the actor's competence compensates for it. Whether extending the
method improves reliability or cost then becomes an empirical comparison.

The [two-layer execution account](./theory-and-methodology-form-a-two-layer-execution-system.md)
provides one arrangement: use settled guidance where it applies and fall back
to theory-guided judgment elsewhere. Recurring useful judgments can motivate
new guidance. Fallback is not necessarily a failure, and recurrence is not a
proof that the new guidance is sound.

## Scope

A method can be complete for a declared bounded decision set. This note does
not assert that every real method is incomplete or that open-ended extension
must eventually fail. It identifies which decisions are attributable to the
retained method and which require something else.

The account also separates method coverage from authority, correctness, and
retention policy. A specified criterion can be wrong; a correct recommendation
can lack permission to take effect; and a specified temporary computation need
not be kept as reusable code. Each claim needs its own evidence.
