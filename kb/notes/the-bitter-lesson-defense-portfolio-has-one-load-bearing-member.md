# The bitter-lesson defense portfolio has one load-bearing member

This KB has accumulated several answers to the bitter-lesson objection, and they are not interchangeable: they differ in *role*, and only one carries the compatibility claim. Presented as a chain, they invite a predictable failure — a skeptical reader attacks the weakest member (usually a conditional forecast) and concludes the thesis fell, when the thesis never rested on it. This note catalogues the portfolio by role, so consumers — the introductory article first among them — can cite the load-bearing member alone and route everything else.

**The load-bearing member.** [The bitter lesson selects production methods, not representational forms](./the-bitter-lesson-selects-production-methods-not-representational.md): the lesson constrains how useful structure is produced and revised, not the form in which it is retained. A scalable loop can search over theories, instructions, tests, schemas, and programs, evaluate candidates, and retain the selected ones as addressable artifacts — in that architecture the artifacts are products and working state of learning, no more contrary to the lesson than learned weights. This alone removes the categorical objection, and nothing else in the portfolio is needed for that removal. Its reflective complication — [machinery persists by warrant, not position](./machinery-persists-by-warrant-not-position-in-a-reflective-loop.md) — extends the same member.

**The two companions that keep it honest** (kept beside the core wherever it is stated):

- *The concession*: no current artifact, vocabulary, or decomposition is promised permanence — stable guidance may migrate into weights, and structure that stops earning its marginal value should be [relaxed or removed](./codification-and-relaxing-navigate-the-bitter-lesson-boundary.md).
- *The empirical burden*: compatibility is not success. Whether an evolving artifact layer improves useful work per unit of human judgment against stronger models and simpler memory is an open comparison — the [ablation-baselines proposal](../reference/proposals/ablation-baselines-for-the-declared-objective.md) holds the design space for testing it.

## The rest of the portfolio, by role

| Claim | Role | Not |
|---|---|---|
| [Selection removes unearned reach, not structure](./bitter-lesson-selects-against-unearned-reach-not-against-structure.md) | Epistemic criterion: what the lesson's selection actually tests, and a failure mechanism for any loop whose gate confirms fit | A defense — it explains outcomes; it does not license artifacts |
| [Exact implementation does not validate a requirement](./exact-implementation-does-not-validate-a-requirement.md) | Hardening methodology: how cautiously to codify, per artifact–requirement–objective path | Evidence that artifact layers survive scaling |
| [Use tests a decomposition locally](./use-tests-a-decomposition-locally-rationale-makes-transfer-testable.md) + self-use ([the instrument reading](../reference/commonplace-as-an-instrument.md)) | Instrumentation: composition failure surfaces what local checks cannot — missed retrievals, recurring corrections, hidden human patching | Proof that the decomposition is right |
| [Commitments create ground truth](./commitment-not-derivation-creates-new-ground-truth.md) + [reproduction does not transfer authority](./parametric-reproduction-cannot-replace-an-authoritative-record.md) | Answer to the *separate* absorption objection: what governed current state a model copy does not automatically replace | A defense of the whole methodology — it secures a role for authoritative state, in whatever representation supplies currentness, attribution, and revisability |
| [Scaffolding recurs at the moving frontier](./scaling-absorbs-scaffolding-at-fixed-difficulty-not-at-the-frontier.md) | Conditional forecast: external structure recurs while assigned difficulty tracks capability and some function stays advantageous to externalize | Something an introduction requires the reader to accept |
| [Goal-holding interpreters fail soft](./a-goal-holding-interpreter-fails-soft-workarounds-tax-a-bounded-budget.md) | Failure-economics of the interpreted layer: why detection must be engineered where workarounds absorb failures | A bitter-lesson claim at all — it enters the portfolio only through maintenance economics |

The reclassification matters in both directions. Attacking the frontier forecast or the reach criterion leaves the compatibility claim standing; conversely, establishing compatibility proves none of the downstream claims — which artifacts deserve codification, which will be absorbed, which functions recur — each of which must earn its own keep in its own note.

## Scope

- Roles are about the *introductory argument's* dependency structure, not about the claims' truth or importance: the instrumentation and methodology members do more daily work in this KB than the load-bearing member does.
- The portfolio is the current inventory, not a closed set; a new defense enters by having its role classified here before any outward text leans on it.
