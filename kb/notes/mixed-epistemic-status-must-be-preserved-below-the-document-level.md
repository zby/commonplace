# Mixed epistemic status must be preserved below the document level

One prose artifact can contain observations, conclusions entailed by them, and explanations merely compatible with them. A document-level judgment such as “hard evidence” or “plausible reasoning” collapses those differences. KB authoring and review must preserve epistemic status at the level of material claims and inferential transitions.

Whether scientific framing, explicit evidence-and-reasoning sections, or claim-classification steps help LLMs preserve these boundaries is empirical. Their effects should be tested by varying output structure and process structure separately.
