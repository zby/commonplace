# Notes

## Purpose

Keep short pieces about agent-operated KBs that someone can use as written. Name the occasion: answering a recurring objection, illustrating a distinction, or comparing options for a particular reader. No further work or promotion is expected.

Use `kb/theory/` when the contribution is the explanation itself. Use `kb/work/` for material that still serves an investigation, and `kb/log.md` for observations awaiting a use. A workshop's closure is not by itself a reason to move its contents here.

## Quality

Title the note with the question or situation a reader would look up. Use the description to distinguish its use from nearby notes. The body should work without the conversation or workshop that produced it. Attribute borrowed material and link to the evidence for factual examples. When the theory already exists, link to it rather than reconstructing it.

## Outbound links

| Label | Destinations | Reader need |
|---|---|---|
| `see-also` | notes, theory | Find a companion example or the fuller argument. |
| `evidenced-by` | instructions, reference, sources, reports | Inspect the instruction, system description, source or retained result behind an example. |
