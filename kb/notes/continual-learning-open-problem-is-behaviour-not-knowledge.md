# Continual learning's open problem is governing behaviour change, not storing knowledge

Start from the objective. Continual learning means a system keeps improving after deployment; this is the broad criterion in [Learning is not only about generality](./learning-is-not-only-about-generality.md) — any durable change in a system's capacity to adapt to its environment. The requirements below follow from that objective, not from a preference for any one substrate, so anyone pursuing continual learning inherits them. Data engineering already persists, versions, and retrieves *content* once it has been chosen: databases, file systems, vector stores, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), agent-memory records, user profiles. On [behavioral authority](./definitions/behavioral-authority.md) — who consumes an artifact, through which channel, with what force — that is the [knowledge-artifact](./definitions/knowledge-artifact.md) side: content consumed to inform. It is mature infrastructure. It does not decide what to retain, trust, reconcile, forget, or activate, and adding content does not change the rules or authority paths that govern future action. The open problem is the other side: durable *behaviour-changing* writes — [system-definition](./definitions/system-definition-artifact.md) writes that instruct, constrain, route, validate, or train.

Because deployment supplies evidence that is online and sparse rather than batched and abundant, the objective foregrounds two demands on those writes. A write must be **cheap and sample-efficient to install** — the loop closes during deployment, faster than a retraining cycle, often from a single episode (see [Deploy-time learning is the missing middle](./deploy-time-learning-is-the-missing-middle.md)) — and **validated without regression**, since continual adaptation compounds any silent degradation (see [Scheduler-LLM separation exploits an error-correction asymmetry](./scheduler-llm-separation-exploits-an-error-correction-asymmetry.md)).

Those writes can take three [representational forms](./definitions/representational-form.md): **distributed-parametric** (weights, adapters), **natural-language** (prompts, rules, specs), and **symbolic** (code, schemas, tests). The forms trade off systematically — on installability and [sample efficiency](./theory-mediated-learning-may-improve-sample-efficiency-under-shifts.md), on [verifiability](./verifiability-gradient.md), on capacity, on the explanatory [reach](./first-principles-reasoning-selects-for-explanatory-reach-over.md) a governing theory can carry, and on the [addressability](./only-explicit-retention-is-durable-writable-and-addressable.md) that lets a single commitment be named, criticised, and revised — and no form dominates. That general trade space, and how the three improvement loops should relate, belongs to [representational-form coevolution](./treat-continual-learning-as-representational-form-coevolution.md); here the point is only that weights alone do not meet the objective, so its writes span all three forms.

What continual learning adds on top of that trade space is the governance problem. Storing and retrieving content stays infrastructure: a stored fact can sit in weights, notes, or even the live context window and never become [contextually active](./knowledge-storage-does-not-imply-contextual-activation.md) — never change what the agent does — and even when it does activate, it changes only what the agent knows or considers, not the rules that govern future action. The open problem is narrower and harder than "manage knowledge": autonomously *choosing* which behaviour-changing write to make, *validating* it against open-ended behaviour without regression, *coordinating* the write across forms, and *deciding which form a lesson belongs in* — codifying a rule once it stabilises, relaxing it back to prose when it turns brittle ([codification and relaxing](./codification-and-relaxing-navigate-the-bitter-lesson-boundary.md)). Mature release engineering already governs hand-authored readable changes; what remains open is doing this autonomously, at deployment speed, across all three forms at once.
