# Commitment audit: costly entrenchment and option value

## 1. Claim delta

### Finding 1 — The shortened title widens the claim past structural commitment

Status: resolved

Resolution: `candidate.md` restores the exact incumbent title, including
“structural,” so the title and existing backlink meaning retain the target's
scope.

Anchor: `draft.md:10`, “Current-task fit alone does not warrant costly
entrenchment.”

Recommendation: clarify

Basis: `original.md:8`, the brief's fixed central claim, and incumbent
reconciliation I1 all retain “costly structural entrenchment.” Dropping
“structural” makes the title read as a claim about any entrenchment even though
the definitions and Scope limit the argument to types, schemas, vocabularies,
routing conventions, validators, and similar dependency-forming choices. It
also changes the title text named by existing backlinks. The title must carry
the structural choice boundary rather than leave it to body context.

### Finding 3 — Reversible adoption lost the incumbent's narrow-scope condition

Status: resolved

Resolution: The candidate opening now says that present fit can warrant
adoption “at the narrowest useful scope,” before distinguishing that adoption
from entrenchment.

Anchor: `draft.md:12-25`, especially “deserve adoption without deserving
costly entrenchment” and the adoption/entrenchment definitions.

Recommendation: clarify

Basis: original line 14 and incumbent reconciliation I4 preserve adoption “at
the narrowest useful scope,” while revising only the unconditional instruction
to keep every choice replaceable. The draft preserves reversible adoption but
never restores the narrow-scope condition. That condition limits how much
task-local structure present fit can warrant before dependencies themselves
create the costly-reversal branch.

### Finding 4 — “Bounded evidence” is not itself an entrenchment warrant

Status: resolved

Resolution: The candidate names a substantive warrant first, then permits a
bounded probe only when a possible output could satisfy that specific
warrant's own test. It explicitly says boundedness controls probe cost and
dependency footprint rather than conferring warrant.

Anchor: `draft.md:115-118`, “first identify which substantive warrant supports
entrenchment or which bounded evidence could establish one.”

Recommendation: clarify

Basis: the skeleton requires the option analysis to remain downstream of the
three substantive warrants. It separately allows a bounded probe when a
possible result can change the later entrenchment decision. The draft's new
phrase can be read as if boundedness made evidence warrant-bearing, or as if
one generic evidence route could establish any of an enduring derivation,
earned transfer scope, or an actual coordination need. Boundedness controls
the cost and dependency footprint of the probe; a possible output must still
satisfy the specific warrant's own test.

### Finding 6 — Replaceability is correctly conditional rather than a default virtue

Status: resolved

Resolution: Kept in `candidate.md`. The live-option, discriminating-evidence,
availability, and cost conditions remain the required replaceability boundary.

Anchor: `draft.md:79-97`, especially the three conditions on a meaningful
option and “nominal replaceability has no option value for that choice”; also
`draft.md:144-155`.

Recommendation: keep

Basis: the draft applies option reasoning only when present commitment destroys
or burdens an alternative, delay is feasible, the alternative remains usable
after lead time, and a named observation or probe has a possible result that
changes selection, timing, modification, or abandonment. It then defers the
sufficiency judgment to the cost comparison. This preserves the adoption
versus entrenchment boundary, the productive-deferral premise, and the
backlink-sensitive meaning that provisional replaceability is conditional,
while leaving cheap reversible edits outside the branch.

No other material planned commitment is dropped or changed. The incumbent's
unsupported noticeability aside is omitted as directed; its exact `7/139`
observation is not retained without its unavailable evidence and qualification.
