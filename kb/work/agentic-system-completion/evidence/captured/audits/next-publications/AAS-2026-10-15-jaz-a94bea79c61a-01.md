## Consequential discrepancy

### H1 — Replay's per-invocation guarantees omit a known concurrency counterroute

**Source evidence.** Under the frozen source root, `src/jaz/hooks/builtin/replay.py:393–400` explicitly states `SEQUENTIAL INVOKES ASSUMED`: one `_invoke_stack` supplies the active invocation, whereas concurrent `ainvoke` execution can put another invocation on top. It names both sibling-queue mis-serving and latching another invocation's `diverged` flag. The inspected implementation independently supports the mechanism:

- `on_invoke_enter`, lines 509–522, chooses the next child of the stack-top parent and pushes it.
- `on_invoke_exit`, lines 524–526, pops only when the exiting ID is on top.
- `on_llm_query_send`, lines 536–541, uses `_invoke_stack[-1]`, without selecting the node by the query event's invocation ID.
- The following send path checks that selected node's latch and supplies its next response. Optional edit comparison compares edit signatures, not an invocation-ID routing proof.

This is static implementation evidence, not an observed failing concurrent replay. No execution is needed to establish that the source's explicitly declared condition belongs beside the general mechanism claim.

**Published mismatch.** Synthesis, “What survives and acquires later authority,” says invocation-tree position/cursor select saved responses and optional divergence checks withdraw future substitution. Its limitations table addresses projection equivalence and replay/export fidelity, but not wrong-invocation routing under concurrency. `runtime.md#rt-rte-replay-response` similarly presents child selection and per-invocation live fallback without the sequential condition. `epistemic.md#epi-rte-replay-check` says withdrawal is per invoke and not automatic sibling withdrawal. Its authority-ledger row says “stack concurrency remain parent-record limits,” but the parent runtime record does not actually state that limit. The memory replay annotation also does not supply it.

The omitted condition matters because the saved response can direct executable Python. A reader could infer that interleaved invocations retain the described invocation-local queue and withdrawal semantics, even though the source says they do not. Generic “not exact-environment reproduction,” unobserved-operation and no-benefit disclaimers do not describe this specific implemented routing failure.

**Unblock condition.** Through a separately authorized publication correction/reanalysis route, carry the sequential-nesting condition and concurrent sibling-queue/divergence misattribution into the public synthesis and relevant replay records. Align the dangling ledger qualification with an actual qualified parent record. Alternatively, supply bounded frozen-source evidence establishing why this source-declared counterroute cannot occur within the reviewed scope and explicitly narrow the public scope accordingly. A live success demonstration alone would not remove the known condition. Revalidate, record the new byte map and obtain a fresh review of the changed publication; this report cannot clear altered bytes automatically.
