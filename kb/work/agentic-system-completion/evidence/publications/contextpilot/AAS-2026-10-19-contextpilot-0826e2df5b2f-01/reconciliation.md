### Connected findings and integration dispositions

**Delivery versus semantic acceptance.** [MEM-RTE-inference-stores](memory.md#mem-rte-inference-stores) and [MEM-RTE-training-stores](memory.md#mem-rte-training-stores) distinguish all-summary inventory push, requested full-entry reads and bounded neighbor displays. The epistemic assessment of [EPI-OBJ-memory-content](epistemic.md#epi-obj-memory-content) concerns warrant, while [RT-BAP-context-policy](runtime.md#rt-bap-context-policy) concerns consequential prompt delivery. These findings converge without equating retention, operational admission or relevant review with evidence-consuming acceptance. The epistemic ledger's bounded lack of post-acceptance integration does not deny implemented indexing or later use.

**Memory integration issue: synthetic trace origin.** [MEM-RTE-training-context](memory.md#mem-rte-training-context) adds code-authored cleanup with two outcomes: a trainable forced-action suffix and non-branchable snapshot, or emergency conditioning only. [MEM-OBJ-trajectory-snapshots](memory.md#mem-obj-trajectory-snapshots) supplies the alternative length-boundary representation. This extends [RT-RTE-training-rollout](runtime.md#rt-rte-training-rollout) and [RT-RTE-actor-update](runtime.md#rt-rte-actor-update); neither declares that every credited action was sampled from the policy. The connection must retain mixed action provenance and the conditioning-only alternative, not infer that all cleanup is trainable. No conflicting finding remains.

**Memory integration issue: restore and merge availability.** [MEM-OBJ-notes](memory.md#mem-obj-notes) and [EPI-RTE-memory-merge](epistemic.md#epi-rte-memory-merge) agree that optional merge concatenates bodies, loses rich fields and lacks default-schema exposure. Memory additionally records its immediate reindexing limitation. A wired configurable handler and the absence of an established ordinary merge caller are different findings, not contrary operation statuses. [RT-OBJ-context-masks](runtime.md#rt-obj-context-masks) and [RT-RTE-context-edit](runtime.md#rt-rte-context-edit) describe explicit restoration only where authorized; memory adds default-schema omission. These records do not assert ordinary availability of every implemented method. Restoration of a view also does not recover an overwritten keyed entry.

**Memory integration issues: distinct containers and admission rules.** The identity dispositions above resolve the snapshot/store overlap question without supersession. [MEM-RTE-inference-stores](memory.md#mem-rte-inference-stores) requires new simple-note content and can preserve an omitted summary; [MEM-RTE-training-stores](memory.md#mem-rte-training-stores) permits normalized empty content and clears an omitted summary. These are implementation differences, not disagreements. The broad within-trajectory write account in [RT-RTE-document-qa](runtime.md#rt-rte-document-qa) does not impose inference's checks on training.

**Conditional workflow and two completion criteria.** The [workflow account](workflow.md#workflow-account) connects retention, cleanup, review-event tracking, retry and artifact handoff to [RT-RTE-document-qa](runtime.md#rt-rte-document-qa). Its distinctions agree with [RT-CLM-fsm-controls](runtime.md#rt-clm-fsm-controls): computed allowed-set enforcement is conditional on configuration, base inference is an alternate path, and recovery reads establish delivery rather than relevant model checking. Driver artifact delivery can follow incomplete protocol execution. The epistemic operational-admission assessment preserves those same qualifications rather than treating an extracted answer as acceptance or correctness.

**Evaluation force remains route-specific.** The three offline evaluator records above annotate artifacts and aggregates without an established QA rollback or memory-revision consumer. Their parser/denominator rules differ: LongMemEval counts parse failures as false, while BrowseComp+ excludes unparsed verdicts. [EPI-RTE-terminal-evaluation](epistemic.md#epi-rte-terminal-evaluation), in contrast, feeds [RT-RTE-actor-update](runtime.md#rt-rte-actor-update) through [RT-BAP-reward-actor](runtime.md#rt-bap-reward-actor). Agreement with an answer reference does not establish the truth of every retained fact or vindicate every ancestor edit. These are separate force and warrant findings, not inconsistent judgments about one evaluator.

**Memory integration issue: unresolved downstream horizons.** [RT-CMP-actor-model](runtime.md#rt-cmp-actor-model), [RT-CMP-compression-model](runtime.md#rt-cmp-compression-model), [RT-RTE-actor-update](runtime.md#rt-rte-actor-update) and the [memory annotations](memory.md#annotations) consistently retain unpinned provider state, unobserved semantic fidelity, untraced production adoption and no established cross-task semantic trace reload. Checkpoint resume and within-trajectory prompt read-back are positive wiring findings with different consumers and horizons. The epistemic conclusion preserves reward-responsive update capacity without asserting exercised learning, reflection, theory criticism or self-improvement. Missing outcome evidence does not cancel that capacity; capacity does not establish beneficial outcomes or validate [EPI-CLM-performance-results](epistemic.md#epi-clm-performance-results).
