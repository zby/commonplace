# ContextPilot synthesis verification

## Verification

The [public synthesis](synthesis.md#bounded-synthesis) supports publication as a bounded implementation account. I checked its substantive statements against the handed runtime, memory, epistemic and workflow records, the reconciliation's identity dispositions, and the report and profile verification judgments. Reconciliation supplies no supersessions or unresolved conflicts. The synthesis answers file is empty and no refusal was supplied. I made no target-source reads and added no evidence.

The evidence basis is explicit and agrees with the [boundary](boundary.md#source-register): SRC-1 at the frozen commit, with implementation, shipped instructions and attributed reported operation, but no inspected execution or causal experiment. The whole-system characterization is limited to ContextPilot responsibilities, not the bundled framework's entire capability surface. The synthesis does not turn the README's performance assertion into a measured result; see [EPI-CLM-performance-results](epistemic.md#epi-clm-performance-results).

The request-to-delivery account preserves the distinctions that determine what the controls guarantee. Ordinary inference excludes the reference answer while retry can supply incorrectness feedback. The FSM rejects names relative to its computed allowed set, not an unconditional legal-state invariant: full-schema fallback, base inference and the separate training controller remain visible beside the claim. Mechanical recovery reads are not described as relevant model checking. Answer extraction and artifact delivery remain distinct from successful finish and correctness. These conclusions agree with [RT-RTE-document-qa](runtime.md#rt-rte-document-qa), [RT-RTE-base-inference](runtime.md#rt-rte-base-inference), [RT-CLM-fsm-controls](runtime.md#rt-clm-fsm-controls) and the [workflow account](workflow.md#workflow-account).

It preserves inference/training admission differences, unverified provenance, and the difference between withdrawing current store content and retracting old delivered copies. Merge and restoration are not promoted from implemented methods to default availability. Backing history is distinguished from edited views; token-window handling remains best effort, and compression or substring extraction supplies no fidelity guarantee. See [MEM-RTE-inference-stores](memory.md#mem-rte-inference-stores), [MEM-RTE-training-stores](memory.md#mem-rte-training-stores), [MEM-OBJ-memory-indexes](memory.md#mem-obj-memory-indexes), [MEM-OBJ-notes](memory.md#mem-obj-notes), [RT-RTE-context-edit](runtime.md#rt-rte-context-edit) and [EPI-OBJ-memory-content](epistemic.md#epi-obj-memory-content).
