[RT-RTE-gym-build](runtime.md#rt-rte-gym-build) establishes persisted generated source/package material, but the storage units leave Gym within an uninspected lifecycle grouping without a separate local files finding. [RT-CMP-rl-model](runtime.md#rt-cmp-rl-model) establishes distributed actor/serving parameters, while the RL storage unit is wholly uninspected because its persistence organization is unresolved. These are local omissions from an explicitly partial inventory, not unsupported emitted values or claims of absence. Files and model-weights already have independent supported witnesses elsewhere in the profile, so the system-level existence union remains valid. Withhold object-level storage completeness and any inference that the uninspected Gym/RL units have no classifiable retained state; consult these records for their supported local facts.
