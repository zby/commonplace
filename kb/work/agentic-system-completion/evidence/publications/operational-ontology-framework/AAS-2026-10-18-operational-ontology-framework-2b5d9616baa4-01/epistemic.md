## Bounded conclusion

The reference prescribes acquisition of organizational facts, a prioritized draft, human accuracy review, permission to proceed, and approved-status/blocker recording. It explicitly rejects treating retrieval or fluent model output as evidence of truth or action completion. Acquisition source warrant remains unknown: naming a source and recording confidence do not establish its truth, freshness, or faithful transport. The review policy supplies a plausible place to withhold reliance, not an observed evidence-consuming acceptance decision. Its criterion is accuracy against confirmed organizational information, for weekly-review status and permission to proceed; no acceptance of a produced ampliative theory is established. See [EPI-RTE-data-import](#epi-rte-data-import), [EPI-RTE-source-check](#epi-rte-source-check), [EPI-RTE-review-disposition](#epi-rte-review-disposition).

The prescribed sorting is a non-ampliative operation if it only reorders unchanged inputs, but neither an actual draft nor a deterministic operational transformer is available. The whole draft's relation is therefore indeterminate, not demonstrated entailed derivation and not established ampliative conjecture. The missing-status rule is a source-supported counterexample to assuming that the design licenses unconstrained inference. The approved-status write-back is a counterexample to assuming that draft-only AI means no organizational write occurs. Retention and next-session continuity are intended; actual read-back, evidence-linked lifecycle integration, and a later consumer's changed behavior remain unestablished. See [EPI-RTE-draft-generation](#epi-rte-draft-generation), [EPI-RTE-status-retention](#epi-rte-status-retention), [RT-RTE-weekly-review](runtime.md#rt-rte-weekly-review).

Human review has policy-level withholding and correction force, with the authorized organizational writer as its intended consumer and the next review as its intended horizon. Completion requires observable action evidence, while high-impact effects require human authority. Those are separate epistemic and operational requirements, neither implemented here. Rule amendment can change policy without producing a truth-apt claim; legal/security/revocation precedence is declared but no executable rejection, withdrawal, cleanup or recovery loop is supplied. See [EPI-RTE-completion-check](#epi-rte-completion-check), [RT-RTE-rule-amendment](runtime.md#rt-rte-rule-amendment), [RT-BAP-action-authority](runtime.md#rt-bap-action-authority).

The implemented publication checker produces bounded evidence about exact file/mode/content predicates and HTTP request outcomes. Its nonzero exits have immediate job-continuation force, not established merge-veto force or epistemic authority over operational facts. No target execution was needed for the deterministic code deduction in [EPI-RTE-public-check](#epi-rte-public-check). This positive implementation finding must not be collapsed into the operational loop's doctrine-only status.

No inspected production parameters, traces or comparisons support claims of model adaptation, improved future capacity, autonomous theory building or exercised self-improvement. These remain independent unresolved properties, not a system-wide negative. Human correction of a work product alone establishes none of them. The public artifact's explicitly conceptual scope is not a product failure; it limits which conclusions this run can warrant.
