The only issue-feed entry was [merged PR #1](https://github.com/nasqret/labyrinth-exploration/pull/1), by the author; there were no open issues, issue comments, PR reviews or inline review comments returned. Its branch was three commits behind main, none ahead. This screens for public overlap, not private intent.

The history moves from the first public release through Codex support and integration checks to client-neutral presentation and the 1.1.0 release. PR #1 reports a particularly relevant test: a fresh referee independently enumerated all 64 four-vertex graphs, rejected a false claim, and the coordinator checked the correction before integration. It also reports distinguishing prior certificate-code validation from replay at a new size. That is stronger evidence about one actual review than the shipped instructions alone. Do not recommend adding fresh referees, native loader tests, a Codex adapter, or pending-review labels as new ideas. The discriminating question is what happens **after** the successful review.

## 1. Follow a corrected claim to its least obvious reader

Commonplace’s [review-freshness rule](../theory/criteria-edits-invalidate-verdicts-process-edits-invalidate-artifacts.md) ties a judgment to the content judged. Its [multiple-consumer note](../theory/agent-memory-requirements/serve-multiple-consumers.md) asks what each reader actually receives. Applied together: a correction accepted in the project record and a successful export do not establish that the paper says what the referee checked.

The local advisory audit found a concrete divergence. The [example writer](https://github.com/nasqret/labyrinth-exploration/blob/019a93da59313e697dc6f28bcfe1588a376c7565/examples/triangle-counts/exhaustive_n8.py#L88-L96) updates `result`, status and references. The [table renderer](https://github.com/nasqret/labyrinth-exploration/blob/019a93da59313e697dc6f28bcfe1588a376c7565/templates/lab.py#L374-L404) prefers a separate optional `result_tex` for LaTeX. Markdown and the dashboard read `result`. A new TeX file can therefore pair the old assertion with the new proof reference. This is not a stale-file or interrupted-write case. The shipped `tc-n8` row has no override; no wrong paper was observed.

**Small test:** seed an old override, correct the plain result and its citation, rebuild, and inspect the assertions in both exports. Compare their meaning, not literal Markdown and TeX strings. An inexpensive policy would mark an override stale when its source assertion changes and require an explicit refresh or removal before export. That catches a missed update; it does not prove mathematical equivalence.

## 2. Test whether a lesson rules out too much

The [analysis of refutation and handoff](../agentic-system-analyses/retained/nasqret--labyrinth-exploration/synthesis.md#from-evidence-to-authorized-use) describes retaining counterexamples and passing lessons to later investigators. Commonplace’s [episode-evidence note](../theory/retaining-episode-evidence-keeps-a-distilled-rule-open-to.md) explains why preserving the witness can matter: a later investigator can challenge the inference from the case to the lesson, not merely accept or discard it. A refutation of a universal claim may leave a useful restricted claim intact. A briefing that prevents all further work on the topic could look efficient while suppressing a discovery.

**Small test:** select one archived refutation for which the maintainer can specify a nearby question the witness does not settle. From one frozen project state, give fresh investigators either the usual lesson or the same lesson with the precise failed assumption and witness pointer. Keep the same underlying files accessible in both arms. Before seeing outputs, define two errors: repeating the refuted step and dismissing the live question on the authority of that refutation. Score the first proposed investigation and its reason, not whether it cites the lesson. Repeat with the same model and budget, and record lookup cost. This follows the [matched-intervention note](../theory/retained-theory-intervention-isolates-one-explicit-surface.md): a difference bears on the briefing change, not all project memory; a null result may mean the investigator reconstructed the distinction from existing files. It is not a second test of referee correctness.

## 3. A progress indicator can hide a contradiction

The [engine analysis](../agentic-system-analyses/retained/nasqret--labyrinth-exploration/synthesis.md#what-the-engine-can-establish) distinguishes the explicit consistency check from standalone projection. For an admissible frontier value marked both realized and impossible, the check diagnoses overlap, but standalone build counts it as resolved, with realized taking priority. The author already tests the fraction on a consistent example. A different regression would deliberately introduce one conflicting value and ask whether a dashboard reader can distinguish it from a settled one. This is about the meaning of the numerator, not a request for more review or a claim that a released project contains such a conflict.

## Consult the research: what changes the advice?

The [README consultation route](../../README.md#consult-the-research-with-your-agent) starts with a project decision, then looks for a Commonplace result and the practice it informed. Two decisions in the [Labyrinth synthesis](../agentic-system-analyses/retained/nasqret--labyrinth-exploration/synthesis.md#from-evidence-to-authorized-use) fit especially well: what a writer receives when turning a verdict into canonical edits, and what a reader sees before deciding a published theorem supports a local premise.

**Writer handoff.** The [evaluation guide](../tags/evaluation-README.md) points to a [pre-registered write-brief pilot](../theory/evidence/full-write-briefs-cut-edit-drift-one-line-briefs-did-not.md). Ten documents, 80 pressure edits and 15 overrides. Among requirements a careful editor could not infer, the original brief lost 5/80, no brief 14/78, a one-line brief 13/80. A same-length reconstruction from a still-faithful document and backlinks lost 6/78. These are item counts, not percentages of failed runs. Primary scoring excluded two runs that asked questions; including them changes some conclusions. One model family and an editing task, not mathematical research. The pilot informed [ADR 092](../reference/adr/092-write-briefs-are-optional-sidecars-named-by-a-validated-pointer.md): a writer gets an optional separate brief when the text cannot reliably convey its constraints. Adapt the delivery of non-obvious exclusions, not the sidecar schema. Test at the handoff Labyrinth already has, not by creating another review role.

**Literature check.** The [claims and grounding guide](../tags/claims-and-grounding-README.md) leads to [a four-claim comparison](../theory/evidence/independent-pass-tightened-three-of-four-pirolli-verdicts.md). A fresh account of the source written without the target, followed by a judge comparing frozen accounts, narrowed three prior verdicts. The fourth stayed unsupported. Several variables changed; neither better accuracy in general nor an effect of blinding alone follows. The [resulting procedure](../instructions/assess-a-claim-bearing-artifact-against-external-literature.md) reserves the extra work for a disputed comparison, an explicit request, or an evaluation. Transfer that narrow trigger to Labyrinth: a claim-blind reading of a disputed literature premise is different from the already shipped fresh check of a proposed proof. Score false narrowing as well as permissive matches.

**Editorial choice.** Lead the author memo with these two transfers. The export mismatch is useful project inspection, but does not demonstrate what consulting Commonplace contributes. Keep it in this research file rather than using it to headline the memo.

## Initial shortlist from project inspection

Lead with the TeX case: it can reverse what the author thinks a successful correction delivered, and it has a small deterministic fixture. Retain the paired lesson test because it measures a cost that a count of avoided dead ends cannot see. Hold the progress indicator in reserve: unlike the export divergence, the separate check already diagnoses its underlying condition. None of these exact tests appeared in the inspected public plan or PR; that does not establish they would be news to the author personally.
