# Experiment 3 results: the worked-out revision won in either position

The [design](./experiment-3-retained-reason.md) varied one sentence: the operator's reason that small changes in a system should not flip membership. All proposers received the generality hint. Fresh Opus choosers compared the original with the committed two-condition draft.

| Outcome | Reason present | Reason removed |
|---|---|---|
| Chose the two-condition draft | 5/6 | 5/6 |
| Proposed removing the third condition | 2/6 | 1 agreed, 1 disputed out of 6 |
| Cited continuity | 6/12 | 0 or 1/12 |

The committed draft won 5/6 when first and 5/6 when second. In the [retained answers](./positive-evidence/), `e3-CW` denotes a choice; `before` or `after` names the version shown first. The three agreed proposals are w15 and w19 (reason present) and [w11](./positive-evidence/e3-WG2-R--w11.txt) (absent). No proposer gave the operator's argument from the store. Scorers agreed on 23/24; w22 offered either weakening or removing the condition, hence both ranges.

Workers reused the reason when available. Its removal did not lower the observed choice rate. This supports repeating the comparison with the **specific worked-out revision**, not recommending the sentence as an instruction.
