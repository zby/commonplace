# Experiment 2 results: one proposal, three choices

In the [two-conditions replay](./experiment-2-two-conditions.md), one worker proposed reporting openness to criticism as a property rather than requiring it for membership. [Answer v01](./positive-evidence/e2-WG2-v01.txt) reclassified five cases. Its reason was that a small change should not flip membership, not the operator's argument that an addressable store already permits later correction.

| Task | Result |
|---|---|
| Propose, generality hint | 1/3 full hits; another kept a weaker third condition and was not a hit |
| Propose, no hint / bias / test each condition / purpose | 0/3 in each arm |
| Choose between drafts | 3/4 chose two conditions: 2/2 when shown first, 1/2 when shown second |

Both scorers agreed on all 19 Opus answers. The [positive choices](./positive-evidence/) are v05, v16 and v19. Their use of the earlier ruling's reason suggested a transfer test; [experiment 3](./experiment-3-results.md) did not find that the sentence improved decisions.
