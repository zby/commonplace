# A frozen LLM needs only the competences the learning loop leaves to it

A selection-based learner proposes changes to retained methods, evaluates them,
and can reject them before they govern later work. In any such loop with a
frozen LLM, a competence is required of the model only if the loop needs it
and the rest of the system does not supply it. Autonomy requires the deployed
system to make its own update decisions, not the model to make every decision.
Reports and outcomes can come from people without people choosing the updates.

## What the model must be able to do when assigned the role

- **Interpret outcomes and propose revisions.** If traces do not already identify
  what should change, connect a failure to plausible causes in the retained
  method and distinguish repairing the work product from repairing the method.
  Where candidate search is delegated to the model, express usable alternatives
  in the store's artifact format. A trace analyzer or repair generator can
  supply either part.
- **Seek discriminating evidence.** If no test service specifies what to check,
  identify an observation or comparison that could favor one candidate over
  another, including no change. If a runner can replay tasks or measure costs,
  the model need only request the relevant check and interpret the result to
  the extent that the runner leaves its meaning unresolved.
- **Judge what the evidence warrants.** If gates do not settle adoption, compare
  the live alternatives under the applicable criteria. Recognize when evidence
  supports rejection, further inquiry, or a bounded trial rather than adoption.
  A fixed gate can decide cases it covers; the model need not invent a scalar
  score for cases left open.
- **Use retained methods in later work.** If the model executes a prose method,
  it must recognize when it applies and follow it in context. If a dispatcher
  invokes an executable method, applicability and execution can live there
  instead. Successful storage alone does not close the loop.

## Where the boundary moves

Scheduling, retrieval, versioning, replay, installation, and rollback can live
in the harness. Running a rollback is distinct from deciding which update
caused a regression: the model needs causal diagnosis only if that decision
is left to it. Likewise, revising the adoption criteria is not required for
selection under fixed criteria. If the loop is meant to revise its criteria
and that role is delegated to the model, it must distinguish a bad candidate
from a check that admits bad candidates and propose a way to test the difference.

A competence is adequate relative to its interface and workload, not to unaided
performance. To test a claimed requirement, keep the rest of the loop fixed
and remove the model's contribution at that interface. If the loop still makes
the relevant decisions on fresh cases without human intervention, that
competence was not required of the model in that configuration.
