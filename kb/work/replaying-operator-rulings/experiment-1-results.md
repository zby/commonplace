# Experiment 1 results: one proposal admitted fixed-measure learners

Across four [rounds](./experiment-1-fixed-measure.md), **1/64** Opus answers asked to propose changes reached the ruling. In round 4, the purpose hint said to distinguish the general term from the project's particular kind, and workers could change the definition, checks and supporting note. [Answer u03](./positive-evidence/e1-WD4-u03.txt) removed the fixed-measure exclusion and moved the affected cases inside the term but outside the kind. The other two workers with the same hint used it on different features.

Both scorers agreed on target hits. In round 2 they differed on how to classify three misses. The same purpose hint with fixed checks gave 0/3. One hit is a case to reproduce, not evidence that either the hint or permission caused it.
