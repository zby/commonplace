# Experiment 5 results: choosers usually took the first draft

In the [elaboration test](./experiment-5-elaborated-demotions.md), all six writers made the assigned change. Each worker-written draft was compared with the original in both orders.

| Worker-written revision | Chosen when first | Chosen when second |
|---|---|---|
| Demote legible store (target) | 4/4 | 0/4 |
| Demote judgment in the loop (decoy) | 2/4 | 0/4 |
| Demote open to criticism (target) | 4/4 | 0/4 |

Both scorers agreed on all 24 choices. In 22/24 the first draft won. The two exceptions concerned the same decoy draft. Neither target showed a preference independent of order.

By contrast, the committed two-condition draft won 5/6 from **each** position in [experiment 3](./experiment-3-results.md). It supplied new distinctions about how correction works, rather than only removing the condition. Asking writers to develop what replaces a removed condition is a possible next test, not an established explanation.
