# Experiment 4 results: enumeration supplied candidates, not adoption

In the [enumeration test](./experiment-4-enumerated-demotions.md), all three generators covered the three numbered conditions, splitting them into 12–15 requirements each. Separate Opus workers judged one-sentence proposals to move a condition out of membership.

| Condition to demote | Adopted when judged alone | Adopted when all three were judged |
|---|---|---|
| Legible store (target) | 0/3 | 0/4 |
| Judgment in the loop (decoy) | 0/3 | 0/4 |
| Open to criticism (target) | 0/3 | 0/4 |

Two scorers agreed on all 16 answers. No verdict adopted even a weakened proposal. On the same state, [experiment 3](./experiment-3-results.md) had 10/12 choose a worked-out revision demoting the third condition. The tasks differed in the detail supplied, the default (choose versus reject a change), and how the checks were presented. This comparison does not isolate the cause.
