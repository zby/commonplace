## 1. Condition 2 should require a judgment to keep the change, not a judgment that it improves

**Change:** Reword condition 2 so that each change is kept or dropped by an on-balance judgment, made in the loop, about whether keeping it serves future work. That judgment may be a decision taken while the verdict is open. It no longer has to be "a judgment … that the change improves the system's future work."

**Reason:** The draft says "Open" is the usual verdict for a recent change. It also says keeping a change is a decision, not a claim that the change was learned. As written, condition 2 therefore excludes most of the retention in Commonplace, which the term exists to cover. The reversibility note shows the gap: there, keeping the instruction is justified without any judgment that it improved later work.

## 2. Make the legible store a mark of a kind within the term, not a condition of membership

**Change:** Remove "legible store" from the three conditions. Add a stated kind, such as *legible deliberative learning*, in which retained changes are readable and can be judged one at a time. Commonplace's distinctive claim then attaches to that kind.

**Reason:** "Deliberative" names revisable on-balance judgment about what to keep. A team that keeps or reverts whole checkpoints by such a judgment deliberates in that sense, even though its store is weights. The draft's own note, that legibility is what makes judging a single change possible, is a claim about why one kind works better. It is not a reason for excluding other kinds from the term.

## 3. Resolve the contradiction about retention by a fixed check

**Change:** Replace "a change kept because a validator passed meets the condition" with a statement that a fixed check meets condition 2 only when the named system can itself question and revise that check.

**Reason:** As written, that sentence contradicts the exclusion "Retention bound to a fixed measure … fails the second condition." It also contradicts check case 4, where the repairer alone is outside the term and the team plus the repairer is inside. The rewording ties the validator case to the outermost-loop rule the draft already states, so it does not rest on the idea of a "degenerate judgment" that any fixed rule could claim.
