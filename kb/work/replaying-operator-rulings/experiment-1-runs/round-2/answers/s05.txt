## 1. Condition 2: require a retention decision, not an on-balance judgment of improvement

**Change.** Replace "kept or dropped by an on-balance judgment that the change improves the system's future work" with something like "kept or dropped by a judgment, made within the loop, about the change's expected effect on the system's future work."

Then add a claim about a kind within the term. Commonplace aims at retention that is later tested by on-balance verdicts.

**Reason.** The draft itself says "Open" is the usual verdict for a recent change, and that keeping such a change is a decision. The reversibility example adds that a prospect of correction can tip the choice to keep without being evidence of improvement. So, as written, most of Commonplace's own retention fails the second condition. Requiring the project's strict "every reasonable measure" judgment as a condition of membership also builds the project's own method into the general term.

## 2. Make "fixed measure" depend on whether the loop can question it, and resolve the loop rule

**Change.** Replace "not bound to one measure fixed beforehand" with "not bound to a measure that the named loop cannot question or revise." The checks file already uses that wording.

Then reconcile three things:
- the note that a validator pass "meets the condition";
- the exclusion of "retention bound to a fixed measure";
- the rule that conditions are "checked on the outermost loop."

The fix is to say that a fixed check meets the second condition only for a named system whose own loop can revise the check.

**Reason.** As drafted, a change kept because a validator passed both meets the condition and is excluded. Meanwhile, check case 4 places the repairer alone outside and the team plus repairer inside. "Fixed beforehand" would also exclude every validator written before use, even one Commonplace can revise. That conflicts with putting all of Commonplace's learning under one term.

## 3. Restrict "each change" to the changes claimed as learning

**Change.** Say that the conditions quantify over the changes put forward as learning in the named store. They do not cover every write to it. Narrow the exclusion "Retention without a judgment" to match.

**Reason.** Commonplace, like the knowledge-centric self-improvement (KSI) protocol in the checks, keeps logs, attempt records and other writes by default. If every write must pass the second condition, the whole store fails. The checks file flags this directly: whether the draft's claim is about the distilled guidance or about every write in the KB changes KSI's placement.
