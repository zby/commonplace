## 1. Delete the section "When a change was learned" and replace it with a link to the on-balance note

Remove the section, the sentence "Whether a particular change was learned is a separate claim, defined below," and the misuse line about reporting a kept change as learned. The draft itself says the term has no success condition, so a second definition of success does not belong inside it. The copy is also already lossy: it leaves out the note's three choices that fix the comparison (the alternative, who gains and who loses, and the period), and the note's account of which measures are reasonable. A one-line pointer to the note would let the verdicts have one home that cannot drift.

## 2. Reword condition 2 so the judgment is about keeping a change, not about whether it improves the system

Change "Each change is kept or dropped by an on-balance judgment that the change improves the system's future work" to roughly: "The loop decides on balance whether to keep a change for the system's future work, and that decision is not bound to a measure it cannot question." That is close to the definition-checks file's wording of J. As written, the condition conflicts with the draft's own later text, which allows keeping a change whose verdict is open "as a decision", including when reversibility tips the choice "not as evidence that the instruction has improved later work". Dropping "each" also removes a problem the checks raise: if every write to the store had to qualify, a store with unjudged logs (KSI, or Commonplace's own logs) would fail, and the term could no longer cover all of Commonplace's learning.

## 3. Delete the "degenerate on-balance judgment" bullet and the sentence "checked on the outermost loop"

The bullet says a change kept because a validator passed meets condition 2. That contradicts the exclusion "Retention bound to a fixed measure" and case 4 of the checks, where the repairer on its own is outside on J. The outermost-loop sentence conflicts with the draft's own team example, which places the inner training loop outside, and with the checks' rule against crediting an automated loop with its designers' judgments. Naming the system, together with the existing bullet "A loop with a fixed measure may sit inside a deliberative one", already places validator-gated changes correctly, so both of these can go.
