**1. Delete the note "A deterministic check is a degenerate on-balance judgment."**
Its sentence "a change kept because a validator passed meets the condition" contradicts the draft's own exclusion of "retention bound to a fixed measure." It also flips check case 4, which places the repairer alone outside on J. In Commonplace, validator-kept changes already count through the outermost-loop rule, because the system itself runs the loop that writes and revises validators, so the note adds nothing except the contradiction.

**2. In condition 2, replace "Each change is kept or dropped by an on-balance judgment" with "What the store retains is decided by an on-balance judgment," and say that a standing judgment about a class of changes counts.**
"Each change" makes every write to the named store need its own judgment. The checks show this changes KSI's placement once its automatic attempt log is in the store. It would also exclude much of Commonplace, which the draft says the term must cover. Once change 1 removes the degenerate-check note, this wording is what keeps validator-passed patches inside: the outer loop's open judgment that the validator suffices for that class of changes is the retaining judgment.

**3. Delete the section "When a change was learned" and the misuse line about reporting a kept change as learned. Replace them with one sentence pointing to the on-balance note's verdicts.**
The draft says the term has no success condition, yet this section restates the note's verdict list almost word for word as a definition of "learned." That redefinition conflicts with the Simon sense the draft itself cites: a change that is better on some measures and tied on the rest is a lasting gain in capacity, but here it is "not learned." The verdicts belong to the on-balance note, where they already live, and the process definition is clearer without a second, narrower meaning of "learned."
