# Three proposed removals

## 1. Condition 2: remove "that the change improves the system's future work"

Change "kept or dropped by an on-balance judgment that the change improves the system's future work" to "kept or dropped by an on-balance judgment."

**Reason:** The draft says "Open" is the usual verdict for a recent change, and that keeping such a change is a decision, not a judgment that it improves anything. ADR 085's deletion and the reversibility-tipped routing instruction are both kept without that claim. As written, condition 2 excludes these Commonplace changes, and it also excludes check case 6, which keeps a prompt "after weighing mixed outcomes."

## 2. Notes on judgment in the loop: delete the bullet "A deterministic check is a degenerate on-balance judgment"

**Reason:** Its sentence "a change kept because a validator passed meets the condition" contradicts the draft's own exclusion of "retention bound to a fixed measure." It also contradicts check case 4, where the link repairer alone is outside on J. It erases the line between a fixed-measure loop and the deliberative loop around it, and the draft already places that line through its outermost-loop rule.

## 3. Condition 2: remove the quantifier "Each" (read "Changes are kept or dropped…")

**Reason:** The checks point out that if every write to the named store must pass a judgment, any store that also takes default writes fails. Attempt logs are one example, and the KSI placement shows this. Commonplace's KB is such a store, so the term would fail the purpose stated in the draft: to name all the learning in Commonplace. Without the universal quantifier, writes kept by default simply fall outside the term, as "retention without a judgment" already says, and they no longer disqualify the whole system.
