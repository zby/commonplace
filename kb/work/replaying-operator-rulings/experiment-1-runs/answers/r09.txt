## Three most important changes, most important first

### 1. Replace the "deterministic check is a degenerate on-balance judgment" bullet, and tighten condition 2's "one measure fixed beforehand"

**Change:** Condition 2 should exclude retention bound to any fixed rule the loop cannot question, however many measures that rule combines. The validator bullet should say that a passing check can settle an individual retention only when the same named loop holds, and can revise, the judgment that the check is enough for that question.

**Reason:** As written, "a change kept because a validator passed meets the condition" contradicts three things:
- the exclusion of "retention bound to a fixed measure";
- the PRODIGY verdict;
- check case 4 ("repairer alone: outside on J").

Its example is also wrong for the retention question: a link-resolution check cannot show that a link reaches the intended page, let alone that the patch improves future work. "One measure" also lets a fixed multi-test gate like Recuris's pass, though "several tests in a fixed rule are still a fixed rule."

### 2. Replace "checked on the outermost loop" with a rule that classifies each retention by the loop that makes it

**Change:** State that a retention is classified by the loop that makes it. An outer deliberative loop learns the rules and measures it sets for inner loops, such as a repair rule or a validator. It does not learn each change those inner loops keep by fixed measure. Then check that the claim of covering "all the learning in Commonplace" still holds under this rule, or qualify it.

**Reason:** The draft says the conditions are checked on the outermost loop, yet its own model-training example classifies the inner loop separately ("the training inside it is not"). Case 4 places the team-plus-repairer only "for its repair rules." Without this rule it is unclear whether Commonplace's validator-gated inner loops are covered. That decides whether the term really names all the project's learning.

### 3. Fix the scope of "each change" in condition 2

**Change:** Say whether condition 2's "each change" covers only the changes offered as learning in the named store, or every write to that store. Say what happens to writes kept by default, such as logs, attempt records and ingests.

**Reason:** Read as "every write," the draft's own exclusion of "retention without a judgment" puts any store with append-only records outside the term. That would include Commonplace's own KB. The checks show this changes placements (KSI's distilled guidance versus its attempt log). It also lets a claim about one curated subset quietly become a claim about the whole store.
