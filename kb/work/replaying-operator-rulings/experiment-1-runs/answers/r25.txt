## Should a fixed-measure learner be outside the definition?

Yes, it should stay outside. The term only has to cover the learning in Commonplace. Commonplace's fixed-measure loops, such as its validators, run inside an outer loop that Commonplace runs itself, so excluding them costs nothing.

A standalone learner that keeps whatever passes a measure it cannot question is not Commonplace's learning. Examples are a gradient-training run, Recuris, or PRODIGY on its own. Letting such learners in would remove the contrast that gives the term its use.

The draft is not consistent on this point, though. Condition 2 says the judgment is "not bound to one measure fixed beforehand." The note on deterministic checks says "a change kept because a validator passed meets the condition." Check case 4 says the link repairer on its own fails J. The changes below settle that conflict.

## Proposed changes, most important first

**1. Rewrite condition 2 as a delegation test, and qualify the deterministic-check note.**
- **Change:** A change meets condition 2 in either of two ways:
  - it is kept by an on-balance judgment; or
  - it is kept by a measure that the same named system has judged sufficient for that class of change, where that system's own loop can also override or revise the measure.

  Then delete the unconditional "a change kept because a validator passed meets the condition."
- **Reason:** At present the condition, the deterministic-check note and case 4 give three different answers for a validator-gated change. The delegation test matches the checks' J condition and case 8. It covers Commonplace's validator-gated changes and keeps standalone fixed-measure learners outside.

**2. Limit "each change" to the changes the system keeps for its future work.**
- **Change:** Condition 2 should apply to those changes, not to every write in the store. Records such as logs and attempt traces should be explicitly outside the quantifier.
- **Reason:** As written, one unjudged write to the store takes the whole system out of the definition. Commonplace makes such writes, so the definition would fail its stated purpose of covering all the learning in Commonplace. The checks show that the KSI placement flips on this one wording question.

**3. Correct the PRODIGY background bullet.**
- **Change:** Replace "taken together with Minton … it was deliberative learning." The new wording should say that Minton's research, whose store was the utility formula and code, was deliberative learning, and that PRODIGY's retention of rules was not.
- **Reason:** The checks say an automated loop must not be credited with judgments its designers make elsewhere. Case 5 says that a verdict made outside a loop is not the step that retains its changes. As written, the bullet would let any fixed-measure learner in once its designer is added, which undoes the exclusion discussed above.
