**Decision:** I will not adopt the proposed wording. I will revise condition 2 so that it states its positive requirement and matches the wording the checks already test.

**Changed text (condition 2 only):**

> 2. **Judgment in the loop.** Each change is kept or dropped by an [on-balance judgment](../../theory/on-balance-judgments-can-guide-improvement-without-a-global-measure.md) that the change improves the system's future work. The judgment is a step of the learning loop. The loop may serve a fixed objective and may use fixed measures. It is not bound to a measure it cannot question: it can keep or drop a change against a measure's verdict, for a stated reason about future work.

**Reason:** The proposed wording would move several cases inside the term. PRODIGY alone, Recuris and checks case 7 all revise judgments of expected contribution, yet none can question its retention rule. Those cases are the exclusions the term exists to draw, and the draft's "Retention bound to a fixed measure" bullet would be contradicted. The reviewer is right that the old phrase "fixed beforehand" defined the condition mostly by an absence, and that it blurred a fixed objective with a fixed measure. Commonplace itself works under a declared objective, so the new text permits a fixed objective and states the positive property: authority inside the loop to depart from a measure. That authority is a matter of membership, while how well the loop uses it remains the capability question the draft already treats as a conjecture under test, and no placement in the checks changes.
