**Decision:** I will not adopt the proposed wording. I will rewrite the last sentence of condition 2 so it states positively what the loop can do, instead of defining the condition by the absence of a fixed measure. No other passage changes, and every placement in the checks file stays where it was.

**Changed text (condition 2):**

> 2. **Judgment in the loop.** Each change is kept or dropped by an
>    [on-balance judgment](../../theory/on-balance-judgments-can-guide-improvement-without-a-global-measure.md)
>    that the change improves the system's future work. The judgment is a step
>    of the learning loop. It may rely on a measure, but the loop can judge that
>    the measure does not settle a case and keep or drop the change for a
>    stated reason about future work.

**Reason:**
1. The reviewer is right that "not bound to one measure fixed beforehand" reads as a pure absence. It is also misleading, because the validator in case 4 is fixed beforehand, yet the team plus repairer is inside.
2. The proposed wording would remove the boundary the term exists to draw. A loop that criticizes and revises content but must keep the score winner would become a member. That flips case 7, the repairer alone in case 4, Recuris and PRODIGY taken alone, all of which the checks file says should stay stable.
3. The draft already treats the quality of judgment as a matter of capability ("whether a language model's judgments are good enough is the conjecture under test"). Whether the loop may decide against its measure is a different thing: it is a structural fact about the named system, so it belongs to membership.
4. The clause "open to criticism and revision" would repeat condition 3 inside condition 2.
