Reason:
1. The objection treats a fixed objective and a fixed measure as the same thing. The on-balance note keeps them apart: Commonplace has a declared objective that "bounds which measures are reasonable rather than collapsing them into one score." So deliberating under a fixed objective already passes, and the added sentence states this.
2. The proposed wording would flip settled placements without justification. The checks treat whether the loop may overrule its measure as the J discriminator. That test excludes the lone repairer (case 4), the score-bound builder (case 7) and Recuris. It also separates case 7 from case 8, where "the additional capability changes J."
3. The question the reviewer wants moved to capability is about authority, not quality. It asks whether the named loop is permitted to decide otherwise. The draft already treats how well a judge judges as the open conjecture, not as a membership test.
4. "Open to criticism and revision" restates condition 3, so merging it into condition 2 would blur the S/J/C distinctions the checks rely on.
