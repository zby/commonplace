## 1. Change what condition 2 says the judgment is about

**Change:** In condition 2, replace "an on-balance judgment that the change improves the system's future work" with something like "an on-balance judgment, made for the sake of the system's future work, about whether to keep the change."

**Reason:** The linked note gives "improves on balance" a strict sense: every reasonable measure favours the change. The draft's own later sections say "Open" is the usual verdict for a recent change, that keeping is a decision and not learning, and that reversibility can justify keeping without being evidence of improvement. As written, condition 2 requires a judgment the system normally cannot make, so most of Commonplace's retention either fails the condition or gets misreported as improvement.

## 2. Scope the quantifier "each change"

**Change:** Say which writes the condition covers. For example: "each change proposed as learning to the named store." Separately say that writes kept by default, such as logs and attempt records, are not themselves learning claims.

**Reason:** The draft also says "retention without a judgment is outside the term." So if "each" covers every write, any store with default-kept writes fails, and that includes Commonplace taken whole. That contradicts the stated aim of covering all of Commonplace's learning. The checks file already shows the reading that matters: KSI's placement flips depending on whether its attempt log is included.

## 3. Reconcile the validator note with the loop-boundary rule

**Change:** Qualify "a change kept because a validator passed meets the condition." It should hold only when the named system also contains a loop that can question whether that measure suffices and can decide otherwise. Then replace "checked on the outermost loop that the system runs itself" with "checked on the loop the claim names."

**Reason:** Unqualified, the note contradicts the draft's own placements: PRODIGY "taken alone fails," and the training loop inside a research team "is not" deliberative learning. It also contradicts checks case 4, where the link repairer alone is outside on J. The outermost-loop rule makes it unclear whether inner loops can be named and judged at all, though the draft names and judges them in its examples.
