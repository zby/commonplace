**Decision:** I will not adopt the proposed wording. I accept part of the objection: condition 2 should state positively what the loop must be able to do, not define deliberation as the absence of a fixed measure. I am rewriting condition 2 and the one autonomy sentence that repeats the old framing.

**Changed text, condition 2:**

> 2. **Judgment in the loop.** Each change is kept or dropped by an
>    [on-balance judgment](../../theory/on-balance-judgments-can-guide-improvement-without-a-global-measure.md)
>    that the change improves the system's future work. The judgment is a step
>    of the learning loop. It may rest on a measure fixed beforehand, but the
>    same loop must be able to question whether that measure is enough for the
>    question and, for a stated reason about future work, retain otherwise. A
>    loop that must keep whatever a measure selects does not meet this
>    condition, however much it reasons along the way.

**Changed text, "The autonomous claim is about the outermost loop" bullet, fourth sentence:**

> The judge must then read the store and judge on balance, using no measure
> that it cannot question.

**Reason:** The reviewer is right that reasoning and revisable criteria are separate properties, and the old wording blurred them. The draft's own note on deterministic checks already admits a change kept because a fixed validator passed, so "not bound to one measure fixed beforehand" did not even match the draft. But the proposed wording keeps the wrong property: "judgments … open to criticism and revision" is satisfied when PRODIGY revises its utility estimate, or when case 7 replaces yesterday's score winner. That would move PRODIGY taken alone, Recuris and case 7 inside, contradicting "What the term does not cover" and the joint check. Whether the loop may override its measure is a structural fact about the named loop, as Recuris's gate shows by sitting outside the permitted patch surface, so it belongs to membership; only how well the judge does this is a matter of capability. The new wording matches the checks' statement of J ("without being bound to a measure it cannot question") and changes no placement in either table.
