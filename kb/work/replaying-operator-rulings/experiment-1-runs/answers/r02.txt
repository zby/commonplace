# Three proposed changes to the deliberative learning definition

## 1. Condition 2: the judgment should be about keeping the change, not about whether the change improves the system

**Change:** Replace "an on-balance judgment that the change improves the system's future work" with "an on-balance judgment whether to keep the change for the system's future work." The link to the on-balance note stays. This removes a requirement rather than adding one.

**Reason:** As written, the condition imports the linked note's strict meaning of "improves": every reasonable measure favours the change. But the draft itself says that "Open" is the usual verdict for a recent change, that keeping a change is a decision rather than a finding that it was learned, and that reversibility can justify keeping a change without being evidence of improvement. Read literally, condition 2 therefore either excludes most of Commonplace's real retentions or requires the misuse the draft forbids: treating a kept change as learned.

## 2. Condition 2: replace "not bound to one measure fixed beforehand" with "the loop can question or set aside any measure it uses"

**Change:** Use the checks file's wording in place of the draft's. The sentence "a change kept because a validator passed meets the condition" can then stay as a consequence of the definition rather than an exception to it.

**Reason:** The current text contradicts itself. A validator is a single measure fixed beforehand, so condition 2 excludes the validator case that the "degenerate judgment" note admits, while "What the term does not cover" and check case 4 (the repairer alone) exclude it again. The property that actually separates the cases is whether the loop that keeps the change can question the measure. Case 4 (team plus repairer) and case 7 (the builder that must keep the score winner) both turn on exactly that.

## 3. Condition 2: make the quantifier range over what the named store retains, not every write

**Change:** Replace "Each change is kept or dropped" with "What the named store retains is kept." Leave the choice of store to the claim, as the scope paragraph already requires. This rewords the existing quantifier rather than adding an exclusion.

**Reason:** The checks show that the KSI placement flips depending on whether the attempt log or the distilled guidance counts as the store. Commonplace also writes many things it does not retain by judgment. With the current wording, the stated purpose of covering all the learning that happens in Commonplace fails on incidental writes that were never meant to be learning.

## What I would leave alone

I would not add anything to condition 3. Its "undo where possible" instruction is, if anything, a candidate for loosening to "changes what it does in response," which is how its own notes and check case 3 read it. That change ranks fourth.
