# Three proposed changes

## 1. Make condition 2 a retention decision, not a judgment that the change improves

**Change:** Reword condition 2 along the lines of the checks' J: "Each change is kept or dropped by a judgment, made in the loop, about whether keeping it serves the system's future work. The judgment is not bound to one measure fixed beforehand." Also drop the claim that the judgment is "that the change improves."

**Reason:** Under the note's definition, "improves on balance" means every reasonable measure favors the change. Yet the note, ADR 085 and the draft itself keep changes whose verdict is open, as decisions: "Keeping a change is not the same as having learned it", and reversibility "is not evidence that the instruction has improved later work." As written, condition 2 excludes exactly the decisions Commonplace makes most often, so the term fails its stated job of covering all the project's learning. It also brings back the success-like claim that the term explicitly disowns.

## 2. Qualify the deterministic-check note to agree with case 4 and Recuris

**Change:** Replace "So a change kept because a validator passed meets the condition" with: "A change kept because a validator passed meets the condition only when the named system's own loop can question whether that measure suffices for the question, and can change it." Then cite case 4 (team plus repairer inside, repairer alone outside) as the worked example.

**Reason:** The unqualified sentence places the link repairer and Recuris's gate inside. The checks place both outside on J, and their reason, "a measure it cannot question", is the definition's own. As it stands, the draft and its checks disagree on the clearest boundary case, and readers will cite whichever text suits them.

## 3. State the Commonplace-specific features as claims about a kind, not as traits of deliberative learners

**Change:** In the background contrast, the third bullet attributes Commonplace's traits to "the learner": its store holds "partial theories, procedures and constraints, with most knowledge of the world in a language model's weights." Rewrite that bullet, and the parallel phrasing in the other bullets, as "Commonplace, one kind of deliberative learner, …". Collect these traits, together with the autonomous-LLM conjecture, in a short subsection such as "A kind within the term: Commonplace."

**Reason:** The draft says a human engineering team does deliberative learning, yet that team's world knowledge is not in model weights. The contrast therefore turns project traits into apparent membership conditions. Keeping them as an explicit claim about a kind preserves the general term and makes the project's actual bet visible and testable.
