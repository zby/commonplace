# Before and after a replay

Check the designs, results, active claims and git status before launching. Session case 2 is not definition experiment 2. Only run a commissioned case. Reserve the case and pinned source state with an exclusive `mkdir claims/<case>-<commit>` (create the parent if absent). Record owner, scope, output path, launch status and next step. On collision or apparent abandonment, stop and reconcile ownership. Do not edit another worker's files or shared indexes during a run.

Freeze materials, question, conditions, answer budget, prediction and scoring before launch. Keep the arm key, later ruling and other responses away from workers. Use fresh workers and blind scoring; balance order for choices. Record failed calls and scorer disagreements; do not replace answers for quality.

After scoring, retain the design and a result with counts for **every arm**. Keep individual evidence only for agreed target hits, choices supported across both orders, or separately labelled unplanned successes verified against an accepted correction. Identify each retained answer by case, condition and original ID; distinguish proposing from choosing. Do not count disputed or incidental answers as agreed hits. Keep transient run bundles outside the workshop. Once results are verified and ownership reconciled, clear the completed claim and transient evidence.
