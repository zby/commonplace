# Three proposed changes to the deliberative-learning draft

I tested each condition against the other two. Condition 2 (J) excludes two kinds of case that the other conditions do not, and neither kind should be excluded. Condition 1 (S) does necessary work, but the draft gives it the wrong reason. Condition 3 (C) mostly excludes the right cases: fixed-benchmark displacement, as in cases 9, 13 and 7a.

## 1. Condition 2 should require a decision weighed on reasons about later work, not a judgment that the change will improve it

**What to change.**
- **Definition:** Reword condition 2 as: "Each change is made by a decision that weighs, on balance, reasons about the system's later work. The decision may be taken while the comparison is Open."
- **On-balance note:** Separate the *deliberation*, which may end Open and still decide, from the *verdict* of Improvement.
- **Checks:** Add a case like ADR 085. A rule is adopted while knowingly accepting a loss and while the comparison stays open. The expected placement is inside.

**Reason.** Condition 2 links to the note's definition, under which "better on balance" means every reasonable measure favors the change. The draft says "Open" is the usual verdict, and the note's own example, ADR 085, commits to deletion "without claiming it won". So the condition as written excludes the typical Commonplace change, which neither S nor C excludes. It also conflicts with the draft's own point that keeping a change while its verdict is open is a decision, and that reversibility can favour that decision without being evidence of improvement.

## 2. Condition 2 should be judged per claimed class of changes, and a judged rule or retention decision should satisfy it

**What to change.**
- **Definition:** Replace "each change to the store" with "each change in the class the claim names". State that the judgment may be made about the rule that produces the changes, or about retaining them, as well as about admitting them.
- **Session-trace note:** Rewrite the note that calls stored session traces "learning but not deliberative". Traces kept under a deliberated storage rule, and later curated, are inside.
- **Checks:** Add a case where a system appends everything and then prunes entries on later evidence. The expected placement is inside.

**Reason.** The draft says it exists to put *all* learning in Commonplace under one name, yet it places Commonplace's own trace storage outside. The checks already raise the matching quantifier problem for KSI. Admit-everything-then-prune is the one design that only J excludes: C is met by the evidence-driven pruning. The draft itself says admission, use and holding "can each be changed separately", so excluding this design contradicts its own account.

## 3. Condition 1 should be grounded in what the judgment needs, not in targeted criticism

**What to change.**
- **Definition and notes:** Restate S's rationale as follows. The store's contents must be readable by whoever makes and criticizes the judgment, so that the judgment can be about what the change says. Move "legibility enables targeted criticism" to a remark about addressable stores.
- **Checks:** Update the discriminators for cases 5 and 16 to give this reason.

**Reason.** S alone excludes gradient training and Schmidhuber's incremental self-improvement. Case 5 concedes that the fixed loss passes J, and case 16 passes C by coarse rollback. Those exclusions are intended, but the draft justifies S by localized blame, which the "attribution is graded" note says C does not require. As written, S looks like an unmotivated condition on the medium, and "legible" is undefined: weights are readable numbers.
