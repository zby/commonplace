## 1. Condition 2: require a judged decision, not a judgment of improvement

**Change.** In the definition, rewrite condition 2 as: "Each change to the store is made by a decision that weighs its likely effect on the system's later work and could have gone against it." Keep the link to the on-balance note as the account of how such weighing is done. Delete "that the system will do its later work better with the change than without it." Update the **J** summary in the checks to match.

**Reason.** The note reserves "better on balance" for the case where every reasonable measure favours the change. It says that decisions are often taken while the verdict is Open, and that they should be reported as decisions. ADR 085's deletion rule is its own example of this. As drafted, condition 2 therefore excludes ordinary Commonplace changes, which defeats the stated purpose of covering all of Commonplace's learning. The draft's own gloss, "a decision is a judgment that could have gone against the change," is already the more general condition, so promoting it removes a requirement rather than adding one.

## 2. Count rule-produced writes through the rule that produces them

**Change.**
- **Definition:** add one sentence: "A write made automatically by a rule counts as part of the change that installed the rule; conditions 2 and 3 are checked on that rule."
- **Definition, rule note:** delete "Storing session traces is learning but not deliberative."
- **Definition, "Change without a judgment":** narrow it to systems where no judged rule stands behind the defaults.
- **Checks:** resolve the open question "Which writes count as changes?" with this rule. KSI's attempt log and case 2's logging rule then read the same way. No placement moves.

**Reason.** The draft says the term covers all learning in Commonplace, yet it names session-trace storage as Commonplace learning that falls outside the term. The checks already flag the "each change" quantifier as unresolved. Moving judgment to the rule level fixes both problems with one general statement instead of a list of exceptions, and it agrees with what case 2 and the rule note already say.

## 3. Replace "When a change was learned" with a pointer to the note

**Change.** Cut the section's definition of "learned", its five verdicts and two of its three usage points. Replace them with: "Whether a particular change was learned is an on-balance verdict on that change for the declared horizon; use the verdicts in [the on-balance note], and report a change let stand under an Open verdict as a decision." Keep "This system learns is a summary of per-change claims" as one line.

**Reason.** The section restates the note's verdict list almost word for word, so two copies of the same list will drift apart. It also sits awkwardly in a definition that says it has no success condition. Keeping the verdicts in one place shortens the definition and leaves the term about a way of learning, as its own text intends.
