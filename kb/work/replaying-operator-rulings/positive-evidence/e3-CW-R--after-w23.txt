**Adopt Version 1.**

1. Version 1 keeps the term to what its name and stated purpose support. Deliberation adds the judgment, and "learning" adds keeping the result in a store. 2. Version 2 makes correction a membership test. That excludes ordinary cases the KB needs to describe, such as an agent that decides lesson by lesson what to record but never revisits a lesson, and the Darwin Gödel Machine (case 13).
