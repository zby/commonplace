**Correction:** `tests/scenarios/write-a-note.md` is wrong to say installed projects "copy COLLECTION.md and the type-specs, so the overhead files and paths match." Under the shipped one-tree model, only starter `COLLECTION.md` contracts are scaffolded. The `cp-skill-write`/`cp-skill-connect` skills, global types like `types/note.md`, and the methodology notes (not `commonplace/kb/notes/`) are read in place from the installed package, at the location `.commonplace/library.md` gives. **Consequence:** a maintainer checking context budgets against that scenario would inspect project paths like `kb/types/note.md` and `kb/instructions/cp-skill-write/SKILL.md` that don't exist in installed projects, and would miss the library-location lookup, so the scenario's source and hop accounting needs re-deriving for the installed variant.
