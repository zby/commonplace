**Correction:** The "Commonplace repo vs installed project" variant in `tests/scenarios/write-a-note.md` is wrong. Installed projects do not copy the type-specs or skills; per `scenario-architecture.md`, the project holds no copy of the library, so `types/note.md` and the `cp-skill-*/SKILL.md` files are read in place from the installed package at the location `.commonplace/library.md` names. Only the user's own collections, with their starter `COLLECTION.md`, live under the project's `kb/`.

**Consequence:** A maintainer using that scenario to check context budgets in installed projects will look for paths that don't exist there, such as `kb/types/note.md`, `kb/instructions/cp-skill-write/SKILL.md` and the escalation target `commonplace/kb/notes/`. They will also miss the library-location lookup, which costs one read per session outside Claude Code.
