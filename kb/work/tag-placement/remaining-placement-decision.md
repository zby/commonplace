## Boundary applied

On 2026-09-26 this pass checked the eight remaining assignment findings
against their live tag heads. Seven assignments are removed and one is
retained. No inclusion rule changes. A note's primary subject need not match
every tag, but each assigned tag needs a substantive argument, worked case,
or enabling condition covered by its head.

includes conditions enabling discovery, and the epiplexity examples develop
how a bounded learner gains access to patterns. They need not also establish
the warrant of an accepted conjecture. The curated description now states
that basis instead of relying on the presence of the entry as evidence of fit.

arms. What changes is how much linked evidence the reviewer reads. Its
contribution concerns review execution and detection, so review-system replaces
claims-and-grounding. The curated route moves with that correction.
