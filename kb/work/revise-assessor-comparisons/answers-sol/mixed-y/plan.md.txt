must-keep:
  - On-balance judgments can guide improvement without a global measure.
  - Commonplace chooses changes by asking whether they yield more useful and better-warranted knowledge work for the human judgment spent, on balance. This is a practical choice for Commonplace, not a universal measure.
  - A diagnostic comparison can establish which message to keep without deciding which release to adopt; the worked Python 3.11 versus 3.10 release comparison weighs bundled gains and compatibility losses rather than counting them.
  - The hypothetical library-hours choice can change with whose access the library prioritizes even when the attendance facts are unchanged.
  - The routing example contrasts a routing gain with a possible, not observed, failure on tasks near the agent's effective context limit. The balance can change with the task mix or the cumulative load of the routing table.
  - For the decision at hand, a decision-relevant unknown warrants investigation when a plausible answer could reverse the choice; if the preference holds across plausible answers, action need not wait for complete knowledge.
  - Analysis can expose trade-offs but cannot supply value weights by scoring features; giving reasons makes the judgment open to criticism without establishing that an agent judges well.
  - An on-balance judgment does not override a binding requirement.
