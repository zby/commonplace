Proposed purpose: Help a deployed-system designer distinguish stored experience from durable learning and decide which behaviour-changing updates need an adoption decision.

**Stop before passes.** A designer who checks a proposed prompt edit before releasing it is making an adoption decision. A designer whose deployed model takes an online gradient step need not have a candidate waiting for approval. The note treats both prompts and weights as writable, but its opening tells both designers they need the first mechanism.

**First sentence a hostile reviewer would attack:** “Continual learning, as the field uses the term and as this note uses it, asks for more: the system does the selecting itself.” No evidence of that convention is supplied; the direct-update case matters more than the missing citation.

**What reads well:** Using the same stored object first as advice and then as an instruction shows why its force cannot be inferred from its bytes.
