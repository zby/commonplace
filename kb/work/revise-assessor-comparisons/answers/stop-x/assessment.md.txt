Purpose: Help a deployed-system designer distinguish storing experience from learning that changes later behaviour, and identify which changes need a release decision.

**Stop before editing.** A designer deciding whether to gate an online weight update needs to know whether the gate makes the update learning or makes it safer to deploy. The note gives reasons for the latter and a classification rule that suggests the former.

**First sentence a hostile reviewer would attack:** “Continual learning, as the field uses the term and as this note uses it, asks for more: the system does the selecting itself.” Where is the selection decision when an online learner applies the gradient from each batch directly?

**What reads well:** The example of one object read as advice or as an instruction shows why its stored bytes cannot settle its authority.
