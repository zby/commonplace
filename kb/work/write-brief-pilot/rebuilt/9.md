# Brief: Software house as a second test

## Governing question

Besides the knowledge-base arrangement in the lead article, is there a second arrangement that would test the theory-builder bet with a stronger falsifier? What would it cost in claim strength?

## Audience and reader update

The readers are technical outside readers who come from the lead article. They should leave understanding four things. First, what an automated software house is and the dated conjecture about it: fixed models available by 2026-09-02. Second, why such a house counts as a theory builder, since its program theory is stated as commitments that are consulted, criticized, and revised. Third, why software supplies external assessment that a knowledge base lacks. Fourth, why the open-ended claim is harder to establish.

## Target claim or purpose

This is a supplement. Its job is to state the conjecture precisely and to set out four conditions a witness run must meet: holding and application, coherent revision, automated continuation, and practical reliability. These conditions exist only in this article, so they must stay here complete and exact. The supplement also explains why this arrangement is a companion to the knowledge-base test and not the main path.

## Must keep

- The draft banner.
- The definitions of "automated", "operates practically", and "open-ended", with the comparative human-staffed standard.
- The multi-tenant example of program-theory commitments.
- Naur's tension, resolved by the extra premise the KB identifies, while conceding that theory cannot be fully written down.
- The requirement that procedures be retained outside the weights.
- The three items of external assessment, and the point that an outside signal does not locate the fault.
- A run supports only a version bounded to its workload, horizon, and budget.
- The failure clause for each witness condition.
- The survey result: none of the reviewed systems is shown to be an automated house.

## Exclusions

- No claim that a witness exists.
- Do not rely on the open-domain-becomes-software-house conjecture.
- No protocol detail, which belongs to the testing supplement.

## Scope, modality, and terminology

The claim is an existential conjecture with a date cutoff. Keep "software house", "program theory", "witness run", and "external assessment" aligned with their KB definitions.

## Reserved decisions

- Changing the cutoff date or the four conditions.
- Promoting the conditions into a note.
- Reversing the ordering between the two arrangements.
