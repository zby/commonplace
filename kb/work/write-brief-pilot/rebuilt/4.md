# Brief: The Bitter Lesson defense portfolio

## Governing question

Commonplace has collected several replies to Sutton's Bitter Lesson: production method versus representational form, selection against unearned reach, function-level absorption, the moving frontier, sample efficiency, codification and relaxing, and commitments and authority. Which of these actually rebuts the inference that learned results must live in model weights? What do the others contribute? And does any of them defend the present hand-crafted bootstrap?

## Audience and reader update

Readers are agents and maintainers who invoke the Bitter Lesson in arguments about Commonplace's direction. That includes writers of articles on whether a theory builder can learn, and reviewers who test whether the program overclaims. The risk the note corrects is portfolio inflation: citing several replies together as "our defense" when they answer different questions and most are hypotheses or methods. After reading, the reader should know that one premise carries the narrow rebuttal. The theory-guided bootstrap is a first strategy under incomplete global evaluation, not a vindicated path. And the program owes an account, artifact by artifact, of how learning is meant to outgrow the bootstrap.

## Target claim or purpose

A title-as-claim synthesis note, to be cited as a unit. It makes three claims:

1. The production-method versus representational-form distinction is the only member needed for the narrow form-only rebuttal. The Bitter Lesson selects production methods, so it does not force every learned result into weights.
2. That rebuttal only creates conceptual room for learned explicit artifacts. It does not defend continuing hand production, show that artifact learning scales, or show that Commonplace is on the right path.
3. Theory-guided bootstrapping is justified only as a provisional first strategy. The reason is that global theory fit currently lacks a fixed evaluator, so system use serves as the initial selection environment. That strategy stays answerable to explicit failure conditions and to rival learning methods.

## Must keep

- A one-sentence statement of the Bitter Lesson with a link to Sutton's essay.
- The explicit limits of the narrow rebuttal.
- The argument for a first strategy. Local checks such as truth, validity, consistency, or bounded prediction do not decide relational fit to a larger working theory. No fixed evaluator currently decides fit, so claims are exposed to building, operating, and repairing the system. Keep the insistence that this is not a reason to postpone computation. Recurring judgments should become tests, validators, critics, search objectives, or code.
- The conditional-compatibility requirement from the outgrow-the-bootstrap note, stated as necessary and not as a defense. Keep the six questions the program must answer for each human-supplied artifact or production decision. Keep the long-run criterion: acquiring family-specific production knowledge as claimed reach widens, not competence in predefined domains. A system with many hand-built ontologies stays a bundle of predefined solutions.
- The role table for the other members. Each row says what the member contributes and what it does not establish. State plainly that absorption is a concession and that the moving-frontier and sample-efficiency claims are hypotheses.
- The four-level current position: narrow compatibility, working conjecture, first strategy, open comparison. Only level one is a rebuttal. Calling all four a defense would hide the main uncertainty.
- The failure conditions under which the first strategy is rejected or narrowed. These include self-confirming use, truth checks and system-fit judgments that cannot be kept apart, human production growing while computational search stays peripheral, a bespoke ontology and evaluator for every new domain, and direct methods winning at comparable total cost.
- The Scope boundaries. "Load-bearing" is relative to the narrow rebuttal only. The bootstrap is path-relative. Objectives, commitments, and grants of authority may stay supplied. Fixed general methods and warranted kernels may remain. The missing global-fit oracle is a current, defeasible condition.

## Exclusions

- No claim that the current decomposition, artifact ontology, or bootstrap is correct. Using Commonplace as an instrument exposes failures in these; it does not validate them.
- No ranking of the other members' practical importance.
- No claim that natural-language or symbolic carriers are permanent.
- No defense of hand-authored empirical or procedural competence by way of commitments and authority.
- No empirical evidence that the strategy works. That belongs to the testing articles and the open comparison.
- Do not re-derive the component arguments. Each has its own note, and this note routes to them.

## Scope, modality, and terminology

- The rebuttal is a universal-logical claim about what the Bitter Lesson argument licenses.
- The strategy is presented as a conjecture and commitment with stated refuters. Keep the conjectural force visible in the title, description, and "Current position" section.
- Keep "production method" and "representational form" as the registered axes.
- Use "load-bearing" only in the narrow sense defined in Scope.
- Call the bootstrap a "first strategy", not a "defense".
- "Global fit" or "system fit" names the relational property that local checks leave open. Keep it distinct from truth.

## Reserved decisions

- Promoting any level-two to level-four commitment into a rebuttal, or adding a second load-bearing member.
- Naming the alternative first strategy or the baseline learning method the comparison will use. The note leaves this open deliberately.
- Choosing which recurring global judgments to operationalize first.
- Any edit that softens the failure conditions, because other documents rely on them as refuters.
