# Brief: gradual bitter-lesson compatibility

## Purpose

The hub article (job 0) must state the research program: the leverage order,
adverse selection, the two milestones meeting at the evaluator, and the
build-plan reading. It cannot do that yet, because the program's honest scope
is unformulated — and because the KB forbids using it before it is classified.

The bitter-lesson defense portfolio
closes with an operative rule: *"The portfolio is the current inventory, not a
closed set; a new defense enters by having its role classified here before any
outward text leans on it."* The operator's position is a new defense. Until it
has a row, no article may rest on it.

The result is for the hub article to cite rather than argue, and for a reader
to see what the program does and does not claim.

## The position to formulate

Stated loosely by the operator, and deliberately not polished here — polishing
it is the task:

> We do not know whether our methods can reach complete automation. But the
> bitter lesson can apply gradually. Whatever portion of a path has moved to
> scalable production is governed by the lesson and is compatible with it. The
> remainder may be hard, and its difficulty does not retroactively convert the
> moved portion back into hand-built knowledge that will lose. A portion large
> enough to be practical is worth having, and is not merely scaffolding
> awaiting absorption.

What is new here is not the content of any existing defense but its **scope**:
every row now in the portfolio assesses compatibility for a methodology or an
artifact class. This assesses it **per portion of a path**. That is why it
needs its own row rather than an edit to an existing one.

## Three linked pieces, one distinction

Do these together. They turn on the same question — what an acceptance test
does — and formulating them separately produces three answers that do not line
up.

1. **Classify the new defense** in the portfolio's role table: what it
   contributes, and what it does not establish. The second column is the one
   that matters; every existing row earns its place by bounding itself.
3. **Settle the disanalogy premise.** The claim that our localized artifacts
   are not the hand-crafted features that lost needs a stated reason. The
   sharpest candidate already in the KB is
   selection removes unearned reach, not structure:
   structure survives when its reach is earned by a reject-capable acceptance

### The tension that most likely reshapes the formulation

Two classes of retained artifact appear to survive absorption for **opposite**
reasons:

- *Commitments* survive because there is nothing to converge on — no ground
  truth, no oracle (commitments create ground truth,
  reproduction does not transfer authority).
- *Structured theories* survive because their reach **is** earned — they have
  a reject-capable test.

A tempting synthesis says the bitter-lesson residue and the adverse-selection
residue are one residue. That looks wrong: an independent oracle is what
protects the second class, and an independent oracle is also what makes a
decision transferable to search. The classes overlap rather than coincide.
Resolve this, dissolve it, or record it as an open problem — but do not let a
formulation assume the tidy version.

## What is fixed

- **No fraction.** The operator's shorthand was "99% / 1%". P4's characteristic
  failure is literally *"Percentages; hour counts; activity counts; self-scored
  prose"* (target problems). State the claim over
  decisions classified by the reason each resisted transfer — the residue table
  in warranted transfer
  — not over a proportion. A remainder small in count can be large in cost;
  a percentage asserts the opposite.
- **The standing concession survives.** The portfolio concedes that no current
  artifact, vocabulary, or decomposition is promised permanence, and that
  stable guidance may migrate into weights. A defense that quietly withdraws
  that concession is a regression, not a strengthening.
- **The separation rule holds.** *"The Bitter Lesson concerns scalable
  production methods and possible power. It must not silently become an
  autonomy theorem"* (article roles).
- **Reconcile, do not ignore,**
  scaffolding recurs at the moving frontier.
  Its frontier clause is a conditional friend; state the condition, or a
  reviewer uses the KB's own note against the article.
- **Fold, do not spawn.** A bounded claim goes into the note it qualifies. A
  new note needs its own argument that no existing note can carry.
