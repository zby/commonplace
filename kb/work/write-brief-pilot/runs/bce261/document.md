# A recorded composition loop can establish a local correction without validating the method

A distributed writing workflow can establish more than that a check was attempted in a specific case. Suppose a preserved challenge identifies a load-bearing fault. If independent adjudication confirms both the fault and that the final response resolves it, the trace establishes a local artifact outcome. The response may correct or narrow the claim, or reject it altogether. The trace does not establish that the method is reliable across cases, matches solo composition, or that any one stage caused the improvement. Those claims require different evidence.

For unsettled substantive claims, one candidate record contract separates three operations. **Commit-and-expose** records the pre-acceptance claim, constraints, premises, and inferential route. **Challenge-and-locate** records premise-linked objections, counterexamples, missing support, and a proposed fault location rather than a global grade. **Respond-and-decide** records a status for every reported challenge. **Investigate** is an interim status; a final disposition rejects the challenge with cited support, revises or narrows the claim, rejects the claim, or retains explicit uncertainty at acceptance. [Putting ideas into words](../sources/putting-ideas-into-words.md), [thinking through conjecture and counterexample](../sources/how-to-think-in-writing.md), and [learning by iterated writing](../sources/learning-by-writing.md) motivate these bundles through reported practice rather than controlled comparisons. Across them, writing exposes a commitment, locates a weakness in it or its support, and revises or discards it. That sequence motivates the contract's three recorded handoffs; it does not establish that the handoffs preserve the practices' epistemic effects. The taxonomy is neither canonical nor validated.

Preserving the input, report, and disposition makes the recorded handoffs inspectable. Checker and acceptor remain distinct roles; either may be filled by a human, agent, or policy when that allocation has its own warrant. Testing an allocation also requires recording who or what occupied each role and under what conditions. By contrast, **naive prose delegation** supplies a seed and accepts generated prose after only a global read or approval, retaining no operation-level trace. Its artifact record therefore cannot support this audit. Rendering, transcription, and stylistic editing are outside scope when the substantive commitments and checks are fixed elsewhere.

Evidence must match the claim's level. A local correction requires independent adjudication of both the reported fault and the final response. The adjudicator must confirm that the resulting claim resolves that fault, not merely that it changed. Here, **independent adjudication** is a separate role that applies a stated criterion to the preserved fault and response; a different actor alone does not establish independence. Critic reliability requires discrimination between adjudicated supported and unsupported arguments. Tests should include true conclusions reached through invalid routes, because [producing a sound route is not the same as evaluating the route presented](./reasoning-production-is-not-reasoning-evaluation.md). When nominally separate checks are combined as independent evidence, their error correlation must also be measured. Comparing whole-loop performance requires a specified comparator, role allocation, and measurement rule; attributing the result to one stage requires stage-level evidence. The supplied evidence gives **commit-and-expose** no completeness criterion and provides neither calibrated prose critics nor a loop-versus-solo comparison.

Neither actor identity nor approval substitutes for those tests. Reviewing finished prose may invite passive assent, as a [practitioner account of substantive AI writing](../sources/why-almost-never-use-ai-to-write-anything-substantive.md) argues. Human approval can establish authority to admit an artifact, but it cannot establish that its route was checked or that a reported fault was corrected. Actor choice may affect performance, so it must be evaluated rather than used as a proxy.

These evidence tiers keep distinct outcomes separate. A corrected or justifiably rejected claim is an **artifact outcome**. A **human-understanding outcome** needs an independent probe of whether the human can restate, update, or apply the implicated claim, route, or uncertainty; assent or copied restatement is insufficient. A **later-system outcome** requires relevant retrieval and changed use or decision, not merely persistence. **Acceptance** records admission authority but proves none of these outcomes. The supplied evidence supports the record design and the conditional local artifact result, but not general method validation.

## Open Questions

- Can blinded prose critics distinguish invalid routes from valid arguments with useful discrimination and sufficiently uncorrelated errors?
- What task-specific criterion can show that `commit-and-expose` captured every load-bearing commitment?
- Does recording commitments before showing generated prose reduce anchoring in the acceptor?
- Which measured outcome, if any, reaches a solo-composition baseline under a distributed allocation?
