## Result

The seven-commit set contains 28 note changes: 23 are **grounding only**, four
are **faithful rescopings**, and one was **narrowed to survive review**. The
seventh commit, `750d91cb`, touched only an ingest and the cohort ledger, so it
adds no note row.

The sole third-class change is
[`bitter-lesson-selects-against-unearned-reach-not-against-structure`](../../notes/bitter-lesson-selects-against-unearned-reach-not-against-structure.md).
Before the sweep, its body called the earned-reach criterion a Commonplace
analysis, labelled Sutton `abstracted-from`, labelled DomainBed and IRM
`evidenced-by`, and specified a refuting survey. Commit `92314f92` nevertheless
replaced that divergence claim with the weaker claim the cited records could
directly support. That used a source-grounding boundary to decide the permitted
strength of a target-side conjecture.

Classification is at note-contribution level. Correcting or deleting an
external example is grounding only when the note's title, description, and
operative argument survive. A core change is faithful rescoping when the
pre-change note itself already exposes the overstatement, or when a descriptive
comparison depends on an external fact the record does not establish. It is
narrowing to survive review when source limits replace an explicitly local
conjecture rather than merely bounding its evidence.
