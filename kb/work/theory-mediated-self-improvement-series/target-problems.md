# Target problems

Each problem below is stated on its own, without reference to any source
tradition, and at a level where optimization target, feedback, information
structure, and failure mode stay visible. That is the precondition for a
problem match, since
[problem matches guide method search and mechanism matches bound transfer](../../notes/problem-matches-guide-method-search-mechanism-matches-bound-transfer.md):
a source can be matched to a problem only after the problem exists
independently of it. The [match register](./match-register.md) records which
sources match which problem and what each match licenses.

| ID | Problem | Optimization target | Feedback | Information structure | Characteristic failure |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | **Bearer.** What can hold a program-specific theory well enough to modify the program coherently when a new demand arrives? | Coherent extension: changes that fit the existing structure rather than patch it | Whether a modification preserves the structure and purpose; observable only after the change | Some decision premises are recorded, others are not and cannot be regenerated from the artifacts | Repair that is locally correct and destroys what the part was for |
| P2 | **Transfer.** On a declared path, how does a decision leave the human cut set with warrant, and what is left? | Fewer decisions that require a person, at fixed or better warrant | A warrant record per transfer and the outcomes of later episodes | The residue is adversely selected; the next decision is harder to warrant than the last | Apparent warrant at the evaluator: captured evaluator, viability-only gate, exported decision; displaced review and repair; degraded remaining judgments |
| P3 | **Operative theory.** How does retained natural-language theory come to guide operation with binding force, stay revisable, and be codified when it stabilizes? | Theory that guides changes and is tested by their results, on the same path | A mediation trace: the theory cited where the decision was made, and the outcome read back against it | The theory is interpreted, not executed; consumption path decides whether it binds | Inert documentation; drift between stated and consumed theory; self-sealing evaluation |
| P4 | **Measurement.** How are two states of one system's allocation compared without a scalar? | An ordering that detects movement of the human cut and of leverage | The recorded cut set per path and horizon; accepted outcomes per total human effort | Functions differ in stakes and decomposition; hours confound allocation with ambition | Percentages; hour counts; activity counts; self-scored prose |
| P5 | **Production and form.** Can localized artifacts selected by search or learning compete with weight learning as corpora, dependency density, horizons, and available compute grow? | Task success at comparable total cost, including evaluation and maintenance | Matched end-to-end comparisons with a no-update and a weights-only baseline | Artifact systems lack a default credit-assignment mechanism; decomposition is hard to revise | Fixed decomposition inheriting its mistakes; evaluation costing more than the failures it avoids |
| P6 | **Workshop execution.** How are purpose, information, and decision rights allocated between the operator and the agents executing this workshop across sessions, when review evidence arrives during execution? | Task changes that preserve the operator's intent | Closure conditions, the ledger, and hand-backs when a change would alter a claim | The operator holds purpose and bounds; agents hold execution evidence; both inherit the Commonplace doctrine when their verified consumption paths supply it, which lets the framing be a delta | A condition satisfied that no longer serves the intent; an agent reframing a claim to survive review |

## Relations among the problems

- P1 and P2 meet at the evaluator. A composite that could pass P1's bearer
  tests would be one that can warrant its own coherent modifications; P2 says
  that is exactly the decision class the residue concentrates.
- P3 is the mechanism side of P2's first two residue classes: representation
  and settlement are what retained theory supplies.
- P4 serves P2: without an ordering, transfer cannot be shown to have
  happened.
- P5 is orthogonal to P2. It asks which representational form learned content
  should take; P2 asks who supplies the decision. A result on one does not
  settle the other.
- P6 is P2 applied reflexively to the workshop: the operator's decisions are
  the workshop's human cut, and the hand-back rule is its warrant boundary.

## What is deliberately not a target problem here

- Whether any current system is "self-improving" by category membership; the
  cluster's definition already classifies that, and it is not what the
  series argues.
- General claims about human cognition, organizational behaviour, or military
  command; those belong to the sources and transfer only as far as a
  mechanism match reaches.
