# Article roles

This is a provisional argument map, not a commitment to article count, titles,
or reading order. The [shared model](./shared-model.md) is the canonical compact
synthesis.

## Public surface

1. **[A research program for learning software factories](../../articles/a-research-program-for-learning-software-factories.md)** is the compact entry point. It restricts the recursive case to factories configured for factory-valued products; makes a better successor the direct production target rather than a side effect of ordinary software work; defines *better* as preserving the prior declared scope and continued factory-development capability while improving a relevant production dimension; introduces Naur's stronger theory-bearing constraint for coherent modification; distinguishes retained from held theory; presents natural-language theory as the contingent implementation bet; and states the minimum tests, bootstrap disclosure, and failure conditions.
2. **[The decisions that stay human, and what would move them](../../articles/the-decisions-that-stay-human-and-what-would-move-them.md)** owns the full fixed-boundary, human-cut, closure, warrant, and transfer argument.
3. **[The Bitter Lesson does not require everything to live in weights](../../articles/the-bitter-lesson-does-not-require-everything-to-live-in-weights.md)** owns the full scaling argument, domain-extensibility, Sutton comparison, alternatives, and detailed failure conditions.
4. Experimental protocols and results become articles only when the evidence warrants them.

The program statement is the entry point, not the container for every supporting argument. Canonical derivations live in notes or companion articles; the program statement repeats only what is needed to state the research program.

The bearer module is carried by the [Naur note](../../notes/naur-equates-machine-execution-with-formulated-criteria.md) and the [coherent-search note](../../notes/program-theory-sustains-search-under-delayed-feedback.md). There is no separate Naur article.

## Research-program statement contract

Keep the program statement editable as five bounded units:

1. **Better software factories.** Start with a factory whose declared family includes factory-valued products. Make the successor factory the explicit object of construction, evaluation, and revision; define the declared comparison; and keep continued factory-development capability inside the meaning of *better*.
2. **Coherent modification and held theory.** State Naur's functional constraint, distinguish retained from held theory, and explain fallible theory-guided search, backtracking, recovery, and delayed feedback.
3. **Natural-language implementation bet.** Explain why LLM-interpreted natural-language theory may coordinate heterogeneous machinery and make retention, activation, and causal use experimentally separable while remaining one contingent carrier.
4. **Evidence and tests.** Begin with the narrower Naurian bearer test in ordinary software projects, then give the factory an explicit successor-construction task. The stronger result is a directly evaluated better successor, not an incidental factory update from ordinary product work.
5. **Bootstrap and failure conditions.** Disclose the human-inclusive Commonplace boundary, state the compact progress criterion, retain only the narrow Bitter Lesson bridge, and name the strongest conditions under which the strategy should be narrowed or abandoned.

An agent edit should normally target one of these units. An integration pass checks the configured product family, direct improvement relation, held-theory distinction, evidence path, current actor allocation, experimental controls, and companion boundaries; it should not default to expanding the full article.

## Shared constraints

- Producing software factories is not a capability of every software factory. The recursive case begins only when factory-valued products are inside the configured family.
- A factory building another factory establishes constructional reach, not improvement.
- The successor factory is the intended product of the improvement task, not a side effect of unrelated software production.
- Learning occurs when evaluation-driven revision changes retained theory or machinery used in later construction decisions.
- A better successor is at least as capable on the declared prior scope, including continued factory production and improvement, and strictly better on at least one relevant production dimension.
- Minimal factory construction does not require theory. The stronger theory-bearing constraint is for open-ended coherent modification.
- Retained theory is not held theory. Holding includes recognizing relevance and bringing project-specific theory to bear on novel demands without an external actor naming the connection.
- Human theory-holders use partial, fallible theories to guide search, diagnosis, backtracking, executable change, and recovery. The computational comparison is not a demand for a correct first proposal.
- Theory does not replace generate-and-verify, generic search, learned policies, or direct optimization; it may guide and coordinate them.
- Natural language is the tested carrier, not a necessary representational form.
- Distinguish recoverability, contextual activation, causal use, evaluation, theory revision, and changed later construction.
- System-building consequences can test causal usefulness and fit, but they cannot replace factual, formal, or scope evidence.
- The current Commonplace loop is human-inclusive. The bottleneck is not absence of computation but recurring task-specific human judgment.
- Computational closure, self-improvement, compounding, and broad reach are separate claims and do not follow from one better-factory transition.
- Only the form-only inference is rebutted under the Bitter Lesson. The bootstrap is a first strategy under incomplete global evaluation, not a uniqueness claim.
