# Workshop: theory-mediated self-improvement research series

**Posed by:** the operator, 2026-08-27.

## Intent

Get researchers interested in the theory-mediated self-improvement research
program and make substantive research exchange and collaboration possible.

The articles are means. They should make the program legible and credible,
show where claims come from, expose genuine open questions, and make it easy to
criticize, test, extend, or connect the work to other research. Completing the
articles is not success by itself.

This series is for researchers. Practitioner adoption and operational guidance
belong in a separate series.

## Situation

Commonplace already contains substantial theory, source work, article material,
evaluations, and operative machinery, but repeated editing has left the plan
poorly integrated. Two article baselines remain usable; four reviewed drafts
remain evidence for claim recovery rather than prose foundations.

Commonplace is a live human-agent testbed; persistent project-specific theory
for programming agents is the first demanding external application. Neither
case establishes computational closure or independent theory possession.

Researcher incentives are not yet known. Plausible sources of value are
important questions, leverage from prepared material, freedom to challenge the
framing, reciprocal research value, visible credit, and serious engagement
with disagreement. Treat these as hypotheses, not promises.

## Direction fixed by the operator

- Articles serve researcher understanding and engagement; their number, titles,
  and order may change.
- Theory claims are first-class outputs as well as support for articles and
  operative machinery.
- The target architecture is mixed: retained natural-language theory,
  language-model interpretation, symbolic procedures and code, and operational
  evidence have different functions and failures.
- A theory-mediated improvement claim requires one causal path: theory guides a
  change, the result tests the theory, and revision affects later operation.
- Practical usefulness and scientific discrimination are separate, coequal
  criteria.
- Computational closure is task-scoped. Structural closure, capability,
  warrant, and system power remain separate coordinates; see
  [Task-scoped computational closure](./task-scoped-computational-closure.md).
- Source conclusions transfer only as far as the
  [match register](./match-register.md) licenses. Review-defeated drafts are
  evidence for claim recovery, not foundations for successor prose.

## Mission and priorities

Reconcile the inherited material into one credible researcher-facing program
and use the articles to open useful points of contact with it.

1. **Consolidate the program.** Reconcile the
   [shared model](./shared-model.md),
   [task-scoped closure formulation](./task-scoped-computational-closure.md),
   [closure-capability map](./closure-capability-map.md),
   [incumbent ledger](./incumbent-ledger.md), and durable notes. Each
   load-bearing claim needs a clear scope, mechanism, evidence status, and
   possible failure.
2. **Reconstruct the articles.** Treat
   [article roles](./article-roles.md) as provisional argumentative jobs, not a
   commitment to inherited drafts or article count. Each retained article
   should clarify part of the program and expose relevant evidence and open
   questions.
3. **Show the program operating.** Record at least one Commonplace or
   programming-agent path where theory guides a change, evidence bears on the
   theory, and the result is retained, revised, rejected, or deferred.
4. **Make research contact easy.** Expose open questions and supporting
   artifacts without requiring reconstruction of the whole workshop.
5. **Test the invitation.** Put a bounded part of the series before relevant
   researchers. Record substantive responses, objections, independent uses,
   and reasons for disengagement; revise the articles and incentive hypothesis
   accordingly.

These are priorities, not a fixed sequence. Agents may adapt article structure,
audience, examples, outreach, and order as evidence changes.

Hand back decisions that would change the researcher-facing intent, promise an
external reward, set terms for joint work without agreement, distort a claim
for persuasion, resolve an unfixed value trade-off, or create another external
commitment.

This is the workshop's use of
[intent-framed delegation](../../notes/intent-framed-delegation-is-a-control-regime-not-a-short-prompt.md):
the operator fixes purpose and bounds; agents adapt the means.

## Evaluation

Research progress means sharper claims, evidence that changes their status,
useful machinery derived from them, or counterexamples that force revision.
Article polish alone is insufficient.

For computational closure, use the separate task-scoped formulation. A closure
claim must declare task selection, objective and acceptance, system boundary,
permitted exogenous inputs, horizon, resources, and coverage. Closure concerns
where required decisions and transitions occur; capability and warrant need
separate evidence.

For researcher engagement, page views and generic praise are weak signals.
Stronger signals are substantive criticism, independent testing or use,
exchange of evidence or methods, and continuing reciprocal dialogue. An
unsuccessful outreach attempt still counts as evidence when the likely cause is
recorded and used to revise the invitation.

## Outputs

- durable theory claims and their evidence;
- operative methods, instructions, validators, schemas, or code derived from
  sufficiently grounded theory;
- evidence records, including interventions, counterexamples, negative results,
  and theory revisions;
- coherent researcher-facing articles;
- an accessible research surface linking claims and open questions to their
  supporting material; and
- evidence from a bounded researcher-engagement attempt.

Theory claims are both supporting machinery for articles and workshop outputs
in their own right.

## What closes this workshop

The workshop closes when:

1. the program model and durable claims are coherent and all load-bearing
   inherited claims have dispositions;
2. the article architecture has been reconstructed around the admitted program;
3. one recorded Commonplace or programming-agent episode demonstrates the
   theory -> intervention -> evidence -> revision path;
4. the articles expose important open questions and their supporting material;
5. one bounded researcher-engagement attempt produces substantive exchange or a
   recorded negative result that revises the invitation; and
6. source uses stay within match-register boundaries, outstanding reviews are
   resolved, durable outputs validate, and the earlier workshop is closed.

Article completion alone cannot close the workshop. At closure, durable theory,
machinery, evidence, and articles move to their library homes and this directory
is deleted.
