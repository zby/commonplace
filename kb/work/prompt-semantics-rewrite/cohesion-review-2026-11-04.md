## Corrections

- Scoping: the universal opening contradicted the architecture-specific ending. Scoped the opening to flat assembly without enforced local boundaries, moved the frame definition before its first use, and replaced the false flat-versus-bounded contrast with accumulated versus selected inputs. Limited the dynamic-scope and capture analogies.
- Specialization: removed a repeated equivalence disclaimer. Static and dynamic now refer consistently to specialization time, rather than treating the request as both known and dynamic for a fixed invocation.
- Context savings: replaced the unconditional opening cost claim with the mechanism the following paragraphs actually support. Corrected “defers” to “moves earlier” for preloaded tokens.
- Model-resolved indirection: replaced the claim that only a harness can supply formal resolution semantics with the distinction between adopted model semantics and a separately inspectable resolver.
- Typed callables: removed the opening promise of automatic reliability gains and stale claims about current commands. The progression is now interface, illustrative signatures, then semantic obligations.
- Deployment learning: removed claims that every retained constraint improves capacity, automatically composes, matches fine-tuning, or covers most deployment needs. The progression is now candidate benefits, persistence and interactions, then the evaluated learning loop.
- Metacircular methodologies and verifiability: their revised examples connect the mechanisms to their limits. Programming practices remains coherent with these distinctions; no further change was needed in this pass.
