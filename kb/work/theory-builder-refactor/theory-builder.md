# Theory builder

> **Status:** Workshop definition, 2026-09-10.

A **theory builder** is a persistent system that develops and revises explicit,
fallible theories about subjects it is asked to investigate. It uses evidence
to derive and test consequences, identify where a theory may need revision,
produce candidate revisions, and evaluate them.

The definition does not require one representational form or one refinement
algorithm. The theory and the machinery used to manipulate it may be natural
language, symbolic, programmatic, probabilistic, or mixed.

The builder's primary product is theory about subjects other than the builder
itself. A theory builder need not be reflective or autonomous.
