# Theory retention and use: policy under review

> **Status:** Workshop policy draft, 2026-09-17. Separated from the theory
> vocabulary after the operator selected [tentative theory](./tentative-theory.md).
> This preserves the policy questions raised on 2026-09-14 without making
> their answers conditions for using Popper's term. The relevant literature
> is now ingested; the [definitions review](./README.md#definition-review-dispositions)
> still has to reconcile these proposals before promotion.

The operator's earlier requirement was that the builder should not rely on
unwarranted claims as accepted theory. The task is to state what warrants
each kind of reliance, while allowing candidates to be retained for inquiry.
Being tentative does not itself grant permission to consume a claim.

## Proposed obligations

1. **Track support at the granularity evidence licenses.** The supported
   unit may be a claim, conjunction, or integrated model, with a stated
   scope. Comparative support for a model does not by itself identify the
   contribution of each component. The
   [warrant-granularity note](../../notes/theory-warrant-tracked-at-the-finest-granularity-evidence-licenses.md)
   already makes this distinction. The earlier per-claim-only wording was
   too strong; neither a successful test nor a detected failure guarantees
   component-level attribution. Tentativeness also does not require every
   theory to claim a scope larger than the tested one.
2. **Separate candidate retention from reliance.** State the support needed
   for a particular consumption path and the required confidence. A claim
   may guide an experiment without being ready for routine use or
   codification. Candidates below a use threshold can remain targets for
   inquiry; storing them does not make them accepted theory. The
   [starting-warrant note](../../notes/derivation-and-inheritance-give-starting-warrant-earns-scope.md)
   and [costly-entrenchment note](../../notes/current-task-fit-alone-does-not-warrant-costly-entrenchment.md)
   supply the current proposals. The builder still needs to decide which
   uses are permitted and how speculative status remains visible.
3. **State what warrants a revision and a sequence of revisions.** The
   proposal requires evidence that a change corrects errors while preserving
   useful fit on tested cases, relative to a declared objective. Whether
   local approvals warrant the resulting sequence is a separate question.
   Review it against the
   [adaptive-evaluation ingest (snapshot required)](../../sources/generalization-adaptive-data-analysis-holdout-reuse.ingest.md)
   before asserting that warranted revisions automatically chain. The
   protocol must distinguish cases used to construct a repair from evidence
   used to assess it.

Assessment of warrant can itself be wrong. Distinguish actual support from
an evaluator's judgment that it is sufficient; a recorded approval is not
proof of the former. The failure of concern is
[false-positive acceptance becoming operative](../../notes/false-positive-generation-is-filtered-before-retention.md).

## The objective clause

The proposal treats a conceptual revision that changes what an objective
commits to as an objective change even if its text remains unchanged. Such a
change must be reported as such. Under the
[objective-revision note](../../notes/revising-an-improvement-objective-is-licensed-from-outside-it.md),
an improvement claim needs a comparison level the revision did not itself
change. What can supply that level depends on the evidence interface; it
does not follow from autonomy alone that no objective change can be licensed.

## Two reachable sets

Distinguish states reachable by any revision from states reachable by
revisions warranted for a declared use under an objective. The
[warrant-bounded proposal](./proposal-warrant-bounded-open-endedness.md)
argues that the second set is closed under the seed objective and admitted
evidence; the [Gödel-machine comparison](./goedel-machine-comparison.md)
contests that inference. No closure claim follows from the term *tentative
theory*. Resolve the evidence-interface and objective questions before
adopting a bound.

## Questions to settle before adoption

- Which consumption paths need different thresholds, and what evidence
  licenses each? An external falsifier provides an outcome signal, not a
  complete retention policy for the internal theories behind that outcome.
- What can be retained solely for inquiry, and how is it kept from being
  consumed as an accepted claim? Questions need no asserted answer;
  observations and log entries can contain claims whose support still needs
  examination.
- Which dependencies must survive revision? Use the
  [ATMS ingest (snapshot required)](../../sources/an-assumption-based-tms.ingest.md)
  and [Hansson ingest (snapshot required)](../../sources/in-defense-of-base-contraction.ingest.md)
  to separate dependency maintenance from evidential warrant and selection
  policy.
- Which guarantees concern eventual learning rather than current permission
  to rely? The [formal-learning ingest (snapshot required)](../../sources/formal-learning-theory.ingest.md)
  is an input to that review, not an automatic proof of the proposed bound.

Resolve each obligation by an adopted policy, an explicit limitation of the
research claim, or a stated reason it is outside the evaluated use. Do not
carry these questions into the library as additional clauses defining
tentative theories.
