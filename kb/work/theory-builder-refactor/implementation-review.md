## Seven review rows

| Issue | Disposition | What has changed; what is still needed |
|---|---|---|
| Interpreter scope and guarantees | Excluded as a prerequisite of the main outcome comparison; attribution questions remain open | The interpreter is a scoped analytic device. No budget-exhaustion-only guarantee establishes faithful completed interpretation. Fidelity, coverage, abstention, and realizability still need assessment for claims that depend on them. |
| Seed and bootstrap claims | Open empirical question | A frozen seed and comparable budget are part of the protocol. The illustrative cases do not establish the seed's ability to acquire a method for new areas or sustained recursive improvement. |
| Extension and model weights | Definition revised; demonstrations pending | Extension requires a retained change, later consumption, and a bounded capability comparison with the seed. An artifact diff is evidence of a change, not the definition of capability. Existing-slot revisions may qualify. Fixed weights constrain this research target, not all builders. |
| Tentative status and retention policy | Vocabulary separated; policy questions remain open | Popper's status does not decide admission or consumption thresholds. Evidence may support a claim, conjunction, or model and scope. Each evaluated use still needs a declared policy; current approval is distinct from warranted reliance. |
| Objectives and closure | Autonomy inference removed; seed closure excluded as a premise | Objective preservation and proxy validity remain obligations inside the main path. New requests need not change the objective. A general theorem about all warrant reachable from a seed remains unestablished. |
| Evidence interface | Specification implemented; observation pending | Boundary, case supply, acceptance role, consumption, protocol, budget, horizon, and acquisition mode are declared. The episodes establish that the distinctions can be stated, not that a project has exercised them successfully. |
| Gödel-machine boundary cases | Categorical exclusion removed; deployment classification remains limited | Utility's internal representation does not decide where acceptance comes from. Report source-supported boundary, observations, evidence access, and continuing responsibility separately. |

## Refinement-interface closing condition

Closing condition 3 remains open. The
[refinement-interface investigation](../theory-refinement-interface/theory-refinement-interface.md)
proposes derive, compare, locate, revise, and evaluate as a machinery
contract. The new external evidence interface specifies the origin and
assessment of outcomes; it does not implement those internal operations or
transfer classical guarantees to interpreted prose.

The remaining reconciliation must separate a useful description of the
task from guarantees of an implementation. In particular, the investigation's
claims that every operation is available for any readable representation,
that the interpreted update space is open, and that certain results transfer
at task level need source and mechanism checks. A trace identifies candidate
repair locations rather than guaranteeing a unique fault; a particular
search algorithm's local-optimum result is not automatically a theorem about
all implementations of the five roles.

The completed ingests are the inputs for that work. This implementation
does not promote the interface note, claim its open questions are solved, or
delete its argument records.

## Promotion and migration readiness

The proposed durable outputs are the theory-builder and externally tested
definitions, the reflective and autonomous conditions, a short borrowed
tentative-theory entry within theory refinement, and the transferable
claim/scope distinction behind the general-case obligations. The ideal
interpreter stays an argument record while its guarantee questions remain
open; the general retention policy is not promoted as a settled universal
rule.

Two doctrine edits join the promotion commit, adopted on 2026-09-17 and
grounded in the [README's Goal](./README.md#goal). In the root instruction
file, beside the statement that the repository uses its own knowledge
system: "Develop Commonplace by using it. Practical demands expose limits
in its theories and machinery; reflective revision turns them into retained
improvements; recorded episodes become evidence for the research program."
In its git rules: "A commit states which justification it carries when more
than one could: product use, reflective learning, or research evidence. A
research-evidence commit cites the protocol record it feeds."

The [adoption plan](./tentative-theory-kb-adoption-plan.md) owns the library
terminology sweep and source/provenance exceptions. Its trigger is adoption
of the conclusions and destinations, not merely completion of workshop
drafting. Promotion must not create library links into the workshop.
Workshop closure additionally requires the unresolved refinement-interface
condition to be satisfied or explicitly changed by the operator.

No first consuming project, model versions, scored task population, budgets,
comparison margin, or assessment horizon has yet been selected. Those are
run inputs; the protocol can be reviewed before they are filled. No empirical
success, external validity, or autonomous continuation is claimed here.
