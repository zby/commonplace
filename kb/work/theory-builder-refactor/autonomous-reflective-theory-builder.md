# Autonomous reflective theory builder

> **Status:** Workshop definition, 2026-09-10.

An **autonomous reflective theory builder** is a reflective theory builder in
which every internal theory-building role is performed computationally.

These roles include interpreting and revising theories, diagnosing failures,
evaluating candidate revisions, changing theory-building machinery when
needed, and selecting the retained successor state. External users or the
environment may still supply questions, evidence, requirements, observations,
and judgments about outcomes.

This is the target learner for the proposed autonomous learning paradigm.
