## 1. Establish a discriminating comparison

Preserve the recommendation to start with [Experiment 2](./experiments.md#2-assumption-failure-diagnosis), not broad article ratings. Use its faults and ambiguous traces; do not duplicate the four existing experiments.

Compare three fresh-context conditions:

- Ordinary: task, evidence and normal repair instruction.
- Checklist: the same facts, with prompts to inspect evidence, consistency and proposed repairs.
- Explicit relation: the same facts, organized as outcome, available action, and environmental assumption; ask what observation could defeat the connection.

All receive identical tools, permissions, clarification opportunities and total work caps. Match checklist length, review slots and requested justification to the treatment. Include the same defeat-check request in the checklist, without organizing it around the action–outcome relation. Supply no treatment-only clues or worked solution. Log actual tokens, calls, elapsed work and operator review; nominal caps alone do not match effort. The treatment–checklist contrast tests organization, not whether receiving an extra premise helps.

## 2. Worked case and bounded pilot

**Proposed fixture:** repair a paragraph claiming “the treatment reduces errors in novices.” Its citation resolves, but the supplied passage reports only experts. Desired outcome: a reader can determine whether the novice claim is supported. Available actions: inspect passages, qualify/remove the claim, or request missing evidence. Candidate assumption: a resolving citation supports the stated population-specific claim. Inspecting the passage defeats that assumption; repairing the URL or headings cannot help.

Give a separate reader only the repaired paragraph and fixed source packet. Ask whether a novice benefit is established and which passage warrants the answer. Score against the packet, not the author's rationale. Also include an unchanged, genuinely supporting passage: indiscriminate qualification must lose credit.

Before any authorized runs, have two reviewers independently check fixture answers and indistinguishable-trace limits. Start with six cases spanning faulty execution, faulty connection, inadequate procedure goal, valid connection and unavailable evidence; propose two fresh runs per condition per case (36 author runs). Freeze packets, scoring and budget beforehand. Markdown fixtures and existing file tools suffice; no runtime development. Retain packets, outputs, diffs, observations, adjudications and audit traces under separately approved paths.

## 3. Interpret and decide

Separate **semantic measures**—correctly identifying the failed implication, supported uncertainty and preserved claim meaning—from **procedural measures**—performing checks, following steps and staying within permissions. Primary outcomes are independent reader decisions and effective repairs; labels and completed checklists are not success.

Blind reviewers to condition where feasible. Examine ceiling effects, conspicuous seeded faults, evaluator expectations, extra deliberation, prompt wording and reader compensation as alternatives. Paraphrase prompts before scaling if results depend on wording.

Stop to repair disputed answer keys or unequal information/budgets. Null results at ceiling are inconclusive; equal outcomes with extra cost favor ordinary guidance. More false blame, lost valid claims or premature refusal count against treatment. Advance only if gains over checklist recur across fault cases without worse valid-case performance within the agreed cost cap; otherwise stop or redesign, not scale by default. This pilot cannot estimate population effects.
