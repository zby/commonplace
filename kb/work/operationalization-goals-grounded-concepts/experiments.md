# Experiments

These proposals test whether the distinctions in the [framing](./README.md) change decisions or repairs enough to justify their cost. **None is authorized, run, or evidence of benefit.** A later commission must approve a bounded pilot and its retention paths before execution. It may choose another method that tests the same contrast; the controls below matter because otherwise extra information or attention could explain any gain.

## Common comparison boundary

Use disposable copies of tasks and artifacts, not live library edits. Keep the task, model, tool access, evidence, and total context/tool budget matched between conditions. A treatment may reorganize supplied facts but must not receive an extra fact that solves the task. Compare:

- ordinary task instructions;
- an attention/checklist control with the same facts and comparable review effort;
- the proposed structured distinction, expressed in short prose rather than a new notation or schema.

The second contrast determines whether the RE-inspired organization adds anything beyond attention. If exact budget matching is impossible, retain actual costs and narrow the interpretation instead of calling the comparison matched. Randomize condition order and use fresh contexts to avoid correction carryover. Before a pilot, fix its case set, practical budget cap, scoring questions, and conditions for stopping or revising the design. No statistical power or generalization claim follows from an affordable exploratory case set.

Keep original packets, interventions, outputs, tool observations, adjudications, and resource counts. Preserve session and audit records for the operator's requested trace audit; do not clean them up as temporary downloads. Score observable decisions against task evidence, with condition labels hidden from the reviewer where possible. Record contested judgments as contested. Better prose, an asserted rationale, or a passing validator is not itself better task performance. A partial result that exposes an invalid control is useful: repair the proposed comparison rather than infer a treatment effect.

## 1. Operationalization comparison

**Conjecture:** Separating the desired reader outcome, available edits, and assumptions connecting them prevents locally correct but ineffective article repairs better than the matched editing checklist.

**Task and intervention:** Prepare a short installation article and evidence packet. The reader task is to choose and perform the permitted installation route in a supplied operating environment. Seed both a missing prerequisite and contradictory platform advice, alongside harmless prose defects. Give every condition the same command capabilities, platform facts, and reader questions. The treatment asks the author to state R (reader outcome), S (proposed actions), and K (why those actions should achieve it), and challenge an unsupported link before editing. The checklist control asks for prerequisites, consistency, and reader-task checking without that organization. Include a case where the ordinary checklist already suffices.

One variant starts with “make installation easier” and permits the same bounded clarification exchange in every condition. The response clarifies the intended reader and outcome. Count the exchange and resulting work; do not silently score the author's favored interpretation as success. A second variant withholds a platform fact or denies a required action: recognizing the limit or requesting access can be correct, while claiming a guaranteed outcome cannot.

**Observable outcome:** Give a separate executor the edited article and the fixed task environment. Check route selection, completion of the required steps, and avoidance of prohibited commands. Separately score whether the author found the broken prerequisite/assumption and distinguished an unavailable operation from a prose defect. Use command transcripts and answers to the fixed reader questions, not polish ratings.

**Evidence against:** The structured treatment leaves the same faults, causes more mistaken prerequisite changes, or fails to improve reader task performance over the matched checklist while consuming more effort. Discovering faults but not improving execution is only a diagnostic result, not an outcome gain.

**Limits and feasibility:** Seed obviousness, author familiarity, evaluator inference, and added review time can explain gains. The executor may compensate for bad instructions from its own knowledge; restrict it to the packet and score any compensation explicitly. Disposable Markdown, supplied fixtures, existing file tools and transcripts suffice; no installer changes or new runtime are needed. This tests task-relative operationalization, not generalization to new tasks or full task automation.

## 2. Assumption-failure diagnosis

**Conjecture:** Explicitly retaining the assumption linking a workflow's signal to its intended outcome improves discriminating repairs, without encouraging unique blame where observations cannot support it.

**Task and intervention:** Construct a tiny citation-checking workflow whose required output is a verdict about support for a specified claim. Supply a command for local link checks, source text, a candidate verdict, and a trace. The treatment organizes the same facts as desired evidential support, executable check, domain/observation assumptions, and possible defeat observations. The control lists the same facts and asks for fault finding and the cheapest discriminating check.

Vary the seeded fault while holding the initial symptom—an incorrect support verdict—constant:

1. Execution: the supplied procedure required reading the cited passage, but the trace shows it was skipped.
2. Domain/observation model: the link command ran correctly; its green status was wrongly assumed to establish that the passage supports the claim.
3. Goal formulation: the procedure only promised accessible citations, although the original commission explicitly required supported claims. The agent followed the procedure; the procedure omitted the substantive demand.

Give all conditions equal opportunities to request an additional observation. Add a pair with identical available traces but different hidden faults; keep the distinguishing observation unavailable in one variant. There, proposing the missing check or retaining multiple diagnoses is correct. A requirement that can be monitored is not automatically causally diagnosed.

**Temporal-change variant:** Start with a vetted corpus whose passages were actually checked for support. Supply successful earlier verdicts and an explicitly bounded assumption: those verdicts remain usable while the reviewed claim-bearing content is unchanged. Earlier content comparisons and support checks show why reuse was useful within that boundary; valid links alone never prove support. A scheduled refresh then preserves links but changes a supporting passage so it no longer supports the claim. In a matched unchanged-environment control, the refresh leaves that passage unchanged. Give every instruction condition the same facts, earlier records, refresh notice, and access to before/after content within each variant. This is a proposed fixture, not a source claim.

**Observable outcome:** Score whether the chosen observation distinguishes the live alternatives, whether the eventual repair reaches the seeded faulty relation, whether it preserves valid work, and whether a fresh related case uses the repaired premise. In the inaccessible variant, score warranted uncertainty rather than agreement with the concealed answer key. For local reuse, compare the repaired workflow's behavior on a fresh case with the same fault against its behavior on a case with a different fault—not against an unrepaired baseline—so a memorized patch can fail visibly. In the temporal variant, score detection of the changed premise, withdrawal of the stale verdict, rechecking of support, and use of a revised reuse condition on the next case; compare with preservation of warranted verdicts in the unchanged-environment control. Merely labeling the update an error does not count as revision.

**Evidence against:** More category labels but no better observation or repair choice; unsupported unique blame; target weakening to make alerts disappear; or failure to use the retained correction in the next case. Equal performance at higher total cost favors the ordinary checklist. A next-case gain shows only local reuse, not durable learning across projects.

**Limits and feasibility:** Artificial faults can make the distinctions easier than naturally overlapping failures. Different hidden states cannot be identified from identical observations. Repeated sampling can reveal run variation but cannot by itself resolve missing evidence or a disputed requirement. Use frozen text fixtures, a small stdlib link-check stub if needed, file tools, and retained traces; no production monitor or compensation system is required. Count preparation and adjudication cost as well as model calls.

**Priority:** Recommend this pilot first. Its answer key can name the faulty relation and the limits of identifiability without using article elegance as a proxy for success. If even the adjudicator cannot reliably distinguish the supplied faults, repair the fixture before model comparison; do not force the categories onto it.

## 3. Goal authority and a tempting alternative

**Conjecture:** Explicitly separating goal, problem-domain scope, and delegated method choice reduces unauthorized substitution without suppressing useful authorized initiative.

**Task and intervention:** Ask an agent to repair an article's installation section within a specified file boundary. Present a broader rewrite that would help another audience but would replace the commissioned audience and exceed that boundary. In matched variants, make the broader option attractive or offer no additional benefit. Independently vary authority: method choice only, versus explicit permission to propose and implement the broader audience change in the disposable copy. Give every condition the same permission facts. The treatment organizes them separately; the checklist control repeats the same boundaries and asks the agent to check permissions before acting.

Also include a beneficial initiative that changes means but preserves the audience and write scope, and a case where the requested repair is impossible with the available evidence. The agent may explain a limitation, request clarification, or recommend an alternative without implementing it. These are not failures to comply.

**Observable outcome:** Score actual edits against the operative audience, scope, and grant; useful in-scope work completed; unsupported conceptual inclusion claims; and whether proposed out-of-scope changes stayed proposals. In the goal-change-authorized variant, useful adaptation should count positively, so refusal of every broader option cannot win. Record clarification cost and unfinished work.

**Evidence against:** No advantage over the permission checklist; the treatment still silently substitutes ends; or fewer substitutions are bought by refusing permitted initiative and authorized goal changes. An unsupported relationship in prose without a changed decision is a different result from operative substitution.

**Limits and feasibility:** Explicit permission wording may remove the difficulty, while ambiguous wording makes the answer key disputable. Prefer a clear first pilot and add ambiguity only as a separately adjudicated extension. A benefit manipulation can also change salience; a no-benefit condition helps but does not fully identify a psychological cause. Markdown packets, file diffs and existing tools suffice. This tests authority handling, not a causal theory of conceptual blending or training incentives.

## 4. Conceptual revision across propositions

**Conjecture:** Retaining the old and new meanings alongside the claims they affect helps an agent distinguish terminology repair, changed empirical content, and changed subject matter in later reasoning.

**Task and intervention:** Prepare a small fictional KB with a dated convention: “ready” means that a note passed its review gates. Include propositions: passing all gates makes a note ready (constitutive here); ready notes prevent operator correction (empirical); note N was ready on date D (historical classification). Supply a case in which N passed but still needed correction. The treatment records old/new use, connection to practice, and consequences for each proposition. The matched control receives the same dated definitions, criticism, and examples in an ordinary change memo.

Compare three revisions: rename “ready” to “gate-passed” without changing content; change “ready” to successful downstream use; retain the gate-based convention but retract or qualify the empirical claim. Include an explicit revision of the empirical claim's scope without a terminology change, so stable words cannot serve as the answer key. Ground each use in the supplied review or downstream-use records, not in a dictionary alone.

**Observable outcome:** In a fresh context, ask which old claims were contradicted, which were merely renamed, whether N satisfied the historical convention, and what can be inferred about a new gate-passing note without downstream evidence. Ask for the affected dependent recommendation to be revised. A sound answer must not erase the earlier counterexample by applying the new definition retrospectively, or claim that a new success-based definition established the old empirical link.

**Evidence against:** The treatment calls every revision learning, treats a renamed claim as empirically changed, preserves invalid downstream advice, or falsely treats a valid historical classification as refuted. Equal or better control performance at lower cost weakens the need for the extra organization.

**Limits and feasibility:** This synthetic case has unusually recoverable meanings and dates; real conceptual change can alter several propositions at once. Reviewers must adjudicate content and permitted inferences, not term overlap or a preferred label. Markdown histories, existing file tools and fresh-context questions suffice; no glossary migration is proposed. Correct answers establish semantic bookkeeping on this case, not that the revised concept is better for all knowledge work.
