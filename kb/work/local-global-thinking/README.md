# Local and global thinking: when should a connection become the focus?

The operator posed this question on 2026-11-21: effective work requires focus, but what connections do we lose by choosing what to focus on? One possible move is to stop working on either of two things separately and make their relation the object of inquiry. Does that give us a useful account of when to stay with a local question and when to change focus?

The operator singled out the [unresolved frontier](../../reference/definitions/unresolved-frontier.md), the possible further work still discoverable across the KB. When revising a note, we at least know which note needs attention. When choosing what to investigate, we may not yet have the question. For example, an exception left unexplained in one workshop and a blocked choice in another might depend on the same assumption. If so, investigating that assumption becomes an option that neither workshop offered on its own. This is an illustration, not a recorded case. Keeping both observations discoverable makes the comparison possible; it does not tell anyone to make it.

Commonplace already makes this change of focus in [the connect skill](../../instructions/cp-skill-connect/SKILL.md). It starts from one artifact, searches for others and asks what a reader would gain by following a particular connection. Its report proposes connections; it does not add links or decide what work to do next. The interesting comparison is between finding a useful connection *for a known artifact* and noticing a connection that *changes which problem is worth working on*. When does the first accomplish the second? When is following a plausible connection just a distraction?

Close when a Commonplace case lets us say what the narrower focus made possible, what it left out, and whether attending to a relation changed a question or decision. Extract a durable note only if the distinction would help someone choose their next move; an existing explanation that already suffices is also a reason to close. This is an investigation, not a commission to redesign connect or build a frontier index.
