# Premise decomposition: Naur's retained passages do not establish a human-only theory bearer

**Note:** kb/notes/irreducibility-to-rules-bounds-text-alone-not-text-plus-interpreter.md
**Register:** claim
**Central commitment (one sentence):** Within its retained-source scope and at the undeclared and therefore universal modality, the note claims that Naur's quoted requirement for world-sensitive application judgment identifies a human programmer as the contributor but does not establish that humanity is necessary to bear the capacity, leaving a separately competent text-plus-interpreter composite unexcluded rather than demonstrated.

## Premises and counterexamples

1. **Within the retained passages, “someone,” “the agent who has knowledge,” and “the programmer” identify the role or actual contributor of the required world-understanding without making membership in the human class necessary for any possible bearer.** — DOUBTFUL — In ordinary 1985 usage, “someone” denotes a person and “programmer” denotes a human occupational role; on a constitutive reading of those words, a nonhuman system with the stipulated functional discriminations still would not be the kind of bearer to which Naur assigns the decision. The intervening word “agent” permits the note's role reading but does not settle which reading governs. — GLOBAL — instance
2. **The retained assertion that modification requires world-sensitive judgment beyond any limited criterion set applies to every program-modification case covered by the note's unqualified opening formulation.** — DOUBTFUL — A closed-world controller whose admissible changes and selection conditions are finite and exhaustively specified can receive a new request that is correctly implemented by choosing and validating an enumerated option, without a fresh world-side similarity judgment; the note later discusses novel demands but does not state a scope condition that excludes bounded modification domains from the opening requirement. — LOCAL — instance
3. **Naur's phrase “any limited set of criteria or rules” targets an explicit criterion set offered in place of judgment rather than the complete effective implementation of every possible interpreter.** — DOUBTFUL — A fixed LLM-plus-artifact deployment has finitely specified weights, inference code, and decoding rules, so under a literal effective-procedure reading it is itself one limited rule-carried system, not an interpreter outside the phrase's range. The retained passage contrasts rules with a knowledgeable agent but does not define which of these two senses of “rules” controls; narrowing the conjectured candidate to interpreters not covered by the argument would preserve the bearer claim. — LOCAL — instance
4. **A statement that a human programmer supplies a required capacity in Naur's account does not, absent a human-restricting meaning or premise, entail that only humans can possess that capacity.** — HOLDS — “A programmer supplied the required world knowledge in these cases” is compatible with another kind of bearer supplying equivalent knowledge in another case; the active hunt found no valid route from actual contributor to exclusive bearer other than the contested lexical restriction isolated in premise 1.
5. **A judgment's irreducibility to a limited decision list does not entail that written propositions cannot inform the judgment or that text plus an independently competent bearer cannot exercise it.** — HOLDS — A situated programmer can consult a written relevance decision or rejected-alternative rationale and still contribute the unenumerated discrimination needed for a new case; this supplies a countermodel to the alleged entailment rather than a defeater of the premise.
6. **Retained text without an interpreter does not itself perform Naur's mapping, justification-selection, or novel-demand judgment capabilities.** — HOLDS — Executable text was the strongest apparent counterexample, but it acts only through execution semantics: if those semantics contribute no world-sensitive discrimination, the text still lacks the stipulated judgment, and if they do contribute it, they occupy the interpreter side of the note's distinction.
7. **A candidate theory-holder must possess the relevant world discriminations and the particular program theory, and its success must be dispositional across occasions rather than a single acceptable output.** — HOLDS — A system that produces one plausible modification from general language competence but cannot explain the design mapping or extend it on a later demand is a counterexample to treating one output as possession, not to these requirements; preloading or copying a particular theory also satisfies the note's earlier “possess or acquire” formulation rather than escaping it.
8. **Claiming that the retained passages leave a bearer class unsettled does not require showing that a current LLM-plus-artifact system satisfies the capacity conditions.** — HOLDS — Source entailment and present implementation are separable: every current candidate could fail the three conditions while the retained capacity statement still lacks a premise excluding all other bearers, subject to the human-denoting language tested in premise 1.

## For the human

Look first at premise 1: decide whether Naur's retained uses of “someone” and “programmer” merely name the contributor in his examples or restrict the theory-bearer category to humans, because the latter reading would put the missing exclusivity premise inside the quoted text.
