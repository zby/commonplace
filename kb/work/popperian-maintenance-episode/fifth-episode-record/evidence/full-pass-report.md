## Warranted contribution

**Collection/type fit:** FITS — after the particular Naur passages are scoped, the note retains a transferable claim about separating a capacity requirement from an assertion about its bearer. It is a truth-apt context-engineering claim rather than a description of Commonplace or a procedure.

**Reader and prior:** The intended reader is an agent or maintainer making KB-architecture and context-engineering decisions, from the repository purpose and the `kb/notes/` collection contract (authoritative audience fallback). The incumbent title, description, opening distinction, and explicit statement that the note works through Naur select the contribution (artifact evidence, advisory about authorial intent). The closest KB baseline already says that theory-mediated self-improvement needs interpretation plus retention and that retained rationale should preserve premises its interpreter cannot regenerate. Connect also surfaced the broader mechanism that semantic work can be relocated but not eliminated. None of those artifacts performs the clause-level, source-bounded reading of Naur that this note can contribute.

**Update:** The retained Naur passages establish that program theory needs world-sensitive application judgment beyond an enumerated criterion set, but the quoted relevance-decision passage alone does not establish that only a human can exercise that judgment or that retained text cannot assist another competent interpreter.

**Why a generic treatment would not supply it:** A generic account can distinguish rules from judgment or text from interpretation. It does not supply the note's specific evidential delta: in the retained relevance passage, the requirement that someone understand the world and the statement that the programmer contributes that understanding are adjacent but logically distinct claims. The update preserves that local reading while refusing the incumbent's broader claims about every irreducibility argument, the whole essay, the 1985 technology landscape, or current LLM competence.

**Warrant:** The ingest retains Naur's three capability passages, the statement that the required similarity cannot be reduced to a limited set of criteria or rules, and the relevance-decision passage that separately states the world-understanding requirement and names the programmer as its contributor. Those extracts warrant the positive application-judgment requirement and the negative claim that this quoted passage does not itself derive bearer exclusivity. They do not warrant a source-wide absence claim about the essay; semantic/grounding-alignment therefore failed the incumbent. Critique-note independently found that fresh human interpreters already existed in Naur's transfer problem and that a generic interpreter does not thereby acquire program-specific theory. Premise decomposition defeated the incumbent's universal scope with an end-to-end computability counterexample and left the meaning of “rules” globally doubtful. The composite-substrate application remains a conjecture conditional on acquiring the program-specific capacity; it is not evidence for the source reading.

## Disposition

**keep (reframe)** — reframe: the title overreaches in scope and category by treating all irreducibility-to-rules arguments as artifact-local arguments against enumerated criteria; the warranted claim is that Naur's retained passages do not establish a human-only theory bearer. The frontmatter disposition value remains `keep` because the source-bounded contribution is distinct and no existing artifact owns it.
