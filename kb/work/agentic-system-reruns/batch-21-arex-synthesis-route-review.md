promised clearance.** The accepted records contain enough of the named
installed-library-state witness, and the frozen criteria supply the test, to

address the operator refusal to that exact synthesis version. This returns to

analyst. It does not authorize execution here.

accepted records cannot support an honest bounded account, the frozen route

## The handed records are sufficient for an attempt

The relevant criterion is [reflection](../../notes/definitions/reflective-system.md):
a process inside the declared system uses a representation of selected aspects
of that system, with causal connections from aspect changes to representation
updates and from representation-mediated operations to later behavior. It is an
architectural, aspect-relative claim; observed improvement, model introspection
and autonomous operation are separate questions. The frozen records contract
states the same two-direction test. No new criterion is needed.

These records are not merely an audit lead requiring a new analyst to discover
its mechanism. They already identify the representation, its derivation, its
consumers and its connection to future retained guidance. Report verification
explicitly accepts both named records; profile verification independently
recognizes computed access state and helper-bound enforcement. A synthesis
assessment can reason over those facts without adding an operative mechanism.

There is nevertheless a real tension to contain: report verification's closing
paragraph says reflection remains unestablished, and the old synthesis extends
that into a blanket two-direction uncertainty. Retaining that verifier as an
input does **not** make its broad assessment infallible. Conversely, synthesis
cannot silently replace it with a new settled record or declare reconciliation
supersessions. A public limitation may expose the mismatch, identify what the
specific records establish and what remains unresolved, and restrict the
assessment accordingly. If doing that would still mislead readers, `problem`
is mandatory. That stopping option is what makes this a bounded attempt rather
than an unsupported promise that synthesis alone can settle every inconsistency.

A qualified negative is therefore permitted in principle, not preapproved on
these facts. It needs a concrete link or boundary explanation that survives
comparison with the table above. Absence of exercised use cannot by itself
defeat a wired architectural capacity. A new-source explanation that changes
what the records say is outside this attempt.

## Feedback and independent verification remain distinct

For any later authorized attempt, the parent must pin the synthesis digest and
one immutable synthesis-specific findings packet, recheck member/latest-output
equality after completed reset, and deliver the operator refusal before any
advance. Explicit version addressing is required by this proposal; it is not
a compare-and-swap or ownership guard. Native transport is `--findings TEXT`,
not `--findings-file`. The packet should identify the two accepted record IDs,
the existing assessment's mismatch, both causal directions to dispose of, the
frozen criterion, and the option to decline with evidence or stop with `problem`.
It should preserve unrelated text and all carried limits, especially PassNet
feedback-inventory and saved-workflow/run-sidecar deletion limits. Do not hand
the author the prior audit as trusted source evidence or require its conclusion.

The ordinary synthesis correction channel supplies previous synthesis, refusal
and declared `answers`; unstructured operator findings count as one blocker.
The author must answer that blocker and correct its dependent public passages,
or explain declining it on the handed evidence. An unchanged primary output
cannot be rescued by artificial prose changes; native answers/closure rules
still apply. The packet must not broaden the role's mission: the generic worker
rule about checking frozen evidence does not override synthesis's specific ban
on adding evidence and reconciling records.

A fresh synthesis verifier then judges the exact new synthesis, its answers and
handed accepted records under frozen `verify-synthesis.md`. It neither edits
the account nor adds evidence. Retained report/profile verification are valid
workflow inputs, not freshly repeated independent investigations and not
independent verification of the new synthesis. The synthesis author's response
is an argument, not proof of repair. This route substitutes neither synthesis
for an aspect analyst nor authorship for independent verification.

Only ordinary successful checks, fresh synthesis verification/application,
assembly and publication can rebuild current public bytes. A normal blocker or
`problem` takes precedence. A fresh exact-byte audit must then decide whether
the public disposition resolves H1; native acceptance and `publishable` alone
cannot release the integration hold.
