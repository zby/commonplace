4. **No unsupported upgrade from source wiring to successful learning.** The historical review's stronger evaluative language is not treated as a fact to preserve. P overview's description and P synthesis explicitly withhold demonstrated capacity gain and reliable transfer. The synthesis states there was no target execution, supplied target trace or causal comparison, and distinguishes analyst tool activity from evidence of Voyager running.
