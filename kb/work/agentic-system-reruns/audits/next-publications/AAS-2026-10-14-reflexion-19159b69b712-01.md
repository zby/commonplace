### 1. WebShop difference is a supported correction, not lost coverage

Frozen `webshop_runs/webshop_trial.py:206–244` constructs `EnvironmentHistory` with at most three plans, but calls the action model with `init_prompt + prompt[-(6400-len(init_prompt)):]`. That separate string accumulates current actions/observations, not the history object's plan-rendered base. `env_history.py` confirms plans enter `_cur_query`, survive history reset, and appear when history is rendered. `generate_reflections.py` appends failed-task plans and explicitly supplies previous plans to the later reflection query.

The old broad “harness guarantees prompt presence” account is therefore unsafe for WebShop actions. The new synthesis, “What survives an attempt, and where it goes,” correctly states reflection-to-reflection delivery without action guidance through this channel. Runtime `RT-RTE-webshop-trial` and memory `MEM-OBJ-webshop-memory` make the same distinction. No hold is warranted for declining to preserve the historical generalization.

### 2. HotPotQA mechanism survives; historical displays are intentionally not carried as operation

Frozen `hotpotqa_runs/agents.py:74–145, 260–333` confirms strategy-specific last-attempt replacement, accumulated reflection, combined latest trace/diagnosis, and prompt consumption. CoT reset does not clear reflections; choosing NONE suppresses new reflection rather than erasing old advice. `set_qa` changes question/key without clearing retained memory. The current runtime describes NONE as a trigger condition, not a clean-memory guarantee. Memory explicitly records API-reassignment contamination and limits shipped same-question findings. Thus the historical clean-baseline caution is not contradicted by a new guarantee, although its exact wording is not repeated.

The September sampled historical diagnosis/display is not reproduced as observed-run evidence. Boundary and synthesis explicitly say historical result bodies were not examined and no target code was executed. Their diagnosis-content, causal-benefit and checkpoint-reload uncertainty is public. This is a declared evidence-layer reduction, not evidence that the old display is false or absent. The synthesis retains strategy differences, answer-key-bounded acceptance and unresolved checkpoint consumption. No consequential qualification needs adding for this selected comparison.
