### 1. Source-to-wiki architecture and consumer distinctions remain covered

The historical account's central contribution is source compilation, not an autonomous enclosing agent loop: imported sources, extraction/resolution, page plans, drafting, provenance and later retrieval. The new synthesis preserves that causal account and distinguishes app scheduling from task-bounded model calls. It also retains SQLite, compressed prose, derived indexes/vectors, cross-session accumulation, outbox ordering and secondary index effects. Independent complete read of frozen `mcp-server/index.ts` confirms four requested tools (`search_wiki`, `read_page`, `list_pages`, `wiki_stats`). HTTP tool results are not evidence of what a host agent places in its prompt. Public synthesis, “Reading and feedback have different completion boundaries,” preserves this distinction. The historical Commonplace borrowing suggestions are not required coverage in a source-first characterization; their absence is not a defect.

### 2. The new account correctly narrows historical chat-compounding claims

The historical account says chat creates a candidate using at least three pages and approval promotes it to wiki memory. The new publication separates candidate creation from ordinary successful adoption and does not merely repeat that claim.

Independent source chain:

- `app/src/app/api/chat/route.ts`, complete read: uses retrieved `pages.length >= 3`, not verified citation count; passes the caller's unchanged session and `pagesUsed` to `insertChatDraft`.
- `app/src/lib/db.ts:2236–2255`: inserts those page IDs into `page_plans.source_ids`.
- `app/src/lib/approve-plan.ts`, complete read: interprets that field as source IDs, inserts provenance within the page/outbox/FTS transaction, and catches transaction failure as `commit_failed`.
- `app/src/lib/db.ts:47–62,496–502` and `scripts/migrate.py:45–52`: foreign keys enabled; provenance inserts reference `sources(source_id)`.

Thus disjoint page/source IDs obstruct ordinary FAQ installation. This is a static branch conclusion, not an executed failure or measured failure rate. The synthesis explicitly retains coincident-ID and empty-citation manual-save exceptions and withholds end-to-end ordinary compounding. Its description, also used in the overview, mentions constrained FAQ adoption. No audit-only repair is needed.

### 3. Historical retrieval shorthand is replaced with the actual delivery conditions

The historical account describes top-five full content and later summaries. Independent complete reads of frozen `app/src/lib/retrieval.ts` and chat handler confirm index/model selection below the estimated 6000-token threshold, otherwise hybrid lexical/vector ranking; fallback to popular pages; file content capped at 8000 characters; then 2000-character top-five and 300-character lower-page prefixes in the synthesis payload. It does not retrieve the recent tail once the session grows. The public synthesis states earliest rows, clipping, fallback and possible overall prompt truncation. I did not independently re-audit Python's full prompt builder. The publication consistently withholds relevance, complete evidence and causal benefit guarantees.
