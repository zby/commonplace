## Consequential semantic findings

### 1. The newer support qualification is necessary and present

The historical account says span checks can reject unsupported claims and describes provenance-first ingest. `S/src/wiki/ingest/spancheck.py`, `extract_claims` and `check_page_spans`, actually iterate only `[^src:N]` markers and use `token_set_ratio` against source slices. `S/src/wiki/eval/ingest_eval.py`, `faithfulness`, returns 1.0 when no marked claims exist. `S/src/wiki/ingest/pipeline.py`, `locate_span`, allows fuzzy alignment; `_check_page` consumes lexical/schema issues, not semantic entailment judgments.

`P/synthesis.md`, “From a source document to a candidate page” and “Evaluation, future capacity and method change,” explicitly states those limits, including unmarked assertions, lexical rather than semantic faithfulness, and missing provider-output comparisons. This is a justified narrowing, not lost coverage. The overview description also warns that lexical checks and human admission do not establish truth or compounding benefit.

### 2. Review is an admission route, not a universal checked invariant

The historical account's “Human review is the promotion boundary” and “git commit on accept” can be read too broadly. `S/src/wiki/review.py`, `accept_page`, moves with `page_path.replace(dest)` before the audit/Git block, permits `commit=False`, and performs no full content revalidation. A same-filename destination can be replaced. These distinctions change what admission and recovery guarantee.

`P/synthesis.md`, “From staging to durable knowledge,” already states limited preview, optional editing, absent full post-edit checks, direct library acceptance, alternate curated seed admission, optional Git completion, replacement and lack of rollback. I independently checked the acceptance primitive; the seed-driver claim was checked against the report verifier's executed complete source reads, not independently reanalysed here. No missing public qualification was found.

### 3. Pull retrieval survives; universal freshness and no internal body delivery do not

The historical account correctly distinguishes ranked IDs from an answering context compiler, but loosely describes query as searching current nonsuperseded pages and says it does not push memory into a model call. `S/src/wiki/retrieve/query.py`, `retrieve`, loads live pages but queries retained indexes, fuses their IDs, and in the nonreranked branch returns IDs even when absent from the live map. The optional reranker receives `pages[pid].search_text()` for surviving candidates. Thus caller-facing pull and internal model input supply can coexist.

`P/synthesis.md`, “From retained pages to selected results,” preserves the positive implemented retrieval account while explicitly stating snapshot/live-state mismatch, dangling IDs, item rather than token budgets, optional dependency failure, internal reranker/bootstrap supply and absent external answering integration. `P/profile-verification.md` preserves the same operation-level distinction. The historical pull-only summary is not grounds to erase these qualifications.

### 4. No execution or improvement pass is inferred

The public synthesis opens with code-grounded scope and says no target code, tests or model calls were executed. Its closing section withholds deployed benefit, exercised self-improvement, semantic acceptance and a controlled negative for trace learning. Its limitations table carries missing historical raw/index artifacts, opaque providers, reviewer criteria, schema-cache visibility and external answering traces. The overview distinguishes deterministic checks from semantic certification. These public limits are sufficient for the inspected claims; this audit is not their repair location.
