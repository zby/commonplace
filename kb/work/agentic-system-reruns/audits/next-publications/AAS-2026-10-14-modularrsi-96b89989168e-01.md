### H1 — Missing public qualification: enabled review can pass without a verdict

**Source:** S, `src/harbor/agents/terminus_2_modular/self_evo/online_evo.py:1030–1155` (`review_edit`) and `:1682–1710` (caller).

- Timeout returns `_ReviewResult(passed=True, verdict="skipped", reason="review timed out (infra)")` at lines 1083–1092.
- Other exceptions likewise return `passed=True`, `verdict="skipped"` at lines 1093–1098.
- Missing structured verdict takes another pass-through branch unless its review-health breaker trips, lines 1106–1135. The comments specify three consecutive skips or over 10% of at least ten reviews; this audit did not independently recompute the helper's thresholds. Timeout/error returns precede that health-accounting path, so they must not be presented as necessarily governed by that breaker.
- The enabled-review caller gates on `review.passed`, not on an actual accepting verdict. These are source-supported possibilities, not claims that a particular historical candidate was promoted after a skip.

**Published mismatch:** P `runtime.md`, `RT-RTE-candidate-admission` selection predicate says “mechanical gates and configured review must pass.” P `synthesis.md:32` describes configured model review and rejection without exposing its fail-open behavior. The limitations table discusses gate targets and temporal coverage, not absence of a substantive review verdict. Post-review repair is a different limitation: a candidate may also proceed without any accepting review in the first place. “Under the enabled protocol” does not make this behavior clear to a reader assessing scrutiny before executable admission.

**Required resolution:** qualify the admission record and public synthesis to distinguish a passed Boolean gate from substantive review acceptance, and distinguish timeout/error skips from missing-structured-verdict breaker handling. Preserve the narrower smoke/sanity and non-performance qualifications already present.

### H2 — Public account treats dormant partial-file acceptance as operative

**Source:** same file, `_ReviewResult` at `:974–984`, `review_edit` return at `:1137–1155`, and caller at `:1712–1741`.

The producer explicitly says file-level partial accept is disabled because dropping files with stale supersession metadata could retire the wrong parent. The caller retains an `if review.drop_files:` branch and `_apply_review_drops` helper, but this inspected producer does not supply flagged files to that branch.

**Published mismatch:** P `synthesis.md:32` says review can “drop files”; `workflow.md:50` says the reviewer can “partially accept files”; `runtime.md`'s admission record describes review partial acceptance dropping files and rerunning smoke. `report-verification.md` repeats inspection of “partial drops” without distinguishing dormant caller code from live producer behavior. This is not merely an omitted mechanism: it positively overstates a current admission/recovery capability.

**Required resolution:** withdraw active partial-file-acceptance claims or explicitly characterize the dormant caller/helper separately from current whole-candidate review. Any proposed alternate active producer must be located in this frozen source and connected to the actual caller; it cannot be inferred from the branch's existence.
