### 1. Model-aware publication: correction, not lost capability

The historical review says integration resolves a target model before writing that target's archetype, implying effective model-specific guard filtering at delivery. The new synthesis says integration calculates filtered counts but writes saved narratives unchanged; explicit target-model synthesis is the filtering path.

Direct inspection of `src/synapptic/integrate.py` confirms this distinction. `full` and `filtered` affect `excluded_count`; `combine_archetypes(global_archetype, project_archetype, slug)` supplies the unchanged text to each writer. Searches in `synthesize.py` and `cli.py` separately locate the `target_model` argument propagated into synthesis and its narrative filter. Public qualifications in synthesis, “Admission, retention and publication have different force,” and its freshness/recovery limitation are necessary and present. The newer account should not preserve the historical implication.

### 2. Benchmark attribution: stronger public qualifications are warranted

The historical account foregrounds individual guard testing and calls the path reviewed-by-benchmark prompt policy. The new synthesis distinguishes per-guard heuristic removal from default whole-archetype batch treatment, and refuses isolated efficacy or real coding benefit.

Direct inspection of `benchmark.py:1178-1302` confirms whole-profile WITH versus empty-profile WITHOUT prompts, 300-character respondent snippets in batch judging, separate raw trace writes and compaction of missing verdict positions. CLI search locates the batched dispatch at `cli.py:722-727`. These support the public restrictions rather than a claim that every benchmark label isolates an individual guard. The synthesis also explicitly carries the report verifier's omitted `benchmarks/traces` retention surface in its limitations table. This omission is therefore a recorded public inventory limit, not an audit-only repair.

### 3. Session-end maintenance is not automatic refreshed delivery

The historical review emphasizes the session-close learning loop and automatic maintenance. The new synthesis retains that positive mechanism but distinguishes it from refreshed external guidance after every session.

Read the complete bundled `src/synapptic/bundle/hooks/synapptic-session-end.sh`: selected exit reasons detach `extract`, `merge`, then `synthesize`; there is no integration call or success chaining. A live PID skips the event without scheduling a retry. The public hook paragraph and stage-specific delivery qualifications preserve the actual supported update path without promising external activation. No target hook was executed.
