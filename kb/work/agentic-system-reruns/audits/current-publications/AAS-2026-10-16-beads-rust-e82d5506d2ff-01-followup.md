## Consequential historical and source challenges

The historical review covers the same owner/repository but commit
`c807e36f81881ec0a7b12d4218c5234436656201` (reported release 0.5.7), not this
run's frozen revision. It is a coverage lead, not a factual oracle or reusable
clearance. No old checkout was fetched and no old-run operational evidence was
used. Source locators below are relative to `S` at the frozen commit.

1. **MCP workflow-policy and close parity: a real revision difference.**
   Historical policy absence and mutate-then-warn closure are not true of the
   inspected new routes. `src/mcp/mod.rs:493–598` loads policy for reads and,
   under write/sync authority, refreshes it before invoking the mutation closure.
   `src/mcp/tools.rs:2543–2665` rejects blockers/children and evaluated close
   violations before `update_issue`, using a default non-bypass update.
   `P/runtime.md#rt-rte-mcp-request` and `#rt-rte-close-issue` accurately describe
   this stronger implementation; synthesis retains path/bypass/import limits.
   This is not proof of universal interface equivalence. MCP claim-specific
   parity was not independently established and is not promised publicly.

2. **Claiming is conditional, not a distributed or authenticated reservation.**
   `src/storage/sqlite.rs:7865–7904,8270–8318` rechecks eligibility and assignment,
   permits conditional same-actor reclaim, and compares captured assignment,
   status and defer values in the update. This supports the new account's
   refinement of the historical unassigned-claim description.
   Plain status updates do not carry the `expect_unassigned` guarantee.
   Runtime, workflow and synthesis keep selection, claim and external work
   distinct; no host execution or globally exclusive Git-branch claim is implied.

3. **Ancestor delivery and instruction drift remain material and public.**
   `src/inheritance.rs:104–201` selects at most root/parent blocks, skips empty
   content and emits provenance. `src/cli/commands/update.rs:568–669` emits these
   only in the nonstructured, nonquiet output branch; failure after update is
   nonfatal. Structured-show attachment is visible at `show.rs:262–286,323`.
   The synthesis explicitly excludes a universal structured-update/MCP delivery
   guarantee, bounds blocks rather than tokens, and distinguishes delivery from
   compliance. The historical mismatch is not lost.
   `.claude/skills/br/SKILL.md:157–175` still says sync is never automatic;
   `create.rs:241,342–351` attempts automatic export and reports its failure.
   The public synthesis calls out that contradiction and separates export from
   external Git publication.

4. **Gate reports license admission, not task truth.**
   `src/storage/sqlite.rs:4671–4721` selects history by issue/transition/revision,
   folds ascending reports by case-folded gate/provider, and
   `src/close_policy.rs:1475–1503` accepts any passing provider for a named gate.
   Thus a later same-provider fail displaces its pass, while another provider's
   pass can still satisfy the gate. The new epistemic records and synthesis
   preserve these countercases and provider-authentication limits. They are
   stronger than merely recording a Boolean, not independent correctness review.

5. **Some historical auxiliary detail is not reproduced, without a false
   completeness claim.** Scheduler `NoMailSnapshot`/`Ambiguous` stale treatment
   persists at `scheduler.rs:518–613`; missing Mail evidence is explicitly not
   abandonment proof. The new account records fixed ranking but does not fully
   analyze this scoring policy. The optional MCP cache remains file-witnessed
   (`mcp/mod.rs:380–430`, now including policy); graph-health stale counts depend
   on the clock (`mcp/resources.rs:916–953`). These older concerns must not be
   assumed fixed. They do not contradict a published freshness or ranking
   equivalence guarantee: none is made. The public limitations explicitly
   withhold complete endpoint/evaluator coverage and ranking equivalence.
   This bounded loss of detail does not defeat the useful issue-memory account.

6. **Profile omissions were disclosed, not silently repaired by verification.**
   `src/cli/commands/agents.rs:1300–1397` constructs replacement guidance, skips
   confirmation with force and writes the existing instruction artifact. It
   supports automatic force-branch admission and revision. The profile does not
   separately inventory these two mechanisms. Profile verification records both
   limits; the public synthesis carries each as its own limitation row and
   withholds mechanism-complete comparisons. The partial axes already contain
   other supported automatic-write/revision findings. This is a recorded local
   comparison limit, not grounds for withdrawing the positive system-level
   findings or requiring source reconstruction.

The overview is a navigation/validation landing, not the full explanation; it
links synthesis and verifications. Necessary substantive qualifications are
already in the public synthesis, not supplied solely by this audit.
