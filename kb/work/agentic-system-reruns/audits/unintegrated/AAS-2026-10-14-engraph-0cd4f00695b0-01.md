## Discrepancy-led findings

The historical `kb/agent-memory-systems/reviews/engraph.md` was read as a coverage
lead, not as authority. It uses the **same source commit**. Its coarse claims
about indexed writes, move learning and pull-only read-back therefore warranted
checking rather than explanation by revision drift.

1. **Move adaptation is retained, with stronger eligibility qualifications.**
   The old review says it found no qualifying trace extraction, while describing
   operator-move feedback. The new synthesis explicitly retains move-fed
   selector adaptation, not session mining or online weight training. Independent
   source inspection of `src/placement.rs:232–341` confirms centroid consumption,
   positive below-threshold suggestion generation and the metadata/creator
   guard; `src/watcher.rs:482–611` confirms vector averaging, conditional centroid
   adjustment, logging and metadata stripping. The published synthesis names
   recognized labels (`claude-code`, `cli`, `mcp-server`, excluding `http-api`),
   explicit-folder/type-rule bypasses, proxy-feedback uncertainty and absence of
   measured benefit. The classification change is not a demonstrated regression
   or contradiction: its operative event-to-selector mechanism is visible.
   Public aggregate-key/count and global-reset limits are preserved in synthesis
   and reconciliation; this audit did not independently rederive the entire
   centroid lifecycle.
2. **The broad indexed-write account is properly narrowed publicly.**
   `src/writer.rs:1021–1060` rewrites disk and stored mtime without rebuilding
   chunks; `src/serve.rs:711–729` records the successful rewrite for watcher
   suppression. The final report verifier's paired source read of
   `src/watcher.rs:279–423` independently returned the matching-mtime skip.
   The synthesis explicitly warns against assuming ordinary event delivery
   repairs edit/rewrite search state, and distinguishes later disk reads from
   indexed selection. No hidden uniform-freshness promise was found in the
   overview, synthesis or final verification. This is a useful correction of the
   historical shorthand, not an omission to repair in this audit.
3. **Health and context do not silently regain stronger guarantees.**
   Independent `src/health.rs:60–79` inspection confirms the stale detector
   returns an empty vector. The public synthesis discloses the stub, index-relative
   diagnostics and potentially retained unresolved-link derivatives. It also
   distinguishes requested note delivery from automatic internal selector-state
   supply. Scoped searches/read-back passages in `memory.md` and
   `memory-profile.md` retain that distinction; internal push does not claim
   unsolicited vault text enters a host conversation. The historical pull-only
   shorthand is not preserved as an exhaustive boundary claim.
4. **Public qualification carriage is adequate for this acceptance.**
   Fully read overview, synthesis, boundary, reconciliation, report verification
   and synthesis verification. The overview describes bounded truth/freshness/
   improvement and explicitly says deterministic checks are not semantic
   certification. The synthesis carries both settled report-verification limits
   in prose and its limitations table, along with unexecuted target/model/client
   limits, identity lineage/freshness risks and incomplete supporting-path coverage.
   Those qualifications are public, not repairs supplied only here.
