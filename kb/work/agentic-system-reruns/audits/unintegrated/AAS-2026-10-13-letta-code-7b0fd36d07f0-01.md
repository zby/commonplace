## Findings and replacement comparison

Read both public overviews and syntheses, candidate boundary and report
verification. Followed consequential delivery and confinement claims into S;
used retained verification limits to bound rather than repeat the analysis.

1. **Local update horizon is correctly qualified.** S
   `src/backend/local/local-backend.ts:896–975` stores the new revision and
   core-memory metadata but retains the old compiled content, returning a
   transient update. `src/backend/dev/pi-stream-adapter.ts:594–604` adds it to
   the request-local message array. P synthesis mechanism 2 states the resulting
   one-request horizon and need for full compilation. The incumbent mentions
   the update without this horizon. This is a concrete reason to prefer the
   candidate for local-memory decisions, not evidence of observed failure.
2. **Confinement is not presented as unconditional.** S
   `src/agent/subagents/sandbox.ts:88–115` returns null on opt-out or missing
   backend; `src/agent/subagents/manager.ts:223` forces unrestricted mode.
   P synthesis explicitly carries best-effort confinement, shell bypass and
   post-approval rewrite qualifications. It also separates default api-mode
   server behavior from inspected local behavior and denies observed benefit.
3. **Known internal limits are public.** P synthesis carries the superseded
   memory-worker owner conflict, crashed-worker recompilation exception,
   shared-skill lineage gap, partial authority inventory and understated wired
   skill invalidation. These prevent an exhaustive profile reading; they are
   not silently promoted to complete comparisons by this audit.
4. **Replacement is not an unqualified coverage improvement.** The incumbent
   characterizes listener effect checkpoints, scheduled ingress, App Server
   authentication and ordinary Task notification-to-parent continuation. The
   candidate concentrates its representative workflow on reflection and does
   not retain that same runtime detail. Its stronger memory-delivery and
   isolation qualifications support considering replacement, but not claiming
   a strict coverage superset. No exhaustive alternate-route audit was made.
