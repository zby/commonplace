## Consequential findings

1. **Core coverage is retained, with stronger qualifications.** Both accounts
   describe external collector/editor/scorer responsibility, strict score
   selection, metadata-only diagnosis, twelve-turn review history, limited
   checkpoint identity and separate skill utilities. Frozen `training.py`
   confirms direct callback handoff and `candidate_score > baseline_score`;
   rejection preserves the reference but cannot undo mutation. The new synthesis
   states the comparability/isolation conditions and absence of held-out benefit.
   No consequential regression was found in these historical themes.

2. **The synthetic workflow does not demonstrate review-driven repair.** Frozen
   `examples/quickstart.py::edit` ignores specification, failures and groups;
   `collect` assigns reward from `revision >= 1`. The synthesis leads with this
   limit and distinguishes source-supported branches from observed success.
   This improves on the older review's more general lack-of-experiment warning.
   The overview description also withholds real editing, generalization and
   review-attributable improvement.

3. **The numeric exceptional-input limit is real and publicly carried.** Frozen
   `evolution.py::_collect_trajectories` excludes missing rewards and rewards
   greater than/equal to the threshold, without enforcing finite rewards.
   NaN can therefore enter failure review. `mean_trajectory_score` also lacks a
   finite-value check. `report-verification.md#limits` records this; the synthesis
   qualifies the ordinary extraction account and its first limitation row.
   This is not a hold-worthy qualification present only in this audit.

4. **The reflection emphasis changed without hiding the underlying route.** The
   archived review emphasizes checkpoint progress controlling later review calls.
   The new account emphasizes adapter-mediated harness modification and withholds
   exercised semantic change or revision of Ecdysis's own review method.
   `runtime.md#rt-rte-checkpoint-recovery` and the synthesis still preserve the
   progress-control route: saved `next_turn_index` skips completed turns;
   completed state returns cached advice. Frozen `fdcr/review.py` confirms both.
   This is a changed assessment emphasis, not an omitted recovery mechanism or
   evidence of reflective theory building.
