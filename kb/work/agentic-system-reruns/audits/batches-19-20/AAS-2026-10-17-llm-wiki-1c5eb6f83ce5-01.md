Archive lines and Dashboard at maintenance/status consumers” into a known unit

reporting is not evidence of “automatic learning authority.” No `learning` value
appears anywhere on that axis. The downstream ranking finding is not a substitute
for the omitted update-consumer finding. Partial uncertainty about external L1
payloads cannot erase a locally supported positive. The omission hides the
system's distinctive use-feedback mechanism from structured comparisons.

missing positive. An audit-only explanation cannot repair the structured output.
