A case-insensitive search of all published members for deprecation and first-party/new-deployment wording found none. `P/overview.md` correctly links the members and disclaims semantic certification; neither its description nor the linked public synthesis carries this missing status.

**Why material:** The source's intended-use boundary changes the practical interpretation of the host-integration account. The publication is not a product recommendation, and deprecation does not erase its implemented architecture. Nevertheless, presenting that architecture without its source-native legacy applicability omits a consequential fact about the system being characterized. The generic statement that README release/security history was uninspected does not communicate the known, explicit new-deployment restriction. This is a small local source-account/public-qualification repair, not a reason to discard the trace-to-memory findings or relabel the whole boundary as inaccessible.
