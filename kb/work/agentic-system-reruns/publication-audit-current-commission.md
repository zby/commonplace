## Audit and root-depth judgment

Use [audit-a-rerun](./audit-a-rerun.md), frozen source/method boundaries and fresh

Read prior instructions/contracts needed for the report; explicit scope here
prevails over the older fixed-intake batch-18 exclusion for completed results.

Each new audit chooses integrate, integrate with recorded limits, or hold. A
positive result needs its necessary qualifications already public. Bind the
current recursive file set to the completed effect and assembled manifest;
verify source/method identity and material discrepancies against frozen source.
Read the historical leads, distinguish revision/scope differences and inspect
operational traces where needed. Do not infer worker compliance from acceptance.
Validate the report and publication with the exact prepared commands. Report
unavailable sources/traces, comparison limits and deterministic notices honestly.

For every new hold, additionally trace the earliest evidenced faulty output and
the verification barrier that should have caught it. Inspect relevant attempts,
version pins, source conditions and pinned missions. Classify:

- **Deep:** boundary or source/analyst records need reconstruction; choose an
  actual author job at or before the faulty inputs. Record separate fault origins.
- **Local rework:** accepted source records remain useful; the error originates
  in classification, verification assessment or public synthesis/limit carriage.
  Name the verification restart and the author correction job separately.
- **Operational/authority:** unauthorized action, ownership or collateral effects
  require disposition; a fresh content run cannot erase the history.
- **Evidence or integration stop:** missing binding, uncertain effects or
  canonical-incumbent transfer stop; do not invent semantic depth or clear it.

A case can have multiple categories. A shallow classification issue does not
make an independent source-report defect shallow. Missing evidence remains a
bounded gap. Stop before substantial new analysis or an expanded source boundary.
