# Batch 19: where the held errors start

## Decision

Prepare **seven recovery runs**, with Supermemory first. Do not launch them yet.
The other sixteen held candidates are excluded from this batch: eleven have
canonical-incumbent transfer stops, four have operational-only holds, and Agent
Skills has a reserved-owner/compliance stop in addition to its content error.
The [batch ledger](./batch-19.json) records the exact selected jobs and inputs.

The depth investigation covers **23 held runs**, not the separate 33 unaudited
publications or batch 18. Nineteen have content or mixed holds. Seventeen of
those nineteen have at least one fault already in the boundary or analyst
reports. AREX-Skill's faulty assessment appears in verification and becomes
explicit in synthesis; GBrain loses supported conditions in synthesis itself.
Consequently, restarting only synthesis verification was generally too shallow
for these recorded holds. This is a selected problem set, not a measured
workflow failure rate; several historical candidates inspect identical sources.

All four isolated reports were read completely by the parent. Parent recursive
hashing independently confirmed every one of the 23 current publications matches
its completed publication effect, with no open attempt in these runs. Twenty-two
maps also equal the immutable intake; Supermemory instead equals the renewed
publication bound by its re-entry audit. These are current observations, not
complete worker-compliance certification or a lock against later changes.

not by editing accepted files. A verification restart can request author
corrections; an author restart deliberately reconstructs the faulty account.
The selected depth below follows the latter for source/report errors, except
AREX, whose retained source records support re-judgment without rebuilding them.

A fresh canonical boundary-identity scan found no incumbent for these seven
sources in the current 135-set corpus. The reset closures were calculated
read-only from their actual plans and judgments. Source acquisition and run/
method/source identities remain. ContextPilot and Autogenesis must regenerate
the boundary document, not silently retain its faulty narrative; no refetch or
new source boundary is needed. Their renewed scope and qualifications require
independent comparison rather than an assumption that all boundary prose stayed
unchanged. AREX's earlier assessment scope is partly ambiguous; this selection
asks the appropriate verifier to resolve it, not to install a predetermined
positive classification.

The reports and this selection are recovery planning evidence. They must not be
passed into the ordinary no-feedback analytical handoffs. A later author may
independently retain or narrow claims; only a fresh exact-byte audit can determine
whether the known defects were resolved. No hold is cleared by selection.
