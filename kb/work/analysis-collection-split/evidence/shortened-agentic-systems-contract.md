# Writing conventions for kb/agentic-systems/

## Purpose and quality

This collection explains external agentic systems and harnesses, including
memory and knowledge as whole-system lenses. Its contribution is an evidence-grounded
account of source-native operation, bounded synthesis and explicit limitations.
The quality goal is fidelity and economy. Explain native operation before using
Commonplace concepts; qualify mappings and preserve evidence limits. A reader
must be able to reject a mapping without losing the operational account.

## Placement and maintenance

The root holds README.md, COLLECTION.md and `instructions/`, `types/`, `reports/`.
`reviews/` holds ordinary accounts and generated reviews; `comparisons/` holds
cross-system writing and tables. `instructions/` owns the research method,
shared analysis contracts, landscape synthesis and taxonomy maintenance. General
procedures, including the Commonplace transfer scan, remain in `kb/instructions/`.
Edit canonical instructions; projections provide checkout skill discovery.
Research skills are not promoted to every initialized KB. No nested collections.

`reports/state/` is ignored working state, excluded from ordinary validation;
selected state/outputs receive explicit checks. Workers write only assigned
outputs; code owns acceptance, copies and cleanup. Each run keeps its opening
method commit; earlier runs finish there or remain recovery evidence.

`reports/retained/` holds frozen accepted sets. Other research evidence uses
names distinct from run IDs. Substantive corrections require a new run.
`reports/retained-archive/` preserves historical bytes/contracts and pins, excluded
from current-schema sweeps and comparisons. Only an explicitly authorized layout
migration may change paths, types, required links and derived hashes; preserve
findings, sources, record/run identities and historical method/synthesis provenance,
and retain a verified old/new hash map.

Complete runs publish workflow-owned generated reviews; publication is mandatory.
Do not hand-edit them. Correct inputs/method and rerun. Unmarked authored accounts
remain author-maintained. The generated-review, run-state and set types govern
publication identity, membership, byte pins and recovery checks. Retain accepted
members unchanged; the memory member's comparison profile is authoritative.
Trials supply no separate semantic clearance. Comparison readers verify retained
pins and keep code-grounded and doc-grounded populations separate.

## Authoring

Open authored accounts with one line stating the evidence basis and capture date.
Typed members use their type's evidence format. Descriptive titles name the
system/feature; argumentative accounts use claim-shaped titles and `title-as-claim`.
Member descriptions follow their type; ordinary descriptions identify subject
and scope. Worker fragments keep the job's headings. Instructions use imperative
titles and trigger descriptions; their quality goal is executability and precision.

Before assessing theory pathways, read the Theory account in the
[shared record contract](../../../agentic-system-analyses/instructions/agentic-analysis-records.md).
It supplies independently evidenced conditions and separate learning,
persistence, addressability and reflection assessments; analytical jobs already
receive it. Before editing instructions, shared contracts or local types, follow
[method maintenance](../drafts/method-maintenance.md).

Durable analyses exclude current Commonplace differences, adoption ideas and
watch items: those belong in separately commissioned transfer state under
`kb/reports/state/agentic-system-transfer/`, retained until disposition and then
replaced/deleted by its workflow. Do not feed transfer state into stable analyses
or public comparisons. Transferable theory belongs in `kb/notes/`, raw captures
in source snapshots, Commonplace descriptions in `kb/reference/`, unfinished work
in `kb/work/`.

## Types and links

Use global types under `types/` or local types under `agentic-systems/types/`.
Frontmatter-free Markdown is text.

- Sources: tracked ingests, never snapshots; `derived-from`, `evidenced-by`, `see-also`.
- External: evidence used, preferably version-pinned; do not prospect the open web; `evidenced-by`, `see-also`.
- Notes: theory mappings; `rests-on`, `defined-in`, rare `is-evidence-for`, `see-also`.
- Agent-memory systems: related subsystem accounts; `contains`, `part-of`, `compares-with`, `see-also`.
- Local retained analyses: supporting overview/member/record; `see-also`.
- Reports: separately retained evidence; `evidenced-by`, `see-also`.
- Reference: direct Commonplace analogues; `see-also`.
- Instructions: directly mapped procedures; `procedure`, `see-also`.
- Method links: composition/conditions/targets; `composition`, `precondition`, `invokes`, `applies-when`, `operates-on`, `rests-on`, `see-also`. Reviews, archives and workshops are not method execution inputs.
