## What the counts establish

The automatic-writing-and-push query now has two supported matches in the
selected population of three: Mem0 and Dynamic Cheatsheet. This is a lower-bound
support count, not a claim that the third system lacks either mechanism, nor
that both mechanisms always occur on one route.

Mem0's automatic writing and internal push count at wired basis while manual
writing and application pull remain afforded. Those mixed complete profiles
stay excluded from strong complete-profile statistics. Dynamic Cheatsheet also
supplies strong positives under partial coverage; opaque provider state does
not erase inspected local mechanisms. Its partial profiles stay excluded from
complete-set distributions even where each listed member has strong evidence.

All three faithfulness-tested assessments are not-determinable. None becomes a
negative or a demonstrated benefit. Entropy remains available for eligible
complete sets. With three rows, the analyzer's minimum of five paired rows
prevents an informative redundancy result; its zero fallback is not evidence
of independence. Document-grounded separation is fixture-tested, not yet tested
on a real document-only member of this pilot.

The [new synthesis trial](./per-value-synthesis-trial.md) uses the same frozen
population and full canonical records. It records its own query and semantic
checks. The [first acceptance record](./comparison-acceptance.md),
[first ledger](./query-ledger.md) and [first synthesis](./synthesis-trial.md)
remain historical. Full-corpus public comparisons and earlier public syntheses
remain stale/historical; this bounded trial does not replace them.
