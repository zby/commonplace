## Reconstructed governing result

The stable method is to analyse **routes**, not assign one epistemic label or oracle to a whole system. Start with the object and transformation, then trace each consequential check: what it targets, when it runs, what results it can produce, what implemented force consumes the result, what truth-apt content the evidence warrants, and what later behavior the result permits. Finish with separate route-level statements about acquisition, reshaping, derivation, conjecture, testing, acceptance, integration, adaptation, and use. [Inference from Workshop framing, ARC case, Typed target, Warrant granularity, Behavioral authority, Brief]

This method answers two questions that must not be collapsed: whether a route creates new candidate content, and whether any route earns warrant for that content over a named scope. Novel language, a stored synthesis, a grade, a successful outcome, or later use answers neither question by itself. [Brief; AI Research OS case; Outcome/process; Warrant granularity; Review system]

### Why the six cases support this method

| Case | Route-level lesson for the instruction | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| ScienceFlow | A task-metric evaluator can select an anchor and feed it into later iterations while the retained state exposes no claim or hypothesis object. Task success and operative retention therefore do not by themselves constitute explanation or claim production. | [Four-case baseline] |
| Ontology draft | Measurement can accept attestations while hypotheses or explanation remain represented but unscored and non-authoritative. Its operator's later lab-tooling intent makes this a possibly deliberate scope choice, not automatically a defect. | [Four-case baseline; Operator response] |
| Eigenius | Formal, type, certificate, or proof checks can differ by commit route. Successful formal checking does not automatically warrant source-content truth, encoding fidelity, or explanatory quality; an epistemic grade can bound later reliance only where consumers enforce it. | [Four-case baseline; Warrant granularity] |
| Commonplace | Structural validation and verdict-kind gates have different targets and force from open-ended critique. A fresh baseline records applicability of evidence to snapshots, not endorsement, global approval, or handling of findings. | [Four-case baseline; Review system] |
| AI Research OS | A system can generate and universally retain fluent syntheses yet expose no reject-capable content-acceptance transition. Labels and citations represent provenance or status, but do not contain authority when the production loop consumes the content without a semantic gate. | [AI Research OS case; Proposal loop] |
| ARC | One system can contain prediction admission, consequence grading, replay, search eligibility, plan applicability, queue continuation, and task completion routes with different targets, timing, and force. Epistemic authority and operational authority must therefore be reported separately per route. | [ARC case] |

The first five cases suggested a system-level participation/containment taxonomy; ARC falsified its adequacy unchanged. The durable part is the question asked per route, not the old cells or response names. [Workshop framing; AI Research OS case; ARC case]

## Definitions and distinctions to frontload

The eventual instruction must define these terms inline. An executor should not need to load the theory notes or workshop. [Instruction collection; Instruction type; Brief]

### Analysis units

- **DEFINE: epistemic architecture.** Use this as a comparison plane: the system's routes for acquiring or producing truth-apt content, checking it, granting or withholding reliance, retaining or integrating it, and letting it affect later behavior. It is not a membership class; a system may have a degenerate architecture with no claim object or acceptance route. [Inference from Workshop framing and Brief]
- **DEFINE: epistemic object.** The typed thing whose content or status a route produces, checks, accepts, or consumes. Inventory concrete system objects rather than assuming one object called “the model.” Candidate examples include observation, source claim, derived claim, conjecture, explanation, executable model, plan, task outcome, and scorecard; a system may require other object types. [Brief; ARC case; Typed target]
- **DEFINE: truth-apt content.** Content for which the analysis can state what would make it true or false over a named scope. A file, plan, model, grade, or scorecard may contain several truth-apt and non-truth-apt operative parts, so classify the part or route rather than the container. [Inference from Warrant granularity, Typed target, ARC case]
- **DEFINE: target before oracle.** First name the object or claim class and the proposition the check assesses. Only then name the oracle and its domain. A check over an undeclared heterogeneous target can return a result without supplying reusable warrant. A singleton target is allowed when no reusable class is available. [Typed target]

### Transformation classes

- **Acquisition/import** brings external material or a source claim into the system. It may preserve or lose source warrant, but it does not make the system the originator of the imported claim. In the current memory-review contract, acquisition is distinct from curation even when extraction is sophisticated. [Memory review type; Brief]
- **Non-ampliative reshaping** changes representation, order, indexing, size, or grouping without adding truth-apt content beyond what the inputs and declared consumer goal already supply. Summarization/rollup (`consolidate`), deduplication, indexing, and faithful reformatting are candidates; actual classification depends on the output. [Discovery lifecycle; Memory review type]
- **Entailed derivation** makes an unstated consequence explicit when it follows from the supplied premises and declared transformation. It is not an ampliative discovery-lifecycle instance. Warrant can pass through entailment only from warranted premises and only within the derivation's interpretation/formal domain. [Discovery lifecycle; Warrant granularity]
- **Ampliative conjecture** asserts a candidate claim, rule, mechanism, explanation, or general type not entailed by the evidence in hand. The memory-review operation `synthesize` detects this generation boundary—an output asserts something no input stated—but that label establishes only candidate generation, not truth or acceptance. [Discovery lifecycle; Memory review type; Brief]
- **Behavior or policy adaptation** changes future behavior in response to reward, error, viability, gradient, or another signal without necessarily representing a rejectable truth-apt candidate. Treat it as an operational update unless a separate epistemic object and warrant route are evidenced. Proposal-selection is one architecture, not a universal requirement for adaptive improvement. [Proposal loop; Brief]
- **Decision test between reshaping and derivation.** If the output merely restates or compresses the same content, classify reshaping. If it states a new explicit proposition entailed by warranted inputs, classify derivation. If the proposition is not entailed, classify it as conjecture regardless of how fluent or plausible it is. [Inference from Discovery lifecycle, Warrant granularity, Memory review type]

### Warrant, lifecycle, and authority

- **DEFINE: epistemic warrant.** Support that licenses reliance on specific truth-apt content over a specific domain. Keep it at the finest claim, conjunction, integrated model, and scope the evidence identifies. Comparative success of a bundle does not distribute to its components without entailment or attribution evidence. [Warrant granularity; Experimental contrast]
- **DEFINE: discovery lifecycle.** Apply the six phases only to an ampliative conjecture: observation/anomaly, conjecture, consequence derivation, test/accumulation, acceptance, and integration. Revision returns to conjecture; suspension waits; rejection closes the candidate; later counterevidence opens a new instance. Do not force imported, reshaped, or entailed outputs through this lifecycle. [Discovery lifecycle]
- **Testing** compares stated consequences with cases and produces evidence. **Acceptance** is the consuming workflow's recorded decision that the evidence meets a named criterion for the claim's intended use, boundary, and maintenance regime. **Integration** reconnects evidence or changes how the accepted content is organized or used. None supplies a universal threshold for every domain. [Discovery lifecycle]
- **DEFINE: oracle/check.** The human, model, program, environment, proof, measurement, or hybrid procedure that supplies evidence or judgment about a declared target. Checker reliability/strength and check target are independent: a deterministic outcome test can still say nothing about the producing process. [Outcome/process; Proposal loop; Brief]
- **DEFINE: operative result.** A grade, label, report, or check result is operative on a route only when a consumer uses it to change admission, rejection, revision, acceptance, retention, integration, rollback, use, ranking, or continued execution. Recording it is insufficient. [ARC case; Proposal loop; Behavioral authority; Brief]
- **DEFINE: authority route.** A checked target plus its oracle, timing, implemented force, epistemic authority, and operational authority. One object may traverse several routes and one check may have epistemic meaning without continuation force, or continuation force without broad epistemic warrant. [ARC case]
- **Epistemic authority** states exactly what truth-apt content may be relied on as warranted and over what scope. **Operational authority** states what later behavior an artifact/result permits before another check. **Behavioral authority** records the consumer, channel, and force by which retained material becomes consequential. [ARC case; Behavioral authority]
- **Applicability/freshness is not endorsement.** A provenance or freshness check can establish that an artifact still matches current inputs without warranting its content. Completion is not an outcome; an outcome is not global approval; fresh evidence is not handled evidence. [ARC case; Review system]

### Checks that must remain separate

- **Outcome check:** whether the final result met its target. A pass can warrant retaining the episode as a case-level demonstration, but not the claimed producing process, explanation, safe replay, or transfer. [Outcome/process]
- **Process check:** whether the submitted route was valid and faithfully represents what produced the outcome. A reconstructed valid route is not evidence that it was the producing route. [Outcome/process]
- **Explanation warrant:** whether evidence tests the proposed mechanism and its transfer boundary, rather than merely an observable consequence compatible with several explanations. [Outcome/process; Warrant granularity]
- **Component attribution:** whether the observed contrast independently varied the named component. A benchmark or bundle outcome supports at most the configured bundle and observed contrast; detailed measurements do not create missing component counterfactuals. [Experimental contrast; ARC case]
- **Operational continuation:** whether an action, queue, plan, search, or later consumer may proceed. Strong continuation control does not expand the truth-apt content a check warrants. [ARC case; Warrant granularity]

### Evidence layers

For every material finding, label the strongest evidence actually available rather than letting stronger-sounding doctrine upgrade it. [Workshop framing; Memory review type; Memory review skill; Brief]

- **Code-enforced behavior:** an inspected implementation requires, records, rejects, rolls back, or consumes something. This supports mechanism/capability claims at the reviewed revision, not necessarily deployed compliance or effect. [Memory review type; Memory review skill; ARC case]
- **Natural-language doctrine/design:** an instruction, README, schema, or design says what should happen. It supports declared intent or contract, not execution. [Memory review skill; ARC case]
- **Reported operation/outcome:** authors or operators report a run, quantity, behavior, or purpose without supplied run evidence. Preserve the report attribution. [Workshop framing; Operator response; ARC case]
- **Observed run evidence:** inspectable events, traces, or artifacts show that a route ran in the observed case. This still supports only that route, case, and observation horizon. [ARC case; Outcome/process; Warrant granularity]
- **Causal experiment evidence:** a comparison varies a treatment against an observed control. Attribute effects no more finely than the actual contrast and treatment grain. [Experimental contrast]

## Minimum executable procedure

1. **Fix the question, scope, and source boundary.** Record the external system, revision/version, claims being assessed, available source classes, missing evidence, and whether the analysis concerns a memory subsystem, whole agentic system, or a named route. Do not blend code, doctrine, reports, runs, and experiments. [Brief; Workshop framing; Memory review skill]
2. **Inventory epistemic objects before assessing checks.** List the behavior-shaping and truth-apt operative parts the system imports, produces, checks, retains, or consumes. Split bundled containers when parts have different forms, checks, or authority paths. Record system-specific objects rather than forcing the brief's examples. [Typed target; ARC case; Memory review type]
3. **Take the short exit when warranted.** If the inventory shows only storage, retrieval, serving, or direct operational use and no relevant transformation or knowledge-production claim, report that scoped negative result, the inspected evidence, and any evidence gap; do not fabricate an oracle analysis. If the system claims knowledge production despite the absent route, continue only far enough to record the claim/implementation mismatch. [Brief; ScienceFlow lesson in Four-case baseline; AI Research OS case; Typed target]
4. **Classify each transformation.** For each output, decide whether the route acquires/imports, non-ampliatively reshapes, entails/derives, ampliatively conjectures, or directly adapts behavior/policy. State the input-to-output relation that justifies the classification. [Brief; Discovery lifecycle; Memory review type; Proposal loop]
5. **Build the authority-route ledger.** Use one row per consequential check or transition. If one object receives several checks or forces, use several rows. Record an absent check explicitly when the absence controls the conclusion. [ARC case; Brief]
6. **Dispose every candidate truth-apt output.** For imported/reshaped/derived content, state its lineage and warrant route without forcing the discovery lifecycle. For every ampliative candidate, record each discovery-lifecycle phase as evidenced, absent, failed, suspended, or not determinable, and name any acceptance criterion, accepted scope, and integration path. Generation without an acceptance route remains conjecture production. [Discovery lifecycle; Warrant granularity; Brief]
7. **Separate each check's licenses.** State what the result warrants epistemically, what it permits operationally, and what it does not establish. Keep outcome, process, explanation, component attribution, applicability, and continuation findings distinct. [Outcome/process; Experimental contrast; Review system; ARC case]
8. **Compare system claim with implemented and observed routes.** Put the claimed knowledge operation beside the strongest implementation, run, and causal evidence. Treat deliberate lab-tooling or operational scope as a valid purpose boundary while still reporting any mismatch with broader knowledge-production framing. [Brief; Operator response; Memory review skill]
9. **Write a bounded route-level conclusion.** State, for each material route, what the system acquires, reshapes, derives, conjectures, tests, accepts, integrates, adapts, or merely uses; name the warranted object and scope. Do not calculate a system-wide epistemic score or transfer one route's oracle/status to another. [Brief; ARC case; Warrant granularity]
10. **Verify the analysis.** Check that every truth-apt candidate has a disposition, every consequential check has target/oracle/timing/force/authorities, every claim names its evidence layer, every negative result is scoped, and no outcome or label carries broader warrant than its route. [Inference from Brief, ARC case, Discovery lifecycle, Warrant granularity]

## Minimum output schema

The first draft should use readable Markdown tables or compact records, not introduce a permanent controlled-token matrix before trials establish stable values. [Brief]

### 1. Source and claim boundary

- System and reviewed revision/version.
- Analysis question and route/system scope.
- Available evidence grouped as implementation, doctrine/design, reported operation, observed run, and causal experiment.
- Missing evidence and the conclusions it prevents.
- The system's own knowledge-production claims or an explicit statement that none were found. [Brief; Workshop framing; Memory review skill]

### 2. Epistemic-object inventory

One row per operative part with these fields:

`object/part id | system name and description | representational form | source/input and lineage | producer/consumer | candidate truth-apt content or none | claimed role | evidence basis`

This inventory precedes oracle assessment and may split a single file/model into several parts or consumption paths. [Typed target; ARC case; Memory review type]

### 3. Authority-route ledger

One row per consequential check/transition with these fields:

`route id | object/candidate | input-to-output transformation | check target | oracle/evaluator | timing | possible/observed result | implemented force | epistemic authority and scope | operational authority (consumer, channel, force, horizon) | lifecycle disposition | evidence layer and source | system claim versus route | gap/limit`

“No check found” is a valid route finding when supported by the evidence boundary. Do not merge rows merely because they share a file, model, evaluator, or final task. [ARC case; Behavioral authority; Brief]

### 4. Lifecycle disposition

For each ampliative truth-apt candidate, either include fields in the ledger or add a compact record:

`observation/anomaly | conjecture | derived consequence | test/evidence | acceptance evaluator and criterion | accepted scope/status or rejection/suspension/revision | integration/retention consumer | missing phase/evidence`

For acquisition, reshaping, entailed derivation, or direct adaptation, state `discovery lifecycle not applicable` and give the applicable lineage/warrant route instead. [Discovery lifecycle; Brief]

### 5. System-claim versus route comparison

For every consequential public/design claim:

`claimed operation or warrant | doctrine/design support | implemented route | observed run support | causal support | supported conclusion | mismatch/unknown`

This comparison prevents documentation promises, repository outcomes, or benchmark success from being silently reported as implemented epistemic acceptance or component effect. [Memory review skill; ARC case; Experimental contrast]

### 6. Bounded conclusion

Return route-level sentences under these questions:

- What does the system merely retain, retrieve, reshape, or use?
- What does it acquire with preserved or degraded source warrant?
- What does it derive by entailment, from which warranted premises and within what domain?
- What ampliative candidates does it generate, and how far through the discovery lifecycle does each travel?
- What does it accept and integrate, under whose criterion and through which consumer/channel/force?
- What behavior or policy adapts without a truth-apt claim route?
- Which knowledge-production claims remain unsupported because of missing implementation, run, or causal evidence? [Brief; Discovery lifecycle; Warrant granularity; Behavioral authority]

## How the existing agent-memory review covers the problem

### Existing coverage

- **Storage and retained artifacts:** `## Artifact analysis` already requires storage substrate, representational form, lineage, and behavioral authority at the operative-part/consumption-path level, plus invalidation/regeneration and promotion paths. This supplies much of the object, lineage, form, and later-use evidence needed by the new analysis. [Memory review type]
- **Write side:** the review separates manual/automatic agency, acquisition from curation, and automatic operations over stored memory. It already distinguishes reductive `consolidate` from generative `synthesize`, where the latter asserts an insight or claim no input stated. It also records deduplication, evolution, invalidation, decay, and promotion. [Memory review type]
- **Trace-learning:** qualifying reviews identify traces, extraction/judge, raw and distilled artifacts, timing, scope, form, and behavioral authority. This can expose candidate-generation and learning routes when traces feed durable outputs. [Memory review type]
- **Read-back and operational use:** the review records pull/push direction, signals, selection scope, injection, consumer authority, and whether the system tests that fired memory changes behavior. This shows how retained material can become operationally consequential. [Memory review type; Consumption path]
- **Evidence discipline:** the type and skill distinguish code-grounded from doc-grounded review, capability from deployed behavior, doctrine from implementation, source identity/revision, and unverified quality/effective authority. The skill directs the writer to report implementation/documentation divergence. [Memory review type; Memory review skill]

### Remaining knowledge-generation gap

- The `synthesize` operation identifies that no input stated the output, but the review does not require the output to be typed as a truth-apt candidate or traced from conjecture through consequence, test, acceptance, and integration. It therefore detects novelty without determining warrant. [Memory review type; Discovery lifecycle; Inference]
- `consolidate` versus `synthesize` separates recoverable compression from generative content, but the review has no required category for an unstated yet entailed derivation. It can therefore collapse derivation into either reshaping or synthesis unless the writer volunteers the distinction. [Memory review type; Discovery lifecycle; Inference]
- Artifact-level behavioral authority and read-back authority show how content affects behavior, not what content is warranted. The type does not require epistemic authority and operational authority to be stated separately per check route. [Memory review type; Behavioral authority; ARC case; Inference]
- Trace extraction asks what oracle/judge selects signal, and read-back faithfulness asks whether retrieved memory changes behavior. Neither requires a typed check target or distinguishes outcome, process, explanation, applicability, and component-attribution warrant. [Memory review type; Typed target; Outcome/process; Experimental contrast; Inference]
- The current review does not require a lifecycle disposition for every candidate truth-apt output, a ledger naming target/oracle/timing/force, or a bounded “acquires/derives/conjectures/tests/accepts/integrates/merely uses” conclusion. [Memory review type; Brief; Inference]
- The review skill compares documentation with code and runs semantic QA, but it does not invoke a dedicated epistemic-architecture analysis or require system claims to be aligned row-by-row with implemented, observed, and causal routes. Future integration is explicitly outside this run. [Memory review skill; Brief]

The new instruction should therefore be collection-neutral and callable from an external-system analysis without changing the existing review type or skill. It may reuse evidence already gathered by a memory review, but it must add the object, transformation, lifecycle, warrant, and route analysis itself. [Brief; Inference from Memory review type and Memory review skill]

## Conflicts, limitations, and negative boundaries

- The six cases have unequal evidence. Eigenius, ScienceFlow, AI Research OS, and ARC are summarized through code-grounded workshop readings; ontology-draft claims describe an unpublished design with no observed loop; the operator statement is weak reported intent; Commonplace observations concern this installation rather than every deployment. The method may generalize, but the cases do not warrant universal empirical claims about all knowledge systems. [Workshop framing; Operator response]
- A proposal-selection loop requires search, reject-capable evaluation, and operative retention, but direct gradient-, reward-, error-, or viability-driven adaptation can improve behavior with no candidate acceptance event. The instruction must not force every adaptive system into proposal selection or misclassify a direct update as a weak conjecture loop. [Proposal loop; Brief]
- The discovery lifecycle is specific to ampliative conjectures and supplies no universal acceptance threshold. Domain-specific human, model, program, proof, measurement, environment, or hybrid criteria remain necessary. [Discovery lifecycle; Brief]
- Formal proof or replay can warrant consequences only inside the checked formalization and comparison surface. It does not automatically warrant translation fidelity, omitted premises, explanation quality, transfer, or global truth. [Warrant granularity; Four-case baseline; ARC case]
- Universal retention with later read-time selection can strongly shape what is seen without creating reject-capable epistemic acceptance. Conversely, a scoped lab tracker may intentionally avoid knowledge production. The instruction should report these architectures without treating either as a product-quality ranking. [AI Research OS case; Operator response; Brief]
- A route-level negative is bounded by inspected evidence. “No acceptance route found” is not “no informal comparison occurred,” and “no component effect identified” is not evidence of no effect. [ARC case; Experimental contrast]
- Natural-language, symbolic, and parametric outputs require different inspection methods. The supplied premises support reading text, testing symbolic artifacts, and probing parametric state in principle, but they do not establish one general method for individuating truth-apt claims inside distributed-parametric state. Report the object or lifecycle phase as not determinable when the available probe cannot identify it. [Memory review type; Warrant granularity; Inference]
- The working distinction between reshaping, entailed derivation, and ampliative conjecture depends on an interpretation of input and output content. When entailment or semantic preservation cannot be established, record the ambiguity rather than classify from surface fluency. [Discovery lifecycle; Warrant granularity; Inference]
- An acceptance decision records that evidence meets a criterion for intended use; it is not infallibility. The analysis warrants a scoped architectural description, not a philosophical proof that the accepted content is true. [Discovery lifecycle; Warrant granularity; Inference]

## Misuse guards to carry into the instruction

- Do not infer knowledge production from storage, retrieval, context injection, later use, or behavioral influence alone. [Brief; Behavioral authority; Consumption path]
- Do not infer acceptance from retention, a status label, a grade, a report, freshness, or a check result whose consumer and force are absent. [AI Research OS case; ARC case; Review system]
- Do not call a fluent synthesis knowledge merely because it asserts something novel; classify it as an ampliative candidate until its warrant route is shown. [Memory review type; Discovery lifecycle; Brief]
- Do not let an outcome pass warrant the submitted process, explanation, replay safety, transfer boundary, or component contribution. [Outcome/process; Experimental contrast]
- Do not let proof/formal validity warrant source truth or encoding fidelity outside the formal domain. [Four-case baseline; Warrant granularity]
- Do not call task completion, benchmark score, or later iteration evidence for the causal value of one component without the matching contrast. [ARC case; Experimental contrast]
- Do not assign one oracle, status, participation profile, or authority level to a heterogeneous system when its routes have different targets, timing, or force. [ARC case]
- Do not treat declared purpose as implementation evidence, or lack of theory production as failure when the system's intended job is operational tracking. [Operator response; Memory review skill]
- Do not require natural-language claims, proposal comparison, the Commonplace store, or one universal knowledge ontology. [Brief; Proposal loop]

## Explicit unresolved markers

- **DEFINE: knowledge production.** Recommended operational wording: report that a route *acquires* warranted imported content, *derives* warranted entailed content, or *produces an accepted ampliative claim* only at the granularity and scope its evidence licenses. Report acceptance and integration separately so generation, warrant, and operative accumulation cannot collapse. [Inference from Brief, Discovery lifecycle, Warrant granularity]
- **DECISION NEEDED: relation between acceptance and the headline phrase “produces knowledge” (authoring decision, non-blocking for reconstruction).** The sources clearly separate acceptance from integration. The eventual instruction should decide whether the headline phrase requires both, or whether acceptance earns warrant while integration is reported as a separate accumulation/use fact. The safer route-level output never hides either phase. [Discovery lifecycle; Brief; Inference]
- **DECISION NEEDED: lifecycle presentation (authoring decision, non-blocking).** Use either lifecycle fields inside the route ledger or a separate compact disposition record. Both must cover every ampliative truth-apt candidate; a single system-wide lifecycle verdict is not acceptable. [Brief; ARC case]
- **EVIDENCE NEEDED: first-draft trials.** The brief requires fresh executions on ARC and held-out GBrain to test whether the procedure is concise, executable without clarification, preserves evidence layers, and discriminates different architectures. Trial results may revise procedure wording but must not silently add theory. [Brief]
