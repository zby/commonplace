# Acceptance: analyse an external system's epistemic architecture

## Decision

The candidate is executable on first reading, collection-neutral, and safe to advance to normal promotion and deterministic validation without another cold execution. It keeps one practical purpose: analyse material epistemic routes in either a memory subsystem or a whole agentic system and return evidence-bounded, route-level findings.

## Trial evidence

The two cold trials establish both executability and discrimination:

- ARC completed all six outputs without clarification. It separated inspected implementation from reported campaign operation and absent run or causal evidence. Its findings exposed narrow event-prediction, history-fit, replay, and continuation routes while withholding general-mechanism and component-effect claims (`arc-trial.md` §§ 1, 3–6 and Cold-execution notes).
- GBrain completed the same outputs without clarification despite a missing runtime. It treated that failure only as missing observed-run evidence. It distinguished unchecked synthesis and retention, absent or suspended disposition routes, operational use without epistemic acceptance, and benchmark-bounded policy adaptation (`gbrain-trial.md` §§ 1, 3–6 and Cold-execution notes).
- The materially different conclusions are produced by the same object-first, route-level method. Both trials preserve doctrine, implementation, reported operation, observed run, and causal evidence as separate layers and keep negative claims inside their inspected boundaries.

## Distinction and promotion check

The candidate preserves the load-bearing separations:

- architectural capability is not an observed episode disposition;
- route function is not route availability or activation;
- truth-apt derivation or conjecture is not a non-truth-apt policy update;
- testing, evidence-consuming acceptance, retention, and post-acceptance integration are different functions;
- epistemic license, the operational transition a result changes, and the consumer/channel/force/horizon by which it shapes behavior are different fields; and
- route-level acceptance does not become a system-wide status.

The material-route cutoff is testable because an executor must apply the stated inclusion predicates, record excluded and unassessed route families, name the conclusions those omissions prevent, and withhold a system-complete conclusion when coverage is partial. The candidate contains no ARC or GBrain mechanics, workshop dependency, source narrative, outbound execution dependency, or automatic integration with the memory-review workflow. Its lone Commonplace reference is an explicit prohibition against requiring that storage model, not a collection dependency.

## Re-trial judgment

The post-trial changes are substantial in schema wording but not in analytical purpose or decision logic. They codify distinctions the cold agents already had to represent: ARC separated implemented routes from absent instances; GBrain supplied indeterminate objects, unaccepted retention, operational consumption, and non-truth-apt policy adaptation; both recorded targets, evaluators, timing, force, evidence layer, scope, and consumers. The audit repairs split and label those already-exercised facts, correct the discovery-lifecycle integration boundary, and make coverage finite. They do not add a new evidential claim, source class, system-specific branch, or governing conclusion. The two discriminating executions plus the anchored repair closure therefore suffice; another cold run would be confirmatory rather than acceptance-critical.

Verdict: PASS
