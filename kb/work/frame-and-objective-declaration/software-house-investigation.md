# Software-house investigation: handoff at closure

Two observations from the side investigation mattered to this workshop:

- **The two historical measures concerned different work.** At the time of the investigation the instrument note discussed knowledge work for human judgment spent, while the bootstrap article counted human decisions per verified improvement. The latter concerned the improvement process, not all work done with the KB. Do not treat that wording as current: the article changed in `a0818af2e`.
- **Boundary declarations differ.** The [Commonplace frame](../../reference/commonplace-declared-frame.md) names designated maintainers in established improvement roles and excludes arbitrary advisers. The [software-house](../../theory/definitions/software-house.md) and [theory-builder](../../theory/definitions/theory-builder.md) definitions locate production functions by role. An outside adviser supplying an implementation diagnosis is a useful case for deciding whether the difference matters. This workshop has not ruled on it.

The full investigation, including its evidence, reconstructions and untested conjectures, is retrievable at `c41f85d72:kb/work/frame-and-objective-declaration/software-house-investigation.md`. No conjecture was promoted by this cleanup. The [plan](./plan.md) leaves disposition to closure.
