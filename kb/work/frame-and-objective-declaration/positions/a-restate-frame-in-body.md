## Diagnosis

1. **Silent boundary shift.** The footer of
   [Commonplace as an instrument](../../../reference/commonplace-as-an-instrument.md)
   (L61) says the page assumes the declared frame, where designated maintainers
   are inside the boundary. The body never states the frame and moves the
   maintainer across it:
   - L11 treats human judgment as the denominator, the scarce resource spent on
     the instrument.
   - L17 lists "the components" as artifacts and code only, which puts the
     maintainer outside the instrument.
   - L19 cites the tag-readme trace as a composition witness, although
     [the classification](../../../theory/evidence/commonplace-as-a-reflective-system.md)
     (L50–56) shows problem selection, semantic evaluation and adoption in that
     trace were human.
2. **The boundary question recurs for a structural reason.** The human-inclusive
   boundary chosen in [the boundary cases](../../../theory/the-self-improving-system-definition-classifies-its-boundary-cases.md)
   (L23–31) keeps the definition free of exceptions and makes membership cheap.
   Once membership is cheap, every substantive claim rests on three cuts, which
   [the closure note](../../../theory/methodological-and-computational-closure-track-different-changes.md)
   (L28–64) has to declare: the outer boundary, the internal actor partition,
   and the temporal boundary between seed construction and run-time
   intervention.
3. **Three confounds stay hidden in the instrument note.**
   - *Internal partition.* "The components composed here" cannot separate what
     the machinery carried from what the maintainer bridged. The
     independent-operator test matters because it removes the original
     maintainer's tacit repair.
   - *Temporal boundary.* "Per unit of human judgment spent" does not say
     whether it counts only per-task operation or also the judgment spent
     designing and maintaining the methodology. An instrument can look cheap
     per task while hiding build and maintenance judgment in the seed.
   - *Outer boundary.* Hosted models are outside the frame, so improvement
     observed while operating the KB can come from stronger models rather than
     from retained machinery.

## Remedy

Do not turn the instrument note into a boundary-theory note. Add two or three
sentences to its body that state the human-inclusive frame, the internal
human/computational partition (human judgment is both inside the tag-readme
trace and the denominator), and that model providers are outside.

The report also offered, as an alternative, a broader look at why boundary
drawing carries so much load across the theory cluster. It did not develop that
alternative.
