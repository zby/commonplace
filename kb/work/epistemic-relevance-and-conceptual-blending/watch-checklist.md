# Watch checklist: useful threads and unsupported relationships

**Purpose:** catch and correct a possible failure during ordinary work while accumulating evidence for a better hypothesis. This is a provisional local practice, not a validated remedy or a universal requirement to separate ideas. See the [commission and review point](./README.md).

## When to use it

Use this check when a useful neighboring idea becomes a dimension, component, cause, instance, or common explanation of the current subject without a clear justification; when a broader project goal seems to change the question being answered; or when a response concedes a distinction but keeps the disputed organizing relationship.

Do not run a new literature review for each occurrence. Do not assume that an appealing idea is irrelevant or that a unified explanation is wrong. A checklist hit is a question to examine, not a diagnosis.

## Notice, check, correct, record

- [ ] **Fix the question and roles.** What was the current question? What were the two directions? How did each help answer the question or advance a broader goal? Separate task relevance, practical value, and the relationship being asserted.
- [ ] **Preserve the disputed claim.** Save the shortest sufficient pre-correction passage and a commit/path or available harness-trace pointer. Record whether the issue was noticed unprompted, through this checklist, or after operator feedback. Mark paraphrases and missing context; do not invent a verbatim trace.
- [ ] **Test the relationship, not the headings.** State the asserted relationship in one sentence and identify its warrant. Check a relevant counterexample or consequence. Ask whether a valid grouping by shared purpose was mistaken for identity, inclusion, or a common mechanism. Allow the relation to be justified or unresolved; uncertainty is not proof of error.
- [ ] **Repair only what fails.** Within the current task's authority, remove or qualify an unsupported relationship and revise dependent claims. Keep a worthwhile secondary direction under its actual purpose, or retain it as a deferred question. If the synthesis is warranted, keep it and record the reason. Resource limits can justify prioritization without conceptual conflation.
- [ ] **Check substantive uptake.** Does a consequential example, classification, later inference, or next-step choice now respect the boundary? An apology, separate heading, or statement that the concepts differ is not enough. Record persistent errors or unavailable follow-through rather than claiming durable correction.
- [ ] **Leave a small record and resume.** Add an entry to [observations](./observations.md), including alternative explanations and any checked non-error. Return to the original task. Reassess the hypothesis at the README's review point, or earlier if the case contradicts it.

## Evidence discipline

Distinguish what the artifact shows from what the mechanism might be. Two individually valuable directions and an unsupported relation are observable at the output level. That usefulness *caused* the relation, or that a reward objective caused the behavior, is not established by that observation alone.

Where relevant, distinguish an unambiguous task from a legitimately broader interpretation. Record actual task instructions rather than assuming the intended boundary was supplied. A model's explanation after criticism is another response to evaluate, not a readout of its earlier internal process.

Retain occasional checked cases where the distinction was already preserved, where synthesis was warranted, or where a suspected error was actually harmless presentation. Do not count the literature review's candidate passage as a replicated event, or successive revisions of one mistake as independent evidence. Absence of recorded cases is not evidence of absence; this is opportunistic observation, not a sampled prevalence study.

## Correction under a limited budget

A compact repair can be enough:

> The answer to Q concerns A. B is a useful separate direction for goal G. The evidence currently supports relationship R between them, not the stronger relationship S. Retain the question about B without treating S as established.

This is an illustration, not required wording. The task may instead warrant a common explanation, a shorter cross-connection, or dropping/deferring a tangent whose retention cost exceeds its likely value. Evaluate the repaired relationship and its consequences, not adherence to a response format.

## Return to the hypothesis

At the review point, ask what repeats: admission of the tangent, the particular relationship error, or resistance to changing an established organization. Preserve differences rather than manufacturing one mechanism for every case. Use the retained [competing accounts](./findings.md) and [experimental proposals](./experimental-proposals.md) only when they help discriminate the observed cases. A more substantial hypothesis should explain a bounded pattern and risk a counterexample; it need not be more general.
