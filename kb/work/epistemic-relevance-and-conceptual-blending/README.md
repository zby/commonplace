# Epistemic relevance and conceptual blending

An observational watch on a subtle failure in agent work: **silently folding a useful secondary idea into the definition, steps, or explanation of a narrower requested concept** — instead of asking for clarification or keeping it as a separate concern — and sometimes preserving that blended organization even after a correction acknowledges the distinction.

For example, in the motivating episode ([O001](./o001-operationalization-exchange.md)), the operator asked how to make a methodology more operational without making it more constraining. Extending the methodology to cover more tasks was genuinely useful to the broader project, and the revised artifact counted "enlarging coverage" as an operationalization move — even while noting elsewhere that generalization differs from operationalizing a method for a fixed task. Nothing looked fabricated, yet the resulting document blurred two distinct goals.

## What to watch for

Watch for two observable patterns:

1. **Unsupported relationship assignment (silent blending):** An agent retains two individually useful ideas, A and B, and treats B's usefulness for a broader goal as if it made B part of A's definition, steps, or explanation — rather than asking for clarification or stating B as a separate concern (*"You could also mean X; in that case, consider B"*).
2. **Persistence after partial correction:** After feedback points out the distinction, the agent acknowledges it in prose while keeping the old blended organization in subsequent claims, tables, or decisions.

**What is not the error:**

- Mentioning a productive tangent explicitly as a separate question or contrast (B can clarify A by contrast without being part of A).
- Grouping A and B at the project level because both serve a shared goal (project-level grouping is valid; relabeling it as a conceptual definition or mechanism of A requires its own warrant).
- Synthesizing two ideas when the evidence actually supports the claimed relationship.

The workshop tracks the **observable artifact error** while keeping *why* it happens (project priority, second-guessing the operator, task ambiguity, semantic association, requested answer form, or local editing inertia) open as competing explanations. The hypothesis may need narrowing, splitting, or rejection as cases accumulate.

## Working loop

This watch is local to this workshop and discoverable via the Active Workshops list. It installs no scheduler, recurring alert, global review gate, or new machinery; the operator or an agent already working with this workshop applies it during ordinary work.

When a suspected case arises during ordinary investigation, synthesis, or review:

1. Walk through the [watch checklist](./watch-checklist.md).
2. Record the case briefly in the [observation log](./observations.md), including pre-correction evidence when available. Log checked false alarms and justified syntheses alongside confirmed errors; record unknowns rather than reconstructing missing traces. Multiple revisions within one conversation count as **one episode**, not independent recurrences.
3. Fix a demonstrably unsupported claim locally within the current task's authority — without waiting for a general mechanism to be proved. Preserve valuable tangents separately rather than forcing them into the main explanation, and check whether consequential downstream uses actually change.

## Review point and closure

**When to review:** After the next **three independently arising suspected episodes**, or earlier if a counterexample or well-captured case materially changes the explanation. Three is a practical interpretation of "the next few," not an evidential threshold. Count checked non-errors as well as confirmations; no fixed return date is needed.

**What to evaluate at review:**

- Compare the actual relations across cases, why each secondary idea was useful, what made the asserted relation unwarranted, and whether correction changed later behavior.
- Distinguish competing explanations: task ambiguity, semantic association, candidate selection, requested answer form, presentation-only misplacement, and persistence of an earlier frame.
- Narrow, reformulate, split, or reject the working hypothesis around the observed evidence (including concluding that existing concepts suffice). Counts from a selectively noticed case series cannot establish prevalence or training causation.
- Decide whether any discriminating observation or authorized experiment is warranted next.

**When to close:** Close once the watch reaches a justified disposition — a supported extraction into the right library collection, a documented rejection, or an explicit operator decision to defer or stop. Follow the [workshop lifecycle](../COLLECTION.md): extract durable conclusions, remove the Active Workshops entry, and retire this directory. Do not promote a general LLM-bias theory from a handful of noticed cases.
