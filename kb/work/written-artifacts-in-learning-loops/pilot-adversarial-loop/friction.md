# Friction check: Recorded composition checks make attempted epistemic work auditable but do not establish its effects

**Reviewed snapshot:** kb/work/written-artifacts-in-learning-loops/pilot-adversarial-loop/candidate-v1.md
**Central claim (one sentence):** Recording defined inputs and task-specific traces for commit-and-expose, challenge-and-locate, and respond-and-decide makes the attempted operations and their allocation auditable, but does not by itself establish their quality, effects, or equivalence to solo composition.

## Filter
**Verdict:** SURVIVES

## Signal — thinnest joints
1. **“Yet human writing can likewise stabilize credible nonsense or false precision, so a categorical human-machine contrast is unsupported.”** — UNSUPPORTED — Showing that both human and machine writing can produce error defeats a claim that humans are categorically immune, but it does not rule out categorical differences in mechanism, propensity, or epistemic role.
2. **“Until a comparator and one measured outcome are specified, the defensible claim is only that the workflow makes attempted operations auditable and is designed to test their allocation.”** — THIN — The missing comparator blocks claims about matching or improving on solo composition, but it does not by itself reduce every defensible noncomparative claim about the workflow to auditability.
3. **“The check must discriminate rather than merely agree, and errors across nominally separate checks must be measured for correlation.”** — THIN — Correlated errors matter when checks are combined as if they supplied independent evidence, but the note does not establish that every nominally separate check is combined that way or that correlation is meaningful across checks performing different operations.
4. **“Giving each operation a defined input and a task-specific trace creates a testable allocation of work.”** — THIN — Inputs and traces expose the allocation for inspection, but testability also requires success criteria and adjudicable observations tied to each operation; those do not follow from trace creation alone.
5. **“What matters is tested checking and a substantively adjudicated disposition, not who typed or nodded at the prose.”** — THIN — The preceding reasons show that authorship and approval are insufficient proxies, not that actor characteristics are irrelevant to how well checking or disposition performs.

## For the human
Look first at the move from shared human and machine fallibility to dismissing a categorical contrast, and specify exactly which contrast the premise defeats.
