## Strongest passage — VERSION 1

> “If the loop does not in fact reconstruct the filter — if adversarial review and the maintainer's pushback catch no more than a fluent solo first pass would, or if maintainers rubber-stamp in practice however the system is built — then the defense fails.”

This is the version's strongest passage because it makes the central claim falsifiable and names both ineffective criticism and passive acceptance as failure modes.

## Most important remaining weakness — VERSION 1

“The human must stay the judge” collapses several roles whose separation is decisive: independently checking the argument, re-deriving the implicated claim or constraint, disposing of a challenge, and authorizing acceptance. The version offers no operational protection against anchoring beyond an instruction not to rubber-stamp, and it does not distinguish evidence of artifact change, human-understanding change, later-system behavior, and acceptance. Its review loop is therefore inspectable in outline but not yet evidence that the lost composition operation has been reconstructed.

## Strongest passage — VERSION 2

> “A human-understanding outcome requires evidence that the human can independently restate, update, or apply the implicated claim, constraints, route, or uncertainty; assent or copied restatement is insufficient. A later-system outcome requires relevant retrieval and changed use or decision, not merely persistence. Acceptance records admission authority and proves none of those outcomes.”

This is the strongest passage because it supplies the commission's most consequential distinctions as concrete evidence requirements rather than labels.

## Most important remaining weakness — VERSION 2

The note has no task-specific completeness criterion for **commit-and-expose**. A trace could therefore be complete as a record while remaining shallow as a statement of commitments, constraints, premises, or route. The version names this gap, but its otherwise detailed evaluation design gives no comparable test witness for that first operation, so whether the taxonomy captures enough of composition remains open.

## Hard-constraint failures

- **VERSION 1:** Fails the prohibition on claiming superiority over solo writing without comparative evidence. Its conclusion says the bet is that the distributed loop “thinks better than the solo pen,” while its own caveat and the linked material supply no disciplined loop-versus-solo comparison. Framing the assertion as a bet preserves uncertainty but does not supply the comparison the hard constraint requires.
- **VERSION 2:** None identified. Its local links resolve, its external practitioner sources are explicitly treated as reported practice rather than controlled evidence, and its title, frontmatter, and note shape satisfy the supplied contracts.
