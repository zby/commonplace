Strongest passage, VERSION 1: the evidence-tiering move in the fourth and sixth paragraphs is the note's best contribution. It separates local correction, cross-case reliability, whole-loop comparison, human-understanding outcomes, later-system outcomes, and acceptance authority without collapsing them into one success word, and it uses that separation to preserve a positive but bounded claim.

Most important remaining weakness, VERSION 1: the note's key positive step from inspectable record plus independent adjudication to a "local artifact outcome" is defensible, but it is stated a bit faster than it is argued. A reader can reconstruct why that conditional result follows, but the note could make plainer that the warrant comes from the independent adjudication plus disposition consistency, not from the practitioner essays or from record preservation alone.

Strongest passage, VERSION 2: the fourth paragraph is the strongest. That retreat makes the longer note feel undercommitted rather than better calibrated.
