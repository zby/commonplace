## Strongest passages and remaining weaknesses

**VERSION 1 — strongest passage:** “The claimed outcome must therefore be named before saying that a loop preserves, reconstructs, or recovers composition's effects.” The ensuing separation of artifact outcome, human-understanding outcome, later-system outcome, and acceptance is the most important conceptual contribution because it blocks several otherwise tempting proxy inferences.

**VERSION 1 — most important remaining weakness:** Commit-and-expose is explicitly left without a task-specific completeness criterion. That honesty is well calibrated, but it means the proposed audit trace can show that commitments were recorded without yet showing whether the operation exposed all load-bearing commitments. The note therefore supplies an evaluable design and boundaries, not evidence that composition's epistemic effects have been preserved.

**VERSION 2 — strongest passage:** “The claim is contingent on the adversarial pass having teeth; the architecture makes that available, it does not guarantee it.” This is the version's clearest recognition that an inspectable review opportunity is not equivalent to an effective check.

**VERSION 2 — most important remaining weakness:** The central inference nevertheless makes that equivalence. A contradiction surfacing in some review would be evidence for a particular correction, but merely assigning an agent to render, agents to critique, and a human to accept does not show that the loop reconstructs the composition filter. “Human stays the judge” also leaves epistemic checking, re-derivation, and admission authority bundled together.

## Hard-constraint failures

- **VERSION 1:** None identified. All local links resolve, and no material linked premise was found to carry more weight than its scope supports.
- **VERSION 2:** Ineligible on two hard constraints. First, the material title-level reconstruction claim is unsupported: the linked error-correction premise requires discriminating, sufficiently decorrelated checks, but the version neither establishes nor operationalizes those conditions; the linked inspectability premise establishes inspectability, not effective checking. Second, it claims that the loop “thinks better than the solo pen” despite explicitly supplying no comparative evidence. All local links resolve; the failure is inferential, not structural.
