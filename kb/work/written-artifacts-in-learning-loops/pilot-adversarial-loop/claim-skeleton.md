# Claim skeleton: explicit composition checks are a testable design

Ordered argument plan, not draft prose. Target body: six substantive paragraphs totaling roughly 700–790 words, plus an optional short open-question section.

## Governing claim

**The weakest supported claim is that a human-agent workflow can make three candidate operations of substantive composition explicit and auditable—commit-and-expose, challenge-and-locate, and respond-and-decide; whether an allocation performs those operations, or whether the loop preserves or reconstructs their epistemic effects, remains a testable hypothesis requiring stage-specific validation and a stated outcome comparator.**

- **Scope:** substantive KB claims whose commitment or support is still unsettled; not settled rendering, transcription, or stylistic editing.
- **Confidence:** moderate for the three-bundle synthesis and procedural feasibility; high for the evidential ceiling.
- **Evidence posture:** practitioner accounts identify candidate operations; instruction files show that attempts can be assigned and recorded; local theory supplies failure conditions. None supplies a matched comparator or validates this loop.
- **Excluded conclusions:** functional equivalence with solo composition; demonstrated error recovery; reliable critic independence; improved artifact content; changed human understanding; changed later system behaviour; superiority in quality, speed, or cost.

## Definitions that fix the claim's truth conditions

- **Candidate operation:** a synthesis of work attributed to composition in the practitioner sources. “Candidate” marks that neither the taxonomy nor its relocation has been validated.
- **Commit-and-expose:** make a definite central claim and state the relevant constraints, premises, and inferential route that could make it fail.
- **Challenge-and-locate:** test the artifact's actual commitments and route for counterexamples, missing support, or incompatibility, then identify the load-bearing issue. Fresh context and reader simulation are possible methods, not part of the operation's definition.
- **Respond-and-decide:** direct inquiry at the load-bearing issue and record a substantive disposition: rebut with support, revise, qualify, narrow, switch, reject, or retain explicit uncertainty before acceptance.
- **Auditable attempt:** a stage has a defined input and leaves a task-specific trace tied to that input. This shows that work was attempted, not that its finding was correct or its epistemic effect occurred.
- **Performance evidence:** an independent check that a stage discriminates relevant supported from unsupported cases or causes an apt disposition. A stage name, prompt, report, edit, or approval is not sufficient by itself.
- **Naive prose delegation:** treating fluent rendering and ordinary approval as if the claim and its support had already undergone these checks.
- **Checker / acceptor:** a checker assesses support; an acceptor has authority to admit the artifact. Either role may be human, agent, or policy, and the allocation requires its own warrant.
- **Preserve / reconstruct / recover:** reserved for a stated empirical comparator and outcome. They may name the open hypothesis, not the result established here.

## Ordered paragraph plan

### Paragraph 1 — Answer the governing question at the supported ceiling (about 100 words)

**Job:** state the three-bundle design claim and immediately deny the inference from visible workflow to preserved function.

- A human-agent workflow can expose attempted commitment, criticism, and response as inspectable stages and traces. **Evidence:** procedural existence in `kb/instructions/composition-friction-gate.md` and `kb/instructions/critique-note.md`; inspectability distinction in `kb/notes/inspectable-artifact-not-supervision-defeats-the-blackbox-problem.md`.
- This establishes a testable allocation, not an epistemic counterpart or successful relocation. **Evidence:** allocations must be tested in `kb/notes/human-analogies-suggest-functions-not-component-boundaries.md`; generation without verification is only output in `kb/notes/the-boundary-of-automation-is-the-boundary-of-verification.md`.

**Inferential link:** representability and inspectability are prerequisites for evaluation, not substitutes for it.

### Paragraph 2 — Define the composition filter as three compact bundles (about 135 words)

**Job:** replace the slogan and v1's over-segmented five-part list with the common operational residue of the practitioner sources.

- **Commit-and-expose:** a definite conjecture plus its reasons turns a fluid view into something that can break. **Evidence:** `kb/sources/putting-ideas-into-words.md` and `kb/sources/how-to-think-in-writing.md`.
- **Challenge-and-locate:** neutral reading, premise testing, counterexamples, and weakness finding identify where the view or explanation fails. **Evidence:** the same sources and `kb/sources/learning-by-writing.md`.
- **Respond-and-decide:** targeted inquiry leads to qualification, repair, reversal, rejection, or explicit uncertainty. **Evidence:** all three practitioner loops; false-precision boundary in `kb/sources/when-is-it-better-to-think-without-words.md`.
- Reader simulation, freshness, and outside criticism are methods. Idea generation, stabilization, and changed understanding are adjacent effects or outcomes. No source makes this taxonomy canonical or universal.

**Inferential link:** the sources justify a compact design vocabulary, not a measured or complete model of composition.

### Paragraph 3 — Specify the minimum auditable candidate loop (about 125 words)

**Job:** give maintainers an implementable test object without crediting it with success.

- Record the pre-acceptance central claim, constraints, premises, and route.
- Give a separately run checker that exact object; require premise-linked objections, counterexamples, and explicit uncertainty rather than a global grade.
- Require an acceptor to map every load-bearing finding to a disposition: supported rebuttal, further inquiry, revision, narrowing, switch, rejection, or unresolved marker.
- Preserve the input, report, and disposition so later evaluation can compare what changed.
- Do not infer actor allocation from the solo-writer analogy. **Evidence:** allocation remains open in `kb/notes/human-analogies-suggest-functions-not-component-boundaries.md`; report-only routing is shown by both instruction files.

**Inferential link:** this trace makes attempts and handoffs auditable; it still does not show that the checker found real failures or that the disposition was apt.

### Paragraph 4 — State what would turn an attempted check into performance evidence (about 135 words)

**Job:** attack the stage/report shortcut directly and turn relocation into a falsifiable hypothesis.

- Evaluate critics on known supported and unsupported prose arguments, including true conclusions reached through invalid routes. **Evidence:** route-substitution failure and proposed practical test in `kb/notes/reasoning-production-is-not-reasoning-evaluation.md`.
- Measure discrimination rather than agreement or aggregate acceptance: TPR must exceed FPR for the targeted error class. Measure error correlations across purportedly separate checks. **Evidence:** `kb/notes/error-correction-works-above-chance-oracles-with-decorrelated-checks.md`.
- Test whether accurate findings receive substantive dispositions, not merely edits or acknowledgements.
- A fresh runner, different prompt, or report shape is a decorrelation intervention; none proves independence. The supplied sources contain no prose calibration or before/after case.

**Inferential link:** without stage-specific validation, “counterpart” merely renames the intended function and begs the question.

### Paragraph 5 — Explain why authorship and approval are poor proxies (about 120 words)

**Job:** retain the delegation failure mechanism without replacing it with a categorical human/machine claim.

- Fluent generation may hide an unmet constraint, but the proposed relaxation mechanism is explicitly conjectural. **Evidence:** `kb/notes/llm-generation-relaxes-goals-where-human-writing-stalls.md`.
- Reviewing finished prose can invite anchoring and passive assent. **Evidence:** practitioner argument and example in `kb/sources/why-almost-never-use-ai-to-write-anything-substantive.md`.
- Human writing can also stabilize credible nonsense and false precision. **Evidence:** `kb/sources/when-is-it-better-to-think-without-words.md`.
- Human approval therefore shows neither route checking nor re-derivation. It may establish acceptance authority only; a checker can be human or agent if its performance is independently warranted.

**Inferential link:** evaluate the process by tested work and dispositions, not by who typed or who nodded at the prose.

### Paragraph 6 — Separate outcomes and state the usable rule (about 120 words)

**Job:** prevent acceptance, text improvement, human learning, and system learning from collapsing into “preservation.”

- **Artifact outcome:** requires independently adjudicated corrections, better-supported boundaries, or appropriate rejection.
- **Human-understanding outcome:** requires the human to restate or update the implicated claim, constraints, route, or uncertainty; assent to wording is insufficient.
- **Later-system outcome:** requires relevant retrieval and changed use or decision, not merely persistence. **Evidence:** inspectability/activation/verification distinctions in `kb/notes/vibe-noting.md`.
- **Acceptance:** records admission authority and proves none of the other outcomes.
- Until one outcome and comparator are named and measured, say that a workflow **makes attempted operations auditable** or **is designed to test them**. Do not say it preserves, reconstructs, or recovers composition's functions.

**Inferential link:** the defensible output of the evidence is an evaluation rule and experimental design, not a recovery result.

## Required inferential chain

1. The practitioner accounts share three bundles of work; therefore composition can be analyzed into candidate operations without treating writing as indivisible.
2. Those operations can be represented as stage inputs, task-specific traces, and dispositions; therefore a distributed workflow can make attempted performance observable.
3. Inspectability does not establish verification, and functional allocation must be tested; therefore observability does not establish a counterpart.
4. Discrimination, actual-route checking, decorrelation, and apt disposition are not demonstrated; therefore relocation remains an empirical hypothesis.
5. No comparator or outcome is supplied; therefore “preserve,” “reconstruct,” and “recover” cannot state the result.
6. Acceptance authority and the three substantive outcomes differ; therefore human presence cannot repair the evidential gap by itself.

## Classified unresolved markers

### Resolved for drafting

- **DEFINE — core:** use the three bundles above; state that they are a compact synthesis rather than a canonical taxonomy.
- **DEFINE — counterpart:** do not use it for a named stage or report. Distinguish auditable attempt from validated performance.
- **DECISION — governing verbs:** remove positive “preserve,” “reconstruct,” and “recover”; retain them only in the open empirical question.
- **DECISION — claim strength:** publish the design/evaluation rule, not an architectural-existence or error-recovery result.

### Publishable uncertainty

- **EVIDENCE NEEDED — critic performance:** prose-review TPR, FPR, actual-route discrimination, and error correlations are unknown.
- **EVIDENCE NEEDED — disposition quality:** no supplied case shows that a routed finding caused an apt revision, narrowing, rejection, or uncertainty marker.
- **EVIDENCE NEEDED — comparative outcome:** no matched solo/naive/adversarial comparison supports preservation, equivalence, or superiority.
- **EVIDENCE NEEDED — anchoring:** it is unknown whether pre-draft commitment records or fresh critics reduce passive assent.
- **EVIDENCE NEEDED — human and system outcomes:** artifact changes do not establish human re-derivation or later behavioural transfer.
- **SYNTHESIS LIMIT — filter boundary:** the three bundles are the compact common residue of these practitioner accounts, not evidence that every composition function is included.

### Omit at this claim strength

- Exact critic repetition counts, stopping thresholds, or an oracle-amplification implementation.
- A mandatory human checker or a fixed human/agent role allocation.
- A survey of every benefit attributed to writing, current-model prevalence claims, or general AI-authorship advice.

No blocking marker remains.

## Optional short open-question section for the eventual note

- Can blinded prose critics distinguish invalid routes from valid arguments at useful discrimination and correlation levels?
- Does recording commitments before generated prose is shown reduce anchoring in the acceptor?
- Which measured outcome, if any, reaches a solo-composition baseline under a distributed allocation?
