Preference: VERSION 2

The governing difference is that VERSION 1 treats an available review loop as if it had already reconstructed composition's epistemic effect, whereas VERSION 2 claims only what the record establishes: attempted operations and handoffs become auditable, while their effectiveness remains to be tested. VERSION 2 operationalizes the composition filter as commit-and-expose, challenge-and-locate, and respond-and-decide; separates checker from acceptor; and names the distinct evidence required for artifact quality, human understanding, later-system behaviour, and acceptance. VERSION 1 is more memorable, but its leap from “a contradiction or weakness surfaces” to “the filter has been reconstructed,” followed by a claim that the loop thinks better than solo writing, outruns both its mechanism and its evidence.

Strongest passage — VERSION 1: “Rubber-stamp the agent's fluent output and the loop collapses into exactly the careless workflow he condemns.” The surrounding paragraph usefully distinguishes an inspectable review architecture from effective human judgment and makes passive assent a condition that can defeat the claim.

Most important remaining weakness — VERSION 1: The central “reconstruction” claim is not tied to a measured outcome. Detecting some weakness in a draft does not by itself show that the loop reproduced composition's commitment, criticism, revision, or human-learning effects, and the note never supplies the discrimination or decorrelation evidence its own linked premise requires.

Strongest passage — VERSION 2: The final main paragraph distinguishes artifact outcome, human-understanding outcome, later-system outcome, and acceptance, culminating in “Acceptance records admission authority and proves none of those outcomes.” This is the commission's most important distinction and makes the note directly usable in workflow evaluation.

Most important remaining weakness — VERSION 2: Commit-and-expose still lacks a task-specific completeness criterion, as the note acknowledges. A trace can therefore be complete relative to an incomplete set of commitments, constraints, premises, or inferential steps; the design does not yet say how to detect that omission. The competence floor for whoever performs that check could also be stated more directly.

Hard-constraint failure: VERSION 1 fails the prohibition on superiority claims without comparative evidence. Its final paragraph says the bet is that the loop “thinks better than the solo pen,” while the next section concedes that no comparison establishes that its checks catch more than a fluent solo pass. This also leaves the positive reconstruction claim materially under-grounded. VERSION 2 has no hard-constraint failure found; its local links resolve and its frontmatter, title, and note shape satisfy the supplied contracts.

Extra length: Yes. VERSION 2's added length buys three named contributions: an operational decomposition of the composition filter, an evaluable protocol for critic discrimination/correlation and challenge disposition, and the four-way separation of artifact change, human understanding, later-system behaviour, and acceptance. Its calibration paragraph is dense, but the added material is argumentative and test-defining rather than generic padding.
