# Friction check: A recorded composition loop can establish a local correction without validating the method

**Note:** kb/work/written-artifacts-in-learning-loops/pilot-adversarial-loop/candidate.md
**Central claim (one sentence):** A recorded composition loop can justify a local artifact-level correction when a preserved challenge and response are independently adjudicated, without thereby validating the loop as a reliable, causal, or solo-equivalent writing method.

## Filter
**Verdict:** SURVIVES

The claim's target properties can coexist. The note commits to a narrow artifact-level result: a preserved fault report plus independent adjudication can show that one claim was corrected, narrowed, or rejected in a specific case. It separately denies that the same trace establishes cross-case reliability, equivalence to solo composition, or stage-level causation. Those are compatible commitments because the note treats inspectable local correction as weaker evidence than method validation.

## Signal — thinnest joints
1. **“Across them, writing exposes a commitment, locates a weakness in it or its support, and revises or discards it. That sequence motivates the contract's three recorded handoffs”** — UNSUPPORTED — The cited practice accounts support iterative writing and criticism in a broad sense, but the note does not show that they warrant this exact three-stage decomposition rather than some other partition or a looser family resemblance.
2. **“Checker and acceptor remain distinct roles; either may be filled by a human, agent, or policy when that allocation has its own warrant.”** — THIN — The note establishes that the roles should be distinguished in the record, but it does not establish that all three actor types can satisfy the competence and independence requirements. The needed warrant is deferred rather than supplied.
3. **“Tests should include true conclusions reached through invalid routes, because producing a sound route is not the same as evaluating the route presented.”** — THIN — The conclusion is plausible for calibrating route evaluation, but the note does not show that this fixture type is necessary rather than merely useful, or that omitting it would materially misstate critic reliability.
4. **“A human-understanding outcome needs an independent probe of whether the human can restate, update, or apply the implicated claim, route, or uncertainty; assent or copied restatement is insufficient.”** — THIN — The note draws a sensible distinction between assent and understanding, but it does not justify why copied restatement never counts as evidence of understanding or which probe would separate rehearsal from genuine uptake.
5. **“A later-system outcome requires relevant retrieval and changed use or decision, not merely persistence.”** — THIN — This is a workable measurement boundary, but the note states it more as a requirement than as an argued inference. It does not establish why persistence plus later availability cannot count toward the outcome being named.

## For the human
Look first at the jump from heterogeneous writing-practice sources to the note's exact three-handoff contract; that is the place where the author's missing concretization is still doing the most work.
