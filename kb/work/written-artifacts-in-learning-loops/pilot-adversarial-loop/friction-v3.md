# Friction check: Recorded composition checks make attempted operations auditable but do not establish their effects

**Note:** kb/work/written-artifacts-in-learning-loops/pilot-adversarial-loop/candidate.md
**Central claim (one sentence):** Recording a defined three-operation composition loop and its task-specific handoffs makes the attempted operations inspectable, but those records, authorship, and approval cannot establish whether the loop works or reproduces an effect of composition without outcome-matched evidence.

## Filter
**Verdict:** SURVIVES
Auditability of attempts and agnosticism about effects can coexist: the trace witnesses which inputs, challenges, and dispositions were recorded, while the note explicitly withholds any inference from that record to the correctness of a check, the justification of a disposition, or a downstream effect.

## Signal — thinnest joints
1. **“A blinded comparison on a named outcome can test the whole loop”** — THIN — Naming the outcome and blinding an assessor can reduce ambiguity and some bias, but those features alone do not specify the comparator, allocation or confounding control, and measurement rule needed to attribute a difference to the whole loop. The closing sentence adds a named comparator, but the causal test still needs concretization.
2. **“A human-understanding outcome requires the human to restate or update the implicated claim, constraints, route, or uncertainty”** — THIN — This rules out assent as sufficient evidence, but the note does not establish restatement or update as a necessary measure of understanding rather than one candidate measure; task use could display understanding without restatement, while a restatement could be copied without understanding.
3. **“A defined input and preserved task-specific trace expose each stage and handoff” → the workflow can “make three candidate operations auditable”** — HOLDS — The later loop names the pre-acceptance record, challenge report, and per-challenge disposition, and the conclusion is limited to inspecting attempted operations and handoffs rather than auditing their completeness, correctness, or effects.
4. **“These three operations are a compact synthesis of practitioner accounts”** — HOLDS — The linked accounts supply definite claims and premises, challenge through rereading or counterexample, and revision, qualification, or switching; the note stays within that support by calling the bundles candidate operations rather than a complete or validated taxonomy.
5. **“Preserving the input, report, and status or disposition record makes attempts and handoffs inspectable. It does not establish that the checker found a real fault or that the disposition was justified.”** — HOLDS — Preservation makes the stated objects available for inspection, whereas the truth of a finding and the warrant for a response require independent adjudication; the reason supports exactly the bounded conclusion drawn.

## For the human
Look first at what experimental design “a blinded comparison” commits to; the note needs the comparator, allocation, and measurement conditions if that phrase is meant to support causal evidence for the whole loop.
