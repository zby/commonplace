# Blind comparison result

## Outcome

**The source-directed candidate wins, 3–0.** The ordering was balanced as closely as possible for three judges: the incumbent appeared first twice, and the candidate appeared first once. Every judge preferred the candidate after the hidden mapping was revealed.

The candidate satisfies every pre-registered decision condition:

- three of three judges prefer it;
- no judge found a material grounding or hard-constraint failure in it;
- all three judges found a hard-constraint failure in the incumbent's unsupported solo-writing superiority claim;
- the final semantic audit has no WARN or material blocker;
- the source-grounded acceptance review is `PASS`; and
- all three judges said the additional context buys named argumentative contributions.

This result selects a promotion candidate. It does not authorize promotion, replacement, or rename.

## Context cost

The candidate is 1,054 words; the incumbent is 765 words. The cost is 289 words, or about 38%.

All three judges found that cost justified. They named the same purchases in slightly different groupings:

- a three-operation account of commitment, challenge, and disposition;
- a preserved trace that makes attempts and handoffs inspectable without certifying them;
- discrimination, adjudication, and correlation conditions for testing critics;
- separation of checker roles from acceptance authority and actor identity; and
- distinct evidence requirements for artifact change, human understanding, later-system behaviour, and acceptance.

The candidate is not uniformly easier prose. Judges called its evaluation paragraph dense, while the incumbent remained more memorable. The candidate won because the added material was load-bearing and made its title safe to cite, not because it was shorter or more vivid.

## Shared remaining weakness

Every judge identified the same unresolved design gap: **commit-and-expose has no task-specific completeness criterion**. A trace can faithfully record the commitments and route it contains while omitting a load-bearing commitment that was never exposed. The candidate states this gap rather than hiding it, so it does not block the auditability claim or this comparison. It does block any later claim that the operation is complete or that the loop preserves composition's full epistemic effect.

One judge also routed the competence floor of the checker for more explicit treatment. The candidate currently leaves actor choice empirical and requires performance testing; it does not claim that every possible checker is competent.

## Decision

Under the pre-registered rule, `candidate.md` is the experiment's winner. The incumbent remains the library artifact until the user chooses whether to replace it in place, rename the note to match the narrower title, extract a different durable artifact, or decline promotion.
