# Claim skeleton v1: distributed composition functions

Ordered argument plan; not draft prose.

## 1. Governing claim and claim envelope

**Governing claim (weakest supported):** For substantive KB work whose claim is not already settled, the core operations of the writing-is-thinking filter are separable enough to be allocated across a human-agent process: a designed loop can make definite commitment, route and constraint exposure, outside-route testing, failure localization, and claim revision/narrowing/rejection explicit stages. This is an architectural existence claim about operation coverage, not evidence that a present loop performs those operations effectively or preserves the outcomes of solo composition.

- **Scope:** substantive, unsettled claims; excludes settled rendering and purely stylistic editing.
- **Confidence:** moderate.
- **Evidential basis:** inference synthesized in `reconstruction.md` from practitioner accounts of commitment, criticism, and revision; the allocation principle in `kb/notes/human-analogies-suggest-functions-not-component-boundaries.md`; and procedural witnesses in `kb/instructions/composition-friction-gate.md` and `kb/instructions/critique-note.md`. The procedures establish specifiability/implementability, not efficacy.
- **Excluded conclusions:** functional equivalence; completeness across all composition functions; comparison or superiority versus solo writing; demonstrated error recovery; improved artifact content; changed human understanding; changed later system behaviour.

### Rival claims considered

1. **A disciplined human-agent loop preserves all epistemic functions of solo composition, or outperforms solo writing.** Stronger than the governing claim and unsupported: the reconstruction reports no controlled or matched comparison and no evidence that one loop covers every function. **Scope:** any comparative/full-equivalence claim. **Confidence in rejection:** high. **Basis:** explicit evidence limit in `reconstruction.md` and the brief's prohibition on unsupported comparison.
2. **A loop with fresh critics recovers some errors that naive delegation misses.** Stronger empirical claim. It is a plausible hypothesis only if checks discriminate, have sufficiently different failure modes, trace the actual route, and lead to correction or rejection; none of those properties is calibrated for prose here. **Scope:** present human-agent prose review. **Confidence:** low. **Basis:** conditional discrimination/decorrelation argument in `kb/notes/error-correction-works-above-chance-oracles-with-decorrelated-checks.md`, transferred route-checking argument in `kb/notes/reasoning-production-is-not-reasoning-evaluation.md`, and absence of outcome evidence.
3. **Inspectable generated prose plus human approval already preserves the filter.** Asks less of the process but infers too much: inspectability only makes criticism possible, and approval may be anchored passive assent. **Scope:** ordinary review of fluent drafts. **Confidence in rejection:** moderate-high. **Basis:** `kb/notes/inspectable-artifact-not-supervision-defeats-the-blackbox-problem.md`, `kb/notes/vibe-noting.md`, and practitioner evidence in `kb/sources/why-almost-never-use-ai-to-write-anything-substantive.md`.

## 2. Definitions and comparisons needed for truth conditions

- **Substantive, unsettled work:** composition is still searching for a tenable commitment; contrast with rendering an already settled structure. **Scope:** boundary of the claim. **Confidence:** moderate. **Basis:** local conjectural mechanism in `kb/notes/llm-generation-relaxes-goals-where-human-writing-stalls.md`.
- **Naive prose delegation:** an agent renders an idea, outline, or notes; fluency plus ordinary review is treated as if the underlying commitments had been worked through. The omission is epistemic operation coverage, not human keystrokes. **Scope:** contrast class. **Confidence:** moderate. **Basis:** the same local mechanism plus practitioner evidence about passive assent.
- **Writing-is-thinking filter (operational core):** (1) commit to a sufficiently definite claim; (2) expose constraints, premises, and inferential route; (3) test that actual route from outside its originating intention, including counterexamples and missing support; (4) localize failures; (5) revise, narrow, switch, or reject before acceptance. **Scope:** accept/reject-relevant core, not every benefit of writing. **Confidence:** moderate. **Basis:** synthesis of `kb/sources/putting-ideas-into-words.md`, `kb/sources/how-to-think-in-writing.md`, `kb/sources/learning-by-writing.md`, and the Filter/Signal procedure; no source gives this exact five-part definition.
- **Adjacent functions:** inquiry routing, reader simulation, idea generation, stabilization/relay, and changed human understanding may arise around the filter but are not asserted to follow from operation coverage. **Scope:** boundary of the core definition. **Confidence:** moderate-low. **Basis:** functions are separately described in the practitioner sources and `kb/sources/when-is-it-better-to-think-without-words.md`; the boundary is a synthesis choice.
- **Instantiate a counterpart:** a stage has an identifiable input, performs the named operation, and leaves an inspectable output/disposition for the next stage. A prompt bearing the operation's name is not enough. **Scope:** truth condition for the governing architectural claim. **Confidence:** moderate-low. **Basis:** inference from the procedural witnesses and the inspectability/verification distinction; not directly validated.
- **Preserve:** avoid unqualified use. In the governing claim it can mean only explicit coverage of a defined operation subset, not functional equivalence, equal reliability, or equal cognitive effect. **Scope:** terminology discipline. **Confidence:** high as a stipulation. **Basis:** reconstruction's unresolved-definition analysis and lack of comparative evidence.
- **Actual-route checking versus conclusion agreement:** the checker must inspect the artifact's own premises and transitions; producing a good independent reason for the same conclusion does not test that route. **Scope:** route-testing stage. **Confidence:** moderate. **Basis:** math-grounded production/evaluation distinction transferred to prose in `kb/notes/reasoning-production-is-not-reasoning-evaluation.md`.
- **Inspectability versus effective criticism:** readable and revisable output enables checking; it does not show independence, discrimination, correction, or soundness. **Scope:** all loop artifacts. **Confidence:** high for the logical distinction, low for any implied effect size. **Basis:** local inspectability, verification-boundary, and vibe-noting syntheses.
- **Epistemic checker versus acceptor:** checking discriminates support; acceptance authority admits a KB mutation. Either role may be allocated separately, and human analogy does not fix the component boundary. **Scope:** role design. **Confidence:** moderate-high. **Basis:** allocation note, inspectability note, and attestation semantics as reconstructed from `kb/types/note.md`.
- **Outcome comparison:** improved artifact content, changed human understanding, changed later system behaviour, and acceptance are four non-interchangeable outcomes. **Scope:** any evaluation claim. **Confidence:** high for the distinction. **Basis:** explicit outcome table in `reconstruction.md`; no supplied evidence demonstrates the first three for this loop.

## 3. Ordered paragraph plan

### Paragraph 1 — State the architectural claim and its ceiling

**Work:** put the composable claim and mechanism first; prevent “can be allocated” from being read as “has been preserved empirically.”

- **Assertion 1.1:** the five core operations can be represented as distinct stages across a human-agent process. **Scope:** substantive unsettled KB writing. **Confidence:** moderate. **Basis:** practitioner operation accounts + allocation theory + procedural witnesses; inferential synthesis.
- **Assertion 1.2:** the supplied evidence does not establish effective execution, equivalence, superiority, or any downstream outcome. **Scope:** this evidence set. **Confidence:** high. **Basis:** reconstruction's evidence audit and missing controlled comparison.

### Paragraph 2 — Define the filter by work performed

**Work:** replace the slogan “writing is thinking” with the five operations and separate the core accept/reject cycle from adjacent benefits.

- **Assertion 2.1:** definite commitment makes a view criticizable; route expansion gives criticism load-bearing targets; localization plus revision/rejection closes the filter. **Scope:** proposed operational model of epistemic composition. **Confidence:** moderate. **Basis:** detailed practitioner/process evidence in the three composition sources; synthesis, not prevalence evidence.
- **Assertion 2.2:** inquiry routing, reader simulation, idea generation, and stabilization are separable and must not be assumed from one generic critique. **Scope:** wider composition loop. **Confidence:** moderate. **Basis:** functions distributed across practitioner sources; reconstruction explicitly finds no one-check coverage evidence.

### Paragraph 3 — Derive why role relocation is possible

**Work:** carry the argument from functional decomposition to architectural allocation without inferring efficacy.

- **Assertion 3.1:** an epistemic operation is identified here by the work and inspectable disposition it produces, not by whether a human typed the prose. **Scope:** process architecture, not cognitive equivalence. **Confidence:** moderate. **Basis:** allocation principle and inspectable-artifact theory.
- **Assertion 3.2:** existing fresh-critic/report-only procedures witness that commitment, joint enumeration, criticism, routing, and acceptance can be assigned to different stages or actors. **Scope:** implementable procedure shape. **Confidence:** high for procedural existence, low for epistemic success. **Basis:** `kb/instructions/composition-friction-gate.md` and `kb/instructions/critique-note.md` as normative/procedural witnesses.

### Paragraph 4 — Specify the candidate loop and closure condition

**Work:** show the minimum ordered path that would count as instantiating counterparts, while labelling its conditions as candidate necessary/enabling rather than sufficient.

- **Assertion 4.1:** minimum path: definite claim/constraints -> inspectable premises and route -> separately generated route/counterexample checks -> localized issue report -> adjudicated revision, narrowing, switch, rejection, or explicit unresolved marker. **Scope:** architectural witness for the five-operation filter. **Confidence:** moderate-low. **Basis:** synthesis of practitioner loops, procedural witnesses, and reconstruction's candidate conditions.
- **Assertion 4.2:** a critique that cannot change or reject the claim does not close the filter; it is only commentary on a fixed conclusion. **Scope:** filter instantiation. **Confidence:** moderate. **Basis:** practitioner accounts of correction, reversal, and discarded ideas.

### Paragraph 5 — Contrast the loop with naive delegation

**Work:** explain the relevant failure without making a categorical human/machine claim.

- **Assertion 5.1:** fluent rendering can leave constraints or inferential commitments unresolved while making the result look finished. **Scope:** documented possibility, not frequency or human/agent comparison. **Confidence:** moderate-low. **Basis:** conjectural constraint-relaxation mechanism plus practitioner cases of subtly wrong generated wording.
- **Assertion 5.2:** inspectability and human presence do not establish checking; anchoring and passive assent can substitute approval for re-derivation. **Scope:** review of generated drafts. **Confidence:** moderate. **Basis:** practitioner/conceptual anchoring evidence plus local inspectability/verification distinctions.
- **Assertion 5.3:** humans can also force false precision, so authorship is not the diagnostic variable. **Scope:** rejection of a categorical human/machine contrast. **Confidence:** moderate-low. **Basis:** practitioner synthesis in `kb/sources/when-is-it-better-to-think-without-words.md`; no comparative rate evidence.

### Paragraph 6 — Mark what an empirical recovery claim would additionally require

**Work:** keep the stronger hypothesis visible without smuggling it into the conclusion.

- **Assertion 6.1:** evidence of error recovery would require route-focused checks with TPR > FPR, sufficiently altered/counted failure correlations, verifier competence/budget, localized routing, and actual correction or rejection. **Scope:** conditions for an empirical preservation claim. **Confidence:** high as conditional logic, low that current prose critics satisfy it. **Basis:** indirect formal discrimination/decorrelation theory, verification-boundary theory, and transferred route-checking result.
- **Assertion 6.2:** fresh context or a different critic is a decorrelation attempt, not evidence of independence; no prose-oracle rates or correlations are supplied. **Scope:** present procedure. **Confidence:** high. **Basis:** procedural design contrasted with reconstruction's calibration gap.

### Paragraph 7 — Separate roles and outcomes

**Work:** prevent human approval, human learning, artifact repair, and later system learning from collapsing into one result.

- **Assertion 7.1:** epistemic checking and acceptance authority may be held by different humans, agents, or policies; the loop needs an explicit acceptor but human analogy alone does not determine who checks. **Scope:** KB governance architecture. **Confidence:** moderate-high. **Basis:** allocation principle, inspectability theory, and note-attestation semantics.
- **Assertion 7.2:** if the claimed outcome is changed human understanding, the human must be able to restate/update the implicated commitments and route; assent to wording is insufficient. **Scope:** truth condition for human-learning claims. **Confidence:** moderate as an inference. **Basis:** passive-assent evidence, constraint re-derivation theory, and actual-route checking distinction.
- **Assertion 7.3:** artifact improvement and later behavioural transfer each require separate evidence; acceptance proves neither. **Scope:** outcome evaluation. **Confidence:** high. **Basis:** explicit reconstruction outcome distinctions and `kb/notes/vibe-noting.md`'s activation/verification limits.

### Paragraph 8 — Restate the usable design rule and uncertainty

**Work:** conclude at the existential level and give maintainers a diagnostic without implying measured success.

- **Assertion 8.1:** evaluate a delegated writing process by whether it explicitly covers and closes the five operations, not by prose authorship or reviewer presence. **Scope:** design/evaluation heuristic. **Confidence:** moderate. **Basis:** inferential consequence of Paragraphs 2–7.
- **Assertion 8.2:** operation coverage licenses testing the loop; it does not license claims of preserved efficacy, superior text, human learning, or later system behaviour. **Scope:** claim ceiling. **Confidence:** high. **Basis:** absence of comparative/outcome evidence.

## 4. Required inferential links

1. Practitioner accounts distinguish commitment, route expansion, criticism, localization, revision/rejection, and stabilization; therefore “composition” is not one indivisible act. **Status:** moderate-confidence synthesis.
2. If an operation is specified by its epistemic work and inspectable disposition, its identity does not logically require the original writer to perform it. **Status:** moderate-confidence functional inference; avoid cognitive-equivalence language.
3. Procedural witnesses allocate candidate operations to separate stages/actors; therefore distributed instantiation is architecturally possible. **Status:** moderate-confidence existence inference; weakest joint is whether procedural labels amount to real operation performance.
4. Effective error recovery additionally depends on discriminative, route-focused, sufficiently decorrelated checks and closure through revision/rejection. **Status:** strong conditional, unverified antecedents for prose.
5. Those antecedents and outcomes are not demonstrated; therefore stop at architectural operation coverage and publish the empirical questions. **Status:** high-confidence evidential-limit inference.

## 5. Highest-impact weak joints, ranked

1. **Counterpart criterion may be too thin or circular.** A named stage and inspectable report may mimic an operation without performing its epistemic work. If no non-outcome-based criterion can distinguish them, narrow the governing claim from “instantiate counterparts” to “make candidate operations explicit in a procedure.” **Likelihood of changing governing claim:** high. **Basis:** procedures are normative witnesses only; no efficacy validation.
2. **“Preserve” changes meaning with the comparison class.** Operation coverage, detection of at least one missed error, and functional equivalence are different claims. If readers require an empirical comparator for “preserve,” remove that verb entirely. **Likelihood:** high. **Basis:** reconstruction's definition gap and absent comparison.
3. **Critic discrimination and dependence are unknown.** If route checks are non-discriminatory or correlated with generation, even partial error-recovery language fails; the architectural claim survives only after dropping “recover.” **Likelihood:** medium-high. **Basis:** indirect formal conditions; no prose calibration.
4. **Closure is specified but not witnessed as successful.** Critiques can be produced without correct adjudication, claim change, or rejection. This may force “candidate loop” language for the revision stage. **Likelihood:** medium. **Basis:** procedures report problems but provide no downstream outcome evidence.
5. **The five-operation core is synthesized from practitioner accounts.** Different filter boundaries could move inquiry routing, reader simulation, or stabilization into/out of the core; this should change coverage claims, not revive full-equivalence language. **Likelihood:** medium-low. **Basis:** no source supplies the exact definition or universal prevalence claim.

## 6. Unresolved-marker classification

### Blocking — resolved in this skeleton

- **DEFINE — reconstruct/preserve:** resolved to explicit coverage of the five-operation subset; no equivalence or outcome meaning.
- **DEFINE — filter boundary:** resolved to the five accept/reject-relevant operations; inquiry routing, reader simulation, idea generation, and stabilization remain adjacent.
- **DECISION NEEDED — final claim strength:** resolved to the existential architectural claim required by the brief; the empirical preservation rival is unsupported.

No unresolved blocker remains for drafting at this claim strength.

### Publishable uncertainty — must remain visible

- **EVIDENCE NEEDED — comparative outcome:** no solo/naive/adversarial matched comparison; blocks equivalence, superiority, and measured preservation.
- **EVIDENCE NEEDED — prose-oracle calibration:** TPR, FPR, and error correlations are unknown; blocks an error-recovery conclusion.
- **EVIDENCE NEEDED — anchoring:** unknown whether fresh criticism or pre-draft reconstruction reduces passive assent.
- **EVIDENCE NEEDED — coverage:** unknown whether each stage performs its named operation or whether any one check covers adjacent functions.
- **EVIDENCE NEEDED — human understanding:** artifact edits do not show re-derivation or belief change.
- **EVIDENCE NEEDED — later system behaviour:** persistence/inspectability do not show retrieval, activation, or changed decisions.
- **EVIDENCE NEEDED — human/agent contrast:** relative rates of stalls, constraint relaxation, and false precision are unknown; publish only as a reason to avoid an authorship-based rule.

### Omittable at this claim strength

- **DECISION NEEDED — acceptance allocation:** omit a concrete role-holder choice; retain only the distinction between epistemic checking and authority to admit the note.
- **DECISION NEEDED — stopping rule:** omit thresholds/repetition counts from this theoretical existence claim; they belong to an evaluated procedure once prose-oracle calibration exists.
