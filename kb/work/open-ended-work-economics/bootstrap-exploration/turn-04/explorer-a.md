# Turn 4 explorer A: invest where explanation changes a useful decision

## Recommendation

**Propose a small decision-behavior comparison, not a controller or a provenance audit.** Compare current procedure and ordinary records, those materials plus the competent concise correction from turn 3, and those materials plus that correction and a short case explanation. Use four deliberately contrasting situations. Twelve fresh-worker decisions would be a screening exercise, not an estimate of a reliable treatment effect.

The investment worth testing is whether explanation helps a coordinator preserve useful review work while limiting unsupported provenance claims. It is not whether longer answers mention more caveats. Further investment would require an advantage over the competent correction on a consequential decision, without merely shifting work into refusal, evaluator repair or additional reading. A benefit against an omitted rule calls first for correcting the baseline, not building a methodology system.

This is a proposal only. No comparison, review job, source ingest or delegation was run. The purpose remains useful knowledge work and learning to automate methodologies. A packaging comparison cannot decide their relative priority or whether the broad bootstrap deserves continued research.

## Complementarities: why task choice matters before packaging

A **complementarity** here means that an improvement to one activity raises the usefulness of another activity. This is a proposed mechanism, not a demonstrated return:

1. Useful review work encounters a provenance ambiguity.
2. Method development identifies which evidence supports which claim.
3. Later coordination uses that distinction to finish permitted work and withhold only unsupported claims.
4. Automation may repeat that decision with less repair, while useful work supplies further observations of where it fails.

This cycle can fail at every link. A case explanation may already be reconstructible from records. Relevant situations may rarely recur. A coordinator may retrieve the lesson but overgeneralize it into blanket refusal. A harness improvement may remove the ambiguity before the explanation repays its upkeep. Automation can multiply a bad inference as easily as a good one.

The choice of work therefore changes what investment can accomplish. Repeating an obvious known-mismatch case mainly tests rule recall. Choosing only tasks that require authenticated traces rewards refusal and conceals the loss of ordinary useful review. Choosing only ordinary completion tests conceals unsupported assurance claims. The informative pair uses similar missing metadata under **different explicit acceptance conditions**. Correct behavior changes because the commissioned claim changes, not because the worker learns a magic prohibition.

This separates three possible relationships:

| Relationship | Concrete possibility | Evidence needed beyond this proposal |
|---|---|---|
| Reinforcement | A method helps finish ordinary review and correctly bounds a stronger assurance request | Better decisions on both branches, including completion rather than refusal where permitted |
| Competition | Reading and maintaining an explanation displaces reviews without improving decisions | Comparable decision quality plus measured additional burden |
| Dependency without payoff | A trustworthy telemetry path is necessary for a special claim, but explanation cannot create it | Demonstrated absence of required evidence; the right outcome is partial completion, not more packaging |

Current useful work and methodology learning can thus share an episode without having identical returns. This pilot would mostly buy information about a method, not completed user reviews. That opportunity cost must remain visible even if its synthetic decisions are informative.

## Proposed bounded comparison

### Decision and scope

The comparison would inform whether to fund one further explanation-focused trial for review coordination, prefer a concise correction, or redirect effort to a specific baseline or evidence defect. It would not authorize production review, change freshness state, repair the historical incident, or evaluate the quality of review judgments themselves.

Use frozen materials and read-only case packets. Ask each fresh worker for a short decision: what can be selected or finalized, what can be claimed, which evidence supports that claim, and what remains unresolved. Do not ask it to execute the commands. This limits operational risk and distinguishes decision competence from execution reliability.

The future coordinator would fix the applicable policy version, actual model and harness configuration, equal task limits, and scoring key before observing outputs. Stop proposal execution if adequate isolation, the required evidence packets, or independent scoring cannot be provided. Do not convert an incomplete comparison into a claim of superiority.

### Three conditions, with ordinary records in all three

| Condition | Available material | Contrast it supplies |
|---|---|---|
| P: current practice | Current batch procedure, current relevant registry facts, incident account and the ordinary records supplied for that case | Whether an extra correction is needed at all |
| C: competent correction | P plus turn 3's concise correction, unchanged except any necessary correction of factual error before freezing | Whether explicitly concentrating existing distinctions improves behavior |
| E: extra explanation | C plus one short case explanation of how supplied consistency can differ from execution truth, why missing and contradictory evidence differ, and where changed acceptance conditions matter | Incremental value of the explanation over an already competent correction |

Ordinary records are not a fourth, intentionally weak arm. They remain available in every condition. Existing records already contain explanation; the treatment is additional organization and explanation, not the first appearance of understanding.

Give all conditions the same evidence facts and navigation to the common material. Only the extra treatment text differs. Preflight the materials for unique operational facts or answer hints: if E supplies a missing fact, supply that fact to P and C too or explicitly abandon the explanation interpretation. Do not equalize length by padding P and C. The extra reading burden is part of the investment under test.

For this smallest probe, provide the assigned treatment directly. That estimates behavior with known access, not whether an agent would discover the material in ordinary navigation. A later retrieval trial is justified only if the content contrast first offers something worth retrieving. Shared ordinary records remain accessible and may let any condition reconstruct the lesson; that is a valid rival, not contamination to eliminate.

### Four situations selected for opposing errors

These are proposed synthetic cases derived from the inspected policy boundary, not reenactments certified by original incident traces.

| Case | Evidence and commission | Required practical distinction |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Ordinary review, concrete identity unknown | Partition was legitimately known at selection; valid output exists; no exact concrete metadata is supplied | Permit ordinary completion with the allowed unknowns. Do not fabricate identity or refuse all useful review |
| 2. Same evidence, stronger claim requested | As in case 1, but the user specifically requires independent evidence of the realized concrete model | Identify the unsupported assurance and return partial on it. Distinguish any useful review result from satisfaction of the stronger commission |
| 3. Known contradiction despite successful completion | Retained harness execution evidence maps to a different partition than the job; a prior successful finalization is also shown | Do not treat transaction success as authentication or reuse it as evidence for the selected partition. Separate future rerun from historical repair, which requires authority |
| 4. Changed dispatch surface before selection | Old notes show an explicit-selection recipe; current supplied harness facts establish that the worker partition cannot be known before selection | Decline guessed selection and identify the missing fact. Do not reuse the old recipe or expand this into a prohibition on all future review |

Cases 1 and 2 are deliberately paired. Cases 3 and 4 test the check's limit and stale-recipe boundary. The cases are chosen to expose errors, not sampled to estimate their frequency. They cannot estimate production-average value.

Start with one fresh worker per condition per case: twelve decisions, with no shared conversation or worker reuse. Randomize scheduling and remove treatment labels from scoring copies. This is a proposed activity cap, not a measured resource budget or a statistically justified sample size. One pass can uncover a baseline omission or show that the proposed task is nondiscriminating. It cannot establish a stable explanation effect. Do not claim that one lucky E answer is causal evidence sufficient for adoption.

### Scoring and costs

Score decisions against the frozen commission and procedure, not rhetorical quality or citation count. For each response record:

- permitted useful work preserved;
- unsupported assurance claimed or appropriately withheld;
- mismatch, unknown metadata and unknown selection partition distinguished;
- correct next action within authority, without unnecessary escalation;
- evaluator correction needed to make the answer usable.

The refusal control is essential: an answer that refuses all four cases is not safe success. Nor is an answer that completes all four. In case 2, useful partial work must not be reported as satisfying the stronger request.

A scorer should identify the exact mistaken decision and supporting policy passage. If acceptance depends on an unresolved interpretation, mark the case indeterminate instead of awarding the explanation condition credit. Blinding cannot be perfect when responses reveal their materials; retain that limitation. A second assessment of disputed decisions is a possible additional expense, not free infrastructure.

Measure preparation, packet and explanation authoring, worker execution, retrieval/reading where observable, scoring, operator review and repair separately. Record token usage, elapsed time and money only when the runtime supplies those measurements or they are directly measured. Do not infer attention from answer length or execution time from session filenames. Unknown measurements stay unknown. Retain one-time construction separately from per-use cost, without assuming an amortization horizon or adding unlike resources into an invented scalar.

## What would justify the next investment?

The strongest case for a further bounded replication would be **a consequential E-over-C advantage on a changed-condition decision, while E preserves case 1's ordinary completion and does not require extra rescue**. The specific advantage must survive checking for a unique fact, direct answer cue, policy ambiguity or scorer preference. The next investment would then be a repeated, independently scored comparison on untouched variants—not a general controller or a claim that the bootstrap works.

Other possible results have different consequences:

| Observed pattern in the proposed pilot | Decision it could support | What it would not support |
|---|---|---|
| P, C and E all handle the cases | Do not expand this packaging on these cases; reconsider whether a live problem remains | No conclusion that methodology or experience is useless |
| C and E beat P; E adds no discernible value | Prefer the concise correction for a later local test | No argument for extra case explanation or automatic extraction |
| E beats C only because C omits a necessary distinction | Correct the baseline and compare again only if a residual question remains | Calling baseline repair evidence for explanation's special value |
| All fail the same branch | Inspect task ambiguity, evidence availability and procedure clarity first | More explanation by default |
| E improves caution but refuses permitted completion | Reject that apparent gain as a service regression | Trading useful work for a higher refusal rate without authorization |
| E helps but adds substantial burden | Consider a narrowly targeted follow-up if recurring demand or research value warrants it | Claiming net savings or universal use |
| Answers vary without a stable interpretable pattern | End the pilot as inconclusive or propose a priced replication | Selecting the most favorable output as the result |

A twelve-decision screen cannot support “no effect” with useful precision. Its negative outcome can still justify not spending more on this particular probe when it reveals no distinctive mechanism to pursue. That is an allocation decision under uncertainty, not proof that the explanation can never help.

Further replication would buy information. Adoption would require a stronger showing: repeatable decision benefit on relevant work, enough actual or defensible prospective demand, and an acceptable burden including maintenance. The acceptable tradeoff remains an operator choice if it determines whether research displaces user work. No invented break-even use count resolves it.

## Strongest rival and retained conflicts

The strongest rival is competent task-first coordination using current procedures, ordinary records and selective concise repairs. It already embodies substantial methodology investment. A capable worker may reconstruct the entire check boundary cheaply. In that case, extra packaging duplicates understanding, adds maintenance and risks preserving stale instructions. A null packaging result favors this rival locally without rejecting the broad purpose.

A second rival explanation of a positive result is salience: E repeats the important warning or draws attention to the relevant branch, rather than enabling explanatory inference. Testing an unstated consequence and equalizing operational facts weakens that rival but cannot eliminate it. This first screen measures an added package, not a pure mental mechanism. A later equal-length descriptive-summary contrast could investigate mechanism if that distinction would change a decision; it is not required now.

The unresolved conflict from earlier turns remains: we have permission for research without a named service consumer, but no standing allocation rule for continued speculative investment. This proposal does not restrict all methodology research to short-term savings. Conversely, the bootstrap purpose does not make every explanation worth producing. A narrow positive result would justify a local continuation; a narrow negative result would remove one justification, not settle the purpose.
