# Turn 4 integration: test the added package, not methodology against nothing

## Outcome

**Prefer a two-condition comparison against current procedure, a competent concise correction and ordinary records together. Preserve the successful-finalization trap. A favorable result would justify considering another local investigation, not adopting an explanation pipeline or declaring the bootstrap worthwhile.** This is a bounded proposal, not an executed comparison or a resource-authorized protocol.

[Explorer A](./explorer-a.md) proposes three conditions with separately isolated decisions; [Explorer B](./explorer-b.md) proposes two conditions with bundled decisions and conditional repetition. The [critic](./critic.md) correctly separates these two design choices. This integration selects B's marginal contrast while retaining A's strongest case. It does not establish that B's design costs less or that bundling is generally preferable.

The purpose remains useful knowledge work and learning to automate methodologies. The operator's instruction to finish all five turns removes pauses, not the unresolved service/research priority in [turn 1](../turn-01/integration.md). No doctrine deviation, general controller, production review, library change or purpose change is adopted.

## What this turn adds

[Turn 2](../turn-02/integration.md) asked what extra retention supplies beyond a concise correction. [Turn 3](../turn-03/integration.md) supplied a concrete check boundary and a competent correction but no observed failure of that correction. This turn turns that gap into a proposed comparison and identifies which spending decisions different contrasts could inform.

The useful distinction is between **buying evidence about the correction itself** and **buying evidence about an addition to an already corrected baseline**. A's third arm answers the first question as well as the second. B answers only the second. The present marginal decision does not require purchasing both. Omitting the third arm nevertheless leaves the benefit of adding the correction to current practice unknown.

A second change concerns baseline repair. If the explanation alone receives an already-required rule, a positive result is not a fair test of extra explanation against a competent correction. But a concise repair invented after seeing the explanation succeed is a new candidate, not a retrospectively successful baseline. Otherwise any favorable explanation result could be dismissed by copying its conclusion afterward. Prefer the cheaper-looking candidate when warranted, but test its behavior before crediting it.

These are changes to this investigation's proposed decision, not empirical discoveries about worker behavior. Explorer agreement is not independent confirmation.

## Case and evidence boundary

The [current batch procedure](../../../../instructions/run-review-batches.md), reread for this integration, distinguishes a legitimately known selection partition from optional concrete execution metadata. Exact harness launch configuration or telemetry may supply concrete metadata; worker self-report is separate. Unknown concrete identity can permit ordinary completion. Known contradictory metadata must not be forced through finalization, and an unknown selection partition must not be guessed.

Turn 3 records code inspection showing that finalization checks consistency of supplied identity rather than independently authenticating execution. Its incident account reports false provenance surviving successful finalization. This integration uses that as a previous inspection and attributed history, not a fresh code audit or independent recovery of original traces. No external API claim or incident frequency is needed for the proposal.

The unresolved policy tension is preserved: permission to record exact launch configuration is not a guarantee of independently established realized identity. Ordinary review and a commission demanding that stronger assurance have different acceptance conditions. The comparison must not silently impose universal trace authentication or weaken the special commission. Explanation cannot manufacture unavailable evidence.

The [contrast argument](../../../../theory/an-experiment-identifies-only-the-contrast-it-actually-runs.md) supplies a borrowed methodological constraint: jointly varying explanation, repetition, salience and length tests that package, not explanation alone. Its external empirical examples are not premises here. No external-source performance claim is adopted. The critic's adjacent cost case remains an analogy, not a cost forecast used by this integration.

## Smallest informative comparison proposed here

### Decision, completion and limits

The intended decision is whether this extra package merits one further local investigation, whether a specific baseline repair is the better next candidate, or whether to stop this packaging line. Completion would mean a retained comparison of decisions, errors, unresolved judgments and measured or unknown costs. It would not mean successful review execution, historical repair or demonstrated automation.

Use a first pair of fresh isolated worker sessions, each answering the same four-item read-only diagnostic bundle. Prepare one second paired comparison on held-back variants, conditionally available rather than automatically run. The proposed ceiling is four worker sessions. It is an activity limit, **not a measured budget, power calculation or claim of affordability**.

Before any future execution, a coordinator would need to freeze the actual packets and explanation, provide an evaluator, and set an authorized resource ceiling that includes preparation and scoring as well as worker runs. None is supplied by this integration. If those requirements cannot be met within scope, stop partial rather than build testing infrastructure. The present task proposes only; it does not launch this work.

### Two conditions

| Condition | Materials |
|---|---|
| Corrected baseline | Current procedure, turn 3's competent concise correction, ordinary records and common scenario facts |
| Added package | Identical materials plus a frozen short explanation of why supplied consistency differs from execution truth and why evidence supports different claims under different commissions |

Both conditions receive the same pointers and access to the incident and procedure. Ordinary records are not a deliberately weak third or fourth arm. They already contain methodological work and may let a competent worker reconstruct the lesson. That is a serious rival, not contamination to suppress.

Preflight the package for unique operational facts, permissions and direct answer cues. Supply necessary factual premises equally or abandon the intended interpretation. Give the package directly: the comparison concerns use with provided access, not discovery through normal navigation. This controlled baseline is not a measurement of what ordinary workers currently retrieve.

### Four situations with opposing errors

| Situation | Decision the response must preserve |
|---|---|
| Partition legitimately known at selection; concrete identity absent; ordinary review otherwise valid | Permit ordinary completion with permitted unknowns. Neither invent provenance nor refuse all useful work. |
| Same evidence, but a commission demands independently established realized identity | Withhold the unsupported assurance. Distinguish any useful partial review from satisfying the stronger commission. |
| Current execution evidence contradicts the recorded partition, despite an existing successful finalization | Reject transaction success as authentication. Distinguish what useful review content remains from its unsupported attribution; do not erase historical state or claim a future rerun repairs it. Historical repair requires evidence and authority. |
| Partition cannot legitimately be known before selection; old dispatch advice or worker self-report suggests a label | Decline guessed selection and name the missing fact. Do not treat obsolete advice or self-report as selection authority. |

These are stipulated decision problems, not authenticated reproductions of the incident. The third preserves A's successful-finalization trap. Without it, the comparison could reduce to recalling the mismatch rule and miss the case's central confusion. Even with it, passing may still be rule recall: the concise correction already states the check's limit.

Freeze policy, model/harness conditions, item order, task allowance and expected branches before observing responses. Randomize condition assignment and scheduling where feasible; use no shared worker conversation and no correctness feedback between items. Ask for the permitted action, supportable claim and decisive evidence, not an essay. Grade permitted work, unsupported assurance, uncertainty distinctions and rescue needed. Do not reward vocabulary, citations or blanket refusal. Use a condition-blind first assessment where possible, retaining the limitation that answer content can reveal treatment. Unsettled policy interpretations make an item unresolved, not a treatment win.

Bundling is an accepted compromise for this diagnostic screen, not a demonstrated saving. Items can teach each other even without feedback; four answers in one session are not four independent applications. A's independent item contexts avoid that particular influence but require additional launches and repeated setup. Neither design has measured comparative cost. Keep item order equal and report results at bundle level. The second bundle tests robustness to altered presentation, not broad adaptation merely because identifiers change.

## What would warrant another investment?

A repeated consequential advantage for the added package on held-back variants, without false refusal, unsupported assurance or extra rescue, would provide a reason to **consider** a more realistic local test. First exclude unequal facts, omitted existing rules, scoring ambiguity and direct answer leakage. The initial pair could justify consuming the proposed second pair only after those checks. A new baseline repair instead ends this contrast; it does not silently replace the baseline during repetition.

| Possible observation | Appropriate disposition |
|---|---|
| Both conditions handle every branch | Stop this diagnostic packaging line absent another specific uncertainty. Do not infer zero possible effect or reject methodology. |
| Package succeeds because the baseline lacks an existing required rule | Repair the baseline candidate; do not credit extra explanation's special value. |
| A new concise repair becomes apparent after the result | Preserve it as an untested alternative. Do not rewrite the original comparison or assume the repair works. |
| Both fail the same branch | Inspect evidence, task wording, procedure clarity and scoring before adding text. |
| Package improves caution but blocks permitted completion | Treat this as a service regression, not safety success. |
| Package repeatedly improves consequential decisions without those defects | Consider one further local comparison with explicit costs and remaining uncertainty. Do not deploy a general pipeline. |
| Results vary or costs remain unavailable | Retain an inconclusive result. No automatic enlargement until a favorable answer appears. |

A favorable signal is not sufficient economic justification. Further investment must also name the decision additional evidence could change, the narrower uncertainty, its authorized ceiling and the alternative it would displace. Recurrence may be rare, harness changes may remove the problem, and grading may cost more than the benefit. Conversely, a limited measurement repair may be worthwhile after an inconclusive result if the uncertainty matters. No automatic continue/stop formula follows.

## Competition, reinforcement and purpose

Useful user work can expose a provenance problem. Method development can retain the evidence distinction. Later automation might apply it with less repair. Those are plausible reinforcing mechanisms, not demonstrated links in this trial. A successful local reminder could count as system improvement; it need not create competence absent from the model. But producing the reminder, successfully reusing it and learning to produce better methods remain different achievements.

The activities compete when scenario construction, explanation writing, evaluator effort or maintenance displaces a useful review or a simpler correction. A harness fix may remove the failure source more directly; no such implementation is authorized here. Extra text can also preserve obsolete advice. For this proposal, the immediate return is information about a package, not completed user reviews.

Measure preparation, new package authoring, worker use, observable retrieval/reading, grading, operator review, repair and recovery separately. Keep tokens, elapsed time, money and operator minutes distinct; unknowns remain unknown. Existing artifact costs are sunk for deciding whether to reuse an available correction, but remain relevant to a broader claim that producing methodology repays its expense. Maintenance and future demand cannot be assigned invented break-even values.

The strongest rival is competent coordination with current methods, ordinary records and selective corrections. It is not methodology-free. Its success could reflect previous methodological investment. A narrow packaging loss therefore does not reject learning, and a win does not show that a system learned to produce the package or that broad automation research should displace service. Making immediate payback mandatory, abandoning the bootstrap, or granting indefinite research allocation would change the purpose. None is adopted; consequential priority choices remain unresolved despite permission to finish the five turns.
