Use B's provenance case for the clearest application boundary; retain A's supersession case as a distinct warning about what actually consumes a retained method. These are different cases, not independent replications of a general learning mechanism.

## Strongest objections

### 1. The simple rival already contains much of the proposed method

Both explorers correctly reject an artificially weak reminder. But their strong concise rival includes current instructions, local authority, historical pointers and a competent worker. It is not merely a short sentence competing with a self-contained methodology system. It is ordinary assistance using the repository's already developed methodology.

That is the right rival for deciding whether **additional packaging** earns its cost. It cannot establish that developing and maintaining the existing instructions and evidence was unnecessary. Conversely, a longer account winning against the short paragraph alone would not show an advantage over the actual rival with its links available.

**Disposition:** retain the rival, but name the marginal decision: extra case explanation beyond current procedure and ordinary records. Do not translate “extra packaging has no demonstrated advantage” into “methodological investment has no advantage.” Turn 1 already separated artifact-use benefit from the wisdom of producing the artifact; this is an application, not a new distinction.

### 2. B's evidential caution must not silently rewrite ordinary review policy

B distinguishes unknown from contradictory metadata well. There is a further detail worth preserving in integration: the current procedure permits exact concrete values supplied through **launch configuration or execution telemetry**. It does not require a separate realized-execution trace for every ordinary review. Launch configuration may warrant recording what the harness supplied while still falling short of independently proving what executed.

The incident account's stronger statement that an intended string is not execution evidence and the procedure's permitted launch-metadata path should not be collapsed. The surviving tension concerns assurance level. Code consistency, permitted provenance recording and independently authenticated execution are not equivalent achievements.

A later worker therefore needs, in this concrete case:

- the commission's required claim: ordinary partition-scoped review or proof of a particular realized model;
- the current authorized selection rule, mapping and available harness path;
- the source of each identity value: requested, harness-supplied, worker-reported or unknown;
- the fact that successful finalization checks supplied consistency, not actual dispatch;
- the correct branch: proceed under permitted uncertainty, reject a known mismatch, or return partial when stronger required assurance is unavailable;
- separate authority and evidence to repair historical results, if that is the task.

These distinctions can fit a concise correction linked to the current procedure. More prose cannot produce absent execution evidence. If assurance is the blocker, improve or obtain evidence under separate authority; do not treat another explanation as its substitute.

**Disposition:** accept B's mechanism; retain the policy tension explicitly. Its proposed variants test decision behavior, not actual model identity unless an independent execution record supplies that answer. The procedure's acceptance standard remains unchanged.

### 3. A's sequel is stronger than its original “surprise” as evidence available here

ADR 042 reports that the dialectical case did not fit the old three-way classification. But “descriptive work with specialized attribution and maintenance clauses” remains a serious conceptual rival. The ADR rejects it; the rejection is an argument, not something five reported notes prove merely by existing. Without inspecting the worked material and the original classification's commitments, this turn cannot independently adjudicate that rivalry.

The later correction is better supported by the inspected decision text: profiles implied a continuing relation, whereas the useful reuse operation was creation-time copying. This does not prove that continuing shared contracts could never help. ADR 069 rejects building such machinery without observed need while preserving complete local authority.

**Disposition:** retain A's original case as an attributed rationale for an authorized decision. Do not say the taxonomy was empirically disproved here. Keep the narrower sequel: a retained artifact's label does not establish a consumer or an ongoing authority relationship. The question “what later decision consumes this?” is useful even when the historical taxonomy dispute remains unresolved.

### 4. Both reports specify recoverability, not successful recovery

The reports identify information a worker could use. Neither observes a fresh worker finding it, understanding the distinction, resisting a misleading cue, or applying the right branch. A's weak retrieval join is plausible, not an observed failure. B's concise correction is a candidate, not a demonstrated sufficient intervention.

There are at least three distinct possible failures: the relevant material is absent; it exists but is not reached; or it is reached and misapplied. Longer retention directly addresses only some instances of the first. It may worsen the second through reading burden and still fail on the third. These are mechanism-based possibilities, not measured effects.

**Disposition:** say “contains the needed distinctions” rather than “makes the method reliably executable.” The evidence supports inspectability and case-specific reasoning. Comparative reliability, costs and later learning remain unknown.

## Practical disposition and next investment

Within the existing purpose, carry forward the two bounded findings: use current authoritative instructions for routine application, and retrieve additional case evidence when the decision actually depends on omitted premises. Do not make this a ban on exploratory research without a named consumer. No library edit or new capture system is warranted by these reports.

For turn 4's investment question, the useful next move is to identify **one marginal addition to the competent baseline** and the decision it could change. B's case offers a tractable candidate: would extra explanation change appropriate action when metadata is missing versus contradictory, beyond the current procedure and concise reminder? First determine whether a fresh worker fails under that baseline; a special explanation is not automatically the missing component. If no authorized behavioral probe can be run, retain a comparison proposal and its evidence limit rather than call textual analysis a trial.

This is advice to the coordinator, not a launched experiment or required gate. A richer packet could still be worth testing for research reasons despite no demonstrated deficit. Its investment case would then be information gained about a conjecture, not a service improvement already established. Any comparison should retain access to ordinary records in both conditions and account for producing, finding and reading the added material. No amount or cost advantage is known here.

Purpose changes to reject absent operator direction: requiring authenticated traces for every useful task, restricting all exploration to immediate service returns, or abandoning methodology automation because extra packaging has not yet earned adoption. Plan revisions to retain: narrow claims to the actual consumer and assurance level; separate future prevention from historical repair; stop claiming unavailable evidence.
