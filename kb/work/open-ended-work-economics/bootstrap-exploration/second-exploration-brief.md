## Starting distinctions and their status

### Compression progress as a candidate steering principle

[Driven by Compression Progress](../../../sources/driven-by-compression-progress.ingest.md) distinguishes improvement in a learner's compression from already achieved compressibility, and compares old and new compressors on the same history. Its retained quotations also distinguish learnable structure from persistent noise. Those source-side distinctions motivate this inquiry; their usefulness for Commonplace is an untested transfer.

The operator's working interpretation is that surprise exposes a failure of the current account to accommodate an encounter. Investigate which such failures indicate learnable structure rather than merely unfamiliarity, noise or a misreading. Also seek unexplained successes and regularities; a visible defect need not precede a worthwhile question.

Whole-KB scope does not require reading or rewriting the whole KB at each step. Local investigations can challenge shared explanations or reveal connections across cases. A proposed unification must preserve consequential differences and counterevidence. Shorter text, fewer files and a more uniform vocabulary are not by themselves evidence of better understanding. A better account may require more text.

### Approximate accounts and lossy records are different

The conversation identified a useful conceptual distinction: an approximate prediction plus an exact residual can reconstruct an observation. For numerical values, retaining the prediction and its difference from the observation is sufficient; for prose, what counts as a reconstructable residual remains unsettled. Do not call a free-form exception summary lossless without specifying what can be recovered from it.

In practice, not everything can be acquired or retained. Distinguish information never supplied to the agent, information received but discarded, and information retained but absent from the account normally consumed. Better summaries cannot recover details that were never acquired. Pretraining supplies background competence, not an inspectable record of the particular encounter.

This is not a claim that embodied learners preserve everything or that agents lack all background. It identifies a practical question about access to evidence that can challenge the current account. The KB is accumulated evidence and understanding, not a closed substitute for further observation.

### Qualitative claims need not predict exact events

Use [generation confidence does not by itself certify soundness](../../../theory/generation-confidence-does-not-by-itself-certify-soundness.md) as a possible test case, not a mandatory destination. Its no-certification claim constrains an inference; it does not predict that any particular confident answer is false or specify an error frequency. Its empirical confidence–soundness relation requires separate evidence.

Investigate how constraints on inference, qualitative explanations and empirical predictors can contribute to improved understanding without forcing them into one event-prediction format. Distinguish literal coding gains from explanatory economy unless a concrete connection is established. A valuable claim need not be discarded merely because its contribution resists a compression measure.

## Questions to pursue together

These are research directions, not five predetermined turn assignments. Let findings determine their order and whether a different formulation is needed.

- **Positive steering:** What makes one unknown worth investigating rather than another? Can expected improvement in understanding guide that choice without merely rewarding novelty, persistent error or fluent promises of future value?
- **Shared understanding:** Which observations challenge current explanations, assumptions or methods? Would resolving them account for multiple cases, or merely conceal distinctions under a broad label? What rival account survives?
- **Acquisition and retention:** What must remain available to challenge, reconstruct or replace the current account? What can be reacquired, from where, and at what cost? How could later work discover that a discarded detail mattered?
- **Progress and forgetting:** What evidence permits a before/after comparison? Comparing on common retained cases guards against changing the evidence to favor the new account, but cannot recover missing acquisition or guarantee future usefulness. How can local progress conceal deterioration elsewhere?
- **Practical mechanisms:** What observable or computable consequences can support an investigation involving qualitative claims? Separate what changes in the account, what counts as progress, and what selects the next investigation. Do not assume a universal score or a full-history evaluation is necessary or feasible.
- **Purpose and resources:** How should curiosity interact with useful delivery, authority, uncertainty and opportunity cost? Keep intrinsic learning value distinct from demonstrated task benefit. Neither immediate service payoff nor indefinite exploration is adopted as the default.

## Revised exploration procedure

For a separately authorized retry, retain five turns as a bounded trial and the first trial's sequential fresh explorer A, fresh explorer B, fresh critic and fresh integrator structure. This keeps the principal deliberate revision in the inquiry instructions, but does not make the retry a controlled experiment or establish that four roles are necessary. Fresh workers may read the retained first-trial record as prior evidence, not as an answer to reproduce.

Before each turn, the coordinator selects a consequential question from the current findings and gives explorers different angles. Do not preassign the five original topic stages. Explorers may follow a promising surprise or regularity without first identifying an immediate service consumer. They must not manufacture unification to satisfy the framing.

The critic tests whether the claimed surprise challenges the actual prior account, whether proposed shared structure preserves differences, which rival explanation matters, and what evidence would discriminate. Cost and authority remain constraints, but criticism must not silently convert research into a service-first admission rule.

The integrator distinguishes useful changes within the purpose from proposed changes to it. Do not automatically select the easiest protocol or packaging question. At each consequential narrowing, state what part of the purpose it serves and what it leaves untouched.

Retain brief decision reasons, not private reasoning transcripts:

- What observation or unresolved regularity motivated the question, and which account did it challenge?
- Why pursue this direction rather than the strongest live alternative?
- Why park that alternative: evidence, scope, cost, lower expected value, or an unresolved choice? If no reason was retained, say so.
- What changed, what resisted the proposed account, and what evidence must remain recoverable?
- What were the available costs and operator interventions? Unknown measurements remain unknown.

At every turn boundary, replan from findings. Return partial results when evidence or tools block progress; a negative finding need not trigger a larger framework. After turn five, report surviving steering mechanisms, retention requirements, limits, and whether the revised procedure deserves another trial. Do not infer superiority from producing more coherent prose.
