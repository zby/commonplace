# Turn 5 explorer B: stop refining the proxy before it replaces the purpose

## Judgment

**The trial changed the proposed questions and comparison, but has not observed improved methodology use or learning. Preserve those conceptual corrections; do not renew the four-role, five-turn format by default. The next allocation should buy an observation that could change a decision, rather than another refinement of the provenance proposal.**

The narrowing to provenance was defensible as a way to obtain a concrete case. It nevertheless under-served the broader bootstrap question: whether doing useful knowledge work can improve the system's ability to develop and automate methodologies. A comparison of extra explanation against an already corrected baseline concerns consumption of one additional representation. It does not test acquisition of the correction, selection of worthwhile learning opportunities, or improvement in later method development.

This is a scope diagnosis, not evidence that provenance research is worthless. I recommend keeping the [turn 4 proposal](../turn-04/integration.md) as an optional local branch, not making it the main plan's mandatory next step. Useful knowledge work and learning to automate methodologies remain the purpose. Neither immediate service priority nor indefinite research entitlement follows from completing the authorized turns.

## Repetition and the risk of self-generated work

Across the integrations, several constraints recur: preserve purpose, keep ordinary records in the rival, do not infer broad transfer, do not equate validation with truth, mark costs unknown when unavailable, and do not mistake a finite episode for productive selection. Those repetitions matter as boundary protection in isolated sessions. They are not accumulating evidence or repeated discoveries.

The problem is not merely verbose reporting. Each integration supplies the next question. Turn 2 selects retention; turn 3 selects provenance packaging; turn 4 designs its marginal comparison. That produces coherence, but also a dependence: a tractable local question increasingly determines what the investigation treats as the next valuable uncertainty. The continuing allocation was fixed by the operator, so these turns were not unauthorized renewal. However, their success cannot establish that an automatic selector would have chosen them well.

The remaining gap is especially clear in [turn 3](../turn-03/integration.md): a competent correction is proposed, but no later worker is observed using it. Turn 4 then adds design detail without observing that baseline. This was permissible proposal work under the commission. It is also exactly the kind of preparation whose marginal value the project meant to investigate.

**Conjecture:** the procedure risks selecting questions for ease of disciplined reporting rather than importance to bootstrap learning. The record demonstrates narrowing and absence of behavioral comparison, not that this psychological or organizational mechanism caused them. The rival explanation is reasonable experimental discipline: first make one uncertain investment sufficiently precise to test.

## Did provenance packaging under-serve the purpose?

Yes, as coverage of the broad question; not necessarily as the best feasible local investigation. The difference matters.

The turn 4 comparison starts with the hard conceptual work already supplied: a selected incident, current policy, a concise correction, and stipulated evaluation branches. Its immediate question is whether another explanation changes decisions. The system's ability to discover an important defect, derive a useful correction, choose where to reuse it, and improve the next method-development episode is outside that contrast.

This leaves three bootstrap uncertainties largely untouched:

1. **Selection:** Does experience from useful work help choose the next methodology investment, rather than merely supply examples for a predetermined packaging question?
2. **Acquisition:** Can the system turn an observed limitation into a usable revision without the operator supplying the decisive lesson or repair?
3. **Later development:** Does retained experience improve the next development episode, including recognition that the earlier method should not apply?

The local [machinery-investment argument](../../../../theory/an-optimal-long-run-learning-strategy-invests-in-its-own-machinery.md) is useful here only as a conditional argument: benefits require assessable improvements, persistence and returns exceeding competing effort. It is not evidence that these conditions hold. The [controller-evaluation account](../../../../theory/a-search-controller-is-tested-by-what-it-brings-to-stronger-evaluation.md) directs attention toward downstream consequences rather than provisional judgments. I borrow that evaluative distinction, not its external empirical or historical premises.

The strongest objection to broadening again is that acquisition and later development are harder to evaluate; returning to them could restart endless conceptual work. The answer is not another general framework. It is to preserve the missing questions while making the next observation small and tied to a real decision. Provenance remains eligible if it addresses such a decision; its already-developed proposal does not give it priority by itself.

## Proposed revision of the main plan

These are recommendations for a separately authorized allocation, not instructions launched here. Completion would be a retained observation and a disposition of one investment, including a partial or negative result. No code, library change, production review or new record system is implied.

**Retain:** useful work, deliberate exploratory latitude, ordinary evidence access, explicit purpose boundaries, and the distinction between delivery, learning investment and observed later benefit. Keep the provenance comparison, but label it as an optional test of an added package rather than the bootstrap's admission test.

**Change the next decision:** choose between testing the added provenance package and observing one experience-to-method-change episode on a worthwhile task. Name what each could change and what evidence is available before selecting. The latter can start with existing records; it need not create new infrastructure. It should identify the original limitation, the proposed revision, who supplied the decisive correction, and a later opportunity to use or decline it. Without a later opportunity, report acquisition only; do not call it demonstrated reusable improvement. Without a relevant comparator, report the episode, not causal benefit.

**Cut from the next allocation:** automatic repetition of all four roles, another general taxonomy of returns, and elaboration of provenance scenarios unless someone is authorized and equipped to run them. Preserve consequential objections by reference rather than copying every caveat into each report. Do not cut authority checks, source grounding or required validation to manufacture savings.

**Reserve:** any standing policy on how much speculative learning should displace present service remains an operator choice. The absence of an immediate consumer is not an automatic rejection under the current purpose. Conversely, the bootstrap aim does not finance every branch indefinitely.

## Strongest simpler rival and what to test next

The strongest rival procedure is **one investigator with ordinary KB access, followed by one fresh critic when the decision warrants independent challenge, with integration performed by the coordinator**. A capable ordinary conversation is an additional rival: it can retain corrections and explore alternatives without separate role files. Neither rival is methodology-free or intentionally forgetful.

A future comparison could give the full procedure and the simpler procedure the same bounded question, evidence access, acceptance boundary and authorized resource ceiling. Assess decision-relevant omissions, usable proposed next actions, unsupported claims and required operator repair, alongside measured resource use. Preserve intermediate differences so the evaluator can identify which role actually introduced a correction. Content may reveal the procedure even if labels are hidden; evaluator independence is limited accordingly. One comparison would be an informative local screen, not a general ranking.

Before buying that process comparison, ask whether choosing the procedure is presently consequential. If the immediate decision is whether a method works, spend the allocation on observing the method instead. Do not create a second methodological research program merely to criticize the first. A role ablation is warranted only if its result could change future scheduling; I do not launch or mandate it.

The strongest rival account of this whole trial is that ordinary discussion could have reached the same constraints and a competent test proposal with less repeated setup. The strongest defense is that visible criticism caught purpose drift and integration combined complementary design details. Both remain compatible with the observed record. There is no estimate of their counterfactual cost or comparative output quality.
