It did not demonstrate learning to automate methodologies, and its final provenance-packaging question covers only a small part of that purpose.** Retain that proposal as one optional local branch, not as the main plan's next compulsory step. The strongest proposed revision is to distinguish a test of using supplied methodology from a test of developing better methodology through experience.

## Did narrowing under-serve the broader purpose?

**Yes in coverage, not demonstrably in investment value.** The scope of the eventual proposed comparison is visibly narrower than the commission. Whether this was the best use of the allocation remains unknown.

The chain was locally reasonable: turn 2 asked what a later worker needs; turn 3 found a concrete case; turn 4 asked what extra explanation adds. Concreteness prevented another general store or controller proposal. The provenance case offers opposed errors—unsupported assurance and needless refusal—and therefore a criticizable diagnostic problem.

But the chain changes which uncertainty is being investigated. The eventual worker receives selected facts, a current procedure, a competent correction and, in one condition, an additional explanation. The evaluator's branches are prepared beforehand. A successful worker could simply use these materials correctly. The comparison does not ask the system to:

- notice a method-development opportunity during useful work;
- choose that opportunity over another investment;
- decide what lesson experience supports;
- create or revise the methodology without a coordinator supplying the decisive interpretation;
- find the method during natural later work; or
- improve a later method-development episode.

Those are not missing controls that must all be added to the provenance test. They are different questions. Adding them would destroy the bounded contrast. The main plan should instead state explicitly that this branch tests **use of an added package with supplied access**, while the broader bootstrap remains open.

This concern is grounded in the existing [retention argument](../../../../theory/factory-learning-is-experience-responsive-retention-that-improves.md): observation, stored text and later improvement do not by themselves identify the causal path from experience to reusable change to changed later production. I borrow that distinction as a conceptual test, not independent empirical confirmation. A local reminder can qualify as useful learning under an appropriate system boundary; demanding broad transfer before crediting any learning would repeat turn 1's rejected overreach.

There is a second narrowing: the surprising case about superseded authority is no longer represented by the chosen provenance contrast. That is not evidence it was worthless. It is a visible alternative that the retrospective should retain rather than make the final proposal look inevitable. Similarly, turn 2's question about recognizing promising unfamiliar possibilities has not been answered by developing an easily specified diagnostic bundle.

## Proposed revisions to the main plan

Intent: preserve the commissioned purpose while making the next allocation answer a named uncertainty. Completion of this revision would be a clear choice or an explicit unresolved choice, not a controller implementation. Only the operator/coordinator can adopt or schedule it.

### 1. Add a disposition of this trial, not a new objective

Proposed parent-plan wording:

> The five-turn exploration clarified distinctions among useful delivery, learning investment and later benefit, and produced an unrun provenance-packaging comparison. It did not establish better automatic search, methodology learning or superiority of the exploration procedure. Treat the packaging proposal as an optional local investigation, not as the test of the broad bootstrap. Keep the objective of useful knowledge work and learning to automate methodologies; the priority between present service and speculative learning remains unresolved where it affects a consequential allocation.

This is a proposed insertion, not an edit made here. The resource-tier model remains supporting analysis. No observed movement between tiers or justified maximand emerged from these turns. The parent's closure condition about what economic guidance adds is still unmet empirically. Conceptually, the trial made the displaced alternative and marginal evidence purchase more explicit; it did not show that doing so improves search choices.

### 2. Choose between questions before choosing another protocol

Three possible next investments should remain distinct:

| Live question | Candidate route | What its answer cannot establish |
|---|---|---|
| Does this extra case package improve decisions when supplied? | The turn-4 two-condition diagnostic proposal, after actual packets, evaluator and resource authorization exist | Acquisition value, natural retrieval or better method development |
| Can experience improve later method development? | A bounded episode of useful work with a visible method-relevant difficulty, followed by another development opportunity; retain who supplied the decisive interpretation and compare with competent ordinary records/selective correction | Broad transfer or indefinite improvement from one episode |
| Does the four-role exploration procedure earn its extra cost? | A future comparable question investigated with a simpler discussion/delegation route and the four-role route under declared resource accounting and downstream assessment | The benefit of any one role unless the contrast isolates it |

The second route is closer to the commission's missing bootstrap link, so I propose considering it before automatically funding the first. It is not yet an executable protocol: the task, acceptance conditions, feasible feedback and resource ceiling are unknown. Do not invent them or make immediate service payback a universal entrance condition. A purpose-preserving research allocation can investigate uncertainty before a consumer is known, but this trial has not authorized an ongoing allowance.

A coarse or negative answer is acceptable. If no suitable episode and evaluator can be named within authorized effort, the next decision can be to park the branch. It should not automatically become a project to manufacture a general evaluator or commitment store. Record design and method-operation changes remain with the adjacent workshops named in the [parent framing](../../README.md).

### 3. Reopen breadth at an allocation boundary

Propose a short coordinator check when a broad inquiry becomes a concrete test: **Which part of the commissioned purpose will this answer, which part will remain untouched, and what alternative inquiry is now being displaced?** This trial could have used that check between turns 3 and 4. It would not necessarily have changed the selection; it would have made the narrowing explicit before investing in protocol refinement.

This is a conjectured procedural improvement, not a demonstrated efficiency gain or a per-step reporting requirement. Exploration must still be able to encounter possibilities before naming their value. The check belongs at consequential renewal, not every speculative move.

## Strongest rival and remaining conflicts

The strongest rival to this procedure is **one competent investigation with ordinary records, followed by targeted criticism where consequential uncertainty remains**. It could preserve purpose, search the KB, identify the concise correction and propose a fair contrast without four fresh roles each turn. Much of this trial reapplied already available distinctions; repeated caveats and full-context loading have real measured resource use but no demonstrated incremental benefit.

The strongest answer to that rival is the actual draft correction record: the critic catches service-first drift and over-demanding learning criteria; the turn-4 integration combines B's contrast with A's trap. Those are useful candidate reasons to retain criticism and plural exploration where alternatives genuinely differ. They are not a measured advantage over the rival. I would propose another selective trial of independent challenge, not adopt four roles for every inquiry and not abolish them on this evidence.

Conflicts to retain:

- A narrow, checkable case can make progress while leaving the principal purpose under-investigated. Broad relevance alone does not tell us the better next allocation.
- A supplied-method test is easier to interpret than a development episode, but it addresses a weaker claim.
- Ordinary records are a competent baseline containing prior methodology investment. Their success cannot reject methodology itself.
- Better prospective questions are legitimate outputs of exploration; they are not yet later benefit. The [controller-evaluation argument](../../../../theory/a-search-controller-is-tested-by-what-it-brings-to-stronger-evaluation.md) motivates following routed work into stronger assessment. Its existence is not evidence this procedure selected well.
- The authorization to finish all five turns was a scheduling intervention, not approval of a service/research exchange rate or continuing research budget.
