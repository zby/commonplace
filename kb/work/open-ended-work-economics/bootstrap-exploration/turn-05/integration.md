## Did the provenance narrowing under-serve the bootstrap purpose?

**It under-served breadth of coverage; its comparative investment value is unknown.** The local chain was defensible: an abstract retention question led to a concrete case and then to a marginal comparison. That avoided proposing a general store or controller without a consumer. But the final comparison supplies the selected case, current procedure, concise correction and evaluation branches to the worker. It asks whether an additional explanation improves decisions when provided.

That does not investigate how the system notices an opportunity during useful work, chooses it over another investment, derives the lesson, retrieves it naturally, or improves the next method-development episode. Those are different questions, not controls to add indiscriminately to the provenance experiment. Adding them all would destroy its bounded contrast.

The strongest objection to reopening breadth is disciplined decomposition: a supplied-method test could expose a limitation before a more difficult development study. The strongest objection to continuing the narrow branch is that tractability can select the next question while the principal purpose remains untested. B's proposed mechanism—selection for ease of disciplined reporting—is a conjecture. The observed narrowing does not establish its cause. Nor does being closer to the broad purpose make a development episode automatically worth funding.

The [retention argument](../../../../theory/factory-learning-is-experience-responsive-retention-that-improves.md) is useful as a borrowed conceptual distinction: experience must determine a reusable change, later work must depend on it, and improvement requires more than storage or chronology. It is not independent evidence that this trial's advice will help. This trial itself contains revisions within one investigation and later integrations preserving them. That is a candidate starting record for later use, not demonstrated reusable improvement in a later investigation. Local learning would deserve credit if established; broad transfer is not a prerequisite for that narrower claim.

The proposed end state is a chosen bounded investigation, or an explicit decision to defer because evidence or authority is insufficient—not a controller, evaluator project or general record system. These changes require separate adoption and scheduling; only this integration is written now.

## Which parts of the procedure deserve another trial?

- **Fresh criticism of consequential claims:** deserves selective retrial. Visible corrections protect purpose and distinguish claims. Its marginal benefit over careful self-review or ordinary discussion is unknown.
- **Two isolated explorers:** deserves retrial when materially different approaches or purchases of evidence are plausible, as in turn 4. Independence within a shared frame did not test whether that frame deserved priority. Do not require two explorers for every refinement.
- **Integration that preserves a rejected alternative's useful part:** retain as an aim. The successful-finalization case is concrete evidence of this contribution. A separate fresh integrator is not shown necessary; coordinator integration is a credible rival whose labor must be counted.
- **Contemporaneous differences and measured cost boundaries:** retain enough to test retrospective claims. They enabled correction of credit assignments and prevented invented cost conclusions. Avoid expanding them into a reporting system without a consumer.
- **A short breadth check at consequential narrowing:** merits a trial, not automatic adoption. It might prevent an optional proxy becoming the default plan; it might merely add another report. Judge it by a later allocation it changes.
- **Five fixed turns with four roles each:** do not renew by default. Completion satisfied this commission but did not establish productive selection or economy. Competent conversation, or one investigator with selective fresh criticism and coordinator integration, remains a strong untested rival.

A procedure comparison is warranted only if its result could change scheduling. It must not become a second automatic methodology program. Required authority, source and validation checks are not candidates for removal to manufacture savings.

3. Can the system recognize promising opportunities under weak feedback, rather than select only readily scored proxies?
