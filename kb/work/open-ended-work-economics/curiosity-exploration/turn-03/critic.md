# Turn 3 critique: clarification must change the next decision

## Verdict and evidence status

**Bounded clarification survives as permission to formulate questions, not yet as a distinct steering mechanism.** Both explorers identify legitimate learning without an immediate service consumer. Neither demonstrates that clarification selects better investigations. The main vulnerability is renewal: a reading cap limits one pass, but successive plausible distinctions can keep the same unsupported promise alive.

This critique compares [A](./explorer-a.md), [B](./explorer-b.md), [coordination](./coordination.md), and the [turn-1](../turn-01/integration.md) and [turn-2 integrations](../turn-02/integration.md). I directly checked the candidate-generation survey, confidence note, and tag trace below. These checks support documentary observations about what the incumbent says, not confirmation of its historical or empirical claims. Proposed safeguards and allocation judgments are conjectures. B supplies a logical illustration, not an executed experiment. No external-source conclusion or literature finding is required here; no source analysis is used as support.

## Does clarification originate questions?

The actual turn-2 account asks selectors to retain an incumbent, rival, judgment-changing distinction, and plausible evidence route. It also supplies question-generating prompts. It does not explicitly prohibit preliminary exploration. Both explorers therefore improve a potentially restrictive implementation, not overturn a demonstrated exclusion. Preserve that qualification: otherwise the new stage wins against a manufactured prior.

A supplies a substantive generation move: examine how the selection of recorded cases might hide possibilities before they become proposals. B supplies another: examine the inferential transition between two claims before deciding which alternatives matter. Neither requires a completed rivalry at entry. These are more informative than “explore something unfamiliar,” because they identify an encounter and a way to inspect it.

However, A already names counterpressures and B rapidly names alternative interpretations. Their examples show that incomplete contrasts can become sharper through reading. They do not show that inquiry can reliably choose fruitful directions without any initial contrast. That stronger claim is unnecessary. Admission can rest on a concrete unresolved relation whose significance is uncertain, without demanding finished hypotheses or a numerical return.

The difference from unconstrained exploration is conditional: a bounded pass must return something that changes the next allocation, including a reason to stop. The three-artifact cap alone cannot provide that difference. Arbitrary browsing can also stop after three artifacts.

## What prevents fluent renewal?

A permits continuation after finding a concrete distinction or evidence route; B requires an unresolved implication and bounded route. Both are vulnerable to invented significance. A worker can repeatedly rename its distinction, locate another adjacent document, and claim improved assessability.

The stronger safeguard is a comparison against the prior *decision state*. Before renewal, identify what the completed pass changed: which apparent question dissolved, which previously unavailable comparison became possible, or which particular missing premise now determines the answer. Preserve the passage or record responsible. Merely adding interpretations, documents, or vocabulary does not establish such a change.

Then distinguish a useful result from entitlement to more work. Finding a missing transcript can be useful learning once while supporting immediate parking. Discovering that an incumbent already distinguishes noticing from drafting can close a proposed theory contribution even if other questions remain imaginable. A fresh question needs its own allocation reason; it must not inherit renewal merely by occupying the same topic.

This is an inspectability safeguard, not a guarantee against persuasive rationalization. It adds comparison work whose cost is unknown. It should remain proportionate and permit a short negative return. It does not require every investigation to help a service consumer or produce an event prediction.

## Actual-incumbent checks change the opportunity ranking

**A's selected opportunity risks repeating held understanding.** The [candidate-generation survey](../../../../reference/where-change-candidates-come-from-in-commonplace.md) already distinguishes noticing a gap, promoting it into a candidate, and incorporating an ephemeral report's suggestions later. It explicitly calls its mechanisms illustrations rather than a catalogue. Thus “not noticed, not formulated, not selected” is not by itself a new conceptual contribution.

A acknowledges this counterpressure, but its strongest remaining question is narrower: can retained cases distinguish those stages in an actual allocation, rather than merely name them? The first trial's parked compression direction was already formulated. Its missing comparative rationale establishes a limit on reconstructing selection, not evidence about opportunities never noticed. Using that case to establish invisible candidate loss would overreach. A's pass remains worthwhile only as a bounded check of evidence coverage, with a genuine no-change outcome.

**B offers the sharper present inferential opportunity, with an important correction.** The [confidence note](../../../../theory/generation-confidence-does-not-by-itself-certify-soundness.md) expressly permits validated confidence for triage and leaves internal probes open. Its heading nevertheless says, “High-assurance acceptance needs another operation”; its description says a “separate check.” B's permissive reading—an earlier-validated acceptance rule without another runtime checker—is therefore not equally explicit in the incumbent. It is a possible reinterpretation requiring argument, not merely recovery of what the note clearly says.

The live question is whether the stronger acceptance requirement follows from additional assurance premises. Non-entailment alone does not supply them. B's stipulated true/false continuations illustrate that logical limit, but repeat the incumbent's no-certification claim. The possible gain is locating where a deductive constraint ends and an acceptance policy begins. That preserves qualitative inferential value without inventing calibration evidence or asserting that high confidence warrants acceptance.

The [tag trace](../../../../theory/evidence/tag-readme-trace-observed-causal-connection.md) already distinguishes relocated verification from separated artifact jobs and records the block-YAML omission. A accurately treats further verification analysis as a bounded attribution question, not a new discovery that code checks things. Its reachability makes it a credible fallback, not automatically the best allocation.

## Strongest alternative and discriminator

The strongest general alternative is ordinary explanation-seeking inquiry that already permits preliminary reconnaissance. It can implement both explorers' methods without a compression objective or a named clarification stage. Nothing this turn separates their outcomes from that rival. Compression remains an optional prompt toward shared structure, not demonstrated additional selection power.

Within the current allocation, I prefer B's assurance-premise question over A's proposed candidate-stage distinction. It has an exact unresolved transition, and checking it could constrain an inference rather than repeat an established taxonomy. This preference serves broader understanding of qualitative claims; it does not claim delivery benefit. Park A's broader lost-opportunity interpretation because the surveyed incumbent already names the stages and the available negative case cannot reveal never-noticed possibilities.

A discriminator for the local choice is whether a bounded examination finds an assurance premise that justifies “another operation.” If present and adequate, B's apparent gap closes. If absent, retain the unsupported transition without concluding that any particular alternative acceptance rule is safe. For A, a retained case with a recoverable selection reason would permit a stage-specific judgment; more accepted end states would not.

A future authorized steering comparison would need comparable opportunity pools and distinguish newly assessable questions from restatements, while counting inquiry effort and preserving non-unifying gains. That is a proposed discriminator, not a commissioned experiment or proof that such comparison is straightforward.
