# Explorer A, turn 5: fund reachable changes in understanding

## Recommendation and evidential status

**Retain bounded explanation-seeking as the practical positive-steering proposal; do not adopt compression as a separate objective. Stop this documentary sequence after its commissioned integration.** Another trial deserves consideration only if it can expose a consequential difference in inquiry selection or evidence consultation. Repeating these arguments with fresh writers would not do that.

The purpose remains useful knowledge work across domains, with methodologies learned through development and use. Understanding can be a legitimate research return before a delivery benefit is demonstrated. It does not follow that every understandable question deserves resources, or that a promising inquiry licenses changing the system.

This proposal synthesizes the [first](../turn-01/integration.md), [second](../turn-02/integration.md), [third](../turn-03/integration.md), and [fourth integrations](../turn-04/integration.md). Their recorded judgments are documentary evidence about this inquiry, not independent confirmation of its underlying mechanisms. The steering proposal and future comparison below are conjectures. No new external-source claim is needed. No behavioral demonstration, automation gain, or superiority over ordinary explanation-seeking has been established.

## What the actual prior permits us to claim

The first trial already allowed surprise-led inquiry. Compression curiosity was parked without a retained comparative reason. Therefore this run cannot claim to have introduced curiosity into an incurious procedure, or recovered why that earlier alternative lost.

The second exploration produced narrower, useful corrections. Turn 1 located friction between an explanatory application and its supporting history. Turn 2 concluded mainly that support needed reclassification and that causal attribution faced acquisition gaps; the scoped recovery mechanism survived. Turn 3 preserved a preliminary clarification route without pretending it had discovered a new omission in the prior procedure. Turn 4 separated preserving captured text from reconstructing an encounter, discovering relevance, and explaining an outcome.

These findings resist an easy progress story. Correcting an example need not improve a mechanism. Identifying a missing premise need not refute a policy. A preservation comparison need not establish that future workers will discover the preserved distinction. The strongest positive result is a more inspectable basis for choosing and ending inquiries, not demonstrated better choices.

## The surviving practical proposal

The proposed unit of investment is **a bounded opportunity to change understanding**, not a document, topic, or promised improvement score. Its practical content has four parts.

**Originate questions from unresolved relations.** Look at a small, purpose-relevant encounter or group of cases: unexplained success, different outcomes under an apparently shared explanation, an unsupported transition between claims, or a method whose contribution is unclear. Ask what the present account does not settle. This directs attention without requiring an error, an immediate consumer, or a preconstructed rivalry. It also permits finding that the existing explanation already suffices.

**Allow clarification before requiring an answerable comparison.** An unfamiliar direction may initially offer only a concrete puzzling relation. A bounded reading pass can determine whether there is a real question, supply vocabulary, locate a rival, or expose inaccessible evidence. The reason to fund that pass is a plausible change in what can be investigated, not confidence that it will yield a positive result. Where necessary vocabulary itself exceeds the available budget, record that limitation rather than classifying the direction as intrinsically unpromising.

**Choose the next investment by qualitative comparison.** State the actual incumbent, prospective change in understanding, reachable evidence, strongest alternative inquiry, and resource constraints. A reachable correction may outrank a broader causal question whose decisive history is unavailable. Conversely, a difficult unfamiliar direction may deserve an explicitly authorized research allowance because familiar cases repeatedly leave the same important uncertainty untouched. Neither choice needs a common numerical scale. When considerations conflict, retain which consideration decided and what remains uncertain.

**Renew only for a changed allocation reason.** Clarification ends with a dissolved question, a reachable distinction, or a named obstacle. Continued inquiry needs something beyond another paraphrase: for example, an identified unread argument that bears on the distinction, a recoverable encounter, or an independently obtained contrary case. A new allocation reason is necessary, not sufficient; it may still be rationalized. The retained before/after decision lets later review challenge it.

This proposal steers positively because it supplies places to look and reasons to invest before a finished answer exists. Its stopping discipline prevents every local clarification from becoming an indefinite research entitlement. It remains incomplete about discovering valuable unfamiliar fields and comparing large, heterogeneous opportunities.

## Purpose, authority, and resources

Learning and delivery should be reported separately. Removing unsupported causal attribution has intrinsic learning value: it changes what we are entitled to infer. Faster completion, fewer failures, and reduced operator attention would be delivery benefits requiring additional evidence. Neither can be inferred from a clearer report. Learning can justify a research allocation under the declared purpose without being relabeled as future savings.

Authority determines feasibility before attractiveness. Within a commissioned inquiry, a worker can choose readings and comparisons; it cannot treat curiosity as authority to acquire restricted material, launch interventions, alter assurance policy, or build a controller. If an answer depends on a purpose or assurance requirement the evidence does not supply, return that gap to the operator. Do not silently choose it to make the inquiry executable.

The resource-tier model is useful as a checklist, not a maximand. Operator judgment, model work, and ordinary computation incur different burdens. Context capacity, retrieval and interpretation effort, privacy, and deferred delivery also matter. Moving work to a cheaper tier does not establish that the result is valuable or reliable.

The [pre-final cost record](../costs-before-turn-05.md) reports sixteen workers, $15.179444 in harness-reported cost and 49.27 summed worker minutes, excluding coordinator and operator effort. These are costs of this documentary sequence, not the cost of a validated steering mechanism. Counterfactual returns remain unknown. The figures justify asking what another allocation could settle; they establish neither waste nor cost-effectiveness. Research need not beat delivery on every occasion, but its continuation must stay within an authorized allowance rather than borrow indefinitely from unspecified future utility.

## Compression's surviving contribution and strongest rival

Compression supplied a useful emphasis in this run: ask whether one account accommodates several cases while preserving consequential differences, and whether apparent improvement hides deterioration on older material. Those are heuristics for attention. This run has established neither literal coding gains nor additional selection power from them.

The strongest rival is ordinary explanation-seeking with bounded reconnaissance, explicit alternatives, recoverable originals, known counterexamples, and fresh acquisition. It can perform every step proposed here. Calling the proposal “compression-informed” describes its motivation; it does not distinguish its operation. A longer explanation can be better. Fewer files, shorter prose, and a uniform vocabulary are not the required returns.

The compression proposal would acquire distinct practical content if it produced a different justified selection, earlier discovery, or lower effort than that strong rival while preserving relevant distinctions. None is shown here. This is a negative finding about the present evidence, not a general refutation of compression-based learning.

## Acquisition, retention, and reconsideration

Acquire evidence relative to the proposed judgment. Attribution needs an encounter and contribution sequence, not just an accepted result. A claim about checking coverage needs what the check examined. An inference-policy question needs the governing requirement as well as the argument. If those inputs were never recorded, a polished synopsis cannot supply them.

Retain the actual incumbent with its qualifications, the evidence selection route, candidate changes, comparison cases, strongest rival, and reason for parking alternatives. Preserve versioned passages when wording or chronology is decisive. Record whether a case is stipulated or observed. Keep the initial cases visible when adding new ones, rather than replacing inconvenient evidence. No free-form exception summary is presumed lossless.

Reconsideration needs more than storage. Before claiming progress, consult older permissions and counterexamples, not only the old prohibitions. Reopen a parked branch when its missing evidence becomes accessible, its relevant use changes, or an encounter challenges the original parking reason. A known search-coverage defect calls for a route capable of exposing what that search excluded; two reworded searches may share the same defect.

Never-acquired evidence calls for fresh acquisition where possible; discarded evidence may require recovery; retained-but-unconsulted evidence requires renewed consultation. Fresh evidence can illuminate a general problem without reconstructing its historical instance. Version locators are contingent access routes. Their availability and reacquisition costs are not guaranteed. These requirements do not select a general storage architecture or justify keeping everything.

## Conditions for another trial, and reasons to stop

The most consequential remaining choice is whether the proposed emphasis changes allocation enough to warrant retaining it separately from ordinary explanation-seeking. A further trial should first establish an observable disagreement between the two approaches on the same available material, before seeing the inquiry outcomes. If both select the same question for the same reasons, that is useful evidence against operational distinctness; do not manufacture different choices by weakening the rival.

Only if a real disagreement emerges should separately authorized work follow the competing evidence routes under explicit, comparable resource limits. Retain what each expected to change, how it selected evidence, what actually changed, and which older distinctions survived. Assessment should separate warranted learning from demonstrated task benefit and include effort. One bounded comparison could challenge local distinctness; it could not establish universal superiority. This is a condition for a future commission, not a test launch or a finished protocol.

Absent an obtainable disagreement and reachable discriminating evidence, stop. Also stop if decisive evidence is unavailable, authority is missing, or further work would merely reorganize the same judgments. Park assurance-policy resolution for missing premises and narrower scope; retrospective attribution for missing encounters; universal compression scoring for absent measurement semantics; broad retention for unproven benefit and cost; and controller or storage design for scope and adjacent ownership. The literature search remains incomplete, with no new source-backed conclusions supplied here.

This result preserves a usable research heuristic and an honest boundary: learning-oriented inquiry is worth considering within purpose and resources, but compression has not yet earned a separate governing role.
