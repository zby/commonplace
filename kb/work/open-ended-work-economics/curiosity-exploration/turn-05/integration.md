# Final integration: retain learning-oriented inquiry, stop this repetition

## Decision and status

**Close this five-turn documentary run. Retain bounded explanation-seeking as a candidate practice, with compression as an optional prompt rather than a governing objective. Do not renew the same prose-only sequence.** Another bounded inquiry deserves consideration when a changed evidence opportunity or consequential uncertainty makes further learning plausible within explicit resources and authority. That is conditional endorsement of further research, not adoption of this procedure or permission to launch it.

The purpose remains useful knowledge work across domains through development and use of methodologies. Learning can justify an investigation before delivery benefits are known. The result does not require a compression score, shorter text, or an immediate service consumer. It also does not justify indefinite exploration.

This account integrates the [fourth integration](../turn-04/integration.md), its predecessors, [A's proposal](./explorer-a.md), [B's challenge](./explorer-b.md), and the [final critique](./critic.md), under [coordination](./coordination.md). Findings about the run are documentary observations. The practical mechanisms below remain conjectures. Source claims are identified separately. Stipulated logical examples demonstrate only their stated implications; no behavioral experiment, causal discovery, automatic-search superiority, or delivery improvement was demonstrated.

## What changed—and what did not

The actual prior matters. The first trial already admitted surprise-led inquiry. Its compression branch was parked without a retained comparative rationale. This run neither introduced curiosity into an incurious process nor recovered that missing reason.

The [first integration](../turn-01/integration.md) located a mismatch between a recovery explanation's application and its supporting history. The [second](../turn-02/integration.md) resolved it mainly as a correction of support plus acquisition gaps. The scoped mechanism—noticed blockage followed by rerouting—survived. Continued success did not establish that recovery occurred; withdrawing an unsupported example did not refute the mechanism. This was a local documentary correction, not novel causal theory.

The [third integration](../turn-03/integration.md) located an additional assurance premise needed to move from “confidence does not entail soundness” to a requirement for a separate acceptance operation. It did not establish that confidence-only acceptance is safe. Its stipulated example illustrated non-entailment, already present in the incumbent. The strongest local rival remained the incumbent interpreted as a scoped high-assurance policy with a potentially adequate justification.

Turn four made preservation requirements more concrete. Clarifying the confidence argument must not erase its permissions for validated triage, distinction between calibration and discrimination, or openness to validated internal probes. Other examples separated restoring captured text, reconstructing an encounter, discovering relevance, and explaining an outcome. These are useful analytical distinctions, not evidence that broad retention caused discovery or that a replacement account is superior.

The cumulative learning is therefore a more accurate account of support, unresolved premises, and inquiry limits. It is not a demonstrated general theory of curiosity. Repeated agreement among workers who read earlier integrations is not independent confirmation.

## Surviving positive steering

The proposed investment is **a bounded opportunity to change understanding**, rather than a topic, document, or promised score. Four candidate mechanisms give this proposal practical content.

1. **Originate questions from unresolved relations.** Inspect unexplained successes, consequential differences among cases assigned one explanation, transitions between claims, and exclusions caused by evidence selection. Ask what the actual account leaves unsettled. A visible failure is unnecessary; so is a forced unification. Discovering that the incumbent already explains the case is a legitimate result.
2. **Allow preliminary clarification.** A concrete uncertainty can warrant reading before a complete rival or decisive test exists. Clarification may supply vocabulary, dissolve a question, locate a missing premise, or reveal inaccessible evidence. Unfamiliar directions sometimes require more than one quick pass; the present record does not determine an adequate allowance.
3. **Compare investments qualitatively.** Consider prospective learning, evidence access, effort, authority, and the strongest alternative inquiry. A reachable correction can outrank a broader attribution question whose decisive encounter is unavailable. Conversely, a difficult direction can merit an explicit research allowance when familiar cases leave an important uncertainty untouched. Record the deciding consideration rather than invent a total numerical ranking.
4. **Renew for a changed allocation reason.** More interpretations alone do not warrant continuation. An identified unread argument, newly reachable encounter, or contrary case can change what another investment could settle. A dissolved question or irreducible acquisition gap can justify stopping. This makes renewal inspectable, but remains vulnerable to rationalized promises of future learning.

These mechanisms address selection before an answer exists. They do not solve autonomous discovery across unfamiliar fields. The run mostly used retained Commonplace arguments and supplied examples, including the confidence note already named in the brief. Accessibility may have favored familiar questions; we lack the omitted-opportunity evidence needed to judge that bias.

## Compression: useful emphasis, unisolated contribution

The retained Quotes in [Formal Theory of Creativity](../../../../sources/schmidhuber-formal-theory-creativity.ingest.md) supply source-side distinctions: Section III-B proposes learning which actions yield expected model improvement; Section II-B counts both predictor and deviations in loss-free compression; Section V warns that recent-history evaluation can miss deterioration on older history. Its warning about persistent prediction error includes learner limitations as well as noise. These claims concern specified learning arrangements, not a tested procedure for qualitative KB inquiry.

Our translation is conjectural. Compression directed attention toward explanations spanning several cases and toward losses hidden by recent improvement. It supplied useful questions, not a measured coding gain. An account can preserve more consequential distinctions while becoming longer. Fewer files, uniform vocabulary, and shorter prose do not establish understanding. No free-form prose exception summary has been specified as a lossless residual.

The strongest rival is ordinary explanation-seeking with bounded reconnaissance, explicit alternatives, recoverable originals, known counterexamples, and fresh acquisition. It can implement every surviving practice. This establishes compatibility, not equal performance: the rival is a generous conceptual account, not an executed comparator. A compression prompt might still improve attention, timing, or effort even if it finds no unique question. No such additional contribution was isolated here.

The broader focused literature search on the lossy-compression axis was **not completed**. Comparisons among compression progress, learning progress, information gain, acquisition costs, minimum-description-length model comparison, and causal credit assignment remain ungrounded literature leads. They are not source-backed conclusions, evidence of novelty, or established transfers into Commonplace.

## Evidence that progress must leave available

Acquisition, retention, and reconsideration have different jobs. The following requirements keep a particular judgment open to challenge; they do not select a storage architecture.

**Acquire what the judgment requires.** Attribution needs encounters and contribution sequences, not only accepted outcomes. Checking-coverage claims need the method's actual scope. Assurance judgments need the governing requirement and justification; empirical validation additionally needs the property, population, method, results, and applicability limits. Better summaries cannot supply information never acquired.

**Retain the comparison basis.** Keep actual incumbent passages with qualifications, candidate changes, version identity where wording matters, initial comparison cases, selection routes, strongest rivals, and parking reasons. Preserve whether cases were stipulated or observed. For this run, important details include search syntax and scope, positional relations between quotations and sources, chronology, critic corrections, coordinator choices, and cost exclusions. A final synopsis alone conceals how claims were narrowed.

**Bring relevant evidence back into consideration.** Before claiming improvement, revisit old permissions and counterexamples, not just prohibitions. Mark new cases as additions rather than silently replacing inconvenient old ones. When a known search limitation matters, use a route capable of exposing its exclusions; rephrasing the same search may not do so. Changed use or newly available evidence can reopen a parked question.

This supports qualitative progress judgments: a warranted attribution changes, an implication is ruled out, a missing condition becomes explicit, or an older permission survives clarification. Exact event prediction is not required. But comparison on common cases can inherit the original selector's omissions, and candidate and cases developed together provide weak independent assessment.

Never-acquired evidence, discarded evidence, and retained-but-unconsulted evidence require different remedies. Fresh acquisition can illuminate a general mechanism without reconstructing an old encounter. A version locator is only a contingent recovery route; access and reacquisition cost are not guaranteed. If different histories leave the same accessible record, that record cannot distinguish them. Thus local improvement can hide deterioration elsewhere, and retained material can remain effectively forgotten. Neither full-history review nor keeping everything follows as a feasible remedy.

## Learning, delivery, resources, and role contributions

Correcting unsupported attribution has learning value because it changes warranted inference. Faster completion, fewer failures, or saved operator attention would be delivery benefits requiring separate evidence. Neither clearer prose nor absence of between-turn operator intervention establishes those benefits. Research may earn an allocation without an immediate customer, but must remain within its authorized allowance rather than claim unlimited future utility.

The tier model survives as a resource checklist, not an adopted maximand: operator attention, model work, and classical computation impose different constraints. Context capacity, acquisition, navigation, interpretation, privacy, and delayed delivery also matter. Moving work to a cheaper tier does not establish value or reliability. Authority limits feasible choices before their attractiveness is compared.

The [pre-final cost record](../costs-before-turn-05.md) covers **sixteen workers only**: $15.179444 harness-reported model cost and 49.27 summed worker minutes. It excludes final-turn work, coordinator work, operator attention, and unknown counterfactual opportunity costs. It is neither final accounting nor evidence of waste or cost-effectiveness. The first trial is not a matched baseline; the coordinator will supply final totals separately.

Documentary role contributions are visible. The turn-one critic checked the recovery mechanism's actual scope. Turn-three exploration located the assurance transition; criticism qualified the incumbent reading and strengthened the renewal condition. Coordination redirected exclusive verification work toward unfamiliar-question selection and later toward preservation. These contributions informed subsequent integrations. They do not show that freshness caused the corrections, that four roles were necessary, or that the sequence outperformed a continuous worker. Coordinator-directed choices also preclude attributing the trajectory solely to automatic curiosity.

## Disposition and live questions

**The revised instructions merit selective further use as research candidates; the same five-turn procedure has not earned automatic repetition.** Stop this documentary sequence. Another inquiry is conditionally worthwhile if it can change a consequential decision through new evidence or clarification, with separate authorization and bounded resources. A literature inquiry could qualify even before a comparator is fully specified.

Accept the critic's correction to A: **advance selection disagreement is not a universal admission gate.** Different approaches may choose the same question but differ in recognition time, acquisition effort, missed alternatives, preservation of qualifications, or warranted learning. A comparative trial needs an observable discriminating consequence, not necessarily different nominations. Initial clarification may instead determine whether a meaningful comparison exists. Any future comparison should settle comparably supported instructions before outcomes, rather than improve one side retrospectively.

Park retrospective causal attribution for missing encounters; exclusive assurance-policy resolution for unsettled authoritative premises and narrower relevance; numerical prose compression for absent measurement semantics; broader incidental retention for unmeasured benefit and cost; and controller or storage design for scope and adjacent ownership. None is refuted. Optional connection discovery through `cp-skill-connect` would require separate authorization and should not postpone closure.

The live questions are whether the shared-explanation emphasis changes inquiry yield or effort; how unfamiliar directions earn sufficient acquisition time; when broader retention beats feasible reacquisition; and which role contributions justify their cost. The result is complete as a documentary disposition and partial on those questions, literature grounding, and comparative effectiveness. Preserve all first-trial artifacts and this run's evidence. No new protocol, implementation, extraction, or deletion follows from this recommendation.
