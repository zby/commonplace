# Turn 5 critique: stop repetition, not question discovery

## Verdict and evidence boundary

**Close this documentary run, retain learning-oriented inquiry as a candidate practice, and do not adopt compression as a governing objective. Qualify A's requirement for prior selection disagreement: it is too restrictive as a condition for every further inquiry.** B's broader condition—a consequential uncertainty, reachable evidence, and bounded resources—is preferable, provided “reachable” allows an initial investment to discover what evidence matters.

This critique reads [A](./explorer-a.md), [B](./explorer-b.md), all four prior integrations, [current coordination](./coordination.md), and the [pre-final costs](../costs-before-turn-05.md). I also inspected the role outputs and coordination cited below. These support documentary observations about contributions and decisions, not causal attribution. Recommendations are research judgments; proposed mechanisms remain conjectures. No behavioral advantage or delivery benefit has been demonstrated. No new external-source conclusion is asserted; prior source summaries are not independent support.

## What actually survives

The motivating problem is selection before a finished proposal exists. The actual first-trial prior already admitted surprise-led inquiry. This run therefore cannot claim to have introduced curiosity, or explain compression's earlier parking when its comparative rationale was not retained.

The surviving proposal supplies useful places to look: unexplained successes, contrasts within an account, unsupported inferential transitions, and exclusions produced by evidence selection. It allows clarification before a finished rivalry, compares prospective learning with evidence access and effort, and asks renewal to change the allocation reason. Those are practical research prompts, not a validated automatic selector.

The findings also resisted stronger stories. Turn 2 mainly corrected supporting examples while leaving the scoped recovery mechanism intact. Turn 3 located a missing assurance premise rather than demonstrating a better acceptance policy. Turn 4 separated preservation from encounter reconstruction, relevance discovery, and explanation. These changes can have learning value without saving time or serving an immediate customer. They do not establish that this sequence was the best available investment.

The final account should keep that asymmetry: the research produced identifiable corrections, while its distinctive steering effectiveness remains unknown. Neither coherent synthesis nor repeated agreement supplies the missing comparison.

## Is ordinary explanation-seeking a strong rival?

Yes, as an account of what could produce these findings. It can inspect assumptions, seek common explanations, investigate successful outcomes, consult counterexamples, and acquire unfamiliar vocabulary. Restricting it to local repair would artificially favor curiosity. Both explorers correctly avoid that contrast.

But “ordinary explanation-seeking” is currently a generous conceptual rival, not a specified comparator with demonstrated performance. Giving it every useful safeguard after observing this run establishes compatibility, not equal cost or equal likelihood of discovering the same questions. Conversely, calling all those safeguards compression would erase the distinction being assessed.

The strongest challenge is therefore narrower than “compression adds nothing”: no additional contribution has been isolated here. A shared-structure prompt might change attention, timing, evidence consultation, or effort even when it produces no unique question. Its usefulness would not require novelty relative to the literature. A future comparison would need comparably supported inquiry practices and opportunities, with their instructions settled before outcomes rather than repaired asymmetrically afterward.

## Selection disagreement is not a universal admission gate

A proposes establishing observable disagreement on the same material before following competing routes. This can expose whether two purported policies differ locally. It is not necessary for every informative comparison, and A's closing “absent an obtainable disagreement … stop” overstates the condition.

Two approaches could select the same question but differ in time to recognition, acquisition costs, missed alternatives, preservation of qualifications, or warranted learning. Requiring different nominations would exclude those differences. It would also favor familiar material where researchers can already formulate alternatives, despite the [third integration](../turn-03/integration.md) explicitly protecting preliminary clarification.

Separate three decisions. Another synthesis of unchanged evidence lacks a clear reason now. A bounded inquiry into an unfamiliar mechanism or literature gap could instead clarify whether a meaningful comparison exists; it need not promise selection disagreement beforehand. A comparative trial needs an observable consequence that could discriminate the approaches, but that consequence need not be a different selected question.

These are conditions for reconsideration, not a launch recommendation. Clarification still needs an authorized allowance, a concrete uncertainty or acquisition opportunity, and a return that changes what can be decided. Vocabulary acquisition may justify a larger initial allowance than one quick pass. Repeatedly declaring unfamiliarity does not justify indefinite renewal. The present record cannot determine the right allowance.

These are identifiable contributions with documentary uptake. They do not establish that freshness caused them or that a continuous worker would miss them. The stipulated probability illustration demonstrates non-entailment within its premises, not new model behavior or a new incumbent distinction.

Coordinator contributions also matter. [Turn-3 coordination](../turn-03/coordination.md) redirected exclusive verification work toward unfamiliar-question selection; [turn-4 coordination](../turn-04/coordination.md) prioritized preservation over exclusive assurance resolution. Automatic steering cannot receive sole credit for a trajectory actively selected by the coordinator. No role-removal comparison establishes four-role necessity, and sequential access to integrations prevents treating agreement as independent confirmation.

## Consequential limits for the final report

The cost boundary must remain explicit: sixteen pre-final workers cost $15.179444 and 49.27 summed worker minutes, excluding coordinator work, operator attention, and final-turn work. These figures neither establish waste nor cost-effectiveness. No recorded between-turn operator intervention does not mean zero total operator burden. The first trial is not a matched baseline. Resource tiers are useful categories, not an adopted maximand.

Retention recommendations likewise remain question-relative. Preserve actual incumbent passages and qualifications, comparison cases, selection routes, stipulated status, corrections, coordinator decisions, rival explanations, parking reasons, and cost exclusions. A final synopsis alone would hide how the account became qualified. Exact captured text cannot restore an unrecorded encounter; a version locator does not guarantee future access. Never-acquired, discarded, and retained-but-unconsulted evidence need different remedies. Broader capture also incurs acquisition, navigation, interpretation, privacy, and context costs. No result justifies keeping everything or guarantees discovery from storage.

The literature gap is material to claims of distinctness, not a reason to discard the learning. B's proposed comparisons among compression progress, learning progress, information gain, acquisition-cost research, and causal credit assignment remain **ungrounded leads**. The requested focused search is incomplete. The final report must not claim novelty, literature consensus, or established transfer into qualitative KB inquiry.

## Disposition and parked alternatives

Stop this sequence after final integration. Retain the candidate practices without presenting them as adopted policy. Reconsider only for a changed evidence opportunity or consequential unresolved decision, with separate authorization and resource bounds; do not require advance selection disagreement for exploratory reading.

Park retrospective attribution because encounter evidence is missing; exclusive assurance-policy resolution because authoritative premises remain unsettled and its scope is narrower; numerical prose compression because measurement semantics are absent; broader incidental retention because benefit and cost are unmeasured; and controller or storage design because scope and adjacent ownership exclude them. These are parking reasons, not refutations. Optional connection discovery through `cp-skill-connect` requires separate authorization and should not postpone closure.

The final result can be complete as a documentary account while remaining partial on comparative effectiveness, unfamiliar-opportunity selection, and literature grounding. That is a supported stopping position, not rejection of curiosity or a claim that learning must immediately pay for delivery.
