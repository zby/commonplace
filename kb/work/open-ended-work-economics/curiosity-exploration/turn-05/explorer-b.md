# Explorer B: stop the compression-specific trial, retain the learning

## Disposition and evidence boundary

**Finish this bounded run without adopting compression-driven steering or renewing the same prose-only procedure.** Preserve its useful inquiry practices as candidates compatible with ordinary explanation-seeking. Another trial is worth considering only when it can resolve a different, consequential uncertainty with reachable evidence and explicit resource bounds. This is a recommendation, not authority to launch work.

The motivating problem remains real: evaluating finished proposals does not explain which unfamiliar investigation should receive effort. But the [fourth integration](../turn-04/integration.md) leaves compression with an emphasis on shared understanding, not additional selection power. Successive qualifications have made the proposal defensible while leaving it increasingly indistinguishable from its strongest ordinary rival.

This judgment uses all four integrations, sampled actual role outputs, coordination, and the [pre-final costs](../costs-before-turn-05.md). It does not use current Explorer A. Findings about this run are documentary observations; recommendations and expected advantages are conjectures. No external-source conclusion is asserted here. Prior integrations' literature summaries are not independent support. No behavioral experiment, causal advantage, or delivery benefit has been demonstrated.

## What would compression add?

The strongest positive rival to stopping is modest, not a universal compressor: explicitly directing attention toward shared explanations and unexplained successes might make valuable relations salient earlier. Comparing an account against old cases might reveal losses that locally persuasive revisions conceal. A heuristic can help even when ordinary inquiry could eventually find the same question. Unique discoveries and numerical coding gains are not necessary conditions for usefulness.

Yet this run supplies no comparison establishing that advantage. The [first integration](../turn-01/integration.md) retained ordinary explanation-seeking with explicit alternatives; the [second](../turn-02/integration.md) recommended bounded contrastive question selection rather than a compression objective. The [third](../turn-03/integration.md) added preliminary clarification and a renewal condition. The fourth added question-relative preservation and alternative consultation. All can be practiced without compression language.

The objection is therefore not “nothing was learned.” It is that learning obtained under an instruction does not establish that instruction's distinctive contribution. Correction of historical support, locating a missing assurance premise, and separating restoration from discovery are useful. None demonstrates that compression supplied a better choice among available investigations. Nor does a retrospective claim that several findings share a structure explain why these questions deserved the next expenditure beforehand.

Keeping compression as an optional prompt is reasonable. Adopting it as governing steering would require a consequential difference in choices, timing, effort, or resulting understanding. Calling every explanatory improvement compression would remove the distinction we need to assess. Insisting on literal code length instead would exclude legitimate qualitative learning without solving this selection problem.

## Missing opportunities, acquisition, and literature

The run still knows more about evaluating accessible questions than discovering unfamiliar opportunities. Its examples mostly start from retained Commonplace arguments, successful changes, and supplied cases. The confidence note was already offered in the brief. These are legitimate research materials, but they do not represent the opportunities outside the present vocabulary or documentary neighborhood.

A bounded reading pass can expose an unfamiliar question. It can also repeatedly favor topics whose significance becomes legible quickly. The current renewal condition—show what changed in the allocation reason—makes choices inspectable, but does not establish that slower vocabulary acquisition is inferior. Equally, unlimited preparation cannot be justified by asserting that valuable topics take time. We lack evidence about which unfamiliar directions were considered, their acquisition demands, and what was forgone. An already-formulated parked branch cannot stand in for never-noticed possibilities.

Acquisition is not solved by retaining better summaries. An outcome record may omit the encounter that would distinguish substitution from non-exposure. A versioned argument may retain qualifications without recording who used them. More retained material may enable future challenge, but its acquisition, navigation, interpretation, and consultation consume resources. The record does not establish how often broader capture produces otherwise inaccessible learning, or whether fresh acquisition would do better.

The requested focused literature search remains incomplete. No returned search result is established by the brief or these integrations. Relevant **ungrounded leads**, not findings, include comparisons among compression progress, learning progress and information gain; active learning under acquisition costs; minimum-description-length approaches to model comparison; and causal credit assignment when several inputs can explain success. The useful reading questions are specific: what chooses a learning opportunity before progress is known, how costly representation learning is handled, and what evidence separates improved selection from improved evaluation. No claim of novelty, literature consensus, or practical transfer follows from these leads. No source destination was allocated, so I have not converted them into source-backed claims.

## What the fresh roles actually contributed

The record supports particular contributions, not the necessity of four fresh roles. The [turn-1 critic](../turn-01/critic.md) checked the recovery note's actual scope and distinguished a misapplied supporting example from refutation of its conditional mechanism. The integration retained that correction. This is a concrete check against overstating discovery, not merely stylistic criticism.

[Turn-3 Explorer B](../turn-03/explorer-b.md) located the transition from non-entailment to a separate acceptance operation. Its stipulated illustration repeated the incumbent logical distinction; its potentially useful addition was identifying the assurance premise needed for the stronger conclusion. The [critic](../turn-03/critic.md) then challenged B's permissive interpretation against the actual heading and description. It also required renewal to change the prior allocation decision, rather than merely add interpretations. The integration carried those qualifications forward. Thus criticism sometimes reduced claims and improved the next decision instead of demanding an immediate service consumer.

Coordination also mattered. [Turn-3 coordination](../turn-03/coordination.md) declined exclusive pursuit of the integrator's reachable verification question and redirected attention toward unfamiliar opportunities. [Turn-4 coordination](../turn-04/coordination.md) selected preservation rather than exclusive assurance-policy resolution. These are observable interventions in scheduling by the coordinator. The trajectory cannot be attributed to compression or autonomous explorer discovery alone.

Freshness could have helped workers challenge inherited interpretations. It could also have imposed repeated reconstruction while shared instructions and prior integrations constrained their apparent independence. We have no same-task continuous-worker comparison, role removal, or counterfactual showing which correction would disappear without a critic. Sequential reading and integration further prevent treating agreement as independent confirmation. The warranted conclusion is that these particular roles supplied identifiable contributions; necessity, superiority, and optimal role count remain unknown.

## Resources without a service-first veto

Before turn five, sixteen workers consumed $15.179444 in harness-reported model cost and 49.27 summed worker minutes, with 665,121 input, 48,957 output, and 6,080,384 cache-read tokens. These are measured accounting quantities, not a valuation of learning. Coordinator work, operator attention, and counterfactual opportunity costs are excluded or unknown. Sequential elapsed worker time omits coordination gaps. Final-turn costs are not yet included.

No new operator intervention between turns is recorded after permission to complete all five. That shows the run proceeded under delegated authority, not that it saved operator judgment overall. Repeatedly reading and qualifying existing records may be affordable; it may also displace better acquisition. Neither interpretation follows from the dollar total. The first trial is not a matched baseline, so comparing totals cannot establish improvement.

Stopping does not require proving that this learning had no immediate customer. Intrinsic learning can justify resources independently of delivery savings. The problem is renewal under diminishing discrimination: another synthesis of the same evidence would probably restate the unresolved rivalry rather than resolve it. That forecast is a judgment, not a measured return. Operator attention, model work, and classical compute remain useful resource categories; moving work downward among them is not the adopted objective.

## A discriminating decision and recoverability

The available decision is **stop repetition now; condition reconsideration on an evidence change**. A future authorized comparison could ask whether the compression emphasis brings consequential questions to assessment earlier or with less inquiry effort than ordinary explanation-seeking, under comparable opportunities and resource bounds. It should allow non-unifying gains and unfamiliar directions, not select only cases already suited to shared explanations. Different nominations alone would not suffice: their warranted learning and acquisition costs would matter. This is a discriminating question, not a new protocol or commissioned experiment.

A bounded literature inquiry could precede that decision if separately authorized. It would earn its cost by clarifying a genuinely different steering mechanism or exposing why the comparison is ill-posed, not by adding prestigious names. If both accounts continue recommending the same choices for the same reasons, stop treating them as distinct operational alternatives in this scope. If a consequential difference emerges but the necessary evidence is unavailable, retain the partial result rather than expand a framework.

Keep recoverable the actual incumbents and qualifications, not only final syntheses; the critic's corrections; question assignments and coordinator overrides; strongest rejected alternatives and parking reasons; comparison cases and their stipulated status; and cost boundaries. Preserve the distinction between never-acquired evidence, discarded detail, and retained-but-unconsulted material. The missing first-trial parking rationale and unavailable encounter sequences must remain missing, not reconstructed from today's preferred account.

Park retrospective causal attribution for missing evidence; exclusive assurance resolution because it needs authoritative premises and does not settle steering; broader incidental retention for unmeasured benefit and cost; numerical prose compression for absent measurement semantics; and architecture work for ownership and scope. These alternatives are not refuted. Optional connection discovery through `cp-skill-connect` would require separate authorization and should not displace closure. The result is complete as a documentary disposition, partial on comparative effectiveness, unfamiliar-opportunity selection, and literature grounding.
