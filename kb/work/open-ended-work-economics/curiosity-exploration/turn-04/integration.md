Exact textual retention also has a narrower achievement than lossless recovery of an encounter. Restored text need not be consulted; consultation need not reveal relevance; relevance does not establish an explanation. These separations are the strongest conceptual result of this turn.

## Surviving acquisition, retention, and reconsideration requirements

**Acquire what the proposed judgment needs.** A documentary preservation claim needs the actual incumbent, candidate wording, qualifications, and comparison cases. Resolving assurance additionally needs the intended requirement and justification. Empirical validation needs the evaluated property, population, method, results, and applicability limits. A statement that validation occurred supplies none of those details. Acquire transient consequential evidence while available when its loss would prevent the intended judgment; this is not authority to capture entire environments.

**Keep the comparison open to disagreement.** Preserve the original passages and version identity, candidate outline, stipulated status of cases, judgments, strongest rival, and unresolved premises. For these examples, recoverability depends on syntax and search scope, chronology, positional relations, and explicit acknowledgment that a parking reason is absent. Version locators are contingent recovery routes, not evidence that targets remain accessible. Historical intent cannot be recovered by composing a plausible rationale today.

**Reconsider through a route capable of challenging the exclusion.** Before claiming improvement, revisit old permissions and counterexamples as well as prohibitions. When a known selector limitation matters, use a route that does not repeat that limitation. Two differently phrased searches are not necessarily independent. Changed use—from triage to acceptance, for example—can warrant reconsidering a previously peripheral qualification. These are candidate consultation triggers, not an adopted schedule or storage architecture.

Retaining some material outside the favored explanation remains a conjectural hedge. No present rule identifies every detail that will matter later. If different histories leave the same accessible record, that record cannot distinguish them. Fresh acquisition may reveal a general limitation without recovering its historical instance. Never-acquired evidence, discarded detail, and unconsulted retained material therefore require different remedies, and sometimes uncertainty is irreducible.

## Strongest rival and the final decision

The strongest combined rival is **ordinary explanation-seeking with bounded reconnaissance, concise records, recoverable originals, known counterexamples, and fresh acquisition**. It can originate questions without a service request and can consult differently when warranted. Nearly all surviving safeguards fit it. Compression has supplied an emphasis on shared understanding and historical deterioration, not demonstrated additional selection power.

Broader incidental retention would gain support from consequential questions traceably originating in material that concise records and feasible fresh acquisition would not expose. Against it count successful reacquisition, unproductive consultation, and capture effort displacing more informative encounters. Costs include navigation, interpretation, context capacity, privacy constraints, and delayed delivery—not storage alone. Costs remain unaggregated in coordination and unknown to this worker; counterfactual returns are also unknown.

The final turn should settle a recommendation, not elaborate a framework. Retain as candidate positive steering: seek unresolved relations in failures or successes; allow bounded clarification before a finished rivalry exists; compare prospective learning, evidence reachability, effort, and authority; renew only when the allocation reason changes. Then ask whether another trial can distinguish this account from the strongest rival at an informative resource cost. Learning value must remain separate from task benefit. Neither immediate service payoff nor indefinite curiosity has priority by default. The tier model can organize resource constraints without making downward transfer its maximand.

Do not recommend another prose-only repetition merely because these accounts are coherent. Another trial is warranted only conditionally on a distinct unresolved choice, reachable discriminating evidence, explicit resource bounds, and separate authorization. Otherwise preserve the partial result and stop. This focus serves useful learning through methodological development while leaving automation, whole-KB coverage, unfamiliar-direction selection, and superiority unresolved.

Park exclusive assurance-policy resolution for missing authority and narrower relevance; keep-everything retention for cost and scope; numerical prose compression for absent measurement semantics; historical motive reconstruction for missing evidence; and storage design for adjacent ownership.
