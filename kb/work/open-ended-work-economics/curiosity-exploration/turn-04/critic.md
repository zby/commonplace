# Turn 4 critique: preserve a challenge route without promising discovery

## Judgment

**Retain question-relative evidence requirements; do not promote them into a demonstrated retention policy.** [Explorer A](./explorer-a.md) supplies a useful preservation comparison. [Explorer B](./explorer-b.md) explains why preserving an answer need not preserve opportunities to question it. Neither establishes that broader retention or alternative consultation improves inquiry enough to justify its cost. The strongest rival remains ordinary explanation-seeking with concise conclusions, versioned originals where available, and acquisition when a question arises.

This critique follows [coordination](./coordination.md) and the [prior integration](../turn-03/integration.md). That prior already distinguished missing acquisition, discarded evidence, and retained-but-unconsulted material. This turn makes their consequences concrete rather than discovering those categories. I checked the confidence note, tag trace, and quote-checking ADR directly. These are documentary observations, not independent verification of historical behavior. No external-source claim or behavioral experiment supports the recommendations below.

## What follows from the evidence

A's preservation requirements mostly follow **conditional on evaluating this particular proposed clarification**. The actual [confidence note](../../../../theory/generation-confidence-does-not-by-itself-certify-soundness.md) permits validated triage, separates calibration from discrimination, and leaves validated probes open. A candidate that prohibits all confidence use or equates a distinct signal with a reliable verifier would contradict those qualifications. Keeping the relevant passages accessible is therefore warranted for assessing that candidate. It does not follow that every future inquiry must carry every qualification in its active context.

A's opening claim that progress is defensible as better separation of premises needs its later qualification attached: better separation is a proposed local clarification, not an established improvement over the incumbent read with its linked context. The constructed candidate and comparison cases were developed together. Passing them would show preservation against selected challenges, not discovery of a superior account. Moreover, A supplies an account outline and conditional requirements, not a completed replacement whose inferential coverage has been independently assessed. The incumbent's strongest reading—as a scoped high-assurance policy—remains undefeated without its intended assurance requirement.

B's evidence is likewise narrower than a general recommendation. The [tag trace](../../../../theory/evidence/tag-readme-trace-observed-causal-connection.md) records a broader check exposing a block-YAML member missed by the search recipe. This supports examining an evidence selector's limitation. It does not show that speculative retention caused discovery, or that periodic alternative consultation would have found the omission sooner. [ADR 046](../../../../reference/adr/046-verbatim-quotes-are-validated-against-their-cited-source.md) documents why URL sets lack positional information needed for pairing. That supports retaining or reacquiring positions for that operation, not indiscriminate capture. The missing parking rationale supports admitting historical uncertainty; it supplies no measured return from capturing more future rationales.

Thus the defensible requirement is conditional: preserve or retain an accessible route to the evidence needed for a claimed comparison. Broader incidental retention remains a conjecture about future inquiry.

## Four achievements that must remain separate

1. **Recovery of bytes.** An accessible exact version can restore its text, including syntax and ordering. A URL set cannot identify which of several possible original arrangements occurred. This is a stipulated demonstration of non-uniqueness, not an observed loss experiment.
2. **Reconstruction of the encounter.** Restoring text does not establish what the worker saw, which query excluded material, what was noticed, or why a choice was made. These need additional contemporaneous evidence where they matter. B's opening suggestion that reconstructing acquired text preserves what was encountered should be narrowed to the captured textual portion.
3. **Discovery of relevance.** Even a complete captured portion does not make its consequential detail noticeable. A different question, query, comparison, or new observation may reveal relevance. Conversely, a later encounter can reveal a general problem without recovering its historical instance.
4. **Explanation.** Once relevance is noticed, a causal or inferential account still needs support. Finding the omitted YAML detail does not establish earlier reader compensation. Finding no parking reason does not establish why compression was rejected. Preservation makes some explanations criticizable; it does not make them true.

These distinctions constrain recoverability needs. Preserve the actual incumbent and candidate wording, stipulated cases, comparison judgments, and unresolved assurance premise. For the documentary cases, preserve syntax and search scope, chronology, positional relations, and the explicit absence of a comparative parking reason. Version locators help only while their targets remain accessible. None recovers unrecorded intent.

## Can advice expose unseen omissions?

Yes, sometimes, without guaranteeing detection. B's strongest practical advice is to consult through a route that does not repeat the known selector limitation. A parser-based check can challenge a text-pattern search specifically because their coverage differs. Calling any second route “independent” would overstate this: two differently worded searches can share the same exclusion.

For genuinely unforeseen omissions, neither researcher can specify the decisive route in advance. “Retain something outside the favored explanation” is a sensible hedge, but selection still occurs: which something, how much, and at whose interpretive cost? Incidental material selected after its importance is known is not evidence that the earlier worker could have chosen it economically. Advice should promise an additional opportunity for challenge, identify the exclusion it addresses when known, and state what remains unexamined. It should not promise preservation of future question formation.

The practical limit is not a reason to require immediate service payoff. Learning can justify a bounded consultation whose result is a new distinction or a dissolved question. It is a reason to keep that learning return separate from faster delivery, fewer errors, or superior search allocation, none of which was measured.

## Strongest rival, surviving costs, and discrimination

The strongest combined rival is **ordinary explanation-seeking supported by concise records and recoverable originals, with fresh acquisition supplying new questions**. It need not keep only polished conclusions, discard all exceptions, or wait for a service request. It can preserve known counterexamples and use a different search when evidence warrants it. This makes the rivalry harder: most proposed safeguards are compatible with the rival rather than distinctive consequences of compression steering.

Broader incidental retention would earn additional support if a later consequential question demonstrably originated from material that concise records and feasible fresh acquisition would not have exposed. Against it count cases where reacquisition suffices, incidental consultation produces mostly false leads, or preservation effort displaces more informative encounters. Relevant costs include capture, navigation, interpretation, privacy constraints, and delayed new acquisition—not storage alone. No comparison here measures these costs or reconstructs the unchosen investigations' returns.

A further blind spot survives: accessible, familiar documents attract repeated analysis while unfamiliar directions need vocabulary acquisition before their promise becomes legible. Requirements for a changed allocation reason can discourage that investment or reward fluent rationalization. Repeatedly refining the confidence example may itself consume the final turn without resolving question origination.

## Final-turn decision focus

Use the last turn to decide **what warrants another bounded trial, and what unresolved choice such a trial would address**, not to add a retention framework. Separate the surviving documentary safeguards from the untested claim that compression-informed steering adds value beyond the strongest rival. A defensible endpoint may be that qualitative question comparison survives while compression has supplied emphasis, not demonstrated extra selection power.

Park exclusive assurance-policy resolution for missing authority and its narrower relevance; keep-everything retention for cost and scope; numerical prose compression for missing measurement semantics; and historical motive reconstruction for missing evidence. Do not treat these as refuted alternatives. General storage design remains outside ownership. Costs remain unknown to this worker. The critique is complete as documentary assessment and partial on comparative effectiveness; no new literature conclusion or implementation follows.
