## Incumbent and constructed alternative

Call the proposed clarification an **assurance-premise account**: an account that states what evidence and acceptance requirement connect a signal to a decision. This is a comparison label, not a new registered concept. It separates four questions:

1. What does generation confidence denote? A probability under the model's distribution, not soundness by definition.
2. What relationship to soundness has been established, for which task and population? This is an evidential question, not an implication of the definition.
3. What must an acceptance decision establish? A requirement concerning an individual output, a tolerated population-level risk, and an operational prohibition are different possible requirements.
4. Which validated operation or rule satisfies that requirement under the present conditions? Separate evidence does not automatically mean a different model, and a different model does not automatically supply independent evidence.

All but the explicit separation of acceptance requirements are substantially present in the incumbent. The candidate should not claim them as discoveries. Its possible gain is making the dependency between questions three and four visible. Its possible loss is weakening the operational warning while making a confidence-based acceptance rule sound available without supporting validation.

The strongest local rival is the incumbent read as a scoped high-assurance policy: accepting an output requires a separately validated check because the intended assurance demands evidence about that output beyond generation probability. Under that premise, the candidate may merely unpack an existing rationale. The note's linked [verification account](../../../../theory/the-boundary-of-automation-is-the-boundary-of-verification.md) emphasizes per-instance discrimination and separately acknowledges error-cost tolerance. That documentary context strengthens the rival; it does not establish the intended assurance threshold or validate any particular checker. I do not use that note's external-source summaries as empirical support.

## Fixed comparison cases: preservation before improvement

The following cases are constructed questions put to both texts, not observed reader responses. They expose different consequences without requiring exact event predictions.

**Bare confidence.** Suppose only a high next-token probability is supplied. Both accounts must refuse the inference that the continuation is therefore true or valid. The same probability premise is compatible with stipulated true and false continuations. That logical illustration demonstrates non-entailment within its assumptions; it neither discovers a new result nor estimates errors. A candidate saying “confidence becomes soundness when assurance is sufficient” loses the distinction by making assurance circular.

**Validated review prioritization.** Suppose task-specific evaluation supports calibration and discrimination adequate for prioritizing review. The incumbent permits triage within that validation's limits. The candidate must preserve that permission without converting prioritization into certification. A compressed slogan such as “confidence is not evidence” fails this case, even if it retains the bare-confidence warning. Evidence of loss is the contradiction between that slogan and the incumbent's triage paragraph; no model experiment is needed to identify it.

**Calibration without discrimination.** Suppose only aggregate calibration is given. Neither account should treat it as identifying which particular answer is wrong. The candidate's phrase “validated relationship” is inadequate unless it preserves what was validated. A clarification that uses one general assurance label for both properties is shorter but less informative. The missing distinction becomes visible only when this case, not merely the successful triage case, is consulted.

**Previously validated acceptance rule.** Stipulate a rule supported for a declared population and permitted risk, with no additional checker run on each accepted answer. This stipulation does not show that such support exists for any real system. It asks whether non-entailment alone prohibits the arrangement. It does not: the rule introduces premises beyond probability alone. Whether the arrangement meets *high assurance* remains unresolved. Conversely, if assurance requires a particular proof obligation on every accepted artifact, an aggregate relationship alone does not discharge it. These contrasting requirements locate the premise that separates policies; they do not justify relaxing the incumbent.

**Internal probe or generated self-assessment.** The incumbent distinguishes verbal self-assessment from the probability producing the original answer. It permits a trained detector, probe, or adversarial re-derivation to recover a distinct signal, conditional on validation, and denies independence by construction. The candidate must preserve all three points. “Use an independent verifier” erases the open probe possibility if independence means external origin; “use another signal” erases validation if distinctness is treated as reliability. No probe performance is established here.

**Direction of association.** Neither account may infer that confident answers are usually false. The incumbent explicitly separates a population-specific anti-correlation hypothesis from the deductive claim. Removing that paragraph because it seems peripheral would leave future readers less able to resist a common overextension. Its preservation matters even though the present assurance question does not require estimating association.

These cases show how a proposed simplification could lose consequential distinctions. They do not show that the fully qualified candidate actually loses them, or that readers understand it better. The candidate passes this constructed comparison only conditionally: its full account must retain the qualifications just named.

## What evidence would warrant qualitative progress?

A supported local judgment needs the actual before-text, candidate wording, common cases, and explicit judgments about which inferences each permits. The improvement would be a newly explicit dependency: choosing an acceptance arrangement requires an assurance premise not supplied by non-entailment alone. If that dependency was already adequately stated in the relevant context, the result is clarification for this inquiry, not improved KB theory.

Discriminating documentary evidence would be an existing statement of the intended high-assurance requirement and an argument connecting it to the required operation. A requirement for per-artifact evidence would support the rival policy reading. A justified risk-limited acceptance rule satisfying the same declared requirement without an additional runtime checker would challenge a universal checker requirement. Different assurance requirements would not discriminate the accounts; they would explain why both policies can be appropriate in different settings.

Forgetting becomes inspectable when a candidate allows an inference the incumbent blocked, blocks a use it permitted, or removes a question it kept open. That is qualitative evidence of changed inferential coverage, not literal coding gain. A broader or simpler explanation has no automatic priority over preserved differences. Readability, actual consultation, and decision reliability would require separate evidence from use; none was acquired here. Learning value is locating the remaining premise and preserving alternative questions. Task benefit, search superiority, and future error reduction remain unknown.

## Acquisition, retention, and consultation requirements

**Acquisition must match the claim.** This documentary comparison requires full incumbent passages, including the description and qualifications, not a title or source analysis. Resolving policy additionally requires the assurance requirement, its authority, the acceptance rule, and its supporting argument. Empirical use requires the relevant validation population, target property, method, results, and applicability limits. None can be reconstructed from the assertion that validation occurred.

**Retention must permit disagreement.** Keep the compared wording and its version identity, the constructed cases and their stipulated status, the rival policy reading, unresolved premises, and reasons for not deciding them. Preserve the triage, calibration/discrimination, anti-correlation, and probe passages even if the final summary concentrates on assurance. A version locator supports reacquisition only while accessible; it does not preserve never-recorded rationale. No free-form exception list is a lossless residual.

**Consultation must expose omitted distinctions.** Retention alone fails if later work sees only the new assurance summary. Before claiming superiority for a replacement account, reconsider the old permissive cases and open questions as well as its prohibitions. Revisit applicability when the proposed use changes from triage to acceptance, the evaluated population changes, or a probe replaces verbal self-assessment. These are candidate consultation triggers, not an implemented system. An older case can reveal a missing distinction; a new encounter can reveal that the retained set was incomplete.

Never-acquired assurance intent, discarded validation detail, and retained-but-unconsulted caveats require different remedies. Reading can repair the third. Reacquisition may repair the second if an accessible record survives, at unmeasured cost. The first requires new evidence or acknowledgment of uncertainty, not retrospective invention. Even retaining this entire comparison cannot guarantee that a future relevant distinction was acquired.

## Disposition and limits

Retain the assurance-premise account as a comparison aid, not a replacement doctrine. It cannot certify an output, validate a probe, set acceptable risk, establish error prevalence, or prove its own future usefulness. Ordinary explanation-seeking with careful counterexamples remains the strongest steering rival and can produce this result without a compression objective.
