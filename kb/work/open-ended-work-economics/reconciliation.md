# Reconciliation map: the brief's six directions against the library

Closing condition 1 of the [framing](./README.md). Written 2026-10-30 over
three search files under [`search/`](./search/) (317 entries, produced by
Opus workers from `rg` sweeps and description reads) and full reads of the
central notes named below. Entries the workers marked "description only"
were not re-read; a verdict that rests on one is marked.

Each direction gets four lists. **Held** names notes that already state the
result. **Restated** names the brief's claims that the library holds under
other words. **Tension** names where the brief and the library disagree.
**Gap** names what neither holds. A verdict follows.

## Vocabulary

Term decisions are in [vocabulary.md](./vocabulary.md); tolerated double
senses are in the
[vocabulary collision ledger](../../reference/vocabulary-collision-ledger.md).
This map uses "search judgment" for an unretained decision about where
effort goes, "commitment" for a retained resolution, "effort allocation"
for the brief's allocation, and "actor" for a bounded person or agent.

## Direction 1: the nature of commitments

**Held.**
- Explanatory vs constructive commitment, and why retraction differs in kind:
  [theory and capacity building make the same kind of commitment](../../theory/theory-and-capacity-building-make-the-same-kind-of-commitment.md).
- Commitment vs derivation as the ground-truth boundary:
  [commitment, not derivation, creates new ground truth](../../theory/commitment-not-derivation-creates-new-ground-truth.md).
- Belief vs choice after retraction:
  [superseded choices are retained, superseded beliefs are not](../../theory/superseded-choices-are-retained-superseded-beliefs-are-not.md);
  conventions are adopted by decision, not refuted
  ([epistemic stance](../../reference/epistemic-stance.md), point 2).
- "Worth investigating" vs "warranted to adopt" as different conclusions
  with different authority:
  [lightweight search control](../../theory/lightweight-search-control-does-not-license-adoption.md);
  [candidacy evidence licenses escalation, not acceptance](../../theory/candidacy-evidence-licenses-escalation-not-acceptance.md),
  which anchors the split in Peirce's economy of research: a
  pursuit-worthiness judgment is not a truth judgment.
- Reversible adoption vs costly entrenchment, with option value confined to
  timing:
  [current-task fit alone does not warrant costly entrenchment](../../theory/current-task-fit-alone-does-not-warrant-costly-entrenchment.md);
  revision cost as a separate axis from acceptance:
  [prototype standing](../../theory/prototype-standing-is-revision-cost-binding-plus-lost-investment.md).
- A practical commitment resting on an untested linking claim:
  [a proximate target is checked for achievement, not for warrant](../../theory/a-proximate-target-is-checked-for-achievement-not-for-warrant.md).

**Restated.** "Avoid collapsing a claim about truth into a decision about
what is worthwhile" is the candidacy/verdict split. The ladder
considering → adopting → acting → maintaining → retracting exists in pieces
(discovery lifecycle for beliefs; adoption/entrenchment for structures;
supersession for retraction). The economic framing adds only that each rung
has a cost, which the entrenchment note already says for the costly rungs.

**Tension.** The brief wants the guess behind each effort allocation (what
the investment will return) made explicit. The library evaluates search control distributionally:
[a search controller is tested by what it brings to stronger evaluation](../../theory/a-search-controller-is-tested-by-what-it-brings-to-stronger-evaluation.md).
Under that note, individual return guesses need not be retained or
tested; the controller is. This is a real disagreement about what to
retain. Resolving it is the one substantive question direction 1 leaves.

**Gap.**
- No note says what kind of thing an effort-allocation decision is. My
  reading: it is a constructive-style commitment of limited authority and
  horizon. It changes what work exists, its reasons demote to provenance,
  and it is retracted by backtracking rather than by changing one's mind.
  The library already has every piece of that except the classification.
  A paragraph in the explanatory/constructive note would hold it; it does
  not need a note.
- Goals and preferences are not treated as commitments. Objectives are
  declared parameters
  ([self-improvement is relative to a declared objective](../../theory/self-improvement-is-relative-to-a-declared-objective.md)).
  See direction 3 for why the library keeps it that way.

**Verdict.** Mostly held. One tension worth a discriminating test (below)
and one classification paragraph worth adding at extraction.

## Direction 2: bounded rationality and the economics of investigation

**Held.**
- Allocation must precede decisive evidence, and more resources do not
  remove the choice:
  [open-ended improvement allocates search before evaluation](../../theory/open-ended-improvement-allocates-search-before-evaluation.md).
  The note says this "is not merely an economic constraint", which is
  stronger than the brief: the problem is structural, not only budgetary.
- When to act on a guess: [ad hoc explanation can be rational when error is cheap and local](../../theory/ad-hoc-explanation-can-be-rational-when-error-is-cheap-and-local.md).
- When deferral is productive: [productive deferral requires option, evidence and convergence](../../theory/productive-deferral-requires-option-evidence-and-convergence.md).
- Qualitative judgment is the de facto position. Pindyck's option theory is
  kept as structure only ("no portable quantitative threshold"); prototype
  standing glosses "expected" as "anticipated, not probability-weighted";
  the cognitive-architecture scan argues selection values are not one
  number. The exception is the machinery-investment note's "expected
  marginal net return" wording.
- The limiting formal case: a Gödel machine's switch-versus-continue gate
  is an expected-utility proof, and it still leaves search allocation
  unsolved ([Gödel machines](../../theory/goedel-machines-are-a-proof-governed-case-of-self-modification.md)).

**Restated.** "Do not assume numerical probabilities" is already the
library's practice. "How actors decide when to investigate further" has
local answers (cheap-error regime, deferral conditions, ADR 120's rule to
keep searching while a consequential conclusion could change, full-pass
closure) and no unifying note.

**Tension.** None with the library's claims. There is one with the brief's
framing: the library's allocation notes do not derive from economics. They
derive from the structure of open-ended search (evaluation unavailable
before a candidate exists). The economic framing would be an added layer,
not the ground.

**Gap.**
- No treatment of metareasoning or value of computation (Russell–Wefald,
  Horvitz), resource-rational analysis (Lieder–Griffiths), satisficing or
  ecological rationality (Simon, Gigerenzer), opportunity cost, or
  explore/exploit. None of these has an ingest. The
  [bounded-optimality ingest](../../sources/provably-bounded-optimal-agents.ingest.md)
  (Russell–Subramanian) exists and no theory note cites it.
- No explicit statement of the library's position on expected-utility
  reasoning. It is consistent across notes but unwritten.

**What the missing literature would add.** Resource-rational analysis is
the important one, because it is a rival explanation of the workshop's main
claim. It explains apparently crude heuristics as optimal under
computational bounds. On that account a routine is adopted because it is
cheap enough and good enough, and its adoption need not track evidence
about its stability. That is a non-epistemic account of routinization. The
library's practice is on the other side:
[progressive constraining commits only after patterns stabilize](../../theory/progressive-constraining-commits-only-after-patterns-stabilize.md)
makes adoption track evidence. The two accounts predict different things
about when a routine should be relaxed; see the tests below.

**Verdict.** The mechanisms are held, under a structural rather than
economic grounding. The literature check (closing condition 2) needs at
least one ingest: resource-rational analysis first, Russell–Wefald second.
Bounded optimality is already ingested and bears on direction 6 more than
here: it makes the program-plus-architecture, not the reasoner, the unit
whose value is assessed.

## Direction 3: recursion and reflective improvement

**Held.**
- The investment argument for the method level, with its conditions and
  erosion: [an optimal long-run learning strategy invests in its own machinery](../../theory/an-optimal-long-run-learning-strategy-invests-in-its-own-machinery.md).
- Why the level above must sometimes be revised:
  [learning inside a fixed decomposition inherits its mistakes](../../theory/learning-inside-a-fixed-decomposition-inherits-its-mistakes.md).
- What reflection buys and does not buy:
  [reflection makes retained lessons second-order](../../theory/reflection-makes-retained-lessons-second-order.md);
  [reflection buys addressability](../../theory/reflection-buys-addressability.md).
- Machinery at the method level is challengeable but persists by warrant:
  [machinery persists by warrant, not position](../../theory/machinery-persists-by-warrant-not-position-in-a-reflective-loop.md).
- How far reported systems reach up the levels, and that every loop sits
  inside a larger one: the
  [self-revision design space](../self-revision-design-space/README.md)
  workshop and its evidence notes.
- Goal change: [revising an improvement objective is licensed from outside it](../../theory/revising-an-improvement-objective-is-licensed-from-outside-it.md).
  Most apparent goal changes are proxy revision, re-indexing, or surfaced
  under-specification; the residue has no improvement claim available;
  Commonplace shows no instance of the residue.
- Stopping: owned by [reflective-improvement-divergence](../reflective-improvement-divergence/README.md)
  (candidate claim: "episode closure, not convergence"); the shipped rules
  are log-and-defer and full-pass closure.

**Restated.** "An investigation is an investment justified by a conjecture;
selecting the method is another; improving it is another" is the
machinery-investment note generalized by one level. The same three
functions (search, evaluation, retention) and the same prior-allocation
problem recur at each level; the proposal-selection and allocation notes
together imply this without saying it.

**Tension.** The brief treats "discoveries change higher-level goals" as a
normal case of the recursive process. The library says a goal change is
not licensed from inside the work governed by that goal, and that
Commonplace has no instance. Keep the library's position. The economic
framing has no mechanism for terminal goal change either; a guess about
return is relative to a goal.

**Gap.**
- The three-level stack is nowhere stated as one structure. Whether it
  deserves a note depends on whether stating it predicts anything the two
  notes do not. Candidate: the second-order note's open question about
  third-order lessons.
- Method selection is treated as a control problem
  ([capable agents need methodology selection](../../theory/capable-agents-need-methodology-selection.md)),
  not as an investment with a cost of selecting and a cost of selecting
  wrongly. This is small and real; it folds into the routine-conversion
  account in direction 6 (selecting a routine is the cheap end of
  investing in one).

**Verdict.** Held, with one bounded addition. Nothing here should be
extracted separately from direction 6.

## Direction 4: general operations for open-ended work

**Held.**
- The six operations (derive, compare, locate, revise, evaluate, apply),
  their task-level descriptions, the mechanical/interpreted/unavailable
  scale, and the finding that in a model-mediated system every operation
  is available by interpretation and none carries a classical guarantee:
  [theory-refinement-interface](../theory-refinement-interface/theory-refinement-interface.md)
  (working document, source checks not done, not promoted).
- The brief's split between "can the operations express the procedure" and
  "does reuse make it cheaper" exists in one sentence:
  [a fixed-model house must retain missing procedures](../../theory/a-fixed-model-house-must-retain-missing-procedures-for-theory-use.md):
  "An operation that is possible but too costly is different from one the
  permitted machinery cannot express." The same note states the economic
  case for shared operations: reusable retrieval, traversal and checking
  tools can make a new theory the only retained change.
- Warnings against designing from abstract operations: scenario
  decomposition, holistic rewrite, and
  [compiling coordination preserves primitive, not aggregate, authority](../../theory/compiling-coordination-preserves-primitive-not-aggregate-authority.md).
- The guard against universality from examples, for factories:
  [universality axis](../../theory/universal-software-factory-needs-a-declared-universality-axis.md).

**Restated.** "Start with the existing interface rather than inventing a
competing one" is already that workshop's boundary. "Avoid declaring
universality on the strength of examples" is the universality-axis rule.

**Tension.** None. The interface workshop asks expressiveness and guarantee
questions; it does not ask the brief's second question. That is a gap in
its scope, not a disagreement.

**Gap.**
- No note expresses a procedure from another domain (science, software
  architecture, legal drafting, exploration) as a composition of operations,
  and no cross-domain reuse evidence exists. The library touches each
  domain once (an experiment-selection note, the software-house article,
  one legal note, the ARC case in a workshop).
- Several operation lists coexist unreconciled: the interface, the
  discovery lifecycle's six phases, the theory-builder Boundary list, the
  reflective operation profile, the change-operations catalogue. This is
  not the brief's concern but it blocks any claim that "a small set" exists.

**Verdict.** The economic framing's contribution is one question to send to
the interface workshop: for each operation, what does it cost interpreted
versus mechanical, and does reuse across theories amortize the cost of
making it mechanical? Domain examples should not be pursued here; they are
evidence for the architecture-level conjecture and belong to its test
(direction 6). The unreconciled operation lists are a separate problem for
that workshop.

## Direction 5: cumulative progress and revision

**Held.** This is the most complete direction.
- Why retention is what makes accumulation possible:
  [ephemeral computation prevents accumulation](../../theory/ephemeral-computation-prevents-accumulation.md);
  [only explicit retention is durable, writable and addressable](../../theory/only-explicit-retention-is-durable-writable-and-addressable.md).
- Backtracking as the retraction path for provisional choices:
  [backtracking keeps lightweight search control provisional](../../theory/backtracking-keeps-lightweight-search-control-provisional.md).
- What to keep: unregenerable decision premises by rule
  ([design rationale](../../theory/design-rationale-must-preserve-unregenerable-decision-premises.md));
  regenerable explanation by cache ledger
  ([LLM recompute cost](../../theory/llm-recompute-cost-inverts-the-store-vs-recompute-default.md));
  choice events but not refuted beliefs
  ([superseded choices](../../theory/superseded-choices-are-retained-superseded-beliefs-are-not.md)).
- Why the two sides get different treatment: the ground-truth inversion in
  the commitment/derivation note. A commitment cannot be recomputed, so no
  cache ledger applies.
- Retention cost: [cheap adoption and weak retirement accumulate cost](../../theory/cheap-adoption-and-weak-retirement-accumulate-cost.md);
  value is observer-relative
  ([information value](../../theory/information-value-is-observer-relative.md));
  what to store needs a declared output spec
  ([open-domain memory retention](../../theory/open-domain-memory-retention-needs-a-declared-output-spec.md)).
- A checked outcome licenses episode retention but not abstraction:
  [checked outcome licenses episode retention, not abstraction](../../theory/checked-outcome-licenses-episode-retention-not-abstraction.md).

**Restated.** "Not everything is worth storing indefinitely" and "different
kinds of retained information have different future value" are both held,
the second across several pairwise notes.

**Tension.** None.

**Gap.**
- No single account compares future value across the brief's kinds
  (knowledge, decisions, rationales, experiments, outcomes). A table over
  the pairwise notes would be a restatement; the brief's discipline says
  not to write it unless it predicts something.
- No model of value decay for retained items. Staleness and retirement
  operations exist; a decay mechanism does not. Speculative; leave in log.
- No theory of what one bounded episode should hand to the next. The
  nearest are the workshop-layer note and
  [active work state is not retrospective memory](../../theory/active-work-state-is-not-retrospective-memory-or-chat-history.md).
  This is the one gap here the economic framing might fill, since a handoff
  is a retention decision under a context bound. Open; no draft yet.
- The commitment note's open question — what retires a commitment with no
  successor — stays open and is not economic.

**Verdict.** Held. Record the episode-handoff gap as an open question;
extract nothing else from this direction.

## Direction 6: why this is an economical architecture for Commonplace

**Held.**
- The strategy and its stated reasons: start from a running system people
  use because use supplies a selection environment where no fixed oracle
  exists ([system use selects theory fit](../../theory/system-use-selects-theory-fit-without-a-fixed-oracle.md),
  description only); the declared objective is useful, better-warranted
  work per unit of human judgment
  ([Commonplace as an instrument](../../reference/commonplace-as-an-instrument.md),
  which also says transfer "has not been demonstrated").
- The failure conditions, verbatim, in
  [a bootstrap fits the Bitter Lesson only if learning outgrows it](../../theory/a-bootstrap-fits-the-bitter-lesson-only-if-learning-outgrows-it.md):
  including "each new domain requires a bespoke ontology and evaluator" and
  "human global judgment and maintenance grow with the corpus".
- How to measure it: the
  [ablation baselines proposal](../../reference/proposals/ablation-baselines-for-the-declared-objective.md)
  (five arms; human decisions, maintenance cost, negative transfer).
- How work becomes routine and returns: the constraining/codification
  cluster, with codification as a bet
  ([codification and relaxing](../../theory/codification-and-relaxing-navigate-the-bitter-lesson-boundary.md)),
  commitment after stabilization, relaxing signals, and the negative
  selection that leaves the agent boundary with less predictable work
  ([preferential codification](../../theory/codifying-predictable-choices-leaves-agents-with-less-predictable-work.md)).
- The closest account of when conversion pays: the repetition section of
  [codify versus LLM decision heuristics](../../theory/codify-versus-llm-decision-heuristics.md):
  "Compare construction, invocation, checking, retrieval, and maintenance
  costs under the same outcome requirements"; recurrence is a cost signal
  and a candidate source, not a correctness criterion; a sound operation
  can be worth implementing after one case, another never.
- What warrants a shared structure: entrenchment warrants and
  [coordination value](../../theory/definitions/coordination-value.md).
  One concrete case of a shared engine retired for its maintenance cost:
  ADR 118 superseding ADR 113.

**Restated.** "Managing search judgments and commitments recursively is economical
under bounded resources" (the work-level conjecture) decomposes into held
results: allocation before evidence, candidacy vs verdict, machinery
investment, retention by ground-truth status. What is not a restatement is
the claim that these are *one* process; see cross-cutting below.

**Tension.** The brief asks why "starting with explicit knowledge
management" is a good strategy. The library's answer is not about knowledge
management; it is that explicit retention is what makes machinery
addressable and therefore revisable by the same loop (reflection buys
addressability), and that use supplies selection. The brief's phrasing
would need to be replaced by those two reasons.

**Gap.**
- The architecture-level conjecture is not stated anywhere. The library has
  its ingredients (software-house endpoint, universality axis, failure
  conditions, measurement proposal) but no artifact says: reusable support
  for commitment management is an economical route to the endpoint, and
  here is what would defeat that.
- No thesis that a small shared interface reduces duplication while
  permitting domain extensions. The nearest structures are
  `configure(G, K_P)`, text contract plus profiles (ADR 042), and guarded
  defaults. The cost side has one direct case (checked inline blocks: "the
  natural-language equivalent of a missing function boundary").
- No single account of when conversion into routine pays. The repetition
  section has four of the five terms (construction, invocation, checking,
  retrieval, maintenance); stability of the pattern is in progressive
  constraining and relaxing signals; frequency is in ADR 040's promotion
  signal. Nothing joins them.
- No cross-domain reuse evidence, and no measurement has been run.

**Verdict.** This is where the workshop's new content lives. Two artifacts
are warranted: a theory note on routine-conversion economics (work-level,
grounded in held notes, with stability as the epistemic term) and a
proposal stating the architecture-level conjecture with its defeaters
(mostly reusing the failure conditions and the ablation proposal). The
"small shared interface" thesis should stay inside the proposal as a
sub-conjecture; it has one case for and one against.

## Cross-cutting findings

### The work-level conjecture is a composition, not a new mechanism

Every mechanism the brief names is held. What the brief adds is the claim
that they are instances of one process. The library has not said that, and
whether saying it is worth anything depends on the test below. The honest
statement: the economic framing re-describes the search-control,
discovery-lifecycle, machinery-investment and retention notes with cost
attached to each decision. The one place cost is missing from the library
is controller evaluation (direction 1's tension).

### The operator's reading survives the map, with a rival

The reading (epistemic work is the shared layer because allocation is only
as good as its guesses, and epistemic work produces the routines) is
consistent with every held note. Constraining is learning in Simon's sense;
codification is a bet on an extracted regularity; adoption follows
stabilization. The rival is resource-rational analysis: routines as
heuristics adopted for cost, not for evidence about stability. The two
agree on most behaviour and disagree on relaxing: the epistemic account
says relax when evidence shows the committed reading was a wrong proxy;
the cost account says relax when a cheaper or better general method
arrives. Both appear in the constraining definition's relaxing clause, so
the library currently holds both without choosing.

### Discriminating tests

1. **Controller cost.** Add opportunity cost to the evaluation of a search
   controller and see whether it changes which controller is preferred on
   recorded Commonplace episodes (connect-report routing, review bundle
   ordering). If the ranking does not change, the economic layer adds
   nothing over "tested by what it brings to stronger evaluation".
   Facts gathered 2026-10-30 in
   [search/controller-comparisons.md](./search/controller-comparisons.md):
   seven comparisons fit the controller definition; cost entered the basis
   in three and reversed none (it agreed with yield or broke a tie). The
   three are all in the automated review pipeline; the routine controllers
   a person still drives have no cost-per-yield record at all. Scope
   condition from the operator (2026-10-30): computational cost is the
   binding consideration only where a controller runs unattended, as the
   review and revision system does; elsewhere the scarce resource is human
   judgment, which the declared objective already counts.
   **Concluded 2026-10-30** against the
   [Russell–Wefald ingest](../../sources/russell-wefald-principles-of-metareasoning.ingest.md).
   Their rule: a computation's net value is its benefit to the decision
   (zero unless it changes the choice) minus its cost, and controllers of
   unequal cost are compared on one scale with the exchange rate swept,
   reporting whether the preference flips within the range. Commonplace's
   recorded practice (yield first, cost as tiebreaker) is that rule's limit
   as the exchange rate goes to zero, which the paper itself notes for A\*.
   So the economic layer changes nothing on the record, and it would matter
   exactly where the rate is not negligible: unattended pipelines, where
   the record already uses cost. What transfers is structural, not a
   computation; the paper needs numerical utilities, a standing default
   action and priors on how a step revises estimates, none of which
   connect routing or bundle ordering has. Three items worth keeping:
   (i) the scoring rule, credit only for a changed later decision and
   every step pays, which sharpens "what it brings to stronger evaluation"
   (the [failure-explanation note](../../theory/failure-explanation-changes-later-branch-decisions.md)
   already says this for failures); (ii) the exchange-rate sweep as the
   form an unequal-cost comparison should take, qualifying the controller
   note's matched-resources condition; (iii) the meta-greedy prediction
   that a myopic controller undervalues setup steps such as reading before
   routing, an experiment not run. Verdict, revised on the operator's reading (2026-10-30): the record
   shows the zero-rate regime because most controllers are still driven by
   a person, and that is the regime Commonplace is leaving as it adds
   unattended pipelines. So the net-value form is not a separate layer now
   and becomes the required comparison as automation grows. Two items
   follow. The controller note's matched-resources comparison stays
   valid in every regime, unattended included: yield under equal budgets
   needs no exchange rate. What unattended runs add is the frequent case
   where controllers' costs differ materially; there, the net-value form
   with the exchange rate swept is an option alongside it, not a
   replacement (Astra's correction, 2026-10-30). And the instrumentation gap is the actionable one:
   cost-per-yield records for routine controllers are cheap to add before
   the pipelines multiply and costly after, which is the
   [machinery-investment](../../theory/an-optimal-long-run-learning-strategy-invests-in-its-own-machinery.md)
   argument applied to measurement. It belongs with
   [review-on-workjig](../review-on-workjig/README.md) and
   [recurring-task-runner](../recurring-task-runner/README.md), the
   workshops building those runs; this workshop records it as a proposal
   candidate and does not build it.
2. **Relaxing trigger** — withdrawn 2026-10-30. The classification does
   not discriminate: discovering a mistake changes costs, and discovering a
   cheaper alternative requires evidence (Astra's objection). The epistemic
   versus resource-rational rivalry is deferred as an open question; no
   extraction depends on it.
3. **Routine-conversion account.** Draft the account with its five terms
   and apply it retrospectively to three conversions and one refusal to
   convert. If the account would have predicted each decision, it has
   content; if every case needs a sixth term, it is a list, not a mechanism.
4. **Not worthwhile.** The generalization is not worth adopting if tests 1
   and 3 both fail: then the economic vocabulary names nothing the
   structural vocabulary misses, and the vocabulary collisions above are
   pure cost. Test 3 ran on 2026-10-30 and passed narrowly: two
   considerations missing from existing guidance, extracted as a section of
   the codify-versus-LLM note rather than a new note.

### Candidate extractions, in order

1. Theory note: when conversion into routine pays (direction 6; tested by
   test 3 first).
2. Proposal in `kb/theory/proposals/` or `kb/reference/proposals/`: the
   architecture-level conjecture with defeaters (direction 6). Read the
   proposal types before choosing.
3. Paragraph in the explanatory/constructive note classifying effort
   allocation (direction 1), applied as a separate commit at extraction.
4. One question sent to theory-refinement-interface (direction 4).
5. Log entries: episode-handoff retention (direction 5), third-order
   lessons (direction 3), value decay (direction 5).
6. Proposal candidate in `kb/reference/proposals/`: cost-per-yield
   records for routine controllers, as the instrumentation unattended runs
   need before net-value comparison is possible (test 1).
7. Qualification of
   [a search controller is tested by what it brings to stronger evaluation](../../theory/a-search-controller-is-tested-by-what-it-brings-to-stronger-evaluation.md):
   the scoring rule (credit only for a changed later decision), and the
   unequal-cost comparison form added as an option when costs differ
   materially, keeping matched-budget comparison; linking the
   Russell–Wefald ingest (test 1). Closing condition 2's source check is met
   for metareasoning by that ingest and for bounded optimality by the
   existing one; resource-rational analysis is deferred with the rivalry.

### Not worth doing

- A commitment ladder note (restatement).
- A retained-information value table (restatement).
- A three-level recursion note on its own (restatement unless test 1
  shows cost changes a decision at the method level).
- Domain procedure examples (evidence for the proposal, not content for
  this workshop).
