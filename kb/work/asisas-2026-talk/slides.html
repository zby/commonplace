01 · The problem

Agents adapt by retaining artifacts — and we classify them by where they're stored

  • Prompts, workflows, validators, routing rules, memories, adapters, checkpoints — all shape future behavior.
  • Today they're managed under storage labels: memory system vector store flat files prompt registry model store
  • Position: storage-first classification is architecturally insufficient. It hides how a retained artifact acts, what review it needs, and how to undo it.
§1
02 · One lesson, many forms

A coding agent keeps mishandling dependency resolution

The system can retain that lesson in very different shapes — and each creates different obligations for review, invalidation, and rollback:

Natural-language reflection

Affects behavior only if retrieval carries it forward.

Derived prompt summary

Can go stale and govern behavior after its source is fixed.

Lockfile validator

Blocks execution cheaply — but must be tested like control logic.

Conflict-check script

Fast and repeatable; fails systematically when the spec is wrong.

Learned scoring weights

Need behavioral probes — there's no record to read.

§1
03 · The vocabulary

A four-field record for every retained artifact

fieldrecordswhy the architect cares
storage_substrate Where retained state persists: files, repos, vector stores, registries, model stores. Access, deletion, versioning, rollback paths.
representational_form How the operative part is encoded and consumed: natural-language, symbolic, distributed-parametric. Default inspection method, review evidence, recurring execution cost.
lineage Source material, or a derived view, index, or learned update — and of what. What must be invalidated when a source changes.
behavioral_authority Consumer + channel + force: from advice through instruction to enforcement and learning input. Who acts on it, through which channel, with what force.

Unit of classification: the operative part and its consumption path — not the stored object. Bundled objects get split.

04 · Representational form, derived

Two axes generate the three forms

localized unit
non-localized
consequences assigned by a defined consumer

symbolic

Code, schemas, validators, route tables. An implementation adjudicates: two consumers disagreeing means one is wrong.

unoccupied — vector-symbolic architectures aim here

reinterpreted on each consumption

natural-language

Reflections, facts, notes, policies, prompt content. No adjudicator: two readings can both be reasonable — underspecification, not a defect.

distributed-parametric

Weights, adapters, embedding spaces, reward models. No addressable unit carries the content.

§5
05 · Inspection follows from the axes

Read, test, or probe — derived, not stipulated

  • A localized unit with defined semantics can be checked against them → test symbolic artifacts.
  • A localized unit without them yields content only to interpretation → read natural-language artifacts.
  • A non-localized artifact offers nothing to point at; only behavior is observable → probe distributed-parametric artifacts.
  • Revisability rides the localization axis alone: a prompt and a validator are both revisable one item at a time; weights are not.
§5
06 · Lineage & behavioral authority

Where it came from, and what force it carries

  • lineage — source material vs. derived view, index, or learned update. Not full provenance: just what to invalidate, regenerate, or retire when a source changes.
  • authority = consumer × channel × force — from advice that may be ignored, through instruction, to enforcement, ranking influence, and learning input.
  • The record exposes failure modes: a stale derived view governing behavior after its source is fixed; untrusted natural language entering high-authority prompt channels; poisoned records hidden behind opaque retrieval.

09 · Honest limits

A classification discipline, not a validated method — yet

  • Corpus assembled opportunistically; seeded by a file/wiki-heavy discussion thread, so distributions describe this collection, not the field.
  • Counts are system-level presence; the vocabulary's real unit is the artifact. Per-artifact statistics are future work.
  • Classifications produced under one saved review contract — reviewer consistency untested.
  • Next: pre-registered sampling frame, design-review and incident-analysis trials — does the record expose risks storage labels miss?
§9
10 · Takeaway

Takeaway

Storage locates retained state. Form, lineage, and authority determine how it acts — and what review it admits.

For each retained lesson, ask: should it stay quick-to-capture natural language, become symbolic control, be surfaced through retrieval, or be learned into numerical state? Those choices differ sharply in cost, coverage, behavioral variance — and in the review and rollback they admit.
