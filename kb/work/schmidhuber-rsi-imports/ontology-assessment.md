## Research direction

Commonplace pursues recursive self-improvement within Schmidhuber's broad
program. Its chosen development path begins with underspecified methodology
used through LLM interpretation. Use and criticism develop that methodology;
parts can be codified when their intended operation becomes clear and the
commitment is useful. The LLM and harness provide executable machinery while
the retained methodology still leaves some judgments open.

Popper's epistemology organizes that development process: its claims remain
tentative, criticism addresses their content, and the resulting problems
guide further work. This rationale needs no historical explanation of why
Schmidhuber chose particular constructions. Natural language alone does not
imply a Popperian process, and formal realizations remain eligible.

This positioning belongs in the research companion. The five mechanisms in
[the import agenda](./imports.md) remain design and experimental questions;
adopting the direction does not adopt their formal guarantees. The
[first experiment sketch](./first-experiment.md) separates improvement through
an interpreted procedure from the later question of whether codification
preserves useful behavior and reduces cost or error.

## What the definitions already cover

**Inquiry and machinery revision.** Capacity for future action already
includes developing theories and choosing further tests. The subject may be
the system's own learning machinery. A theory can therefore guide the search
that develops another theory without requiring a separate kind of learning.
Whether earlier gains make later improvement more productive remains an
empirical question.

**Theories and procedures.** The tentative-theory draft now refers to a
*theory encoded in fixed instructions*, rather than calling every instruction
a theory. A procedure directs an operation; a theory can explain its effects;
a criticism challenges a claim. These roles may share an artifact. Criticizing
a theory about a procedure and revising the procedure as a consequence fits
the current definition. The companion makes that distinction visible in the
research rationale; another base term is unnecessary.

**Evaluation and criticism.** The existing score-selection example already
separates selection from formulated criticism of content. A natural-language
policy selected by an LLM score does not qualify merely because its form and
evaluator are semantic. A formal theory criticized through a tested
consequence can qualify. Neither example settles unknown internal processing
of a model participating in the arrangement. No additional boundary example
is needed.

## Case 14 attribution

Case 14 remains a proof-only revision arrangement under a theory that is
uncriticized by stipulation. It borrows proof-governed switching from the
Gödel-machine construction. A requirement to prove a beneficial switch does
not establish that the complete system lacks content-directed criticism.
The retained source analysis allows observation inputs and formally licensed
revisions to represented machinery, and leaves the theory-builder
classification dependent on the deployment.

The definition and its checks distinguish the stipulated example
from the source family. Deductive criticism remains eligible. Classifying a
particular Gödel-machine system would require evidence about its full process;
this assessment supplies no categorical classification of that family.
