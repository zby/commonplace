# Workshop: Schmidhuber RSI imports

## Commission

The operator commissioned this workshop on 2026-09-21 to assess and develop
five proposed imports from Schmidhuber's recursive self-improvement work for
Commonplace. The operator identified RSI as central to our research and
emphasized the representational opportunity: Commonplace uses natural-language
knowledge and procedures, interpreted and judged by LLMs. Schmidhuber's
framework accommodates both symbolic and neural realizations; we develop
a possibility within that broad framework.

The goal is to determine which mechanisms survive that change of
representation, what they add to existing Commonplace work, and how to test
their value. The five imports are an investigation agenda, not adopted
methodology or established advantages.

## Position within the RSI program

The operator asked to retain this opening clause as the starting point for
clarifying our relationship to the program:

> “True RSI is about encoding the initial learning algorithm in a universal programming language”

— Schmidhuber, introduction to the RSI retrospective, immediately before
Section 1; [retained source wording and locator](../../sources/recursive-self-improvement-since-1987.ingest.md#quotes).

The operator adopted this research positioning on 2026-09-21: Commonplace
pursues recursive self-improvement within the broad research program developed
by Schmidhuber. The quotation's immediate example includes an RNN. Our
realization uses natural language as the retained, revisable carrier of
knowledge and learning procedures, with LLMs supplying interpretation and
criticism. The objective includes improving how subsequent improvements are
made. The works examined do not develop this specific arrangement explicitly;
this is not a priority claim about the wider literature.

## Start with underspecified methodology; codify what becomes clear

Our adopted development path starts with an incomplete, criticizable account
of how to learn, used through LLM interpretation. The LLM and harness supply
executable machinery. The retained methodology can begin as underspecified
natural language, with the interpreter supplying judgments where the method
has not yet settled an operation. We can therefore try a methodological
conjecture and expose it to failure before specifying all its operations.

Use and criticism expose ambiguities and failures. Codify parts when their
intended meaning and operation are sufficiently clear and the commitment is
useful. Other parts
can remain subject to semantic interpretation and revision. Thus
[codification](../../notes/definitions/codification.md) can be an outcome of
learning, decided separately for each part; it need not precede use of the
whole methodology. Codification fixes operational consequences without making
the underlying theory true or exempt from criticism.

This reduces the up-front specification needed to try a methodological idea.
Whether the interpreted method improves later learning, and whether
subsequent codification preserves useful behavior while reducing cost or
error, remain empirical questions. The development path is adopted; its
expected benefits are not established.

## Popper as the ontology for this realization

Popper's epistemology organizes this development path. The methodology's
claims are tentative theories: use them, criticize their content, test their
consequences, and let the resulting problems guide further development.
This applies when the subject is the learning procedure itself. An LLM can
work on a newly formulated problem or objection before a bespoke symbolic
encoding exists; its interpretation and judgment also remain open to criticism.

The connection to Popper follows from our choice to develop the methodology
through explicit conjecture and criticism. Natural-language representation
alone does not supply that process, and formal systems can implement it too.
The [Popper foundation](../conjectural-learning/popper-foundation.md) owns the
source account. The [research companion](../conjectural-learning/notes/commonplace-studies-conjectural-learning-through-retained-theories.md#research-program-and-development-path)
states the adopted arrangement; the [ontology assessment](./ontology-assessment.md)
records why it needs no new membership conditions.

## Scope and evaluation boundary

The workshop owns source comparison, import assessments, experiment sketches,
and candidate drafts within this directory. Creation of the workshop does not
install new instructions, change library definitions, or commission an
experiment run. Library promotion and operational changes are separate
decisions made against concrete outputs.

Natural language is the chosen carrier for interpreted methodology. Its
operative role depends on how a model and surrounding machinery would consume
it; storage alone adds no capacity. Semantic judgments may guide search and
evaluation; their agreement alone does not establish improved outcomes.
Distinguish the source's guarantees from our proposed adaptations, and
distinguish better task performance from better subsequent improvement work.

The initial experimental comparison holds model, task evidence, tools, and
budget fixed while varying retained procedural content. Its system boundary
includes any human performing diagnosis, selection, or evaluation internally;
record those contributions rather than attributing them to computational
autonomy. Outcome assessment must not simply reuse the improver's approval.

Keep optimality guarantees attached to their formal assumptions. Do not
import universal program enumeration, a proof requirement for every rewrite,
or one lifetime reward score as defaults for semantic KB work. Reconsider
them only if a concrete use supplies the required conditions.

## Continuation and closure

Next, assess the five imports against the linked primary-source ingests and
existing coverage. Record for each whether it adds a mechanism, strengthens
an existing claim, motivates an experiment, or should be declined. Choose the
first experimental target from available comparable episodes; the sketch
does not preselect a workflow, model, sample size, or budget. Before execution,
fix those choices and the outcome test in a reviewable protocol.

Keep decisions and their reasons next to the affected import or experiment;
record source locators and limitations there. Operational records of future
runs must identify versions, conditions, costs, and outcomes. No results exist
at workshop creation.

Close when every import has a reasoned disposition, any retained experiment
design has a named owner and is ready for a run or explicitly declined, and
accepted durable conclusions have been promoted under their destination
contracts. Then delete the consumed workshop and remove its active-list entry.
