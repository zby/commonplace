# Addressability: does naming a suspect part improve later revision?

Ontology vocabulary probe, 2026-09-21; no experiment run or commissioned.
Controls are drawn from the [component-comparison workshop](../../explanatory-theories-deployment-time-learning/README.md).
Its [search controls](../../explanatory-theories-deployment-time-learning/experiment-design.md#test-one-role-at-a-time)
apply, but it has no existing comparison of localized and whole-theory
criticism. This tests the companion's [addressability conjecture](../notes/commonplace-studies-conjectural-learning-through-retained-theories.md#three-conjectures).

## Capacity and observable action

Use new KB retrieval failures requiring revision of a theory of indexing and
routing. The capacity is to find effective repairs while preserving useful
knowledge about unaffected cases. Require persistence from development into
a fresh assessment episode. Observe which diagnostic branch is chosen, the
resulting repair, success on the failure family, and regressions on previously
working families. Edit size is not the quality measure.

The system boundary includes the model, harness, theory, testing records,
tools, and participating people. All arms start with the same theory whose
assumptions and scope can be inspected individually. This holds available
structure fixed and tests the benefit of explicitly using it in criticism;
it is not a binary comparison of possessing versus lacking addressability.

## Arrangements

| Arm | Supplied criticism | Revision permission |
|---|---|---|
| Localized | Common failure evidence and objection, plus a named suspect assumption or scope condition and its proposed relation to the failure | Inspect and revise any part, including replacing the whole theory |
| Whole theory | The same failure evidence and outcome constraint, directed at the theory as a whole, without the supplied candidate location | The same inspection and revision permission |
| Mislocalized | The same evidence and comparable form, with a different suspect part | The same inspection and revision permission |

The whole-theory criticism still says why an observed consequence counts
against the theory. It is not merely a low score. The agent may locate a part
for itself; no arm is defined by what it must not think. Mislocalization must
be selected without assessment outcomes. Its purpose is to expose dependence
on the proposed location and possible harm, not to make localization look good
by choosing an absurd alternative.

Hold model, source observations, tools, candidate allowance, selection rule,
retention format, assessment cases, and total resource ceilings fixed. Supply
the full theory in every arm. Allow identical whole-replacement operations;
do not impose minimal edits on the localized arm. Retain each arm's selected
successor and testing record through assessment.

## What this can isolate

Naming a candidate cause supplies diagnostic information. Removing the name
while leaving an unmistakable paraphrase would preserve localization; removing
the diagnosis altogether also changes informational content. The honest
contrast is therefore **supplied localized diagnosis versus whole-theory
criticism**, as worded in the companion. It does not identify the benefit of
document structure, names alone, or local editing independently. Comparing
files with indexed traces would not by itself change addressability either.

Reuse independent outcome scoring, development/assessment separation, and
resource accounting from the peer design. Include failures that preserve most
of the theory, break a scope condition, and overturn a central assumption.
Count diagnostic construction, inspection, and validation work. Test changed
branch choices against their consequences; a smaller textual diff or citation
of the named part is insufficient. Compare the later capacity with the frozen
seed, and use content interventions in separate assessment copies to probe
whether the revised theory guides later repairs. Record uncertainty in the
diagnosis instead of treating a located part as proven faulty.

## Reading the possible results

- Localized criticism beating whole-theory criticism with no extra regression,
  plus improvement over the seed and causal later use, supports learning and
  the bounded benefit of supplying a candidate location. It establishes neither
  a universal preference for local repair nor addressability as a membership
  condition.
- Changed branches without improved repair quality show influence only.
  Gains accompanied by excessive regressions fail the declared preservation
  claim; a favorable average cannot silently waive its harm bound.
- Mislocalized criticism making results worse shows a risk of supplied
  diagnosis. Localized beating only mislocalized criticism does not show an
  advantage over whole-theory criticism.
- No detectable difference leaves benefit unresolved at the chosen resolution;
  both arms might learn, or independently find the same location. Whole-theory
  superiority counts against localization in the tested setting without
  excluding either process from conjectural learning.

The [RSI import agenda](../../schmidhuber-rsi-imports/imports.md#3-preserve-a-route-beyond-inherited-methodology)
suggests trying an alternative starting methodology. That changes another
factor and stays a separate RSI comparison. This probe requires no new term
or definition change. Any later experiment commission must choose cases, resource ceilings,
persistence horizon, diagnosis construction, repetitions, and quality and harm
criteria before any run. Lack of matched material is a feasibility limit,
not grounds to claim that pure structural addressability was tested.
