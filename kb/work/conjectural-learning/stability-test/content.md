# Content: does supplied criticism improve later revision?

Ontology vocabulary probe, 2026-09-21; no experiment run or commissioned.
Controls are drawn from the [component-comparison workshop](../../explanatory-theories-deployment-time-learning/README.md).
This instantiates its [candidate-search comparison](../../explanatory-theories-deployment-time-learning/experiment-design.md#test-one-role-at-a-time),
using the companion's [content conjecture](../notes/commonplace-studies-conjectural-learning-through-retained-theories.md#three-conjectures).
The peer design supplies controls but does not already specify this contrast.

## Capacity and observable action

Use a recurring KB retrieval failure as a candidate task: a seed theory says
which indexing or routing conditions cause omissions; an agent proposes a
repair to a sandboxed retrieval harness. The learning claim concerns capacity
to propose effective repairs for new failures in this family. It must survive
the development context and remain available in a fresh assessment episode.
The action that would show it is choosing an investigation and producing a
repair that improves independently scored retrieval while preserving previously
working cases. An immediate repair alone supports only a shorter horizon.

The declared system includes the model, harness, retained theory and testing
record, tools, and any people contributing diagnosis or selection. Assessment
cases remain hidden from development. Their outcomes are revealed only after
the proposal is frozen. A capacity not exercised remains unestablished by
this probe; it is not thereby absent.

## Arrangements

All arms start from the same seed theory and receive the same task inputs,
failed outputs, authorized test outcomes, and scores. The proposer, tools,
revision surface, candidate allowance, and score-based candidate-selection
rule are the same. Relevant criticism is generated from that common
development evidence; mismatched criticism is prepared from another development
failure. Generate and charge criticism only in those two arms. Score selection
has no unused critic call and may spend the corresponding budget on search.

| Arm | What the next proposal step receives | What persists to assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Relevant criticism | A formulated argument linking the failure to what the seed theory says, including uncertainty | Selected successor theory and its testing record |
| Score selection | The common evidence and scores, without an added formulated reason for failure | Selected successor theory and its testing record |
| Mismatched criticism | Criticism from a different failure, matched in format and approximate length, in the same delivery slot | Selected successor theory and its testing record |

Criticism generation precedes the proposal it might guide. Record its text
before revealing later outcomes. The first contrast tests the arrangement
that formulates and supplies criticism; the third arm checks whether relevant
content helps beyond supplying similarly formed text. A mismatch can actively
mislead: relevant beating mismatched but not score selection does not show a
benefit over omitting criticism. Neither comparison forbids private linguistic
criticism in the proposer, and fixed weights do not fix processing across inputs.

## Controls and learning evidence

Reuse the peer design's independent audit, separate construction and decision
contexts, matched resource ceilings, and held-out change families. Charge
criticism production and consumption to the relevant arm. Give the baseline
access to the same total budget for search; hold candidate allowance fixed
for a matched comparison and report unused budget and actual cost. A separate
resource-allocation comparison may relax that allowance, but has a different
interpretation. Mismatched texts must not contain hidden assessment answers.

Compare post-development assessment with the frozen seed at the same later
tasks and budgets. This supplies evidence of improvement, beyond a ranking
between two potentially poor learners. Intervene on retained, load-bearing
content in separate assessment copies; citation alone does not establish
causal use. Record changed investigation or repair choices and independently
scored consequences. The [evidence ladder](../../../notes/a-complete-theory-path-does-not-establish-improved-capacity.md#evidence-forms-a-ladder)
separates these links; a changed choice alone is not improved capacity.

## Reading the possible results

- Relevant criticism beating both controls, improving over the frozen seed,
  and causally affecting later action supports learning and a bounded benefit
  of this supplied-criticism arrangement. It does not isolate an internal
  reasoning operation or establish superiority across tasks and models.
- Better repair scores without the content-use or later-use evidence support
  an outcome advantage with the mechanism or persistence still unestablished.
  Changed action without better outcomes establishes influence, not learning.
- No detectable difference establishes neither equivalence nor absence of
  conjectural learning. Improvement in all arms is compatible with no added
  benefit from supplied criticism. Worse outcomes count against this
  criticism arrangement, even when its content demonstrably affected action.

The [RSI sketch](../../schmidhuber-rsi-imports/first-experiment.md) makes a
content control a possible follow-up. Here it is needed for the content-specific
inference, which must wait if the control is deferred. The
[updated research comparison](../comparison.md#recursive-self-improvement-precedents)
motivates measuring later search and its costs; it supplies no result for this
probe. Better retrieval is task performance; better later repair work is the
capacity tested here, and one such gain is not sustained compounding.

No definition change is needed. Task instances, model, budgets, horizon length,
repetitions, outcome thresholds, harm bound, and statistical resolution remain
for any later experiment commission. Such a study must be declined or narrowed if
comparable failures or independent assessment cannot be supplied.
