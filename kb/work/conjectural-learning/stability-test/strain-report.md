# Stability result: the definitions can state all three comparisons

Completed 2026-09-21 against the definitions and decision record at
`b1d1c6d3bf950d10cad70cf1c766889f8e0351a8`, including that commit's RSI comparison
additions. This is a specification exercise, not experimental evidence. The
three definitions, decision record, and companion were held unchanged.

## Recommendation to the operator

The definitions are stable enough to migrate: all three conjectures can be
stated as comparisons of supplied evidence, criticism, and retained content
without adding a term or changing a membership condition. The reconstruction
question is now separated into its two required comparisons and can move to
experiment design. The addressability probe tests supplied localized diagnosis,
and the raw-record efficiency comparison varies both retained theory and
criticism; neither licenses a finer causal claim. Practical feasibility and
the conjectured benefits remain unestablished. I recommend accepting this
stability result and preparing the substantial library rewrites; migration still
requires the separate commission reserved by the parent workshop.

## What was tried

The [content specification](./content.md) follows formulated criticism into
later repair capacity, with score selection, mismatched content, and the
frozen seed as different comparisons. The [addressability specification](./addressability.md)
separates naming a candidate part from limiting edit size, allows mistaken
localization and whole replacement, and keeps both arms eligible for
conjectural learning. The [efficiency specification](./efficiency.md) compares
retained theory with criticism-based and input/outcome-based reconstruction,
including context limits, preparation and maintenance costs, stale reuse, and
the indexing example.

Each probe names a future action and a persistence horizon, distinguishes
capacity from its observed exercise, and preserves the possibility of
unobserved formulation in a model. Controls were checked against the
[component design](../../explanatory-theories-deployment-time-learning/experiment-design.md),
the [RSI first experiment](../../schmidhuber-rsi-imports/first-experiment.md),
the [contrast rule](../../../notes/an-experiment-identifies-only-the-contrast-it-actually-runs.md),
and the [evidence ladder](../../../notes/reflective-theory-refinement-needs-interpretation-and-retention.md#evidence-forms-a-ladder).
Neither peer design already states all the required contrasts: the content
and addressability contrasts need specification, and the retention design
needs the criticism-only arm and a precise meaning of raw history.

## Strain register

**No definition strain found.** No missing technical term, unsupported sense
of a defined term, or necessary membership change was found. No wording change
is proposed and no case flips. The following pressure points were resolved
within the existing vocabulary; they remain limits on experimental claims.

| Term or distinction tested | Specification and pressure | Resolution; smallest definition change needed |
|---|---|---|
| Criticism of content | Content: the baseline model may privately criticize | Name the supplied-content intervention; do not infer internal absence. None |
| Capacity and persistence | All: a later action supplies evidence, while an unexercised capacity can qualify | Declare fresh-episode persistence and compare with the frozen seed. Lack of evidence leaves learning unestablished. None |
| Addressable theory | Addressability: both arms can inspect parts; localization also adds diagnostic information | Test supplied localized diagnosis, not absence of structure or a pure naming effect. The companion already makes that claim. None |
| Retained criticism versus retained theory | Efficiency: accumulated criticisms may effectively encode a successor | Inspect the retained content; reject a collapsed contrast. The distinction need not be feasible in every dataset to be meaningful. None |
| Inputs and outcomes; indexed traces | Efficiency: a trace can already contain theory and applicability judgments | Classify retained content by what it contains, not its storage representation. Use the indexing example as a nested comparison. None |
| Tentative theory | All: survival, freezing for assessment, and possible codification might be read as ending tentativeness | Epistemic status persists independently of revision permission or quality. None |
| Conjectural learning and RSI | All: later-search benefit might be reported as recursive compounding or proof-based assurance | Keep mechanism, benefit, compounding, and formal guarantees separate. None |

If an experimental owner later needs a membership change to state its actual
comparison, that returns to the decision record and operator. It cannot be
silently adopted as a protocol detail.
