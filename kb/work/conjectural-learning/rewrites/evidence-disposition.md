## What survives and what changes

The first note preserves the four evidence levels, the same-path requirement
for recurrent claims, interpretation versus reach-assessment, the division of
functions across substrates, the bounded LLM-plus-artifact arrangement, the
Bitter Lesson cost challenge, and the Commonplace, Exo, and Gödel-machine
examples. The ladder's third item now uses ordinary prose for the response to
criticism; it names no replacement operation. Learning requires improved
capacity, a further evidential obligation beyond an observed process.

The old universal requirement of addressable retention is removed. Its useful
engineering claims remain scoped to Commonplace's chosen arrangement.
Reconstruction from retained criticism, whole replacement, and private
linguistic formulation no longer fail membership because of their storage or
visibility. Persistence follows the claimed horizon, and capacity can improve
before actual exercise. Survival of criticism can change reliance or test
choices without replacing text; tentative status stays unchanged.

The second note preserves each causal join, the weakness of repository ancestry
and decorative citation, the need to retain otherwise unrecoverable production
facts, actor allocation, and claim-relative recording. Its diagram now connects
the result of criticism to later operation without demanding a new theory
version. It covers the empirical recurrent claim explicitly; argument-based
criticism and improved but unexercised capacity are not excluded by that shape.

Both drafts distinguish connected mechanism evidence from improvement. Neither
mechanical validation nor observing a complete recurrence establishes that
capacity improved. The causal claim still needs the contrast and evidence at
the strength asserted.

## Source limits retained

- Commonplace supports a bounded human-inclusive pathway and its adopted
  improvement criterion, not general advantage or computational closure.
- Exo remains the inspected-checkout assessment; the review did not run a live
  instance. Rewrite/rebuild/restart machinery does not establish semantic
  reach-assessment, later use of criticism, or capacity improvement.
- The Gödel machine remains a formal construction with conditional warrant,
  not an observed recurrent learner. The old suggestion that it supplied all
  learning functions is narrowed to the self-representation and proof-governed
  switching it actually supports. Switching alone classifies neither the
  presence nor absence of content criticism in a complete system.
- The sample-efficiency conjecture is no longer an opening premise for the
  evidence distinctions. Its dedicated note owns that conjecture. The retained
  artifact's comparative value remains open here.
- No numerical result, experiment, new source claim, or definition change is
  introduced. The fourteen cases keep their adopted classes.
