# Fix list from the second independent check

Checked on 2026-09-21 at the operator's request, after the
[note/operational review](./package-review.md) and the
[article review](./article-review.md). Seven per-bundle readers compared each
staged draft with its library original, its disposition, and the settled
basis: the three definitions, the companion, the
[decision record](../definition-decision-record.md), and the
[closure plan](../closure-plan.md). Each item under "Fix before migration
review" was rechecked against both texts unless it says otherwise. This check
initially changed no draft. Its findings are preserved below; the resolution
record at the end identifies the subsequent fixes. This is not a migration
approval. The initial hashes and counts under "What held" describe that
review's inputs; current hashes and counts live in the consolidated accounting.

Paths below are relative to `rewrites/kb/` for drafts and to the repository
root for originals. Line numbers refer to the file bytes recorded in
[accounting and validation](./accounting-and-validation.md); recheck them
after any edit.

## What held

No draft keeps an old membership condition: addressability, a retained
theory, recurrence, or observer access as a requirement, or failed revision
counted as learning. No draft overcorrects: comparison arms stay
arrangements, reflection keeps causal self-representation, and builder
membership keeps addressability and continuing responsibility.

All 14 source hashes and 16 candidate hashes and word counts match the
accounting record. All 16 candidates pass `commonplace-validate --full` by
explicit path. All 129 files outside this workshop that link a renamed or
retired path are covered by the [consumer inventory](./consumer-inventory.md).
Inbound fragment counts match the dispositions. No invented source finding
was found; inline quotes match the pinned snapshots. The testing supplement's
hypotheses, refuters, dates, and ten declarations are unchanged apart from
rebased links.

Most findings below are one kind of defect: a claim, qualification, or
commitment in an original disappeared, and the disposition does not record
where it went. Each fix is either a restored sentence or a disposition row
that retires the claim with a reason.

## Fix before migration review

### 1. Result type keeps the old membership test

`types/agentic-system-analysis-result.md:313`, unchanged from the original,
asks the synthesis to state "whether the system retains a revisable theory on
its decision path". The same file's rewritten route paragraph at `:267` says
whole replacement and reconstruction do not by themselves exclude learning.
A system that replaces theories whole, or rebuilds them from retained
criticisms, qualifies in the route record and fails the synthesis property.

Fix: replace the first clause with the conjectural-learning property, for
example whether criticism of an operative formulated theory improves the
system's capacity for future action. Keep the sentence that the three
properties are independent, not a grade or a ladder. The skill has no
counterpart sentence. Correct the
[operational disposition](./operational-disposition.md)'s statement that only
the admitting-route paragraph changes.

### 2. Testing supplement weakens a pre-commitment

Original `kb/articles/testing-the-theory-refinement-program.md:470-473`: "We
expect the first real run to show a definition to be wrong somewhere. The
plan is to revise the definition and retain the run record as evidence, not
to adjust the protocol so that the definition survives." The draft at
`articles/testing-the-theory-refinement-program.md:541-544` says only that
"changing a definition does not turn a failed outcome into a successful one".
The prohibition on adjusting the protocol is gone, and the
[testing disposition](./testing-article-disposition.md) presents the two as
equivalent.

Fix: restore the original sentence. It does not depend on the retired
ontology.

### 3. Testing supplement does not say the criticism arm is inside the term

The treatment table at draft `:419` adds "Formulated criticisms and their
results, used to reconstruct a theory", and `:488` calls that arm "a
reconstruction control". Decision-record case 3 classes reconstruction from
retained criticisms as inside conjectural learning; only case 4, records of
inputs and outcomes, is a comparison of retained content. As written, a
reader will take a win by the criticism arm as refuting the paradigm. It
bears only on the retention conjecture.

Fix: after "The two reconstruction contrasts must remain distinct" at `:424`,
add one clause saying that reconstruction from retained criticisms is itself
conjectural learning and records of inputs and outcomes alone are not. Drop
"control" for that arm at `:488`.

### 4. Reflective builder widens its condition without saying so — resolved

Original `kb/notes/definitions/reflective-theory-builder.md:9-10` required a
builder that "performs reflective theory refinement", which was revision in
response to evidence. The draft at
`notes/definitions/reflective-theory-builder.md:9-10` requires only that it
"revises a theory of its own behavior-determining organization". A builder
that rewrites its self-theory for any reason now qualifies. The
[builder disposition](./builder-disposition.md) records only a change to
"ordinary revision language".

This is a scope question for the operator, not a wording fix. Either add "in
response to criticism" to the opening, or state in Scope that reflection
requires no criticism. Record the choice in the disposition.

**Operator resolution, 2026-09-21:** criticism remains one route for general
builders, and the reflective subtype inherits that openness. Keep both staged
definitions as written. The old reflective definition's inherited
error-correction restriction is deliberately dropped, with the reason and
affected hypothetical builder recorded in the
[decision record](../definition-decision-record.md#builder-revision-and-criticism-resolved-2026-09-21)
and [builder disposition](./builder-disposition.md#criticism-is-one-route-for-either-builder).
No conjectural-learning case changes class. This resolution replaces the two
suggested edits above; no criticism requirement is added to either definition.

### 9. One consumer instruction should be a relabel, not a retain

Fix: move it to an explicit relabel item, narrowing "improvement attribution"
to a claim of observed recurrence.
