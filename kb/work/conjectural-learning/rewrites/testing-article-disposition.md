The mechanism-conjecture table explains content, addressability, and total
cost; it is not a replacement protocol. Adding the retained-criticism row
makes explicit the already-settled reconstruction distinction. The article
leaves practical treatment separation to experiment design and supplies no
new schedule, fixture, assignment rule, or experimental result. Reconstruction
from inputs/outcomes can include private criticism during use; fixed weights
do not hold that internal processing constant. An advantage is a contrast
between tested arrangements, not an isolated effect of criticism or a
conservative estimate merely because a control might also criticize.

Sample efficiency remains target-observation cost, separate from total cost.
Reuse of a useful supplied theory remains separate from selecting one by
estimated explanatory-reach. The whole-program raw-record comparison remains
quoted as adopted; the component table clarifies retained content without
rewriting that hypothesis or its refuter.

## Follow-up failed-test constraint, 2026-09-21

The permission to revise tests in `#component-experiments-that-can-run-first`
now carries the workshop's requirement for grounds to doubt a failed test's
measurement or relevance before replacing or weakening it. A changed test
cannot establish compliance with an unchanged external requirement; changing
that requirement still needs its external authority. This preserves the
existing constraint without changing the adopted hypotheses, ten protocol
declarations, or run-path account.
