## Source-to-draft changes

The skill changes only the ontology portion of step 3, its dependent admitting
route wording in step 4.9, the corresponding Verify item, and the affected
footer links, apart from link rebasing for the workshop location. The result
type changes only the admitting-route paragraph under Shared records, apart
from link rebasing. All frontmatter, headings, and fenced code blocks remain
byte-identical to their originals. Result fields, controlled values,
conclusion statuses, epistemic statuses, canonical IDs, schema reference,
output paths, permissions, specialist handoff, publication, and recovery
protocols are unchanged.

The old operation/loop classification is replaced by ordinary descriptions
of applying, deriving from, criticizing, revising or replacing, and retaining
or reconstructing theories. The same account now governs producer and result:

- Formulation, operative use, content-directed criticism, its result, and
  improved capacity receive separate evidence and conclusion statuses.
- A criticism can leave theory text unchanged while changing reliance or test
  selection. Revision and a connected path do not establish improved capacity.
- Capacity can improve before its next exercise. Later-capacity claims require
  persistence to that time; later-use and recurrent-use claims require their
  corresponding consumer evidence.
- Addressability and retained content are described separately. Whole
  replacement and reconstruction do not exclude learning. Raw input/outcome
  records do not establish what a reconstructor does internally.
- Rules can express a theory without preserving why they were adopted. Missing
  historical rationale does not establish absent formulated content criticism.
  A rule set is classified by its actual claims, not its storage label.
- Unknown model processing remains unknown. Absence needs bounded evidence;
  opacity alone establishes neither formulation nor its absence.
- Reflection requires a self-representation's causal connection in both
  directions. Its subject alone is insufficient; direct modification and
  successful learning are not requirements. Self-theory revision is scoped
  to the narrower reflective-builder claim. Independent review caught the
  initial draft's overrestriction; the skill and type were corrected together.

The footer replaces the two retired ontology definitions with the settled
workshop definitions of tentative theory, conjectural learning, and
addressable theory. It links the staged evidence note for evidence limits.
The old sample-efficiency link and classical-source link are removed from this
operational block: neither an advantage conjecture nor a classical repair
precedent defines the classification the worker must perform. Their substantive
claims remain owned by their notes and sources. The reflective-system link
retains the causal-connection requirement.

## Composition review and remaining boundaries

Read the direct memory specialist, epistemic lens, transfer scan, and the
result-consuming landscape synthesis. Their arguments, outputs, authority,
statuses, and canonical-ID interfaces do not change. The memory specialist
already reports retained rationale and its consumer; the new coordinator
wording now prevents mistaking absence of that rationale for absence of
criticism. The epistemic lens's truth-apt-content and acceptance distinctions
remain independent. Transfer and synthesis preserve the exact result's
source-native findings and evidence bounds. No sibling change is required to
these interfaces by this pair.

The existing `trace_learning` comparison axis has a deliberately narrower
operational contract: trace-fed writes to durable behavior-shaping artifacts
or parameters, with no observed improvement required for its wired
classification. Its name and values remain unchanged. Both drafts explicitly
say this field alone does not establish conjectural learning. Redefining or
renaming that axis would change persisted comparison semantics and is outside
this ontology-only task. Historical results remain bound to their original
method; the draft does not retroactively reclassify them.

No unresolved definition-scope decision blocks this pair. The separate
builder membership question is not imported: these instructions assess
learning and reflection without asserting that every learner is a theory
builder. The empirical-versus-proof distinction is handled by allowing
criticism through argument or testing; proof under uncriticized premises
alone is not identified as criticism.
