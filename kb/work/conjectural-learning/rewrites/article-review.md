## Lead article

The [lead draft](./kb/articles/learning-by-theory-refinement-with-fixed-models.md)
passes this review. It is understandable without prior KB vocabulary: the
opening states the process and chosen arrangement, the exporter example
distinguishes applying an unchanged theory from criticism and improvement,
and subsequent sections return to that case when explaining the mechanism
conjectures. The example is explicitly hypothetical. No measurement is
inferred from its plausibility.

The replacement preserves the original's essential contributions:

- The input-list and included-snippet example remains, with the corrected
  entry-point assumption. Local editing illustrates addressability without
  making it a learning condition.
- Natural-language interpretation and codification retain their respective
  uncertainty and checking roles. The need to build new checks survives.
- Descriptive assumptions, implementation choices, and external requirements
  remain distinct. Tenant isolation cannot be weakened merely to make a
  failure disappear, and evaluator changes do not move requirement authority
  inside the builder.
- Retention, retrieval, validation, maintenance, rollback dependencies, and
  negative transfer remain part of the cost account. Bitter Lesson
  compatibility does not become an asserted scaling advantage.
- Builder responsibility and lineage remain narrower than learning;
  reflection and autonomy stay independent. The article avoids the settled
  definition's then-unresolved universal learner-to-builder cross-reference.
- Whole-program sufficiency, comparison, and reflection hypotheses remain
  separate from the content, addressability, and efficiency conjectures.
  Their summaries retain the adoption date, fixed public-model constraint,
  matched controls, external assessment, and refuters.
- Commonplace's knowledge-delivery arrangement remains the proposed first
  whole-system run. Human-inclusive evidence remains a narrower result;
  the software house remains a companion arrangement.

The old classical-operation and departure exposition is deliberately
replaced, not silently lost. Bounded classical repair precedents remain
elsewhere in the reviewed package. The former preference for revisions with
more explanatory-reach is represented by the separate selector conjecture;
it is no longer a constitutive learning rule. The shorter discussion of
context limits preserves the conditional resource argument through the
research companion and resource proposal without adopting new vocabulary.

No substantive correction was required in the lead. Its draft banner,
byline, status, reader-facing body, and onward links meet the editorial
contract at the workshop staging location. Its workshop links and
`source_notes` must be rebased at promotion as its disposition records.

The body preserves the required evidence boundaries:

- Capacity can improve before exercise; an observed recurrent path does not
  by itself establish improvement. Externally observed payoff remains
  separate from reflection's causal structure.
- Survival of criticism can change reliance without changing theory text.
  Whole replacement and reconstruction remain eligible.
- Fixed weights do not hold processing constant. Raw records do not establish
  absence of private criticism, and a possible hidden process does not make a
  treatment difference a conservative estimate of criticism's contribution.
- Sample efficiency, total cost, and the explanatory-reach selector remain
  separate claims. Null interventions and matching cheaper controls reject
  only the conclusions supported by those contrasts.
- FORTE's classification is invocation-relative; the DGM report does not
  establish continuing theory responsibility. Gödel switching remains
  distinct from the stipulated absence of criticism in case 14, without
  forcing a builder classification or excluding deductive criticism.
