# Migration notes (parked)

Input for the later migration commission; nothing here is authorized. Find
consumers by search when the commission starts. These notes record only what
the definitions and the goals do not already imply.

- **Accounting.** The migration reports definitions before and after, what
  was deleted, shrunk, merged, moved, and added, with word counts. Baseline on
  2026-09-19: [theory refinement](../../notes/definitions/theory-refinement.md)
  2,443 words, [learning by theory refinement](../../notes/definitions/learning-by-theory-refinement.md)
  1,008, the [three-lineages note](../../notes/reflective-theory-refinement-has-three-separate-lineages.md)
  1,810; together 5,261. The current drafts, counted with frontmatter and link
  footers on 2026-09-20 after editorial and review changes: three definitions 2,791
  (conjectural learning 1,568, addressable theory 665, tentative theory 558)
  and one companion note of 943; together 3,734. These counts cover the
  candidate library artifacts, not the whole workshop. Recount with `wc -w`
  before migration. Net savings and the definition count still depend on what
  survives from the existing definitions and lineages note; adoption must
  account for that remainder.
- **Retiring *theory refinement* widens the rename.** Names built on it need a
  disposition: *reflective theory refinement*, the sample-efficiency and
  evidence-ladder note titles, the lead article and its supplements, the
  `theory-refinement-interface` workshop, and the `AGENTS.md` vocabulary
  entry for *tentative theory*, which also drops *addressable*. Notes
  that mean a single revision link the operation definition today and
  will need a plain phrase.
- **What keeps its place.** The two-axis derivation of addressability and the
  computed-versus-interpreted-consequences analysis come from
  [representational form](../../notes/definitions/representational-form.md)
  and [codification](../../notes/definitions/codification.md), not from the
  classical object. The longer argument in the current definition may belong
  in a note, since a definition is not the place for it.
- **Lineages note.** Replace the classical epistemic-lineage attribution with
  Popper; keep the structural and implementation comparisons that still do
  work.
- **Lead article, later and separately.** Open from Popper, then our two
  conditions, the three conjectures, and the fixed-model study condition.
  Local editing becomes the addressability conjecture. Keep the exporter example and the
  maintenance costs.
- **Minimal-revision bias.** Settled before adoption: do not carry it into
  the new basis. Repository search found the preference only in the classical
  theory-refinement lineage, and the addressable-theory draft already rejects
  importing it. During migration, preserve any concrete claim about retaining
  useful knowledge without turning that into a rule to minimize revision.
- **Experiment controls.** İşcan motivates separating a procedure's labels
  from the implemented procedure; POPPER motivates checking that a generated
  test bears on the intended claim. Neither supplies results for Commonplace's
  runs. The connection reports for the new ingests list further candidates,
  under `kb/reports/cache/connect/sources/`.
