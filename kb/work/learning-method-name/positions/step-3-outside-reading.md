## Wording that affected the reading

- **“Makes and reverses changes” leaves a gap.** A learner with a fixed
  admission criterion can selectively append lessons that guide future work
  and have no way to reverse them. It meets both membership conditions. The
  literal single-loop wording excludes it, and it cannot be double-loop.
- **A decision needs a named alternative.** The ranking job can write its
  table every night while declining to promote a particular article. The
  answers differ depending on which edit the reader tests.
- **One sentence still says “three conditions.”** It occurs in the example
  of a fixed measure inside a loop that can revise the measure.

The reader found the name natural for the routing team and the selective
memory writer. It needed the explicit fixed-measure sentence to include the
ranker and the validator. For *learned*, it recovered the comparison of later
work with and without the particular edit, for a declared purpose and
horizon, across every reasonable whole measure. None of the eight vignettes
establishes that verdict.
