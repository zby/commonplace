Updated on 2026-09-05 after the operator asked whether the machinery was ready
for a rerun. The [preceding triage](./c10-oh-my-pi-audit.md) identified the

## Deployed behavior and interfaces

The canonical [producer skill](../../instructions/analyse-agentic-system/SKILL.md)
is loaded through the existing skill projections. Its input/output paths,
authority, lens interfaces and publication commands are unchanged. Its final
integrated check now requires scope agreement, coverage of every qualifying
learning route, justified push selection, and stable canonical identities.
The producer records checked routes and material dispositions in its existing
Semantic verification section. An unsupported known classification blocks
publication; explicit uncertainty within a coherent scope can remain valid.

The [result contract](../../types/agentic-system-analysis-result.md) now makes
the relevant distinctions explicit: local/display material versus opaque
payloads, generated continuation summaries versus raw logs, actual selection
versus names on requested objects, and canonical identity before final
acceptance. The [C10 audit](../../instructions/refresh-agent-memory-review-taxonomy.md)
applies the same questions to published results. The producer performs its
check on the unfinished exact result; it does not call C10's published-input
reader before publication.

Producer step 2 now requires bounded source ranges and aggregate output
budgeting. Step 3 specifies new IDs for split records after sharing, keeping
the combined record's identity explicit. Sparse overlays and the existing
execution-preflight policy remain the applicable rules for repetition and
unexecuted tests.

Composition search covered the epistemic lens, legacy writer, transfer scan,
placement pass, landscape synthesis and C10. Their argument/result protocols
remain compatible. C10 consumes the refined classification semantics and was
updated with the producer and type. No schema, Python implementation,
publication gate, extra review artifact or skill metadata changed.
