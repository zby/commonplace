# Lessons learned

This migration records what the `prose` → `natural-language` change taught beyond the vocabulary result itself.

The procedural lessons have been transferred as a provisional [terminology migration method candidate](../vocabulary-governance/terminology-migration-method-candidate.md). They remain workshop material until another vocabulary shift tests whether the method recurs; this file retains the case-specific evidence until this workshop closes.

## Classification has to precede replacement

The exact `prose artifact(s)` seed exposed four different outcomes: a category replacement, the narrower model-input term `prompt`, a precise artifact noun, and preservation. Expanding from that seed to technical compounds and then to the broad residual audit kept ordinary editorial uses, named review machinery, filenames, and historical records from being swept into the category change. A single replacement rule could not have produced the correct corpus.

This lesson has been promoted into the recovery procedure in [load-bearing vocabulary collisions should be prevented or visibly scoped at write time](../../notes/vocabulary-collisions-prevented-at-write-time-not-read-time.md): terminology remediation is baseline-anchored, classification-led, residual-reconciled, and independently verified. A later `symbolic` / `programmatic` investigation can reuse that durable procedure without this workshop.

## The precise noun check catches abstraction drift

Several seed cases became clearer only after the first category-shaped wording was challenged: wiki pages remained wiki pages, digests and drafts remained those records, the Dynamic Cheatsheet memory remained a cheatsheet, and acknowledgement stopped mutating review documents. The natural-language category is useful when form changes the inference; elsewhere it is needless altitude.

## Consumption path cannot stand in for form

Only the Tendril seed earned a `prompt` disposition because the referent was the actual system prompt. A cheatsheet later inserted into a prompt, a stored memory that may be retrieved, and an instruction file read by a host all retain identities outside that one consumption path. The durable revision rationale in [representational form](../../notes/definitions/representational-form.md) now records this boundary.

## Terminology can trigger structural validation

Changing the dereference note's title from `Prose` to `Natural-language content` pushed it over the 100-character title limit. Deterministic validation caught the failure; the repaired 96-character title preserves the claim. Even a prose-only terminology migration therefore needs per-artifact structural and link validation.

## Residuals are evidence, not cleanup debt

The final broad search is intentionally nonzero. Its surviving occurrences form five explainable classes: ordinary/editorial language, named machinery or locators, explicit migration rationale, historical ADR or archived-proposal wording, and excluded historical replacement reviews. Recording the classes is stronger evidence of completeness than pursuing a misleading zero count.
