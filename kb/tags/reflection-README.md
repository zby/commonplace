# reflection

Notes on reflective systems: systems that read and change their own organization through a representation of themselves. The defining note is [Reflective system](../notes/definitions/reflective-system.md). A self-improving pathway is reflective when it routes objective-bearing evidence into a change to the system's behavior-determining organization through a causally connected self-representation, and later operation depends on that change. The tag gathers what reflection buys (addressable, selectively revisable retention), how far its coverage reaches, and how it fails. It is a child of [self-improving-systems](./self-improving-systems-README.md). Boundary: a system can improve without reflection, as parametric learners do; which retention mechanisms keep a deployed system learning belongs to [continual-learning](./continual-learning-README.md), and reflection is the narrower question of whether the changed object is represented and addressable.

## Definition and what reflection buys

- [Reflective system](../notes/definitions/reflective-system.md) — the definition, relative to selected aspects: an internal process uses a causally connected self-representation of them
- [Reflection buys addressability](../notes/reflection-buys-addressability.md) — the payoff claim: non-reflective retention gives only indirect handles, reflective retention makes the changed object a direct target
- [Reflection makes retained lessons second-order](../notes/reflection-makes-retained-lessons-second-order.md) — a represented lesson can reject or rescope a prior commitment instead of correcting it through the substrate
- [Explicit retention provides direct targets for selective revision](../notes/only-explicit-retention-is-durable-writable-and-addressable.md) — why explicit artifacts are the carrier, and which properties still depend on the boundary and available operations
- [Localized retention pays when sparse changes have bounded impact](../notes/localized-retention-pays-where-change-is-sparse-in-a-matching.md) — the condition under which addressability saves work: a matching decomposition and a small impact closure

## Scope and limits of reflective coverage

- [Reflective coverage is graded across representational forms](../notes/reflective-coverage-is-graded-across-representational-forms.md) — coverage is stated per form and operation; controlling an external dependency does not bring it inside
- [A repeatable operative path keeps a redesign class open to revision](../notes/a-repeatable-operative-path-keeps-a-redesign-class-open-to-revision.md) — the causal path a redesign class needs, from representation through admission to continuity
- [Machinery persists by warrant, not position, in a reflective loop](../notes/machinery-persists-by-warrant-not-position-in-a-reflective-loop.md) — being revisable does not oblige revision; fixed general machinery may stay when its role is earned
- [Gödel machines are a proof-governed case of reflective self-modification](../notes/goedel-machines-are-a-proof-governed-case-of-self-modification.md) — the formal extreme: proof restricts admission but says nothing about how many useful rewrites are reachable

## Failure modes

- [A retrieval miss is a local reflective-path failure](../notes/a-retrieval-miss-is-a-local-reflective-path-failure.md) — a lesson that is not loaded is inert for that task and route, while other paths stay connected
- [Stale self-description conceals its own staleness](../notes/stale-self-description-conceals-its-own-staleness.md) — the checking process consults the artifact that drifted, so reflexive drift has no edit event to catch
- [A consumption channel delivers force without the history that earned it](../notes/a-consumption-channel-delivers-force-without-the-history-that.md) — a self-representation can be promoted to binding force without checking that its authorization covers this use
