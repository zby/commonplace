# Repair dispositions for defeated claims are an epistemic policy with an option space

When the premise-decomposition gate GLOBAL-defeats a load-bearing premise, the full-pass instruction offers three exits: qualify the claim, keep-reframe it ("the strongest claim the artifact warrants"), or delete the note. That set encodes a warrant-now epistemic policy — a defeated claim survives only in whatever form is defensible today. The policy ships implicit and universal: nothing in the disposition vocabulary, the profile catalogue, or the collection contracts names it as a choice, and no alternative form exists for a claim whose crisp version is refuted in detail but whose unification is worth keeping.

The reframing this proposal rests on: which repairs review may take on a defeated claim is not review mechanics but epistemic policy, and different installations can legitimately want different policies. This installation's purpose is decision-support, which the warrant-now standard serves. An installation whose goal is explanation-first inquiry might instead keep defeated-but-fertile conjectures at a declared lifecycle stage, or keep a refuted-in-detail exclusive model as a declared idealization with a validity domain — the standard move in physics, where the ideal-gas law outlives its counterexamples because its corrections are lawful. The design question is where that policy is declared, what options it admits, and what keeps each option honest.

## Current state (as of 2026-08-19)

- **The disposition vocabulary is `keep | delete | merge | rehome`**, with reframe explicitly "a keep, executed in the pass — not a fourth disposition" (`kb/instructions/run-full-improvement-pass-on-note.md`). A GLOBAL-defeated premise the commitment cannot survive without, and that no qualification repairs, routes to delete; a title that overreaches routes to keep-reframe, reached "only when qualification genuinely cannot save the title."
- **Two guards police the reframe path**: the refuter test and the citer test from `kb/notes/narrowing-bought-to-survive-review-is-paid-for-in-content.md`. They have run on two keep-reframes and discriminated both times. They catch analytic emptiness and orphaned citers; nothing checks for genre drift (a causal claim landing as a diagnostic one) or asks whether a priced first-order model was available.
- **First trigger case**: full pass `20260818T132531Z-e11d99` defeated "each standard remedy acts on exactly one of the three objects" in the deviation-taxonomy note and keep-reframed it to "LLM output deviation requires three-way diagnosis." The reframe passed both guards, and a follow-up worked comparison re-earned empirical content (the masking/perturbation discriminator). The episode also shows the gap: the crisp one-remedy-per-object model had no survivable declared form and persists only informally as "primary target." Whether an idealization would have passed an honesty test there is undetermined — the defeating cross-effects are not obviously subordinate corrections — but the vocabulary offered no form in which to run the test.
- **Second trigger case**: full pass `20260818T221445Z-91860b` GLOBAL-defeated three premises of the class-instance-analogy note ("class-based OO fixes a class before its instances exist," "fixes the boundary at authoring time," "instance state only parameterizes a fixed repertoire") with reflective-mutation counterexamples: Python mutating `type(self)`, Ruby singleton methods, per-instance bound methods. Every cited counterexample is one the source domain itself marks as exceptional — routed through a separate reflection API or metaobject protocol, named pejoratively (monkey-patching), charged for by runtimes (JIT deoptimization), or ritualized into governance (changesets, Erlang `code_change`) — and prototype-based OO exists as a rival paradigm precisely because class-based OO would not give up the guarantee. Here the honesty test plausibly *passes*: the defeated claims read as ideal-type claims about the paradigm whose exceptions the paradigm prices, and the gate's two-verdict vocabulary (DEFEATED or HOLDS) could not say so. The conversion has since been executed by direct revision (2026-08-19): the note — now `kb/notes/instantiation-alone-cannot-model-agent-learning-across-sessions.md` — carries the strong claim as a declared idealization with its pricing inline. That is content-level work outside any pass; no gate has attacked the carried pricing, so the adoption criterion below stands unmet, with the next full pass on that note as its natural first run. Records for both cases: `kb/work/popperian-maintenance-episode/` (workshop, will close).
- **The pipeline's repairs all weaken.** Author-side guidance sweeps drafts for absolutes (narrow before review); gate-side defeat forces qualification, reframe, or delete. With no strengthening or modality-preserving repair available, repeated passes ratchet claims toward hedged weakness even where a strong ideal-type claim with declared exception structure was the more useful artifact. Two witnessed cases; in the first, the narrowed claim happened to end sharper — an outcome of the material, not of the procedure.
- **Profiles declare text contracts, not epistemic policy.** `kb/reference/text-contract-profiles.md` catalogues theoretical, descriptive, and prescriptive profiles covering quality goal, title convention, attribution, maintenance semantics, and link grammar. The set is open, guarded by worked-case-first promotion. Repair policy is not among the declared features.
- **The lifecycle vocabulary already exists for one alternative.** `kb/notes/definitions/discovery-lifecycle.md` names conjecture as a stage; a hunch-status framing appears in at least one note (`kb/notes/execution-shaping-determines-directory-placement.md`). There is no disposition that routes a defeated claim to a declared conjecture stage instead of reframing or deleting it.
- **Traits are the existing per-note review-routing surface** (`kb/types/note.md`: "independently checkable review expectations"; gates consume them via `requires_trait`).

## The design space

1. **Document the status quo as policy.** Keep reframe-to-warranted universal, but name it: one paragraph in the theoretical profile's entry in the profile catalogue (and/or `kb/notes/COLLECTION.md`) stating that defeated claims are repaired to their warranted form and why that serves this installation's decision-support purpose. Operativity: consumed by authoring and review agents reading the contract they already read; no new mechanism. Cost: other installations inherit the policy silently with the framework, and the idealization question stays unposable.

2. **Add a declared-modality form within the current policy.** Claims declare their modality — universal, statistical, or ideal-type — via a trait or frontmatter field; undeclared defaults to universal and eats counterexamples exactly as today, which pushes the declaration burden onto authors rather than creating a retroactive escape hatch. For a declared ideal-type claim, the premise-decomposition gate's question changes from "does a counterexample exist?" to "is this counterexample priced, and does the note carry the pricing?", and its verdict vocabulary gains "holds as idealization, exceptions priced" beside DEFEATED and HOLDS. A full pass may then repair a defeated crisp claim by conversion to a declared idealization as one more keep variant. The honesty test that keeps this from being an immunizing stratagem — the pricing independently attested in the source domain (marked separate interface, pejorative name, tooling cost, governance ritual, rival paradigm), never authored after the counterexample lands; an unpriced counterexample still refutes — is a transferable requirement and lives in theory (see Relevant Notes). Operativity: consumed by the pass instruction (new keep variant), the gate specs (modality-conditional behavior), and `kb/types/note.md` (trait registry). Cost: a new degenerate form to police — the guards gain a third failure mode (immunization) alongside emptiness and orphaned citers.

3. **Profile-level repair policy.** The text-contract profile declares which repair dispositions review may take on defeated claims — reframe-to-warranted, keep-as-conjecture-at-declared-stage, keep-as-idealization — and the pass instruction reads the declaration. Installations with explanation-first goals choose differently from decision-support installations. Operativity: no consumer yet; the profile catalogue would need a declared-feature slot and the pass instruction a parameterization it currently does not have — both must be built, which is exactly what the adoption decision needs to see. Cost: epistemic-policy proliferation across installations, and the pass instruction's disposition logic becomes conditional on a document it currently never reads.

Option 2 is a strict subset of option 3's machinery and can ship first; option 1 is compatible with either and is near-free. Under every option, one piece of work is identical: state somewhere authoritative that the current policy is a policy.

## Free choices

- **Where the policy is declared** (options 1 and 3): the profile catalogue, the collection contract, or both — the catalogue names the feature, the contract binds it.
- **Conversion path** (option 2): three exist, with distinct risk profiles. Write-time declaration risks pre-emptive shielding against review; the mitigation is that the pricing test demands domain-attested evidence the author cannot invent, and undeclared modality defaults to universal. Pass-produced repair meets gate resistance at conversion time. Post-hoc direct revision outside any pass — the path actually taken on 2026-08-19 — meets no resistance at conversion and defers the honesty test to the next pass; if the policy allows it, the deferred test should be mandatory on that next pass rather than incidental.
- **Gate design for the honesty test** (option 2): whether the premise gate itself learns modality-conditional behavior, or a dedicated pricing gate (exception pricing domain-attested, not post hoc; validity domain stated in the model's own vocabulary, not enumerated from survived counterexamples) runs beside it. A drift-detection sub-choice rides on shipped machinery: register the pricing attestations as `file-text` snapshot inputs on the declared idealization's review pair in the freshness store, so domain drift — an exception becoming ordinary practice — surfaces as input staleness instead of waiting for a noticed counterexample.
- **Whether conjecture-stage keeping is a separate option** (option 3) or collapses into the existing hunch-status practice plus a frontmatter field — the discovery lifecycle already names the stages; what is missing is only the disposition that routes to one.
- **Naming.** "Idealization" collides with ordinary praise-adjacent readings; alternatives (first-order model, declared-ideal) trade familiarity against the physics lineage.

## Adoption criteria

- **The honesty test run for real, and discriminating.** Two candidate episodes exist (the trigger cases above), and the pricing analysis of the second is done on paper: what remains is applying the test inside an actual pass — one conversion-to-idealization accepted with its pricing attested, or one rejected as unpriced — before the verdict vocabulary ships. The profile catalogue's worked-case-first bar applies to policy features as much as to profiles, and a test never run against resistance is not yet a gate.
- **The declared policy is consumed, not decorative**: the pass instruction's disposition step reads it (option 3) or embeds it (options 1–2); a policy statement no reviewer's behavior depends on repeats the unenforced-mark failure the marks system exists to prevent.
- **The honesty test is stated precisely enough to fail**: a proposed idealization whose corrections are unpriced or dominant must be rejectable by the gate text, not by reviewer taste.
- **No silent inheritance**: whatever ships, a downstream installation reading the profile catalogue can see which repair policy it has adopted.

## Risks

- **Idealization as immunizing stratagem.** The option exists to preserve unification, and the same form absorbs counterexamples indefinitely if the honesty test is weakly enforced. This is the symmetric risk to the analytic drift the narrowing guards police: narrowing degenerates to emptiness, idealizing degenerates to unfalsifiability.
- **Policy without cases.** Option 3 built before a second installation or second episode exists would fix profile machinery ahead of any force demanding it — the implementation-plan-first mistake this collection's contract warns against.
- **Guard dilution.** Every added repair form widens what the refuter and citer guards must discriminate; if their application stays manual, more forms mean more unguarded paths.
