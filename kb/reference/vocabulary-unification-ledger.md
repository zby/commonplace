## Maintenance

Add a candidate when a comparison supplies a specific common account worth testing. The record explains what each concept contributes and the conditions under which the concepts come apart. Keep extended investigation in a workshop.

When adopting a common account, record it and any retained distinctions in the relevant definitions or theory notes and update affected consumers. When rejecting a candidate, retain the useful distinction in the relevant notes. Remove the resolved entry and record the resolution in the commit.
