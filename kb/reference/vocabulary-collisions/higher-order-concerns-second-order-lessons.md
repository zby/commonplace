# Higher-order concerns / second-order lessons

Keep the full compounds and explain the revision dependency locally. Do not use bare “higher-order” as a synonym for reflective or register a shared hierarchy. Separate definitions are not needed for this bounded use.

## Senses

Revision: concerns whose resolution may discard dependent editing work, scoped in the [improvement proposal](../proposals/one-improvement-procedure-over-atomic-instructions.md). Reflection: lessons that explicitly target a represented prior commitment, in [second-order retention](../../theory/reflection-makes-retained-lessons-second-order.md).

## Why these uses are retained

The compounds name different relations: revision dependency and the object of a lesson. Neither implies the other.

## When to reconsider

An artifact uses the two as interchangeable premises, or an ordering rule needs a shared formal hierarchy.
