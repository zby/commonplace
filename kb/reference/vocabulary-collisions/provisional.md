# Provisional

Use "provisional" only of search judgments. Theories are "tentative"; retained commitments take no adjective.

## Senses

Technical: the status of a branch choice that backtracking can revoke ([backtracking note](../../theory/backtracking-keeps-lightweight-search-control-provisional.md)). Everyday: hesitant or temporary, which the [tentative theory](../../theory/definitions/tentative-theory.md) definition excludes from "tentative".

## Why these uses are retained

The technical use predicates a search judgment and is linked at its source.

## When to reconsider

"Provisional" is predicated of a theory or a retained commitment in a library note.
