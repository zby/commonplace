# Ingest: Microsoft Retired Smart Client Software Factory

## Classification

This is an official product-status statement: a Microsoft download page marks the May 2007 Smart Client Software Factory as retired and briefly describes the package. Author: Microsoft is the direct authority for the disposition and contents of its own downloadable product, but the page is not an independent evaluation of the product or the wider software-factory program.

## Summary

Microsoft's product page states that the Smart Client Software Factory is retired and that no future releases are planned. It describes the factory as an integrated collection of guidance for composite smart-client application development, including QuickStarts, reference implementations, how-to material, patterns, and Visual Studio .NET extensions. The page therefore establishes the discontinued status and composition of one named Microsoft factory, but it gives no retirement date, reason, adoption history, or evidence that the product's method failed in use.

## Extractable Value

4. **Treat the displayed 2024 publication date as page metadata, not an event date.** The product is versioned May 2007, but the page does not say when the retirement decision occurred. [quick-win]

## Limitations (our opinion)

The page is authoritative for Microsoft's current product status and the package description, but it is a sparse vendor record rather than an outcome study. It reports no retirement date, decision rationale, usage or adoption evidence, maintenance history, customer results, comparison with alternatives, or definition of success and failure. The displayed July 2024 publication date appears to describe the current page record, not the retirement event. Retirement could reflect product strategy, platform change, maintenance economics, demand, technical shortcomings, or some combination; this source cannot distinguish among them. Because it concerns one named Smart Client factory, it cannot establish the fate of Microsoft's entire Software Factories strategy or of customer deployments such as Aspen.
