# How do we value notes?

## Stakes

Root `AGENTS.md` and each collection's `COLLECTION.md` set baseline scope and quality bars, such as [explanatory-reach](../first-principles-reasoning-selects-for-explanatory-reach-over.md) (how broadly an explanation transfers beyond the cases that produced it) in `kb/theory/` or fidelity and economy in `kb/reference/`. Those goals decide what a collection admits in principle, but they do not exhaust how to rank, keep, or reject candidate notes.

The question becomes urgent as autonomous production increases note volume. Every retained artifact adds maintenance, navigation, and exposure cost; [maintenance capacity must match harmful-artifact inflow](../maintenance-capacity-must-match-harmful-artifact-inflow.md). Without a clearer way to distinguish high-value notes from merely valid ones, autonomous authoring can accumulate well-formed clutter faster than review and curation can keep up.

Model upgrades add a second pressure. Because [information value is observer-relative](../information-value-is-observer-relative.md), a note's value depends on what its reader can supply unaided. A valuation practice that checks only whether a note helps today can over-invest in prose that stronger models absorb and under-invest in artifacts whose value holds up across upgrades.

This query stays open as long as candidate selection relies on ad-hoc judgment rather than a tested valuation practice; it would narrow or retire once Commonplace adopts and validates an explicit selection mechanism.

## Current answer

Commonplace sets requirements for admission. It leaves room for judgment about which eligible notes are worth keeping. The KB offers guidance for that judgment and proposals for ranking notes, not a single score that decides it:

- **Admission and answerability.** A note enters a knowledge collection only when it is [answerable](../../reference/definitions/answerability.md): its contract can name what referent or outcome it answers to, what correctness or currency property it asserts, and what discrepancy triggers revision or retirement.
- **Validity separate from value.** [Choosing what to learn requires both validity and learning-value gates](../choosing-what-to-learn-requires-both-validity-and-learning-value-gates.md): a note can be grounded and coherent yet redundant or too narrow to justify its maintenance cost. Similarly, [a claim's test record does not determine its fit in a working theory](../a-claims-test-record-does-not-determine-its-fit-in-a-working-theory.md) separates whether a claim is well-tested from whether it helps the current theory handle its problem.
- **Reader update and comparative work gain.** For substantive writing, [warranted reader update is the objective of substantive writing](../warranted-reader-update-is-the-objective-of-substantive-writing.md) asks what nontrivial change the note warrants relative to the intended reader and existing KB beyond a generic treatment. [Defining improvement for open-ended work](../defining-improvement-for-open-ended-work.md) grounds that test in expected downstream work and compares adding a note against a cheaper alternative such as adding a link to an existing record.
- **Evidence from use.** [A claim’s effect on live work is evidence of its fit](../system-use-selects-theory-fit-without-a-fixed-oracle.md). Applied to a note, that suggests a comparison: does withholding or replacing it change where an agent searches, what it diagnoses, or how it recovers?
- **Durability across model upgrades.** [Model upgrades obsolete notes by function rather than uniformly](../model-upgrades-obsolete-notes-by-function-rather-than-uniformly.md): stronger models can erode pure activation cues, weight-resident general knowledge, and fixed-horizon step scaffolding while sparing local commitments, unrecoverable history, shared vocabulary and methodology selection, frontier-shifted structure, and preserved search work. Under the conjecture that [discriminating examples may outlast principles across upgrades](../discriminating-examples-may-outlast-principles-across-upgrades.md), discovery–verification asymmetry gives hard-to-find, cheap-to-verify examples durable value across upgrades and strengthens the case for pairing important abstract ideas in Commonplace with concrete discriminating examples.
- **Structural and curation proxies.** One [proposed score for link candidates](../notes-need-quality-scores-to-scale-curation.md) combines note type, review verdicts, recency, and inbound links weighted by their strength. Its purpose is to decide which candidates deserve a closer look, not which notes to keep. That proposal treats design principles as having zero recency decay, so it does not yet account for capability-driven obsolescence as models improve. A separate [catalogue of possible quality signals](../quality-signals-for-kb-evaluation.md) explores measures such as orphan rate and similarity between note descriptions.

## Unsettled

- **Root questions versus sub-questions inside notes.** Weighted inbound links measure reuse when one note answers a local sub-question raised inside another note. Commonplace's reader-need labels already express that relation: `grounds`, `mechanism`, `defined-in` and `evidenced-by` in [link vocabulary](../../reference/link-vocabulary.md). Thinking also organizes directly around recurring **root questions** rather than a single claim. Without explicit query-centered notes as roots, a top-level note that answers a live question can look orphaned if no other claim cites it. Without such roots, the notes in a tightly linked cluster can also answer each other's sub-questions in a closed loop without serving any real inquiry. We have no established way to represent query-centered root notes or propagate value from them down through answering notes and the notes that answer their sub-questions.
- **Asymmetric selection pressure under autonomous production.** Structural validation, source grounding, and critique gates check validity more strongly than anything checks learning value or reader update. Under the conjecture that [weakly discriminated qualities tend to be underselected](../weakly-discriminated-qualities-tend-to-be-underselected.md), an autonomous authoring loop can enrich accepted notes more strongly for validity than for usefulness—even producing analytic rewrites that pass every gate with near-zero reader update unless guarded by a contribution check such as inbound citers' revealed use ([narrowing bought to survive review is paid for in content](../narrowing-bought-to-survive-review-is-paid-for-in-content.md)).
- **Cold-start selection at admission.** Inbound links and counterfactual system use only become observable after a note has been retained and used. Commonplace has an observation-inbox staging principle ([promote only when future value exceeds maintenance cost](../agent-memory-requirements/promote-only-when-value-exceeds-cost.md)) and a manual log-promotion checklist ([evaluate a log entry for note creation](../../instructions/evaluate-log-entry-for-note-creation.md)), but autonomous production still lacks a tested automated way to filter, quarantine, or trial new candidates before downstream use signals exist.
- **Combining dimensions without Goodharting.** Even where several signals exist (reader update, reuse of answers to sub-questions, root-question coverage, counterfactual use, maintenance cost), we do not yet know how to operationalize multi-dimensional selection—whether through partial-order rules that leave genuine trade-offs open ([necessary preference can guide improvement without a global measure](../necessary-preference-can-guide-improvement-without-a-global-measure.md)), coarse triage checks, or a composite score—without Goodharting.
- **Measuring capability-driven obsolescence and example durability.** Whether stronger models actually erode abstract principles faster than discriminating examples—and whether compact cues or concrete examples activate weight-resident knowledge more efficiently per token—remains an open empirical question requiring cross-upgrade withholding tests.
- **Prioritizing concrete examples without inviting filler.** We lack a practical rule for choosing which important abstract ideas in Commonplace warrant dedicated companion examples first, and when maintaining real-system examples costs more than the discovery work they preserve.
