# What gaps remain in our learning loop?

## Stakes

Showing that a fixed-weight agent can learn *something* across runs is already easy: saving notes, appending reminders to prompt files, or patching a script improves the next few tasks. The hard problem is why ordinary agent memory plateaus so quickly—contradictory patches pile up, context windows fill, retrieval misses the right entry, and performance stalls unless a person cleans up the store ([Continual Learning with Fixed Model Weights](../../articles/continual-learning-with-fixed-model-weights-llm-selected-updates.md)).

Starting from Commonplace's definitions sharpens what the learning loop is trying to automate:

- **System learning, not work-product repair.** Fixing one note or moving one misplaced draft improves an output. A [self-improving system](../definitions/self-improving-system.md) makes an **operative change** to its own retained organization—notes, routing rules, procedures, hypotheses, checks, and code—verified by whether withholding the retained change alters later fresh work.
- **Reflective self-improvement by selection.** Rather than appending every observation directly, the loop runs [selection-based learning](../definitions/selection-based-learning.md): a change to what the system retains is proposed, evaluated with the possibility of rejection, and retained with behavioral authority through a causally connected [self-representation](../definitions/reflective-system.md).
- **The autonomy boundary.** External inputs—user questions, bug reports, reader objections—do not make the loop human-assisted. A person supplying the internal update decisions (noticing a recurring problem, diagnosing it, generating candidates or checks, selecting what to keep, installing it, or revising the criterion) does.

Commonplace already runs this loop with a human operator inside the boundary. This query tracks the specific gaps between that human-assisted loop and an autonomous fixed-weight loop that clears four continual-learning bars: sustained growth without an accretion plateau, acquiring reusable methods and executable tools, targeted credit assignment and criterion revision without a scalar reward, and outgrowing its hand-crafted bootstrap. It stays open while internal update decisions still depend on the operator and no end-to-end automated run has cleared those bars; individual gaps narrow or close as controlled tests validate them.

## Current answer

Recent theory and experimental design narrow where the real difficulties sit, replacing the earlier framing of "missing a global KB quality score" with a more specific architecture:

- **Loop anatomy and KB mutation space.** A [proposal-selection improvement loop](../a-proposal-selection-loop-requires-search-evaluation-and-retention.md) separates search, reject-capable evaluation, and operative retention. Over a knowledge base and its operating machinery, candidate mutations include both content-graph edits—extract, split, synthesize, relink, regroup, reformulate, and retire—and revisions to procedures, review checks, hypotheses, and executable tools.
- **Local comparison instead of a global score.** Because costly search reaches only a few candidates at a time, selection needs a [local comparison among reached alternatives](../selection-based-learning-needs-only-a-local-comparison.md), not a global score over every possible KB state. [Defining improvement for open-ended work](../defining-improvement-for-open-ended-work.md) anchors that comparison in expected downstream work, named alternatives, and standing commitments. One formalization is [asymmetric necessary preference](../necessary-preference-can-guide-improvement-without-a-global-measure.md) (Improvement, Loss, Tie, Open), paired with [bounded reversible trials](../reversibility-can-favor-a-change-by-limiting-future-loss.md) when comparisons stay open.
- **Oracle hardening and amplification—and where they stop.** The [oracle-strength spectrum](../oracle-strength-spectrum.md) frames verification improvement as *manufacture, amplify, monitor*: [spec mining](../spec-mining-as-codification.md) turns observed regularities into deterministic checks, and [error correction](../error-correction-works-above-chance-oracles-with-decorrelated-checks.md) amplifies a weak check into a stronger one whenever it has per-instance discrimination (TPR > FPR) and decorrelated errors, at repetition cost scaling as 1/(TPR − FPR)². That strategy strengthens local filtering over reached candidates under a settled check, but does not close the remaining gaps by itself. Amplification cannot substitute for search, supply missing evidence, resolve value trade-offs, or localize causal blame. [Stagnant per-instance discrimination](../the-augmentation-automation-boundary-is-discrimination-not-accuracy.md) limits naive LLM self-assessment, while [shared content biases](../error-correction-works-above-chance-oracles-with-decorrelated-checks.md) and [the field's consensus prior](../reviewers-share-the-fields-prior-so-interpret-findings-by-stance.md) survive multi-model voting. [Evaluation automation is phase-gated by comprehension](../evaluation-automation-is-phase-gated-by-comprehension.md) of observed failures, and repeating a check of the wrong property only hardens the wrong target.
- **Conditional demands on the frozen base model.** The harness can offload enforcement, trace capture, replay, and context budgets. The frozen LLM is demanded only where rules and search enumeration do not settle a step: interpreting an unencoded prose procedure, diagnosing a failure to reach a repair outside current search, or judging an unsettled trade-off ([what a fixed-weight selection loop still needs from its base model](../base-llm-competences-for-autonomous-selection-based-learning.md)).
- **Decision-by-decision transfer and separate outcome testing.** Commonplace moves update decisions from the operator to computation one kind at a time—either by [codification](../definitions/codification.md) into deterministic checks or by delegating an open judgment to an LLM under a written procedure. Transfer is judged by withholding the operator ([boundary contraction](../computationally-directed-self-improvement-is-a-reallocation.md); [Bootstrapping Autonomous Selection-Based Learning](../../articles/bootstrapping-autonomous-selection-based-learning.md)); whether transferred decisions improve fresh downstream work is tested separately ([Testing Autonomous Selection-Based Learning](../../articles/testing-autonomous-selection-based-learning.md)).
- **What adjacent systems establish and leave open.** [Pi Self-Learning](../../agentic-system-analyses/retained/mcollina--pi-self-learning/memory.md#mem-obj-index-ranking) shows that automated trace extraction, multi-signal ranking, and injection are straightforward while lesson validity stays unverified. [Knowledge-Centric Self-Improvement](../../sources/knowledge-centric-self-improvement-2607.19592.ingest.md) gives a bounded positive case: task forums, cross-task discussion, and scoped distillation—where "the distiller LLM is instructed to keep claims that are actionable, evidence-grounded, and scoped, and to drop vague advice that does not name the condition under which it applies" ([Knowledge-Centric Self-Improvement](../../sources/knowledge-centric-self-improvement-2607.19592.ingest.md), verbatim)—produced positive zero-shot held-out transfer on selected Polyglot and ARC-AGI-1 splits. That protocol still relies on fixed symbolic benchmark evaluators for attempt outcomes; it does not establish automated curation for open-ended KB maintenance where questions evolve and no comparable solve-rate oracle exists.

## Unsettled

- **Autonomous noticing and structural candidate search.** Validators can flag broken links or schema errors, and an LLM can draft a patch once pointed at a failure. What remains missing is detecting *which* recurring failure, contradiction, or missing abstraction merits a system update without an operator spotting it, and reaching structural mutations—synthesizing across notes, regrouping, retiring obsolete structure, or building a reusable procedure or tool—rather than appending local exceptions.
- **Active inquiry and faithful local judgment under a budget.** Handing an LLM a pre-curated packet already does much of the judgment before the model runs. In [tool-using inquiry](../../articles/can-an-llm-judge-which-changes-are-worth-making.md), no validated LLM judge yet reliably identifies which missing fact could flip a verdict, reads record coverage limits alongside results (treating absence in incomplete logs as missing evidence rather than safety), lets retrieved answers actually move its verdict rather than rationalizing a prior bias ([faithfulness over legibility](../revision-guided-by-rationale-needs-faithfulness-not-just-legibility.md)), and leaves genuinely contested or under-evidenced comparisons open.
- **Where LLMs qualify as amplifiable weak oracles—and at what cost.** It remains untested on which KB update decisions current LLMs actually clear TPR > FPR on downstream quality rather than local plausibility or consensus bias, whether [composite structural and neighborhood signals](../quality-signals-for-kb-evaluation.md) or [pairwise comparison](../brainstorming-how-to-test-whether-pairwise-comparison-can-harden.md) raise base discrimination after controlling for position bias and Goodharting, which metamorphic or external checks decorrelate shared priors cheaply enough that 1/(TPR − FPR)² amplification stays net-positive, and whether accumulating soft checks hits a softness ceiling on assessing claim scope.
- **Clearing the accretion plateau across long horizons.** Appending rules and notes is local and cheap; pruning, merging, refactoring, and retiring them require distributed evidence and migration. We lack tested mechanisms that pair validity checks with learning-value selection ([How do we value notes?](./how-do-we-value-notes.md)) and active retirement ([How do we fight bloat in agent-produced systems?](./how-do-we-fight-bloat-in-agent-produced-systems.md)), and that reliably decide when to promote recurring prose rules into deterministic checks or code.
- **Credit assignment without a chain rule in a mixed prose-and-code store.** When a later regression appears in an interdependent store, locating the responsible upstream edit (or combination of edits) is a [debugging problem](../credit-assignment-without-a-chain-rule-is-a-debugging-problem.md) weakened by softer traces (a loaded prose file does not prove which sentence steered behavior), implicit semantic coupling across tasks, and diffuse failures such as context saturation. Keeping a growing mixed store modular, traceable, and cheaply replayable without human refactoring remains open.
- **Selective double-loop criterion revision.** For a class of changes such as routing or review instructions, a single-loop learner cannot recover when its adoption check misleads ([single-loop and double-loop learning](../definitions/single-loop-and-double-loop-learning.md))—amplifying a misleading check only hardens the wrong target. Revising a working criterion autonomously—repairing a faulty check while leaving sound checks untouched when failures stem from another cause, and revising the check without discarding the standing commitment it serves—has a proposed first experiment ([repairing a misleading routing check](../../articles/testing-autonomous-selection-based-learning.md#a-first-experiment-repairing-a-misleading-routing-check)) but no completed run.
- **Whole-loop net gain and hypothesis payoffs without self-grading.** Passing isolated admission, correction, or criterion-revision checks does not establish net gain once search, inquiry, upkeep, human supervision, and pre-rollback losses are counted against static, append-only, fixed-gate, and human-assisted baselines on fresh work. Separately, whether retaining [addressable hypotheses](../definitions/addressable-theory.md) improves sample efficiency under structured shifts or supplies causally faithful explanations remains untested.
- **Outgrowing the hand-crafted bootstrap.** A hand-built starting harness fits the Bitter Lesson [only if automated learning outgrows it](../a-bootstrap-fits-the-bitter-lesson-only-if-learning-outgrows-it.md). No operator-removal test has yet shown that the hard update decisions—noticing problems, diagnosing causes, and judging trade-offs, beyond already-stabilized routine checks—transfer to computation and keep improving as experience and compute grow.
