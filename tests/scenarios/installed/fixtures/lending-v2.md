# Aster Lending Library policy, version 2

This policy takes effect immediately and supersedes version 1. It changes the maximum camera-kit loan duration. The tripod duration and equipment inspection requirements remain unchanged.

## Camera kits

A member may now borrow a camera kit for at most 72 hours. This replaces the previous 48-hour limit. The limit applies to the complete camera kit, including its case and supplied accessories.

## Tripods

A member may borrow a tripod for at most seven days. The tripod limit is separate from the camera-kit limit, including when a member requests both items for the same project.

## Equipment checks

Staff must inspect a returned camera kit before lending it again. Recording the return does not replace the inspection. Check that the kit's supplied parts are present so that the next member receives usable equipment.
