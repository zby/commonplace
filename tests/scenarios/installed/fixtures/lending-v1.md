# Aster Lending Library policy, version 1

This is the lending policy for camera kits and tripods borrowed by members of Aster Lending Library. Staff should use these limits when answering member questions and agreeing return times.

## Camera kits

A member may borrow a camera kit for at most 48 hours. The limit applies to the complete camera kit, including its case and supplied accessories. Staff should agree a return time within this limit when recording the loan.

## Tripods

A member may borrow a tripod for at most seven days. The tripod limit is separate from the camera-kit limit, including when a member requests both items for the same project.

## Equipment checks

Staff must inspect a returned camera kit before lending it again. Recording the return does not replace the inspection. Check that the kit's supplied parts are present so that the next member receives usable equipment.
